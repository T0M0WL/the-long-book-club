The Art of Organic Technology

Coined by author Ken Liu in The Grace of Kings, silkpunk melds historical East Asian antiquity with biomechanical engineering—ox-sinew ballistas, bamboo airships, and bio-enhanced jade. Narrated by masters like Kevin Shen and Natalie Naudus, these sweeping sagas prioritize philosophical statecraft, rebellion, and cultural depth over standard Western medieval tropes.
