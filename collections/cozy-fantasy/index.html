Best Long Cozy Fantasy & Slice-of-Life Audiobooks

Escape apocalyptic stress with heartwarming low-stakes fantasy, charming magical sanctuaries, and soothing vocal performances that soothe your daily commute.

Low Stakes, High Comfort

Cozy fantasy strips away world-ending perils in favor of seaside orphanages, magical bakeries, and gentle communal healing. Pioneered by novels like TJ Klune’s The House in the Cerulean Sea, these slice-of-life listens prioritize warmth, eccentric found families, and comforting humor—proving that emotional stakes can be just as compelling as battlefield clashes.
