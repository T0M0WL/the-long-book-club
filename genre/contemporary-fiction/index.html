Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • The Goldfinch by Donna Tartt (32h 24m)

    Narrated by: David Pittu

    32 hours of art and grief. A Dickensian novel for the modern age, centered on a stolen painting.

  • A Little Life by Hanya Yanagihara (32h 51m)

    Narrated by: Oliver Wyman

    33 hours of heartbreak. A devastatingly beautiful portrait of male friendship and trauma.

  • American Gods by Neil Gaiman (19h 36 hours)

    Narrated by: Neil Gaiman, Dennis Boutskiaris, Daniel Oreskes, Ron McLarty, Sarah Jones,

    Shadow Moon enters a hidden world where ancient myths live among us. Almost 20 hours of masterful full-cast, cinematic storytelling directed by the author himself.

  • Demon Copperhead by Barbara Kingsolver (21h 3m)

    Narrated by: Charlie Thurston

    21 hours of fierce Appalachian grit. A modern, Pulitzer-winning reimagining of David Copperfield.

  • The Bee Sting by Paul Murray (26h 10m)

    Narrated by: Heather O’Sullivan, Barry Fitzgerald, Beau Holland, Ciaran O'Brien, Lisa Caruccio Came,

    A 26-hour tragicomic saga. Funny, heartbreaking, and structurally brilliant. Worth every minute of your credit.

  • Caledonian Road by Andrew O'Hagan (22h 51m)

    Narrated by: Michael Abubakar

    A massive 23-hour chronicle of modern London. Tackles everything from aristocrats to drill rappers in one ambitious sweep.

  • The Little Friend by Donna Tartt (24h 22m)

    Narrated by: Donna Tartt

    24 hours of Southern Gothic suspense. A fierce young girl unearths dark small-town secrets, narrated by Donna Tartt.
