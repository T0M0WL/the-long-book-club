Best Long Dark Academia Audiobooks

Dark Academia listeners famously divide into two camps: Purists who want grounded psychological tragedy driven by human hubris (like The Secret History and If We Were Villains), and Expansionists who embrace brooding Gothic campuses fused with occult or rule-based magic (like Ninth House, Babel, and The Atlas Six). Explore both sides in our long-form audio library.
