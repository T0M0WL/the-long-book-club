Best Long British Literature Audiobooks

Curated selections for British Literature. Explore 4 long-form audiobooks totaling over 76 hours of immersive listening.

All TLBC British Literature Audiobooks

  • World Without End by Ken Follett (45h 34m)

    Narrated by: John Lee

    46 hours of medieval mastery. Four lives collide across plague, war, and gothic architecture in Kingsbridge.

  • The Thursday Murder Club by Richard Osman (12h 25m)

    Narrated by: Lesley Manville

    Four retired sleuths investigate unsolved murders in Kent until a real body turns up. Witty, warm British mystery.

  • The Reddening by Adam Nevill (12h 19m)

    Narrated by: Conner Goff

    Over 12 hours of prehistoric folk horror. Ancient bones unearthed on the Devon coast awaken a blood-drenched ritual.

  • The Sweetness at the Bottom of the Pie by Alan Bradley (6h 36m)

    Narrated by: Emilia Fox (UK) / Jayne Entwistle (US)

    An 11-year-old chemist sleuth investigates a dead body in her cucumber patch. Jayne Entwistle’s hilarious 6.5-hour debut.
