Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • Les Misérables by Victor Hugo (67h)

    Narrated by: Bill Homewood

    60 hours of revolution and redemption. The musical is great, but the novel is a universe unto itself.

  • The Goldfinch by Donna Tartt (32h 24m)

    Narrated by: David Pittu

    32 hours of art and grief. A Dickensian novel for the modern age, centered on a stolen painting.

  • A Little Life by Hanya Yanagihara (32h 51m)

    Narrated by: Oliver Wyman

    33 hours of heartbreak. A devastatingly beautiful portrait of male friendship and trauma.

  • Anna Karenina by Leo Tolstoy (35h 35m)

    Narrated by: Maggie Gyllenhaal

    35 hours of high-stakes Russian drama. Often cited as the greatest novel ever written.

  • A Storm of Swords by George R.R. Martin (47h 34m)

    Narrated by: Roy Dotrice

    47 hours of twists. The Red Wedding awaits in the series' most shocking volume.

  • Dune Messiah by Frank Herbert (9h 7m)

    Narrated by: Scott Brick, Simon Vance, et al.

    9 hours of consequences. A tighter, tragic coda to Dune that deconstructs the hero myth.

  • Wuthering Heights by Emily Brontë (14h 2m)

    Narrated by: Joanne Froggatt

    14 hours on the moors. A dark, passionate story of obsession that refuses to be tamed.

  • The Hunchback of Notre-Dame by Victor Hugo (19h 2m)

    Narrated by: Bill Homewood

    19 hours in medieval Paris. Hugo's architecture-obsessed tragedy is far darker than the Disney version.

  • The Grapes of Wrath by John Steinbeck (21h 10m)

    Narrated by: Dylan Baker

    21 hours on the Dust Bowl highway. Steinbeck's Great American Novel is even more devastating and urgent by ear.

  • The Jane Austen Collection by Jane Austen (45 hours)

    Narrated by: Claire Foy, Gugu Mbatha-Raw , Billie Piper, Florence Pugh, Emma Thompson,

    The definitive Austen experience featuring forty-five hours of dramatised performance. Immerse yourself in the wit and romance of Regency England with an all-star cast.

  • The Idiot by Fyodor Dostoevsky (24h 56m)

    Narrated by: Constantine Gregory

    25 hours of psychological genius. A pure, saintly soul descends into the treacherous parlor games of St. Petersburg.

  • Resurrection by Leo Tolstoy (19h 24m)

    Narrated by: Malk Williams

    19 hours of moral redemption. An aristocrat journeys to Siberia to save the woman he wronged in Tolstoy's final masterpiece.

  • Troy: Stephen Fry's Greek Myths, Book 3 by Stephen Fry (11h 10m)

    Narrated by: Stephen Fry

    The legendary ten-year siege of Troy retold with sparkling wit and tragedy. 11 hours of Stephen Fry in audio.

  • The Rise of Endymion: Hyperion Cantos, Book 4 by Dan Simmons (29h 47m)

    Narrated by: Victor Bevine

    Nearly 30 hours of cosmic climax. Aenea and Raul Endymion unlock the ultimate mysteries of the Shrike and humanity.
