Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • Cryptonomicon by Neal Stephenson (42h 53m)

    Narrated by: William Dufris

    43 hours of code-breaking. WWII espionage meets modern computing in this geek masterpiece.

  • The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich by William L. Shirer (57h 11m)

    Narrated by: Grover Gardner

    57 hours of Nazi history. The definitive account of the darkest chapter in modern history.

  • The Amazing Adventures of Kavalier & Clay by Michael Chabon (26h 15m)

    Narrated by: David Colacci

    26 hours of Golden Age comic books, escapism, and wartime New York. A Pulitzer-winning American epic.

  • Churchill: Walking with Destiny by Andrew Roberts (50h 28m)

    Narrated by: Stephen Thorne

    50 hours that feel like a breeze. The definitive portrait of the man who saved the world.

  • Carrion Comfort by Dan Simmons (39h 27m)

    Narrated by: Mel Foster, Laural Merlington

    39 hours of mind games. Psychic vampires manipulating history in a sprawling thriller.

  • Eye of the Needle by Ken Follett (11h 1m)

    Narrated by: Samuel West

    11 hours of WWII espionage. A ruthless German master spy with a stiletto races to alert Hitler before D-Day launches.

  • Restless by William Boyd (10h 11m)

    Narrated by: Rosamund Pike

    11 hours of dual-timeline espionage. A daughter discovers her eccentric mother was an elite WWII British secret agent.

  • A History of Fascism, 1914–1945 by Stanley G. Payne (21h 35m)

    Narrated by: Michael Kramer

    The definitive 21-hour comparative history of interwar authoritarianism, ultranationalism, and wartime collapse.
