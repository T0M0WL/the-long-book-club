Best Long War Audiobooks

Explore our selection of epic War audiobooks.

Welcome to our curated collection of the best and longest War audiobooks, all 20+ hours in length.

Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • Les Misérables by Victor Hugo (67h)

    Narrated by: Bill Homewood

    60 hours of revolution and redemption. The musical is great, but the novel is a universe unto itself.

  • The Way of Kings by Brandon Sanderson (45h 37m)

    45 hours of world-building. The start of the Stormlight Archive masterfully balances magic and character.

  • A Game of Thrones by George R.R. Martin (33h 46m)

  • The Pillars of the Earth by Ken Follett (40h 55m)

  • War and Peace by Leo Tolstoy (61h 8m)

    Narrated by: Thandiwe Newton

  • The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich by William L. Shirer (57h 11m)

    Narrated by: Grover Gardner

    57 hours of Nazi history. The definitive account of the darkest chapter in modern history.

  • Gone with the Wind by Margaret Mitchell (49h 7m)

    Narrated by: Charlton Griffin

  • The Two Towers by J.R.R. Tolkien (16h 44m)

  • The Return of the King by J.R.R. Tolkien (18h 12m)

    Narrated by: Rob Inglis, Andy Serkis

  • Words of Radiance by Brandon Sanderson (48h 14m)

  • Oathbringer by Brandon Sanderson (55h 5m)

  • Rhythm of War by Brandon Sanderson (57h 26m)

  • A Clash of Kings by George R.R. Martin (37h 16m)

  • A Storm of Swords by George R.R. Martin (47h 34m)

    Narrated by: Roy Dotrice

    47 hours of twists. The Red Wedding awaits in the series' most shocking volume.

  • A Feast for Crows by George R.R. Martin (31h 10m)

  • The Well of Ascension by Brandon Sanderson (27h 14m)

  • Judas Unchained by Peter F. Hamilton (41h 22m)

  • Doctor Zhivago by Boris Pasternak (21h 56m)

  • Wind and Truth by Brandon Sanderson (62h 48m)

  • Shōgun by James Clavell (53h 33m)

  • Iron Flame by Rebecca Yarros (28h 16m)

  • Churchill: Walking with Destiny by Andrew Roberts (50h 28m)

  • Fourth Wing by Rebecca Yarros (22h 2m)
