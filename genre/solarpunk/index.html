Best Long Solarpunk Audiobooks

What happens after the wasteland when humanity chooses to rebuild? Explore hopeful, reconstructive sci-fi audiobooks where solar architecture, rewilded cities, and cooperative communities fight to heal the planet.

Welcome to our curated collection of the best and longest Solarpunk audiobooks, all 20+ hours in length.

Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • The Ministry for the Future by Kim Stanley Robinson (20h 42m)

    Narrated by: Jennifer Fitzgerald, Fajer Al-Kaisi, Ramon de Ocampo, et al.

    Over 20 hours of full-cast climate sci-fi. A global mosaic of humanity fighting to rebuild and heal the planet.

  • Walkaway by Cory Doctorow (19h 15m)

    Narrated by: Amber Benson, Wil Wheaton, Mirron Willis, Amanda Palmer, et al.

    19 hours of rebellious solarpunk sci-fi. Outcasts abandon corporate cities to build open-source abundance.

  • A Half-Built Garden by Ruthanna Emrys (15h 50m)

    Narrated by: Emily Ellet

    Nearly 16 hours of hopeful solarpunk first contact. Ecological networks negotiate Earth's future with alien visitors.

  • New York 2140 by Kim Stanley Robinson (22h 34m)

    Narrated by: Suzanne Toren, Robin Miles, Peter Ganim, Jay Snyder, Caitlin Kelly, et al.

    Over 22 hours of full-cast Solarpunk sci-fi. A flooded Venice-like Manhattan fights off billionaire speculators.

  • The Terraformers by Annalee Newitz (13h 36m)

    Narrated by: Emily Lawrence

    13.5 hours of sweeping ecological Solarpunk sci-fi spanning centuries of planet-building and animal-human solidarity.
