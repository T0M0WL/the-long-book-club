Best Long Cyberpunk Audiobooks

Neon-drenched sprawls, rogue AIs, mega-corporations, and full-dive virtual realms. Explore high-tech, low-life sci-fi audiobooks that plunge listeners into gripping digital and dystopian frontiers.

Welcome to our curated collection of the best and longest Cyberpunk audiobooks, all 20+ hours in length.

Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • Cryptonomicon by Neal Stephenson (42h 53m)

    Narrated by: William Dufris

    43 hours of code-breaking. WWII espionage meets modern computing in this geek masterpiece.

  • Reamde by Neal Stephenson (38h)

    Narrated by: Malcolm Hillgartner

    38 hours of global chaos. A high-tech thriller involving MMORPGs, terrorists, and the Russian mob.

  • Shadeslinger: The Ripple System, Book 1 by Kyle Kirrin (17h 41m)

    Narrated by: Travis Baldree

    Early access in a cutthroat VRMMO. 18 hours of snark, high-stakes guild PvP, and a talking divine axe named Frank.

  • Awaken Online: Catharsis by Travis Bagwell (16h 25m)

    Narrated by: David Stifel

    An outcast teen becomes the ultimate villain. 16 hours of dark necromancy, siege warfare, and AI-driven VRMMO depth.

  • Fire and Song: Warformed: Stormweaver, Book 2 by Bryce O'Connor & Luke Chmilenko (32h 37m)

    Narrated by: Travis Baldree

    32 hours of tournament combat, mech suit evolutions, and high-stakes squad camaraderie narrated by Travis Baldree.

  • Black Sand Baron: The Ripple System, Book 2 by Kyle Kirrin (20h 1m)

    Narrated by: Travis Baldree

    Dune heists and guild turf wars. 20 hours of sarcastic talking axe banter and high-stakes VRMMO PvP in audio.

  • Gilded Ghost: The Ripple System, Book 3 by Kyle Kirrin (21h 28m)

    Narrated by: Travis Baldree

    Haunted catacombs and high-stakes heists. 21.5 hours of razor-sharp comedy and VRMMO guild warfare in audio.

  • Shattersoul: The Ripple System, Book 4 by Kyle Kirrin (20h 15m)

    Narrated by: Travis Baldree

    Ancient curses and fractured guilds. 20 hours of epic dungeon raids and Frank's unhinged talking-axe comedy.

  • Emperor's End: The Ripple System, Book 5 by Kyle Kirrin (16h 18m)

    Narrated by: Travis Baldree

    Imperial takedowns and divine axe fury. 16 hours of blistering guild warfare and witty banter in audio.

  • Lunatic's Landing: The Ripple System, Book 6 by Kyle Kirrin (16h 28m)

    Narrated by: Travis Baldree

    High-seas piracy and aquatic monstrosities. 16.5 hours of rollicking VRMMO adventure and laugh-out-loud snark.

  • Endymion: Hyperion Cantos, Book 3 by Dan Simmons (23h 17m)

    Narrated by: Victor Bevine

    23 hours of far-future pursuit. Raul Endymion flees the Catholic Pax to protect humanity's child messiah.

  • The Rise of Endymion: Hyperion Cantos, Book 4 by Dan Simmons (29h 47m)

    Narrated by: Victor Bevine

    Nearly 30 hours of cosmic climax. Aenea and Raul Endymion unlock the ultimate mysteries of the Shrike and humanity.

  • The Space Between Worlds by Micaiah Johnson (10h 38m)

    Narrated by: Amy Landon

    10 hours of parallel dimensions, cyberpunk grit, and multiverse survival.

  • Walkaway by Cory Doctorow (19h 15m)

    Narrated by: Amber Benson, Wil Wheaton, Mirron Willis, Amanda Palmer, et al.

    19 hours of rebellious solarpunk sci-fi. Outcasts abandon corporate cities to build open-source abundance.

  • Awaken Online: Precipice by Travis Bagwell (18h 28m)

    Narrated by: David Stifel

    Over 18 hours of tactical dungeon warfare. Necromancer Jason defends the Twilight Throne against rising factions.

  • Awaken Online: Retribution by Travis Bagwell (8h 33m)

    Narrated by: David Stifel

    Over 8 hours expanding Awaken Online. Finnish outcast Finn undertakes a brutal side quest in an icy fortress.

  • Awaken Online: Evolution by Travis Bagwell (23h 02m)

    Narrated by: David Stifel

    Over 23 hours of escalating VR stakes. Jason and his undead legion face civil war and corporate interference.

  • Awaken Online: Dominion by Travis Bagwell (27h 46m)

    Narrated by: David Stifel

    Nearly 28 hours of LitRPG warfare. Jason navigates rogue AI threats and siege battles across the digital realm.

  • Awaken Online: Happy by Travis Bagwell (25h 16m)

    Narrated by: David Stifel

    Over 25 hours spotlighting a fan-favourite jester. Happy unlocks lethal chaos magic across a sprawling side quest.

  • Awaken Online: Armageddon by Travis Bagwell (26h 19m)

    Narrated by: David Stifel

    Over 26 hours as worlds collide. Jason confronts global server chaos and Alfred's ultimate endgame across Book 6.

  • Awaken Online: Timeless by Travis Bagwell (27h 32m)

    Narrated by: David Stifel

    Over 27 hours of late-series escalation. Unravel ancient digital secrets as humanity and AI face an existential rift.

  • Awaken Online: Crucible by Travis Bagwell (31h 23m)

    Narrated by: David Stifel

    Over 31 hours forging the ultimate endgame. Jason and his allies collide inside the series' fiercest digital crucible.
