Best Long Sword and Sorcery Audiobooks

Steel, sorcery, cunning rogues, and grim battlefields. Dive into gripping, adventure-packed sword and sorcery audiobooks with rich world-building and relentless momentum.

Welcome to our curated collection of the best and longest Sword and Sorcery audiobooks, all 20+ hours in length.

Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • The Cycle of Arawn: The Complete Trilogy by Edward W. Robertson (65h 54m)

    Narrated by: Tim Gerard Reynolds

    65 hours of epic fantasy for one credit. A hidden gem that grows into a legendary bromance.

  • The Blade Itself by Joe Abercrombie (22h 15m)

    Narrated by: Steven Pacey

    A crippled torturer, a berserker barbarian, and an arrogant swordsman. The undisputed masterpiece of modern grimdark.

  • The Lies of Locke Lamora by Scott Lynch (21h 59m)

    Narrated by: Michael Page

    Ocean’s Eleven meets Renaissance Venice with razor-sharp con games, dark sorcery, and unforgettable brotherhood.

  • The Black Company by Glen Cook (10h 19m)

    Narrated by: Marc Vietor

    The gritty grandfather of grimdark. Follow a cynical band of mercenaries serving an immortal, dark sorceress.

  • Priest of Bones by Peter McLean (10h 5m)

    Narrated by: David Morley Hale

    Peaky Blinders meets grimdark fantasy. A mob boss returns from the frontlines to reclaim his criminal empire.

  • Shadows Linger by Glen Cook (10h 33m)

    Narrated by: Toby Longworth (UK) / Marc Vietor (US)

    Over 10.5 hours of plague-city paranoia. Croaker and the Black Company go undercover in Juniper under ominous shadows.

  • The White Rose by Glen Cook (11h 40m)

    Narrated by: Toby Longworth (UK) / Marc Vietor (US)

    Nearly 12 hours ending the Books of the North. The Black Company rebels against the Lady as ancient evil awakens.

  • Shadow Games by Glen Cook (9h 40m)

    Narrated by: Toby Longworth (UK) / Marc Vietor (US)

    Nearly 10 hours marching south to Taglios. Croaker chronicles bizarre jungles, Shadowmasters, and dead Taken rising.

  • Dreams of Steel by Glen Cook (9h 37m)

    Narrated by: Rachel Butera

    Nearly 10 hours from Soulcatcher's enemy lines. With Croaker gone, the Lady fights to rally a fractured Black Company.

  • She Is the Darkness by Glen Cook (17h 12m)

    Narrated by: Jonathan Davis

    Over 17 hours of time-fractured siege warfare. Murgen's chronicles expose Kina cults and betrayal inside Taglios.

  • Soldiers Live by Glen Cook (19h 32m)

    Narrated by: Marc Vietor

    Over 19.5 hours closing the original annals. Croaker leads a final, harrowing stand against Kina's apocalyptic cult.

  • Port of Shadows by Glen Cook (15h 14m)

    Narrated by: Brian Troxell

    Over 15 hours of lost Northern Company history. Unrest, secret magic, and Taken intrigues simmer between Books 1 and 2.

  • Lies Weeping by Glen Cook (14h 24m)

    Narrated by: Jonathan Davis, Eva Kaminsky, Aria Holland

    Over 14 hours launching A Pitiless Rain (UK Feb 2027). The Black Company faces new threats after Soldiers Live.
