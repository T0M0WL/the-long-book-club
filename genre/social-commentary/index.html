Best Long Social Commentary Audiobooks

Explore our selection of epic Social Commentary audiobooks.

Welcome to our curated collection of the best and longest Social Commentary audiobooks, all 20+ hours in length.

Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • Les Misérables by Victor Hugo (67h)

    Narrated by: Bill Homewood

    60 hours of revolution and redemption. The musical is great, but the novel is a universe unto itself.

  • David Copperfield by Charles Dickens (36h 30m)

    Narrated by: Richard Armitage

    36 hours of Dickens' favorite child. A warm, personal coming-of-age story narrated by Richard Armitage.

  • Anna Karenina by Leo Tolstoy (35h 35m)

    Narrated by: Maggie Gyllenhaal

    35 hours of high-stakes Russian drama. Often cited as the greatest novel ever written.

  • Middlemarch by George Eliot (32h)

    Narrated by: Maureen O'Brien

    A 32-hour study of provincial life. Smart, unhurried, and startlingly modern.

  • Bleak House by Charles Dickens (35h)

    Narrated by: Miriam Margolyes

    35 hours of legal satire. Dickens at his peak, tackling a lawsuit as endless as the audiobook itself.

  • Great Expectations by Charles Dickens (18h 44m)

    Narrated by: Martin Jarvis

    19 hours with Pip. Dickens' tightest plot explores class, ambition, and the lies we tell ourselves.

  • Vanity Fair by William Makepeace Thackeray (31h 22m)

    Narrated by: Helen Taylor

    31 hours without a hero. A sharp, cynical satire of society that feels uncomfortably modern.

  • The Hunchback of Notre-Dame by Victor Hugo (19h 2m)

    Narrated by: Bill Homewood

    19 hours in medieval Paris. Hugo's architecture-obsessed tragedy is far darker than the Disney version.

  • The Grapes of Wrath by John Steinbeck (21h 10m)

    Narrated by: Dylan Baker

    21 hours on the Dust Bowl highway. Steinbeck's Great American Novel is even more devastating and urgent by ear.

  • Demon Copperhead by Barbara Kingsolver (21h 3m)

    Narrated by: Charlie Thurston

    21 hours of fierce Appalachian grit. A modern, Pulitzer-winning reimagining of David Copperfield.

  • The Complete Novels of Charles Dickens: Part One by Charles Dickens (199h 26m)

    Narrated by: Juliet Stevenson, Jason Isaacs, Jonathan Keeble, David Rintoul, Malk Williams, Ben Allen

    199 hours. The first six major novels. The heavyweight champion of value per credit.

  • Resurrection by Leo Tolstoy (19h 24m)

    Narrated by: Malk Williams

    19 hours of moral redemption. An aristocrat journeys to Siberia to save the woman he wronged in Tolstoy's final masterpiece.
