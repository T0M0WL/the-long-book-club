Best Long Surrealism Audiobooks

Curated selections for Surrealism. Explore 5 long-form audiobooks totaling over 147 hours of immersive listening.

All TLBC Surrealism Audiobooks

  • 1Q84 by Haruki Murakami (46h 50m)

    Narrated by: Allison Hiroto, Marc Vietor, Mark Boyett

    47 hours of surreal mystery. Moonshine, assassins, and parallel worlds in Murakami's most ambitious work.

  • 2666 by Roberto Bolaño (39h 15m)

    Narrated by: John Lee, Armando Durán, G. Valmont Thomas, Scott Brick, Grover Gardner

    39 hours of hypnotic literary mystery. Five interlocking novels that orbit the dark heart of the 20th century.

  • The Wind-Up Bird Chronicle by Haruki Murakami (26h 11m)

    Narrated by: Rupert Degas

    26 hours down the subterranean well. Missing cats, psychic sisters, and WWII secrets in Murakami's supreme masterpiece.

  • One Hundred Years of Solitude by Gabriel García Márquez (17h 56m)

    Narrated by: Ben Onwukwe (UK) / John Lee (US)

    18 hours in mythical Macondo. Ben Onwukwe turns Márquez's Nobel Prize-winning family saga into an effortless fever dream.

  • The City and Its Uncertain Walls by Haruki Murakami (17h 26m)

    Narrated by: Brian Nishii

    Murakami returns with a 17-hour dreamscape. A perfect, warm blanket of a listen for long winter nights.
