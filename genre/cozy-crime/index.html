Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • The Thursday Murder Club by Richard Osman (12h 25m)

    Narrated by: Lesley Manville

    Four retired sleuths investigate unsolved murders in Kent until a real body turns up. Witty, warm British mystery.

  • Magpie Murders by Anthony Horowitz (15h 15m)

    Narrated by: Allan Corduner, Samantha Bond

    An editor reads an incomplete manuscript and finds the author dead. A dual-narrated 15-hour puzzle box within a puzzle box.

  • The Appeal by Janice Hallett (12h 31m)

    Narrated by: Gabrielle Glaister, David Timson

    A local theatre troupe murder told via passive-aggressive emails and texts. A brilliant 12-hour full-cast investigation.

  • Still Life by Louise Penny (10h 38m)

    Narrated by: Adam Sims, Ralph Cosham

    A beloved teacher is found dead by an arrow in idyllic Three Pines. Gamache's brilliant 10-hour debut village mystery.

  • The Maid by Nita Prose (10h 1m)

    Narrated by: Lauren Ambrose

    A meticulous hotel maid discovers a murdered tycoon and must clear her name. Lauren Ambrose stars in this 10h locked-room gem.

  • The Sweetness at the Bottom of the Pie by Alan Bradley (6h 36m)

    Narrated by: Jayne Entwistle

    An 11-year-old chemist sleuth investigates a dead body in her cucumber patch. Jayne Entwistle’s hilarious 6.5-hour debut.

  • Moonflower Murders by Anthony Horowitz (18h 30m)

    Narrated by: Lesley Manville, Allan Corduner

    A missing bride leaves clues in an Atticus Pünd novel. Lesley Manville & Allan Corduner star in an 18.5h meta-mystery.

  • Marble Hall Murders by Anthony Horowitz (17h 38m)

    Narrated by: Lesley Manville, Tim McMullan

    Susan Ryeland tackles a cold case at Marble Hall with an unreleased manuscript. 17.5 hours of sublime dual narration.
