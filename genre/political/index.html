Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • Atlas Shrugged by Ayn Rand (63h)

    Narrated by: Scott Brick

    63 hours of philosophy and railways. Love it or hate it, this massive treatise demands your full attention.

  • Leviathan Wakes by James S.A. Corey (19h 9m)

    Narrated by: Jefferson Mays

    19 hours that launched The Expanse. Noir detective meets space opera in the best sci-fi series of the decade.

  • World War Z by Max Brooks (13 hours 56 mins)

    Narrated by: Christopher Ragland, Rupert Farley, Nigel Pilkington, Jennifer Woodward, David Thorpe, Adam Sims, Robert Slade

    The most famous full-cast production in audio history tells the story of the zombie war. A 14 hour multi-voice audiobook featuring global accounts of post-apocalyptic survival.

  • The Hands of the Emperor by Victoria Goddard (31h 37m)

    Narrated by: Niel Hellegers

    A 31-hour cozy epic of friendship, quiet governance, and an emperor who just needs a peaceful holiday.

  • A Memory Called Empire by Arkady Martine (15h 37m)

    Narrated by: Amy Landon

    15 hours of Hugo-winning political intrigue. An ambassador navigates imperial poetry, seduction, and assassination.

  • A Desolation Called Peace by Arkady Martine (17h 32m)

    Narrated by: Amy Landon

    17.5 hours of first-contact linguistic tension. Mahit Dzmare confronts an incomprehensible alien hive-mind.

  • At the Feet of the Sun by Victoria Goddard (38h 00m)

    Narrated by: Grant Cartwright

    38 hours of profound friendship and mythic questing. Kip journeys across the world to find his retired Emperor.

  • Ancillary Sword: Imperial Radch, Book 2 by Ann Leckie (11h 43m)

    Narrated by: Adjoa Andoh

    11.7 hours of AI perspectives and tea politics. Fleet Captain Breq navigates civil war in the Radch Empire.

  • Ancillary Mercy: Imperial Radch, Book 3 by Ann Leckie (10h 53m)

    Narrated by: Adjoa Andoh

    10.9 hours of revolutionary AI justice. Fleet Captain Breq makes her final stand against the Lord of the Radch.
