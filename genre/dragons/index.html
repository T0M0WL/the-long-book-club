Best Long Dragons Audiobooks

Explore our selection of epic Dragons audiobooks.

Welcome to our curated collection of the best and longest Dragons audiobooks, all 20+ hours in length.

Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • A Dance with Dragons by George R.R. Martin (48h 55m)

    Narrated by: Roy Dotrice

    49 hours returning to the North. Jon Snow faces his greatest challenge yet.

  • The Priory of the Orange Tree by Samantha Shannon (25h 52m)

    Narrated by: Liyah Summers

    A 26-hour standalone epic. Dragons, queens, and ancient enemies. No sequels required, just pure immersion.

  • Iron Flame by Rebecca Yarros (28h 16m)

    Narrated by: Rebecca Soler, Teddy Hamilton

    28 hours of high-stakes dragon warfare. Darker, longer, and even more addictive than Fourth Wing.

  • When the Moon Hatched by Sarah A. Parker (20h 13m)

    Narrated by: Sarah Mollo-Christensen, Fajer Al-Kaisi

    A lyrical 20-hour debut. Elemental magic, dragons, and simmering romance. A cozy-yet-epic fantasy.

  • Fourth Wing by Rebecca Yarros (22h 2m)

    Narrated by: Rebecca Soler, Teddy Hamilton

    22 hours of pure obsession. The phenomenon that redefined fantasy romance is the perfect weekend binge.

  • Onyx Storm by Rebecca Yarros (23h 52m)

    Narrated by: Jasmin Walker, Justis Bolding, Teddy Hamilton, Rebecca Soler

    At nearly 24 hours, the stakes have never been higher. The longest entry in the Empyrean saga yet.

  • A Day of Fallen Night by Samantha Shannon (38h 48m)

    Narrated by: Ellie Kendrick, Sheila Atim, Hanako Footman, Saara El-Arifi

    Nearly 39 hours of dragonfire and political fury. A monumental full-cast prequel uncovering the Great Sorrow.

  • Mad Ship by Robin Hobb (35h 19m)

    Narrated by: Anne Flosnik

    Over 35 hours of madness, sea serpents, and dragons. Althea awakens the cursed liveship Paragon with Anne Flosnik.

  • Ship of Destiny by Robin Hobb (38h 24m)

    Narrated by: Anne Flosnik

    Over 38 hours of colossal seafaring warfare. The staggering conclusion to Robin Hobb's Liveship Traders trilogy.
