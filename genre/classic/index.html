Welcome to our curated collection of the best and longest Classic audiobooks, all 20+ hours in length.

Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • One Hundred Years of Solitude by Gabriel García Márquez (17h 56m)

    Narrated by: Ben Onwukwe

    18 hours in mythical Macondo. Ben Onwukwe turns Márquez's Nobel Prize-winning family saga into an effortless fever dream.

  • Midnight's Children by Salman Rushdie (25h 39m)

    Narrated by: Homer Todiwala

    26 hours of Booker-winning magic. Born at the stroke of Indian independence, one telepathic boy mirrors a nation's fate.

  • The House of the Spirits by Isabel Allende (18h 51m)

    Narrated by: Marisol Ramirez, Thom Rivera

    19 hours of ghosts, clairvoyants, and revolution. Three generations of extraordinary women anchor Allende's masterpiece.

  • The Player of Games by Iain M. Banks (11h 26m)

    Narrated by: Peter Kenny

    11 hours of philosophical space opera. A master strategist is dispatched to unseat a brutal empire via an epic game.

  • Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy by John le Carré (12h 8m)

    Narrated by: Simon Russell Beale

    12 hours of supreme Cold War tradecraft. George Smiley unmasks a high-level Soviet mole at the heart of the Circus.

  • The Human Factor by Graham Greene (9h 36m)

    Narrated by: Tim Pigott-Smith

    Over 9 hours of quiet literary suspense. An aging MI6 officer leaks secrets for love rather than communist ideology.

  • A Perfect Spy by John le Carré (19h 59m)

    Narrated by: Rory Kinnear

    20 hours into the soul of a double agent. A disappearing British diplomat pens a secret memoir of his life and treason.
