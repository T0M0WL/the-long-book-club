Welcome to our curated collection of the best and longest Family Saga audiobooks, all 20+ hours in length.

Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • The Brothers Karamazov by Fyodor Dostoevsky (37h 5m)

    Narrated by: Luke Thompson

    A 37-hour murder mystery with a soul. An intense family drama and philosophical debate.

  • Children of Dune by Frank Herbert (19h 42m)

    Narrated by: Scott Brick, Simon Vance, et al.

    20 hours of evolution. The saga continues as the next generation of Atreides wrestle with destiny.

  • Wuthering Heights by Emily Brontë (14h 2m)

    Narrated by: Joanne Froggatt

    14 hours on the moors. A dark, passionate story of obsession that refuses to be tamed.

  • East of Eden by John Steinbeck (25h 32m)

    Narrated by: Simon Manyonda

    Steinbeck's 25-hour magnum opus. A biblical allegory of good and evil set in the Salinas Valley.

  • The Covenant of Water by Abraham Verghese (31h 16m)

    Narrated by: Abraham Verghese

    A sweeping 31-hour masterpiece. Narrated by the author himself, this multigenerational saga flows like a river through time.

  • The Jane Austen Collection by Jane Austen (45 hours)

    Narrated by: Claire Foy, Gugu Mbatha-Raw , Billie Piper, Florence Pugh, Emma Thompson,

    The definitive Austen experience featuring forty-five hours of dramatised performance. Immerse yourself in the wit and romance of Regency England with an all-star cast.

  • One Hundred Years of Solitude by Gabriel García Márquez (17h 56m)

    Narrated by: Ben Onwukwe

    18 hours in mythical Macondo. Ben Onwukwe turns Márquez's Nobel Prize-winning family saga into an effortless fever dream.

  • Midnight's Children by Salman Rushdie (25h 39m)

    Narrated by: Homer Todiwala

    26 hours of Booker-winning magic. Born at the stroke of Indian independence, one telepathic boy mirrors a nation's fate.

  • The House of the Spirits by Isabel Allende (18h 51m)

    Narrated by: Marisol Ramirez, Thom Rivera

    19 hours of ghosts, clairvoyants, and revolution. Three generations of extraordinary women anchor Allende's masterpiece.

  • Anansi Boys by Neil Gaiman (10h 03m)

    Narrated by: Lenny Henry

    Fat Charlie discovers his dead dad was an African spider god. 10 hours of hilarious Audie-winning vocal genius.

  • Drums of Autumn by Diana Gabaldon (44h 47m)

    Narrated by: Davina Porter

    Nearly 45 hours in the American wilderness. Claire and Jamie build Fraser's Ridge while Brianna braves the stones.

  • The Fiery Cross by Diana Gabaldon (55h 32m)

    Narrated by: Davina Porter

    Over 55 hours of colossal historical immersion. Jamie and Claire navigate the War of the Regulation with Davina Porter.

  • Go Tell the Bees That I Am Gone by Diana Gabaldon (49h 27m)

    Narrated by: Davina Porter

    Nearly 50 hours of family drama and warfare. Davina Porter narrates the ninth volume of Gabaldon's epic saga.

  • Ship of Magic by Robin Hobb (35h 21m)

    Narrated by: Anne Flosnik

    Over 35 hours of nautical fantasy. Sentient wizardwood ships, ruthless pirates, and family tragedy by Robin Hobb.
