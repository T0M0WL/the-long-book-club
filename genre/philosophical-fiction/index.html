Epic Audiobooks in this Genre

  • Atlas Shrugged by Ayn Rand (63h)

    Narrated by: Scott Brick

    63 hours of philosophy and railways. Love it or hate it, this massive treatise demands your full attention.

  • The Brothers Karamazov by Fyodor Dostoevsky (37h 5m)

    Narrated by: Luke Thompson

    A 37-hour murder mystery with a soul. An intense family drama and philosophical debate.

  • Moby Dick by Herman Melville (24h 23m)

    Narrated by: William Hootkins

    24 hours chasing the whale. It's more than an adventure; it's a meditation on obsession and nature.

  • The Magic Mountain by Thomas Mann (35h 15m)

    Narrated by: David Rintoul

    35 hours on the mountain. A philosophical novel of ideas set in a pre-war sanitarium.

  • The Idiot by Fyodor Dostoevsky (24h 56m)

    Narrated by: Constantine Gregory

    25 hours of psychological genius. A pure, saintly soul descends into the treacherous parlor games of St. Petersburg.

  • Resurrection by Leo Tolstoy (19h 24m)

    Narrated by: Malk Williams

    19 hours of moral redemption. An aristocrat journeys to Siberia to save the woman he wronged in Tolstoy's final masterpiece.

  • God Emperor of Dune by Frank Herbert (15h 48m)

    Narrated by: Simon Vance

    3,500 years into Leto II's worm reign. Simon Vance articulates the weirdest, most philosophical chapter of Dune.

  • Heretics of Dune by Frank Herbert (18h 4m)

    Narrated by: Simon Vance, Scott Brick

    1,500 years after the Tyrant. The Bene Gesserit face the lethal Honored Matres across 18 hours of cunning intrigue.

  • Chapterhouse: Dune by Frank Herbert (16h 42m)

    Narrated by: Katherine Kellgren, Scott Brick, Simon Vance

    Frank Herbert's final Dune novel. A powerhouse narrator trio brings the Sisterhood's last stand to life in 16 hours.

  • The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August by Claire North (12h 10m)

    Narrated by: Peter Kenny

    12 hours across recurring centuries. A dying man receives a message from the future: the world is ending too fast.
