How to Squeeze Every Drop of Value Out of Your Audible Credits (and Free Trials)

Published on 2026-02-05 by The Long Book Club

Let’s be honest: Audiobooks are an expensive habit. If you are burning a credit every week on a flimsy 8-hour thriller, your wallet is probably weeping and you’re counting the days until the end of the month when your fresh credit appears.

But here is the secret the industry doesn't want you to know: Length is a feature. If you play the game right, you can legally listen to world-class audiobooks for nearly two years for the price of a few cups of coffee. Here is the Long Book Club strategy for squeezing the system until the pips squeak.

## Phase 1: The "Churn" Strategy

Audible operates like a gym membership; they bank on you forgetting to cancel. But if you are proactive, you can ride a wave of discounts. Audible still lets you listen to books purchased with credits even when you cancel your membership. You own those audio books.

### 1. The Free Trial: Most users start with the standard 30-day free trial (1 Credit). But throughout the year, Audible/Amazon frequently have 3 month Free Trial Offers. Wait for these, often around Amazon Prime days, public holidays and Black Friday.

### 2. The "Don't Go" Deal: If you can’t wait and go for a 1 month free trial, when you go to cancel, the algorithm will often panic and offer you a sweetener—usually 3 months for 99p a month. Take it.

### 3. The Reset: Once you have used those cheap credits, cancel. You need to stay unsubscribed for a "cooling off" period (usually 6 to 12 months) before Amazon considers you a "new" customer again eligible for another free trial.

The Problem: What do you listen to during that 6–12 month blackout period? The Solution: You use your cheap credits to buy the Longest Books on Earth.

## The "Commute Calculator"

So now you’ve bagged a total 592 hours of listening time! What does 592 hours look like in real life?

• The Commuter: If you listen for 30 minutes to work and 30 minutes back (1 hour/day), these three credits will last you 592 working days. That is 2.3 years of commuting without spending another penny.

• The Walker: If you walk at 3mph, you could walk 1,776 miles. That’s like walking from Land's End to John o' Groats… and back again! And you would still be listening to The Brothers Karamazov.
