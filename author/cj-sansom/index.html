Trained historian and bestselling author C.J. Sansom brought Tudor England's mud, incense, and political terror to astonishing life in the beloved Matthew Shardlake historical mystery series. Beginning with Dissolution and culminating in the massive Tombland, Sansom's deeply researched legal thrillers become spellbinding historical audio dramas through Steven Crossley and Anton Lesser's narration.
