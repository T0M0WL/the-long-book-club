A founding architect of modern Scandinavian crime fiction, Henning Mankell introduced the world to brooding, melancholic Inspector Kurt Wallander in Faceless Killers and its acclaimed successors. Performed with weary warmth and understated gravitas by Sean Barrett, Mankell's slow-burn procedurals blend humane social critique with windswept Swedish coastal noir.
