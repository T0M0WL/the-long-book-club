Writing as S.J. Parris, journalist and historian Stephanie Merritt sends real-life heretic philosopher Giordano Bruno into the treacherous spy rings of Elizabethan England. Starting with Heresy and Prophecy, Parris's atmospheric historical thrillers blend Oxford academia, religious conspiracy, and sharp detective work—voiced with richly dramatic authority by Laurence Kennedy.
