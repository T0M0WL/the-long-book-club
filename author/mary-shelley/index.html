At just eighteen years old during the volcanic 'Year Without a Summer' of 1816, Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley invented modern science fiction with *Frankenstein*—replacing divine creation with human hubris and moral reckoning. A decade later in 1826, she performed an equally astonishing feat by publishing *The Last Man*, inventing the secular post-apocalyptic novel and following a solitary wanderer across a plague-emptied Europe. In audio format, Shelley's sweeping Romantic prose sheds its nineteenth-century schoolroom intimidation, transforming into intimate, psychologically devastating marathons of isolation, ambition, and humanity.
