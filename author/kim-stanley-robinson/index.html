Hugo and Nebula Award winner Kim Stanley Robinson is the preeminent literary architect of cli-fi, hard terraforming epics, and solarpunk futures—spanning his monumental Mars Trilogy (Red Mars, Green Mars, Blue Mars), the foundational eco-utopian blueprint Pacific Edge, and his polyphonic climate landmarks New York 2140 and The Ministry for the Future. Across weathered solo narration by Richard Ferrone and Stefan Rudnicki alongside rich multi-voice full casts, Robinson transforms planetary engineering and ecological hope into deeply absorbing marathon listening.
