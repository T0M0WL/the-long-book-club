Four-time August Derleth Award-winning British horror master Adam Nevill is one of modern folk horror's defining voices—turning primeval Scandinavian forests (The Ritual) and mist-shrouded Devon coastlines (The Reddening) into relentless survival nightmares steeped in ancient pagan dread. Delivered with gritty British tension by Matthew Lloyd Davies and Conner Goff, Nevill's audiobooks excel at slow-burn paranoia and unforgettable late-night chills.
