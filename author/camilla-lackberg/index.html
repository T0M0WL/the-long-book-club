Dubbed Sweden's rock star of crime fiction, Camilla Läckberg sets her bestselling Fjällbacka mystery series along a picturesque coastal fishing village hiding generations of dark family secrets. Interweaving present-day police investigations with haunting historical flashbacks, Läckberg's gripping audiobooks pair cozy seaside atmosphere with chilling psychological suspense.
