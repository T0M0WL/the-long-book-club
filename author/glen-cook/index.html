Widely credited as the godfather of modern grimdark fantasy, Glen Cook traded shining prophecies for mud-caked mercenary survival in The Black Company. Told through the wry, laconic annals of company physician Croaker, Cook's influential military-fantasy audiobooks—brought to weathered, cynical life by Marc Vietor—inspired a generation of dark fantasy giants.
