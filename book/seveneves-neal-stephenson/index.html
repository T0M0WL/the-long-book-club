About this Audiobook

A catastrophic event renders the earth a ticking time bomb. In a feverish race against the inevitable, nations around the globe band together.

Neal Stephenson\u2019s Visionary Thirty-One-Hour Hard Sci-Fi Epic of Human Survival in Space

Beginning with the shocking, unexplained fragmentation of the Earth's Moon into seven giant chunks, Neal Stephenson\u2019s *Seveneves* launches an existential countdown against human extinction. When scientists discover that the lunar fragments will continuously collide, grind into white-hot meteoric dust, and initiate a catastrophic 'Hard Rain' that will incinerate the Earth's surface and boil the oceans for five thousand years, humanity unites in a desperate space race to construct an orbital Cloud Ark around the International Space Station to preserve our species. Across decades of brutal orbital disasters, political mutinies, and genetic bottlenecks, a tiny remnant of humanity clings to survival against the unforgiving vacuum of space.

In audio format, Stephenson\u2019s hard science survival epic unfolds with the heart-stopping tension of a real-time orbital disaster documentary and the mythic sweep of an evolutionary odyssey. Co-narrators Mary Robinette Kowal and Will Damron deliver a tour-de-force performance across thirty-two hours, with Kowal brilliantly capturing the harrowing, claustrophobic struggles of the ark's astronauts and engineers, before Damron commands the audacious final act set five thousand years in the future. Highlighted in our Sci-Fi catalog alongside epic space sagas like Hyperion, this recording turns orbital mechanics and genetic engineering into an unforgettable auditory pilgrimage.

Delivering nearly thirty-two hours of masterclass speculative storytelling for a single credit, this release provides sensational value for fans of realistic, rigorous science fiction. Stephenson doesn't cut corners with artificial gravity or magical faster-than-light travel, relying instead on genuine physics, human stubbornness, and maternal sacrifice to forge an unforgettable tale of resurrection. Narrator Soundcheck: Mary Robinette Kowal, Will Damron

Co-narrators Mary Robinette Kowal and Will Damron deliver an impeccable dual-voiced performance that expertly matches the grand, bipartite structure of Stephenson\u2019s novel. Damron takes over for the sweeping five-thousand-year leap forward, bringing a fresh, epic cadence to a radically transformed humanity. Pristine sound design, balanced acoustic mixing, and zero listener fatigue across thirty-one hours make this production an absolute standout.
