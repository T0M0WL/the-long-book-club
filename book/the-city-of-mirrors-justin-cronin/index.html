An Unforgettable Thousand-Year Finale: Scott Brick Concludes The Passage with Heartfelt Majesty

In *The City of Mirrors*, Justin Cronin brings his landmark post-apocalyptic trilogy to a breathtaking, emotionally devastating conclusion. With the Twelve destroyed, humanity dares to leave its fortified compounds and rebuild a peaceful civilization. But in the shadows of a ruined Manhattan, the First—Patient Zero, Timothy Fanning—awaits. When Fanning unleashes an apocalyptic swarm of millions against the human settlements, Peter Jaxon, Amy, and the survivors are forced into a desperate last stand that spans centuries and tests the very soul of mankind.

Scott Brick delivers arguably the finest performance of his storied career in this concluding chapter. The highlight of the entire audiobook is Cronin's extended mid-novel novella detailing Fanning's pre-apocalyptic human life at Harvard—a heartbreaking story of obsessive unrequited love and intellectual jealousy that Brick narrates with sublime, tragic intimacy. When the action explodes into the siege of the walled city of Kerrville, Brick drives the auditory tempo with pulse-pounding urgency. Listeners who invested in The Passage and The Twelve will find this ending deeply rewarding.

At twenty-nine and a half hours, *The City of Mirrors* delivers astronomical value on a single Audible credit. Spanning an entire millennium, it closes out a ninety-hour audio odyssey with rare thematic grace and profound literary resonance.
