About this Audiobook

As Emperor Kuni Garu strives to reform Dara, an existential invasion from across the impassable Wall of Storms tests the empire's brilliant young minds, giant war garinafins, and defensive inventions.

Ken Liu's 29-Hour Sequel Triumph of Silkpunk Warfare, Garinafin Beasts, and Constitutional Law

Extending across nearly twenty-nine colossal hours, *The Wall of Storms* builds masterfully upon its predecessor, expanding Ken Liu’s silkpunk universe into one of the most intellectually rewarding fantasy sagas ever conceived. Years after the ascension of Emperor Kuni Garu, the unified archipelago of Dara faces fracture from within as court factions struggle over civil service examinations, imperial succession, and the role of women in scholarship. But all internal political squabbles are eclipsed when a terrifying, technologically alien invasion force—the Lyucu—crosses the reputedly impassable Wall of Storms on the backs of titanic, dragon-like garinafins. To survive, the younger generation of Dara must abandon outdated military doctrines, inventing revolutionary aeronautical engines and chemical weaponry to confront an existential threat.

In audio, *The Wall of Storms* soars to breathtaking heights of suspense and sensory immersion. The clash of cultures between the scholastic traditions of Dara and the ruthless nomadic hierarchy of the Lyucu transforms into a gripping acoustic drama. Listening to the tactical calculations behind aeronautical kite-battles, the mechanics of flaming pitch bombs, and the deep emotional bonds forged between riders and their colossal flying beasts feels visceral through headphones. In company with epic military campaigns like The Fires of Heaven and the grand tactical maneuvers of Words of Radiance, this second installment raises the stakes of epic fantasy without sacrificing moral nuance or emotional vulnerability.

Exchanging a single Audible credit or token for twenty-nine hours of *The Wall of Storms* offers peerless listening value. Liu achieves the rare sequel feat of surpassing the original novel in character complexity, philosophical daring, and battlefield inventiveness. With its sprawling cast of brilliant mathematicians, daring princesses, and ruthless conquerors, this magnificent recording provides dozens of hours of rich headphone immersion that epic fantasy listeners will savor to the final breath.

Narrator Soundcheck: Kevin Shen

Kevin Shen returns with an even more assured, virtuoso performance that handles Ken Liu’s massive ensemble cast with consummate grace. Shen shines in giving distinct, authentic vocal signatures to the younger generation of protagonists, particularly Princess Théra and the scholarly Zomi Kidosu, while lending a guttural, terrifying gravity to the invading Lyucu chieftains. His pacing during the colossal aerial battles across the Wall of Storms is heart-pounding, yet he never rushes Liu’s meditative passages on language, mathematics, and philosophy. Pristine sound staging and impeccable volume control ensure an effortless, engrossing marathon listen.
