About this Audiobook

Across the desert realm of Chandrar, beast masters, gladiator uprisings, and royal intrigue ignite as the influence of the Wandering Inn reaches distant shores.

A Sun-Drenched Twenty-Nine-Hour Desert Odyssey of Colossal Beasts, Ancient Pharaohs, and Glacial Courage

In *The Empress of Beasts*, the expansive world of *The Wandering Inn* travels across the ocean to the sun-baked, ancient continent of Chandrar. Here, amidst towering pyramids, sprawling oasis bazaars, and blood-soaked gladiatorial sands, the legendary Empress of Beasts rules with an iron fist. As Earthers trapped in Chandrar struggle to survive under tyrannical regimes and organize daring slave revolts, stories of a quirky human innkeeper in Izril who offers shelter to all species begin to circulate like wildfire, inspiring desperate rebellions and daring escapes across the desert wastes.

Andrea Parsneau’s narration in *The Empress of Beasts* brings fresh, exotic energy to the saga. Parsneau effortlessly adopts new vocal profiles for Chandrar’s diverse peoples, from regal desert monarchs to ferocious beast riders and terrifying chimeric monsters. Her ability to pivot between grand-scale chariot clashes and intimate, tender campfire conversations keeps listeners riveted across every hour.
