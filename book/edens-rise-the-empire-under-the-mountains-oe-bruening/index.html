About this Audiobook

Deep beneath the mountains, an exiled prince and an ancient matriarchy clash over subterranean technology, forgotten prophecies, and an empire on the brink of total collapse.

O.E. Bruening's 13-Hour Subterranean Epic of Dynastic Betrayal, Ancient Tech, and Rebellion

Spanning over thirteen gripping hours, *Eden's Rise: The Empire Under the Mountains* by O.E. Bruening is a fresh, evocative subterranean fantasy that combines dynastic court politics with relics of ancient lost technology. Carved into the subterranean roots of an impassable mountain range, the underground empire of Eden has thrived for centuries away from the hostile surface. But beneath its glittering crystal vaulted halls and sprawling fungal caverns, decadent noble houses scheme for imperial control. When an exiled heir returns bearing forbidden knowledge of the world above, the precarious equilibrium between religious inquisitors, rebellious mining clans, and mechanical guilds shatters into open warfare.

In audio, Bruening’s atmospheric worldbuilding comes to life with claustrophobic, visceral immediacy. The acoustic environment—echoing stone tunnels, the rhythmic clank of steam-powered miners, and the hushed conspiracies whispered in dark catacombs—is rendered with cinematic detail through headphones. Featuring the peerless dual-narration synergy of Michael Kramer and Kate Reading, the story flows seamlessly between the gruff martial perspective of tunnel wardens and the aristocratic calculations of subterranean noblewomen. Alongside rich subterranean and high-magic epics in our library like The Alloy of Law and the cavernous underworlds of The Primal Hunter, this production delivers gripping adventure from the opening chapter.

For a single monthly credit or token, thirteen hours of *Eden's Rise* offers tremendous pacing and high-concept entertainment. Bruening creates a compelling, self-contained epic that rewards listeners who love detailed world architectures, dynamic action set-pieces, and high-stakes family drama. Bolstered by the undisputed titans of fantasy narration, this thrilling subterranean journey is a wonderful, high-value listen for anyone seeking fresh speculative adventure.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer, Kate Reading

Narrated by legendary duo Michael Kramer and Kate Reading, Eden's Rise benefits from two of the finest voices in the audio industry. Kramer grounds the subterranean military clashes and rough-hewn miners with his commanding, gravelly baritone, delivering fight sequences with rhythmic precision and weight. Reading complements him flawlessly, bringing aristocratic poise, icy court calculation, and delicate vulnerability to the matriarchal rulers of the underworld. Their collaborative chemistry and pristine audio production elevate Bruening’s underground saga into an immersive, thoroughly engaging listening experience.
