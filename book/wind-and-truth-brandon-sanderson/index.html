The Monumental Sixty-Two-Hour Climax to the First Era of the Stormlight Archive

Brandon Sanderson brings the first monumental era of The Stormlight Archive to an earth-shattering conclusion in this colossal sixty-two-hour high-fantasy juggernaut. With the ten-day countdown to the climactic Contest of Champions dwindling to its final desperate hours, Dalinar Kholin, Kaladin Stormblessed, Shallan Davar, and Szeth-son-son-Vallano must confront both their deepest psychological traumas and the terrifying cosmic machinations of Odium. Traveling deep into the heart of Shinovar to cleanse ancient spren honorblades while planetary war tears Roshar apart, Sanderson delivers breathtaking magical clashes, cosmic Cosmere revelations, and long-awaited emotional resolutions that redefine the modern fantasy genre, providing an epic canvas of unprecedented ambition, military strategy, and emotional resonance.
