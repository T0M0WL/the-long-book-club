Domagoj Kurmaić's 25-Hour Explosive Expansion of Time-Loop Espionage and Continental Mystery

Spanning over twenty-five monumental hours, *Mother of Learning: Arc 2* propels Domagoj Kurmaić’s progression fantasy saga far beyond the academic walls of Cyoria. Having confirmed that fellow student Zach Noveda is also caught in the time loop, Zorian Kazinski forms a cautious alliance with his reckless classmate. Realizing that surviving the month-long loop is no longer enough, the duo embarks on daring continental expeditions across Altazia to investigate the Sovereign Gate—the ancient primordial artifact anchoring their recursive reality—while contending with shadowy mind-mage assassins hunting time travelers.

In audio format, narrator Jack Voraces hits his stride, delivering a masterclass in vocal variety as the cast expands to include foreign diplomats, reclusive alchemists, and terrifying telepathic Aranean matriarchs. Listening to Zorian methodically dissect magical contracts, master spatial transport gates, and confront deep-state conspiracies transforms every reset into a high-stakes heist. Grouped with grand speculative world-building in our catalog like The Way of Kings and the recursive strategy of The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August, this second volume completely obliterates middle-book syndrome.
