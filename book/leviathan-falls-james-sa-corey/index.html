About this Audiobook

The monumental conclusion to the nine-book Expanse saga, as humanity battles hive-mind assimilation and cosmic extinction.

The Final Horizon: Jefferson Mays Concludes The Greatest Space Opera of Our Time

In *Leviathan Falls*, James S.A. Corey brings their nine-novel, decade-spanning space odyssey to a deeply satisfying, emotionally magnificent conclusion. As high consul Winston Duarte attempts to link all of humanity into a hive-mind consciousness to survive the extradimensional cosmic predators attacking human minds, the crew of the *Rocinante* mounts one final, desperate resistance. Jim Holden, Naomi Nagata, and Amos Burton must make an unbearable choice: allow humanity to become a hive-mind slave to alien biology, or sever the Ring network forever, stranding thirteen hundred colonies across the light-years with no return.

Narrator Jefferson Mays brings this two-hundred-hour audio odyssey to a breathtaking finish. Having voiced every entry across nine novels, Mays’ familiarity with these characters allows him to deliver their farewells with profound emotional gravity and tear-inducing intimacy. The philosophical resolution avoids hollow space-battle clichés, offering instead an honest, bittersweet tribute to human perseverance, community, and freedom. Fans of landmark series finales like Return of the King and Death's End will find this ending deeply fulfilling.

At nearly twenty hours for a single credit, *Leviathan Falls* completes what is indisputably the gold standard of modern audiobook sagas. It delivers a triumphant, poignant ending that honors every hour you have invested in this universe, leaving you staring at the stars with profound gratitude.
