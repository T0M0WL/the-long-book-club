About this Audiobook

With old gods rising and the Chromeria besieged by internal betrayal, Kip Guile must forge his own path as an elite warrior while a shadowy order of assassins strikes from the dark.

Brent Weeks’s Epic Middle Volume of Clandestine Assassins, Ancient Cults, and Fractured Faith

In *The Broken Eye*, the third installment of the monumental *Lightbringer* quintet, the theater of conflict shifts from open battlefields to the shadowy corridors of the Chromeria. The mysterious Order of the Broken Eye—an ancient sect of assassins who weaponize invisible paryl and sub-red luxin—begins systematically eliminating drafters, threatening to topple the balance of global power. Kip Guile must navigate a deadly labyrinth of conspiratorial intrigue within the Blackguard, while Teia risks her life as an undercover double agent in the assassin cult’s subterranean network, and Gavin is cast out into a perilous, storm-swept sea.

Listening to this nearly thirty-hour recording is an intoxicating deep-dive into fantasy tradecraft and espionage. Simon Vance showcases the full spectrum of his vocal versatility, creating tension-soaked atmosphere in every clandestine meeting and hidden canal passage. Beside companion masterpieces like The Lies of Locke Lamora and Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy, this recording proves that hard magic systems shine brightest when deployed in quiet, deadly undercover operations.

At twenty-nine and a half hours for a single credit, *The Broken Eye* delivers exceptional long-term listening value. Weeks enriches his world’s metaphysics and ancient history without losing momentum, rewarding attentive fans with jaw-dropping revelations. Investing a credit in this production provides a week of transportive, high-tension entertainment that cements *Lightbringer* as an unmissable audio marathon.

Narrator Soundcheck: Simon Vance

Simon Vance brings unparalleled psychological nuance to this thirty-hour middle volume. Vance handles Teia’s terrifying double-agent tension with hushed, breathless intimacy, making her training with the Broken Eye genuinely chilling. His portrayal of the aging, calculating patriarch Andross Guile remains a masterclass in subtle villainy and familial intimidation. Pristine sound design and balanced dynamics make this sprawling audio epic feel focused and immediate from start to finish.
