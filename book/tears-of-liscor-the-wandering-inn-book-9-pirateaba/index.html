About this Audiobook

In the sorrowful wake of the great siege, Erin and her surviving companions navigate profound grief, artistic healing, and the rising crusade of the Painted Antinium.

A Profound Forty-Five-Hour Symphony of Healing, Artistic Catharsis, and the Rise of the Painted Antinium

In *Tears of Liscor*, pirateaba delivers a masterclass in narrative aftermath, exploring the psychological trauma and spiritual awakening that follows catastrophic war. As Liscor buries its dead, Erin Solstice struggles with the profound sorrow of losing cherished friends, finding an unexpected path to healing through communal cooking, storytelling, and supporting the Antinium in their pursuit of faith and individual expression. Led by the artist Belgrade and the warrior Anand, the worker caste begins painting their chitin with vibrant colors, rejecting their hive mind to embrace personal identity and spiritual purpose.

Listening to *Tears of Liscor* on audio is a deeply cathartic and emotionally resonant experience. Andrea Parsneau captures the raw, quiet grief of survivors with breathtaking gentleness, allowing moments of silence and trembling dialogue to convey immense dramatic weight. Yet amidst the mourning, Parsneau expertly brings back the laughter and quirky optimism that makes the series so beloved, particularly in scenes of Antinium art classes and witch covens. Curated proudly in our Slice of Life and Cozy Fantasy hubs, this volume is pure soul food for listeners.

Spanning forty-five hours and twenty-one minutes, this doorstopper offers staggering credit value. Rather than rushing headlong into the next military campaign, pirateaba honors the fallen by giving grief the time and space it deserves, resulting in some of the most moving character development in contemporary fiction. Investing a credit in this production guarantees nearly two full days of therapeutic, beautifully narrated storytelling.
