About this Audiobook

A sweeping epic set in 1600 feudal Japan. English pilot John Blackthorne is shipwrecked and rises to become a samurai and key player in the rise of the Shogun.

The Definitive Epic of Feudal Japanese Intrigue, Samurai Honor, and Cultural Awakening

James Clavell’s monumental historical epic drops listeners directly into the treacherous waters of feudal Japan in the pivotal year 1600. When English navigator John Blackthorne shipwrecks along the rugged coast of an unfamiliar archipelago, he is instantly cast into a lethal world governed by rigid codes of bushido honor, intricate social etiquette, and ruthless political maneuvering. As the cunning warlord Lord Toranaga schemes to outmaneuver the Council of Regents and claim absolute military rule as Shōgun, Blackthorne must rapidly learn the Japanese language, customs, and martial philosophies to survive, transforming from an outcast barbarian into an indispensable samurai advisor caught in the gears of world-altering history.

Narrator Soundcheck: Ralph Lister

Ralph Lister delivers a masterly narration that brings the grandeur and deadly tension of feudal Japan to vivid life. He infuses John Blackthorne with rough-hewn maritime grit while providing Lord Toranaga with subtle, calculating authority and Lady Mariko with poignant grace. Lister’s pronunciation of Japanese honorifics and vocabulary is handled with natural fluency, maintaining immersion without jarring the listener. His measured pacing sustains suspense across fifty-three hours of complex political maneuvering, backed by clean, warm studio production that lets every whispered conspiracy and sword clash resonate with clarity.
