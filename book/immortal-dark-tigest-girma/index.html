About this Audiobook

When her sister vanishes, lost heiress Kidan Adane infiltrates the elite, Ethiopian-inspired Uxlay University—forcing her into a lethal, blood-soaked alliance with the cruel vampire Susenyos Sagad.

Seventeen Hours of Cutthroat Vampire Dark Academia and Enemies-to-Lovers Obsession

Orphaned heiress Kidan Adane was raised far from the clandestine society where human bloodlines are bonded to immortal vampires known as dranacht. When her twin sister June vanishes, Kidan is convinced the culprit is Susenyos Sagad—the ruthless, magnetic vampire bound to inherit her family's house at the elite, Ethiopian-inspired Uxlay University. To uncover the truth, Kidan must infiltrate Uxlay's brutal academic trials, master the dark philosophy of her ancestors, and survive living under the same roof as the immortal predator she has sworn to destroy.

Experiencing *Immortal Dark* on audio immerses listeners in a lush, menacing campus atmosphere where every seminar discussion carries the threat of violence. Tigest Girma reimagines both vampire mythology and East African heritage through a brooding gothic lens, trading Eurocentric tweed for ancient lineage treaties and blood-soaked dormitory politics. Highlighted in our Dark Academia Collection alongside Ninth House and A Discovery of Witches, this seventeen-hour opener is built for listeners who want their academic intrigue razor-sharp and dangerously romantic.

At seventeen hours and one minute for a single monthly credit, *Immortal Dark* delivers high-stakes mystery, visceral moral grayness, and electric enemies-to-lovers tension—launching a trilogy that continues in Eternal Ruin and concludes with Book 3 (*Infernal Vow*), releasing on 7th January 2027.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jordan Cobb

Jordan Cobb commands all seventeen hours with a rich, simmering vocal performance that matches Kidan's repressed rage and calculating focus. Cobb gives Susenyos a low, velvet menace that makes every verbal sparring match crackle in your headphones, while navigating Ethiopian names and Uxlay's intricate house hierarchy with effortless poise and clean studio clarity.
