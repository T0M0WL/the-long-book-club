About this Audiobook

Two rival PhD students at Cambridge must descend into Hell to rescue their deceased thesis advisor in this dark academia fantasy that blends Dante’s Inferno with academic satire.

R.F. Kuang\u2019s Atmospheric Eighteen-Hour Dark Academia Journey into the Underworld in Audio

Set within the elite, smoke-filled scholarly sanctuaries of Oxford and Cambridge before plunging into the mythical abyss of the Underworld, R.F. Kuang\u2019s *Katabasis* follows two brilliant, rival magicians and classical scholars who embark on a harrowing mythological descent into Hell to rescue the soul of their deceased mentor. Armed with occult linguistic grammars, classical poetry, and dangerous metaphysical theories, the scholars must navigate infernal bureaucracy, horrifying psychological trials, and the treacherous landscapes of the afterlife, all while confronting the corrosive rivalries and dark imperial secrets that bind their scholarly disciplines to violence and exploitation.

On audio, Kuang\u2019s dark academic fantasy achieves a chilling, claustrophobic brilliance that holds listeners spellbound in their headphones from the opening incantation.
