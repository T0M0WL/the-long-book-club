About this Audiobook

After a body bearing stolen mementos is unearthed in Oslo and vicious executions sweep Europe, Joona Linna realizes his supposedly dead nemesis Jurek Walter may have returned to slaughter everyone Joona loves.

Sixteen and a Half Terrifying Hours When the Dead Refuse to Stay Buried

When Oslo police break into the apartment of a suspected grave robber and discover a freezer packed with bizarre human trophies—including the skull of Joona Linna's long-dead wife—every certainty built since The Sandman fractures instantly. Days later, a German investigator turns up slaughtered beside clues linking disparate murders across Europe directly to Joona's fiercest enemy, Jurek Walter. Nobody believes a dead killer could walk again—leaving Joona and Saga Bauer dangerously isolated as an unstoppable predator systematically circles their loved ones.

In audio format, *Lazarus* delivers Kepler's bleakest, most relentless survival thriller yet. Pushing Nordic Noir across snowy European borders, its merciless blend of visceral Crime pursuit, frantic Police Procedural forensics, and paranoid Psychological Thriller terror turns every safe house into a trap, testing Joona's Detective instincts and driving deep Mystery dread.

Running sixteen hours and thirty minutes for one Audible credit, *Lazarus* locks listeners inside a brutal cross-continental chase that reshapes the entire franchise toward The Mirror Man.
