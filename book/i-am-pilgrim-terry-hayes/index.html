About this Audiobook

A former head of a secret US intelligence unit is pulled from retirement into a lethal global manhunt to stop a fanatical terrorist biological strike.

The Monumental 23-Hour Forensic Spy Epic That Redefined Modern Thrillers

Spanning nearly twenty-three breathless hours, *I Am Pilgrim* is a modern espionage leviathan, combining the forensic precision of a high-end crime procedural with the globe-spanning geopolitical dread of a classic Cold War thriller. Author and veteran screenwriter Terry Hayes crafts a protagonist known only by his code name, Pilgrim: the adopted son of a wealthy American family who rose to lead an off-the-books intelligence division so secret that even the White House barely acknowledged its existence. Having faked his own retirement and authored the definitive textbook on forensic investigation, Pilgrim believes he has escaped the shadow world. That illusion shatters when a young woman is found murdered in a cheap Manhattan hotel, her teeth extracted, fingerprints dissolved in acid, and face stripped away using techniques lifted directly from Pilgrim's unpublished manuscript.

What elevates this twenty-three-hour epic above standard airport paperbacks is Hayes’ extraordinary patience and structural ambition. The narrative refuses to rush, taking the necessary time to construct the parallel life story of Pilgrim's nemesis, a radicalized Saudi physician known as the Saracen who is engineering an untreatable strain of vaccine-resistant smallpox. In audio, Christopher Ragland’s narration gives each forensic clue, dead drop, and interrogation scene the pulse-pounding tension of a prestige miniseries. As Pilgrim traces financial trails from Bodrum to Geneva and the dusty borderlands of Syria, the expanded canvas allows the high-stakes tradecraft to feel rigorously authentic, proving that true narrative suspense requires generous breathing room to develop profound emotional weight and credibility.

For audiobook listeners seeking the absolute pinnacle of credit value, *I Am Pilgrim* is an undeniable masterwork that justifies every minute of its extended runtime. It serves as the gripping modern anchor for our Espionage collection, standing shoulder-to-shoulder with classic tradecraft masterpieces like Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy and The Day of the Jackal. Ragland’s propulsive vocal performance ensures that your attention never wanders across nearly a day of total listening, delivering a deeply satisfying, high-calorie listening experience that rewards every credit spent with lasting entertainment and white-knuckle suspense.

Narrator Soundcheck: Christopher Ragland

Christopher Ragland delivers a crisp, razor-sharp performance that handles Hayes’ globe-trotting narrative with relentless drive. Ragland brings a cool, analytical detachment to Pilgrim’s forensic internal monologues, contrasting it brilliantly against the desperate, fanatical conviction of the Saracen. His international accents—spanning Turkish police inspectors, Saudi couriers, and American intelligence chiefs—are distinct, clean, and never caricatured. The pacing never falters across twenty-three hours, ratcheting up unbearable tension during the novel’s final nerve-shredding confrontation.
