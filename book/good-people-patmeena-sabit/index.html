Over Ten Addictive Full-Cast Hours Inside Audible's 2026 Audiobook of the Year

Crowned Audible's 2026 Audiobook of the Year, Patmeena Sabit's debut *Good People* is a masterclass in social suspense built around an audacious documentary-style chorus. When a shocking tragedy strikes the Sharaf family—wealthy, admired pillars of their Northern Virginia Afghan-American community—neighbors, classmates, jealous acquaintances, and grieving relatives step up to the mic to testify, gossip, speculate, and quietly expose the fault lines everyone pretended not to see.

Because the entire novel unfolds through interlocking spoken perspectives, the audio edition isn't merely an adaptation—it is arguably the definitive way to experience the story. Pairing brilliantly with fellow 2026 ensemble standout The Disappearers and generational portraits like American Hagwon, *Good People* turns suburban Family Saga tension into an unputdownable Mystery of perception, privilege, and immigrant pressure.

At ten hours and seven minutes for a single Audible credit, it is one of the year's smartest, most bingeable Full-Cast listens.
