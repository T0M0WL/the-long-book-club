About this Audiobook

An inn is a place to rest, a place to talk and share stories, or a place to find adventure. Or it’s just a place to hide from the goblins and tourists. Erin Solstice, an innkeeper with an unusual clientele, must navigate a world of magic, monsters, and more than a few annoyances in this massive slice-of-life fantasy epic.

The Monumental Forty-Three-Hour Cozy Progression Epic of Innkeeping in a Perilous World

When twenty-year-old American college student Erin Solstice finds herself suddenly transported into the dangerous fantasy realm of Izril, she flees a terrifying encounter with a ferocious dragon only to stumble upon an abandoned, derelict tavern in the middle of nowhere. Rather than setting off on an epic warrior quest to slay dark lords or conquer foreign thrones, Erin cleans the dusty floorboards, boards up the broken windows, and claims the humble [Innkeeper] class, opening her doors to wandering adventurers, cynical drakes, eccentric ant-like Antinium workers, and ferocious goblins alike. Author pirateaba weaves a colossal, slow-burn tapestry that seamlessly balances slice-of-life cozy comfort, culinary discoveries, and chess games against sudden, visceral bursts of terrifying military siege, racial prejudice, and high-stakes survival.

Listening to this sprawling epic on audio elevates pirateaba’s expansive storytelling into a cozy, deeply therapeutic listening ritual of unmatched emotional warmth. The gentle clatter of wooden tankards, the sizzle of blue-fruit pasta, and the warmth of a hearth fire contrast sharply with the howl of winter blizzards and encroaching undead hordes, drawing listeners into an extraordinarily intimate fantasy community. Featured as a towering titan in our LitRPG & GameLit Audiobooks and our Cozy Fantasy Audiobooks selections, this forty-three-hour production turns world-building into a living, breathing second home where you will want to linger for hundreds of hours across seasons.

Running at an astonishing forty-three hours for a single credit, this release offers an extraordinary value proposition for fans of expansive, character-rich fantasy literature. pirateaba takes the time to let every friendship, moral dilemma, and culinary breakthrough develop organically, resulting in an emotional payoff during the climactic siege of Liscor that will leave you weeping into your steering wheel. Spending a single credit secures an entire month of comforting, deeply rewarding audio entertainment, launching one of the greatest and longest listening sagas ever recorded in the English language.
