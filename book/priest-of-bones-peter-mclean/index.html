About this Audiobook

Tomas Piety returns home from foreign war as a decorated army priest and gang boss, only to find his criminal empire in the city of Ellinburg stolen by rival syndicates and corrupt authorities.

A Brutal, Swaggering Street-Level Gang War Powered by Traumatized Veterans

Pitch-perfect in its gritty, low-fantasy execution, Peter McLean\\u2019s *Priest of Bones* is widely heralded as "Peaky Blinders with swords and dark sorcery." The story follows Tomas Piety, a former criminal loan shark and tavern owner who was drafted into the queen\\u2019s brutal foreign wars, rising to the rank of sergeant-priest in the Pious Men regiment. Returning from the mud and slaughter of Abath alongside his battle-shocked younger brother Jochan and a crew of hardened veterans, Piety arrives in his hometown of Ellinburg only to discover his illicit business empire has been usurped by rival syndicates and treasonous nobles.

Narrated in a sharp, swaggering first-person confessional voice, the audio edition of *Priest of Bones* is an absolute triumph thanks to the brilliant performance of David Morley Hale. Piety is a ruthless pragmatist who would rather solve disputes with a quiet drink than a bloodbath\\u2014but who will not hesitate to slit throats, burn down warehouses, and recruit terrifying wild sorcerers when pushed. Hale captures Tomas\\u2019s dry, laconic British cadence and profound PTSD with arresting vulnerability. Listeners who relished the cynical con games in The Lies of Locke Lamora or the grim martial realism of The Blade Itself will find Piety\\u2019s ruthless rise through the criminal underworld utterly addictive.

Running at a punchy 10 hours and 5 minutes, *Priest of Bones* is a masterclass in tight, propulsive pacing that delivers maximum entertainment for an Audible credit. McLean crafts a dark, atmospheric city thick with chimney smoke, sawdust taverns, and simmering violence, where family loyalty is the only currency that matters. McLean crafts an authentic, heart-wrenching depiction of post-war trauma, showing how men who survived the bloody trenches struggle to find peace when their only trade is violence. Piety's razor-sharp voice never flinches from the moral cost of power. It is an exceptional, hardboiled crime fantasy that sets up an unforgettable four-part saga for anyone who loves their antiheroes gritty, street-smart, and fiercely loyal.

Narrator Soundcheck: David Morley Hale

David Morley Hale turns in a pitch-perfect, raspy performance that brings Tomas Piety to life with swagger, weariness, and menacing charisma. Hale\u2019s first-person delivery feels like sitting across a corner tavern table from a dangerous veteran recounting his confessions over ale. He skillfully differentiates Piety\u2019s cold tactical logic from his brother Jochan\u2019s unhinged, violent PTSD outbursts, keeping the dialogue punchy, realistic, and steeped in authentic working-class British grit throughout.
