About this Audiobook

Driven by hubris to conquer death, young scientist Victor Frankenstein assembles a sentient creature from charnel-house remnants—unleashing a tragic pursuit from Geneva to the Arctic ice.

Mary Shelley’s Unabridged Gothic Masterpiece Brought to Life by Colin Salmon and Peter Noble

When Arctic explorer Captain Robert Walton’s ship becomes trapped in impenetrable pack ice en route to the North Pole, his crew rescues an emaciated, half-frozen stranger drifting across the floes on a dog sled. That stranger is Victor Frankenstein, a brilliant young natural philosopher from Geneva who offers his harrowing life story as a warning against unchecked ambition. Consumed by a desire to conquer mortality, Victor pieces together a sentient creature from charnel-house remnants and sparks it to life—only to flee in horror the moment its watery yellow eyes open. Abandoned, nameless, and met with violence by every human he encounters, the remarkably articulate Creature educates himself in secret before tracking his creator across the sublime glaciers of Mont Blanc to demand a reckoning.

Listening to the original, unabridged text of *Frankenstein* is a revelation for anyone who only knows the lumbering, bolt-necked caricature of Hollywood cinema. Mary Shelley—who began the story at just eighteen years old during the infamous volcanic "Year Without a Summer" in 1816—crafted an intricate nested epistolary narrative where creator and creation debate John Milton’s *Paradise Lost*, loneliness, and the ethics of science. Paired in our catalog with Shelley’s later apocalyptic epic The Last Man and fellow nineteenth-century Gothic touchstones like Dracula, this Penguin Classics production uses the human voice to restore the Creature’s devastating eloquence.

Running nearly twelve hours in this complete edition supplemented with critical framing, *Frankenstein* delivers tremendous literary weight for a single Audible credit. The dual narration by Colin Salmon and Peter Noble highlights the psychological tug-of-war at the novel’s heart, turning the Alpine confrontations and the Creature’s fireside confession into pure acoustic theater. Both a foundational work of science fiction and an aching meditation on rejection, this is an indispensable classic for every serious audiobook library.

Narrator Soundcheck: Colin Salmon, Peter Noble

Colin Salmon and Peter Noble elevate this Penguin Classics edition into a gripping Gothic chamber drama across nearly twelve hours. Salmon’s deep, velvet baritone commands attention with natural gravitas, lending tremendous dignity and tragic weight to Shelley’s philosophical passages and the Creature’s anguished, eloquent pleas for companionship. Noble complements him with feverish intensity, capturing Victor Frankenstein’s manic obsession and mounting paranoia from the laboratories of Ingolstadt to the Arctic ice. Clean, atmospheric mastering lets every desolate pause linger in the headphones.
