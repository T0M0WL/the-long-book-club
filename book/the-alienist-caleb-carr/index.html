About this Audiobook

In 1896 Gilded Age New York, Police Commissioner Theodore Roosevelt secretly recruits criminal psychologist Dr. Laszlo Kreizler and reporter John Schuyler Moore to pioneer forensic profiling against a brutal serial killer.

Twenty Hours of Gaslit Gilded Age Forensics and Dark Psychological Thriller in 1896 New York

In the spring of 1896, New York City is a roaring metropolis of sky-scraping wealth, corrupt Tammany Hall politics, and squalid Lower East Side tenements. When the mutilated body of an impoverished immigrant boy is found on the unfinished Williamsburg Bridge, newly appointed Police Commissioner Theodore Roosevelt refuses to let the case be buried. Quietly bypassing his own crooked precinct captains, Roosevelt teams up with brilliant 'alienist' (criminal psychologist) Dr. Laszlo Kreizler, *New York Times* crime reporter John Schuyler Moore, and pioneering secretary Sara Howard to hunt a killer using a radical new science: psychological profiling.

On audio, Caleb Carr's *The Alienist* stands as a towering landmark bridging Historical Mystery, Police Procedural, and Gothic suspense. Hearing the clatter of elevated trains, the gaslit slums of Five Points, and Kreizler's quiet, obsessive deduction sessions makes the late-nineteenth-century setting breathe in your headphones alongside Dissolution and The Historian.

Spanning twenty hours and twenty-three minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Alienist* delivers dark period immersion, unforgettable historical cameos, and relentless investigative suspense.

Narrator Soundcheck: George Guidall

Audio Hall of Fame legend George Guidall delivers a masterclass across more than twenty hours. Guidall captures the warm, observant steadiness of narrator John Schuyler Moore, the clinical, haunted intensity of Dr. Kreizler, and the booming vigor of a young Teddy Roosevelt without ever overplaying his hand. Crisp pacing and deep gravitas make every dark alleyway unforgettable.
