The Space Between Worlds

About this Audiobook

Micaiah Johnson explores alternate realities through Cara, a multiverse traveler whose survival across 372 parallel earths depends on the fact that her other selves died young.

Micaiah Johnson's 10-Hour Multiverse Tour de Force of Identity, Trauma, and Alternate Earths

Clocking in at over ten intensely gripping hours, Micaiah Johnson’s *The Space Between Worlds* is a dazzling, razor-sharp science fiction debut that redefines the multiverse narrative with profound social insight, cyberpunk grit, and emotional weight. In an unequal future where interdimensional travel has become a reality, there is a deadly physical catch: no one can visit an alternate earth where their parallel counterpart is still alive. Enter Cara, born in the impoverished, violent wasteland of Ashtown, whose rough upbringing means she has died in 372 of the 380 parallel dimensions discovered. Because of her rare disposability, she is employed by the elite tech corporation of the opulent city of Wiley as a 'traverser' to gather valuable data from the few worlds she can physically enter.

In spoken audio, Johnson’s visceral prose and Cara’s fierce, vulnerable first-person perspective come alive with breathtaking emotional immediacy. Hearing Cara encounter different versions of the people she loves—and confronting the buried traumas of her own past—adds a haunting psychological depth that elevates the story far beyond standard parallel-world tropes. The spoken format turns every dimensional jump into a tense, nerve-shredding tightrope walk between life and death. Featured alongside intelligent sci-fi gems in our library like A Memory Called Empire and The Three-Body Problem, this production demonstrates the immense power of high-concept speculative storytelling that never loses sight of the human heart.

Investing a single credit or token in ten hours of *The Space Between Worlds* provides tremendous narrative satisfaction, emotional catharsis, and intellectual payoff. Johnson crafts a fast-paced, twist-filled corporate conspiracy that doubles as a searing meditation on privilege, resilience, and self-worth. Johnson delivers a fresh, urgent perspective on the multiverse that questions who gets to thrive and who gets discarded when worlds collide, making Cara's journey deeply moving and genuinely unforgettable. It is a thought-provoking, beautifully produced listening experience that will keep you glued to your headphones until the final revelatory dimension is crossed.

Narrator Soundcheck: Amy Landon

Narrator Amy Landon delivers a mesmerizing, emotionally charged performance that captures the raw, defensive survival instincts of Cara with absolute authenticity. Landon navigates the subtle psychological distinctions between parallel versions of identical characters with brilliant vocal nuance, conveying both streetwise cynicism and profound inner vulnerability without missing a beat. Her rhythmic pacing and crystal-clear diction across scientific exposition and violent confrontations make this ten-hour audio production an absolute delight from start to finish.
