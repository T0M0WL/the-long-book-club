The Nightmare

Author: Lars Kepler | Narrator: Seán Barrett (UK) / Saul Reichlin (US)

Runtime: 15h 17m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Psychological Thriller, Mystery, Crime, Police Procedural, Detective

About this Audiobook

When a young woman is found drowned aboard a pleasure boat with bone-dry lungs and a Stockholm arms inspector hangs in an empty locked apartment, Detective Inspector Joona Linna uncovers a sinister Swedish-Baltic conspiracy.

Over Fifteen Hours of Stockholm Arms Intrigue and Impossible Maritime Murders

Following the gruesome suburban horror of The Hypnotist, Detective Inspector Joona Linna steps directly into an eerie double enigma across Stockholm and the misty Baltic archipelago. A young woman's corpse is pulled from an abandoned pleasure cruiser after apparently drowning—yet doctors confirm not a drop of water touches the clothes inside her dry boat cabin. Less than twenty-four hours later, Sweden's senior arms-export regulator is discovered hanging inside an empty, locked apartment with no chair beneath his feet, launching Joona and security agent Saga Bauer into a ruthless international arms conspiracy.

In audio format, *The Nightmare* widens the Joona Linna canvas from claustrophobic family trauma into grand geopolitical Nordic Noir. Lars Kepler threads chilling Crime forensics with sharp espionage-style Police Procedural suspense, creating a cinematic Mystery packed with relentless assassins, naval chases, and cold institutional cover-ups. Fans of intense Scandinavian Detective fiction and razor-sharp Psychological Thriller plotting will feel every gear click seamlessly into place.

Running fifteen hours and seventeen minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Nightmare* proves Book 1 was no fluke—setting key series relationships and menacing shadows that echo directly into The Fire Witness and The Sandman.
