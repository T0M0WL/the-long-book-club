Sidetracked

Author: Henning Mankell | Narrator: Sean Barrett

Runtime: 13h 21m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Police Procedural, Mystery, Crime, Detective

About this Audiobook

After witnessing a traumatized teenage girl burn herself alive in a yellow rapeseed field, Inspector Kurt Wallander pursues a savage axe-wielding killer scalping wealthy Swedish elites during a blistering midsummer heatwave.

Over Thirteen Hours Exploring Mankell's Gold Dagger Masterpiece of Midsummer Terror

Opening beneath oppressive July sun just as Kurt Wallander prepares for a rare summer break after The Man Who Smiled, Ystad police are summoned to an empty rapeseed field where an unidentified teenage girl douses herself in petrol and sets herself alight right in front of Kurt. Days later, Sweden's former Justice Minister is butchered with an axe and scalped along the coast, drawing Wallander and new squad partner Ann-Britt Höglund into a devastating collision between systemic corruption and private grief.

Winner of the Crime Writers' Association Gold Dagger, *Sidetracked* represents absolute pinnacle Nordic Noir. Interlacing searing social conscience with tireless Police Procedural teamwork, Mankell frames brutal Crime and patient Detective intuition inside a heartbreaking human Mystery rather than a disposable puzzle.

Spanning thirteen hours and twenty-one minutes for one credit, *Sidetracked* belongs in every crime listener's permanent shelf—setting a high bar cleanly met by The Fifth Woman.
