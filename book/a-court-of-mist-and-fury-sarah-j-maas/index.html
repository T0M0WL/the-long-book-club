About this Audiobook

Suffering from post-traumatic nightmares after surviving Under the Mountain, Feyre is spirited away to the dazzling Night Court, discovering profound healing, revolutionary power, and an earth-shattering romantic bond.

Sarah J. Maas's 23-Hour Romantasy Masterpiece of Trauma Recovery, Velaris Wonder, and Transcendent Romantic Chemistry

Widely celebrated as the crowning jewel of modern romantic fantasy, Sarah J. Maas’s *A Court of Mist and Fury* expands the saga into a twenty-three-hour emotional tour de force of profound healing, revolutionary self-discovery, and earth-shattering romantic chemistry. Picking up three months after the harrowing horrors Under the Mountain, Feyre Archeron finds herself resurrected as High Fae but drowning in severe post-traumatic nightmares, suffocated by Tamlin’s increasingly overbearing, gilded confinement in the Spring Court. When Rhysand, the terrifying High Lord of the Night Court, suddenly appears to collect on their soul-bargain, Feyre is spirited away to a realm entirely contrary to the monstrous rumors she feared. There, hidden within the misty mountain ranges, lies Velaris—the dazzling City of Starlight—and an extraordinary found family known as the Inner Circle. As Feyre learns to wield her unprecedented multi-court magical abilities and shield her mind, she embarks on dangerous covert operations across Prythian to recover ancient artifacts, all while unraveling the complex, fiercely protective heart of Rhysand in a transcendent, slow-burning romance that redefines her entire existence.

In audio format, Maas’s brilliant deconstruction of fairy-tale romance translates into an extraordinarily immersive listening marathon. The spoken word deepens the emotional weight of Feyre’s depression, making her gradual reclamation of autonomy and passion feel heartbreakingly earned. From the breathless adrenaline of Illyrian aerial combat training atop the Windhaven steppes to the breathtaking majesty of the Starfall celebration, the audio performance carries listeners seamlessly through twenty-three hours of flawless pacing. Positioned at the pinnacle of our Epic Romantasy collection alongside A Court of Thorns and Roses and A Court of Wings and Ruin, this unabridged edition provides the spacious acoustic canvas required for the legendary Chapter 54 and 55 revelations to land with staggering, tear-inducing emotional resonance.

Exchanging a single Audible credit for twenty-three hours of *A Court of Mist and Fury* represents one of the single greatest investments in the entire audiobook medium. Maas crafts an unputdownable narrative that balances high-stakes espionage and ancient fae lore with the most rewarding, deeply respectful enemies-to-lovers romance in contemporary literature. It is an unforgettable, life-affirming listening experience that sets a new high-water mark for epic fantasy, leaving listeners thoroughly spellbound, hopelessly in love with the Night Court, and reaching for the sequel the second the credits roll.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jennifer Ikeda

Jennifer Ikeda turns in a masterclass in vocal nuance across twenty-three marathon hours, delivering what many fans consider her career-best performance. Ikeda charts Feyre’s psychological trajectory from hollow, panic-stricken survivor to confident, radiant High Lady with consummate emotional sensitivity. Her portrayal of Rhysand is peerless—balancing dangerous lethal menace with warm, wry wit and profound vulnerability—while giving Cassian, Azriel, Mor, and Amren distinct, memorable vocal identities. Listeners looking for a cinematic radio-play experience can also explore GraphicAudio’s two-part dramatized adaptation with a full cast and orchestral score, but Ikeda’s solo narration remains the universally adored, emotionally transcendent interpretation that defined an entire generation of romantasy listeners.
