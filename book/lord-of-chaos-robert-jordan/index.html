About this Audiobook

As the Black Tower trains male channelers under Mazrim Taim, Rand is captured by treacherous Aes Sedai, leading to the earth-shattering climax at Dumai's Wells.

The Forty-One-Hour Apex of Male Channelers, Captivity Horror, and the Cataclysm of Dumai's Wells

Widely celebrated as one of the most powerful and dramatically intense installments in modern speculative fiction, *Lord of Chaos* pushes the world of *The Wheel of Time* toward irrevocable fracture. Seeking to build an army of male channelers capable of standing against the Shadow at Tarmon Gai'don, Rand al'Thor sanctions the founding of the Black Tower under the enigmatic False Dragon Mazrim Taim, instituting the lethal ranks of the Asha'man. However, when an embassy of Aes Sedai from Elaida’s divided White Tower kidnaps the Dragon Reborn, Rand is subjected to harrowing claustrophobic torture locked inside a shielding iron chest, sparking an all-out desperate rescue operation across the scorched plains of Dumai's Wells.

Experiencing *Lord of Chaos* on audio is an emotional and visceral triumph that culminates in what many consider the single greatest battle climax in the entire fantasy genre. Michael Kramer’s depiction of Rand’s suffocating psychological trauma inside the chest—intertwined with the haunting, frantic whispers of Lews Therin Telamon—builds unbearable suspense until the legendary order 'Asha'man, kill!' detonates through your headphones like rolling thunder. Highlighted as a crowning titan in our Epic Fantasy hub, this forty-one-hour marathon captures both the terrifying political paralysis of Tar Valon and the earth-shattering unleashing of raw channeling power.

Delivering forty-one hours and thirty-two minutes of world-class storytelling for a single credit, this monumental audio production offers staggering entertainment value. Jordan masterfully balances intricate palace intrigue in Caemlyn, Salidar, and Cairhien with breathtaking action set pieces that redefine the moral landscape of the saga. Investing a monthly credit in this audiobook guarantees weeks of unforgettable listening, capped by a finale that will leave you physically breathless and replaying the final chapters in awe.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer, Kate Reading

Michael Kramer delivers what is widely regarded as a career-defining performance in Lord of Chaos. His portrayal of Rand’s claustrophobic torment inside the chest and Lews Therin’s chaotic madness is bone-chilling, while his thunderous, unyielding command during the Battle of Dumai's Wells is legendary amongst audiobook listeners. Kate Reading matches his intensity, navigating Egwene’s meteoric rise to the Amyrlin Seat with stately elegance and unshakeable resolve. Flawlessly engineered with pristine audio dynamics across forty-one hours, this recording is high fantasy at its pinnacle.
