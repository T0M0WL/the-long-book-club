About this Audiobook

The armies of the Goblin Lord and rival nations converge upon Liscor in a catastrophic, four-way battle that demands unbearable sacrifices from Erin and her friends.

An Earth-Shattering Forty-Three-Hour War Climax of Unbearable Tragedy, Antinium Valor, and True Heroism

Widely regarded as the emotional crucible of the entire saga, *Blood of Liscor* brings the brewing conflicts of the first seven volumes to a devastating, earth-shattering collision. Four separate military forces converge upon the plains of Liscor: the ruthless Goblin Lord’s horde, the disciplined human armies of Tyrion Veltras, the city’s valiant Drake Watch, and the fierce Antinium Free Hives. Caught in the center of the storm, Erin Solstice must decide how much blood she is willing to see spilled to defend her sanctuary, sparking heartbreaking sacrifices from beloved companions who lay down their lives for an innkeeper’s dream of peace.

On audio, *Blood of Liscor* is an overwhelming, emotional tour de force that will leave listeners openly weeping in their cars. Andrea Parsneau gives a performance of staggering intensity, navigating chaotic multi-front battle lines, heroic last stands, and profound personal grief with superhuman artistry. Her vocal delivery of the Antinium soldiers’ awakening to true individuality—and their ferocious defense of Erin—is an acoustic landmark of fantasy audiobooks. Featured as a titan in our LitRPG & GameLit Audiobooks library, this production transforms high fantasy into unforgettable tragedy and triumph.

Clocking in at exactly forty-three hours, this installment represents the absolute gold standard of audiobook credit value. pirateaba refuses to shy away from the real horrors and permanent losses of war, ensuring that every fallen hero’s memory lingers long after the battle cries fade.
