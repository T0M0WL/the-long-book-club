About this Audiobook

Over three relentless days on a muddy Northern hill topped by ancient standing stones, the Union army and the Northmen clash in a brutal, microscopic deconstruction of warfare.

Three Days of Blood, Mud, and Iron: Steven Pacey's 23-Hour Tour de Force in The Heroes

Narrowing the grand canvas of epic fantasy down to three grueling days on a single muddy hill in the North, *The Heroes* is Joe Abercrombie’s most tightly focused and brilliant thematic triumph. The Union army, commanded by Lord Marshal Kroy and the vainglorious Colonel Bremer dan Gorst, collides with the Northern warriors under the merciless King Black Dow. Through the eyes of reluctant warriors, incompetent aristocrats, bitter veterans, and bewildered conscripts, Abercrombie strips away every romantic delusion of martial glory, exposing war as an absurd, terrifying meat-grinder dictated by vanity, mud, and blind luck.

Steven Pacey turns this microscopic battle chronicle into an acoustic masterclass. His standout achievement is the internal monologue of Bremer dan Gorst: while Gorst speaks to his superiors in a ridiculously high-pitched, piping falsetto, his internal thoughts are voiced by Pacey as a volcanic, bitter bass-baritone of lethal resentment and self-loathing. Pacey’s delivery of Abercrombie’s famous POV-relay sequence—where the narrative baton passes seamlessly from one dying soldier to the man who kills him across the battlefield—is one of the most breathtaking narrative spectacles in audiobook history. Listeners who admire the unflinching honesty of The Black Company or Gates of Fire will be transfixed.

Clocking in at over twenty-three hours on a single credit, *The Heroes* provides unmatched narrative immersion. Abercrombie balances harrowing frontline terror with razor-sharp black comedy and surprisingly poignant character studies, ensuring that every clash of iron feels terrifyingly personal. It is an unreserved military fantasy classic that belongs in the library of every serious audiobook listener.

Narrator Soundcheck: Steven Pacey

Steven Pacey's performance in *The Heroes* represents the absolute gold standard of military fantasy narration. The dichotomy between Bremer dan Gorst's high-pitched external squeak and his thunderous internal monologue is executed with breathtaking comedic and dramatic brilliance.
