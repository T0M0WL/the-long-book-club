About this Audiobook

Set in newly independent 1950s India, Vikram Seth's monumental novel weaves four sprawling families, fierce political elections, and a mother's quest to find her daughter a suitable husband into one of the longest, richest panoramas in modern literature.

Nearly Sixty-Nine Glorious Hours Inside Post-Independence India's Grandest Saga

If you want to vanish completely into another century for an entire month of commutes, Vikram Seth's *A Suitable Boy* stands among the greatest long-form reading experiences ever written. Opening with Mrs. Rupa Mehra's fierce declaration to her spirited daughter Lata—'You too will marry a boy I choose'—the novel radiates outward across newly independent 1951 India, linking four unforgettable families through university romances, shoemaking workshops, Urdu poetry salons, zamindari land-reform battles, and the seismic energy of India's first national election.

What makes *A Suitable Boy* sing so brilliantly as an audiobook is Seth's warmth, humor, and crystal-clear storytelling. Unlike dense modernist puzzles, this is generous, Dickensian Family Saga storytelling that welcomes you into every courtyard and parliament chamber. Listeners who fell in love with The Covenant of Water or Midnight's Children will find an equally intoxicating world here—just with double the room to breathe and settle in.

At a staggering sixty-eight hours and forty-four minutes for a single Audible credit, *A Suitable Boy* is one of the finest value-for-credit investments in our entire Historical Fiction library.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sagar Arya

Carrying nearly sixty-nine hours and a cast of dozens without losing clarity is an extraordinary feat, and Sagar Arya makes it look effortless. His crisp pronunciation, warm comedic timing, and distinct vocal silhouettes for matriarchs, politicians, professors, and young lovers keep every tangled family thread immediately recognizable across weeks of listening.
