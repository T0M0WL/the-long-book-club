About this Audiobook

Shortlisted for the 2026 Booker Prize, Marlon James returns to 1988 Kingston, Jamaica, where eight gay men rehearsing an underground play survive a brutal mob attack—igniting a sprawling, polyphonic reckoning with survival, justice, and memory.

Nearly Twenty-Three Electric Hours of Booker-Shortlisted Kingston History

Shortlisted for the 2026 Booker Prize and hailed across *The New York Times* and *TIME* as one of the defining novels of the year, Marlon James’s *The Disappearers* marks his blistering return to the Jamaica of *A Brief History of Seven Killings*. Opening in 1988 Kingston, where eight gay men gather in secret to rehearse an underground play only to be targeted by a savage mob attack, the novel fractures outward into a fierce, polyphonic excavation of survival, state complicity, queer resilience, and the stories history tries to erase.

Like James’s earlier masterpieces, *The Disappearers* was practically born to be heard aloud. Written as a dazzling chorus of distinct Jamaican voices—funny, furious, haunted, and defiant—its shifting registers and rapid-fire patois gain extraordinary clarity and musicality when performed by live actors rather than decoded silently on the page. Standing tall inside our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 alongside fellow Booker finalist John of John, it proves how transformative Full-Cast Literary Fiction can be.

At twenty-two hours and fifty-five minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Disappearers* is audacious, devastating, and impossible to shake.
