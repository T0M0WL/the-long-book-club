Nearly Twenty Hours of Maritime Espionage Alongside Sir Francis Drake Before the Armada

By the summer of 1585, war with King Philip II of Spain is all but inevitable. Ordered by Queen Elizabeth to strike Spanish treasure ports across the Atlantic before the Armada can sail, swashbuckling privateer Sir Francis Drake is assembling a massive expedition in Plymouth. When a respected ship's captain is murdered at the docks—and clear attempts on Drake's own life threaten to sabotage the fleet before anchors aweigh—Giordano Bruno rides west alongside poet-courtier Sir Philip Sidney to unmask the traitor hiding among the captains and crew.

Listening to *Treachery* in audio unleashes S.J. Parris's longest, most swaggering Elizabethan adventure at nearly twenty hours. Merging salty Tudor dockside grit with high-wire Political Intrigue and intricate Whodunnit deduction, this fourth installment showcases why Historical Mystery and sweeping Historical Fiction pair so naturally in long-form audio.

Clocking in at nineteen hours and forty-eight minutes for a single Audible credit, *Treachery* delivers cannon smoke, maritime rivalries, and relentless pre-Armada suspense.
