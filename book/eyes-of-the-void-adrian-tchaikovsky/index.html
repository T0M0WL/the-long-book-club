About this Audiobook

The cosmic world-shapers have returned to crush human worlds, forcing an eccentric salvage crew and a traumatized pilot to venture deeper into the terrifying void of Unspace.

The Thrilling 21-Hour Space Opera Sequel That Blows the Galaxy Wide Open

Spanning twenty-one pulse-pounding hours, *Eyes of the Void* is the electrifying second volume of Adrian Tchaikovsky’s acclaimed Final Architecture trilogy, following directly from the explosive events of Shards of Earth. Eighty years after humanity was nearly driven to extinction by the Architects—colossal, crystalline celestial entities that reshape inhabited planets like children molding clay—the monstrous beings have returned with devastating vengeance, destroying frontier human worlds without warning or mercy.

Returning to the beloved crew of the salvage vessel *Vulture God*, the narrative thrusts traumatized Intermediary Idris Telemmier and cloned warrior Solace into a desperate interspecies quest across a fracturing galaxy. As human colonies disintegrate into civil war and alien alliances crumble under existential panic, Idris discovers that the Architects are not acting on their own volition, but are merely weapons directed by a shadowy cosmic intelligence operating from the deepest abyss of 'Unspace'—the non-dimensional void between stars where human sanity corrodes. Tchaikovsky showcases his unmatched worldbuilding prowess, introducing incredible alien biologies, parasitic cults, and thrilling sub-light starship broadsides.

For audiobook listeners who demand huge interstellar scope, unforgettable ensemble banter, and non-stop momentum on a single credit, *Eyes of the Void* is a triumph. Narrator Sophie Aldred delivers another extraordinary, multifaceted performance that brings every bizarre alien lifeform and high-G space maneuvers to life. Paired with companion space epics like Shards of Earth and Children of Time, it cements Tchaikovsky’s place at the absolute summit of modern Science Fiction listening.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sophie Aldred

Sophie Aldred delivers a dazzling, high-octane performance that surpasses even her work on the first installment. Aldred effortlessly differentiates dozens of alien and human characters, giving Idris his trademark exhausted, empathetic tenderness while imbuing Solace with razor-sharp martial grit. Her depiction of the horrifying psychic presence lurking within Unspace sends genuine shivers down the spine, and her kinetic, urgent delivery during ship-to-ship orbital battles keeps twenty-one hours moving at blistering speed.
