Robert A. Caro's 66-Hour Masterpiece of Raw Political Ruthlessness and the Remaking of Modern New York

Spanning seven turbulent decades across the rise of modern America, Robert A. Caro’s Pulitzer Prize-winning masterpiece *The Power Broker* is the definitive study of unrestricted political power in world literature. At a staggering sixty-six hours, the narrative charts the breathtaking rise and tragic moral decay of Robert Moses, an idealistic urban reformer who transformed himself into the unelected, untouchable czar of New York infrastructure. Armed with autonomous public authorities, billions in bond revenues, and an unbending will, Moses bulldozed entire neighborhoods, displaced hundreds of thousands of vulnerable working-class residents, and single-handedly engineered the modern automotive landscape of parks, beaches, bridges, and expressways that defines the American metropolis today, creating an automobile-dependent empire that permanently reshaped millions of lives across generations.

Encountering Caro’s monumental prose through audio turns what could be an exhausting twelve-hundred-page academic text into a gripping, cinematic political thriller. The spoken word allows the intricate bureaucratic chess games, legislative backroom maneuvers, and tragic human costs of Moses’s grand highway projects to unfold with mesmerizing psychological intimacy. Featured as the crown jewel of our Long Life Stories collection alongside titans like Titan, this audio edition provides the temporal space necessary to understand how institutional power quietly accumulates and irrevocably corrupts even the most high-minded public servant, turning youthful idealism into autocratic disregard for ordinary human beings.

Spending a single credit on sixty-six hours of Caro’s peerless reporting is quite simply the highest return on investment in the entire audio medium. Rather than consuming multiple fleeting ten-hour memoirs, you gain an exhaustive masterclass in American governance, urban planning, and human ambition that completely dominates your daily listening routine for weeks. It is an indispensable, life-altering audio monument that every serious non-fiction listener owes it to themselves to experience, study, and cherish at least once in their reading life, discovering why it remains the gold standard of investigative biography.

Narrator Soundcheck: Robertson Dean

Robertson Dean commands this colossal sixty-six-hour epic with magnificent stamina, gravitas, and quiet moral authority. Dean’s deep, resonant bass-baritone proves the ideal acoustic vehicle for Caro’s rhythmic, investigative prose, maintaining unwavering clarity and engaging dramatic pacing through complex bureaucratic infighting and legislative maneuvering. His subtle vocal shifts convey Moses’s escalating hubris and venomous disdain for political opposition without theatrical excess, backed by immaculate, noise-free studio production across all sixty-six hours.
