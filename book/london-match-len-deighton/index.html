About this Audiobook

When a defected KGB major points to a second high-ranking mole inside British intelligence, Bernard Samson becomes the prime suspect.

A Second Mole, Cold War Berlin Checkpoints, and Samson's Final Gambit

Spanning nearly fifteen tense, smoke-filled hours, *London Match* delivers the explosive, critically acclaimed conclusion to Len Deighton’s iconic 'Game, Set and Match' trilogy, tying together the intricate conspiracies set in motion in Berlin Game and Mexico Set. Having successfully orchestrated the defection of KGB major Erich Stinnes, field agent Bernard Samson expects a moment of respite. Instead, he walks into an operational nightmare: during debriefings at a quiet Sussex safe house, Stinnes drops a devastating bombshell—there is another high-ranking Soviet mole embedded in the upper echelons of British intelligence.

As paranoia consumes Whitehall, Samson's vindictive superiors seize upon circumstantial clues to accuse Samson himself of being the second traitor. Cut off from official support and surveilled by internal security 'Dogs,' Samson must conduct an unsanctioned, desperate investigation across rain-swept London streets and through the deadly shadows of divided Berlin. Facing off against his defected wife Fiona across the Berlin Wall, Samson must execute a breathtaking, high-stakes prisoner exchange at Checkpoint Charlie where every player is lying and a single misstep means execution.

Deighton’s depiction of Cold War intelligence work is unrivaled in its gritty sociological precision and dry comedic bite. The psychological toll of living a life built entirely upon falsehoods permeates every encounter, elevating Samson's personal tragedy into an indictment of the entire secret world. Deighton never relies on sensationalism, grounding the climax in the bleak, authentic dread of real divided Berlin espionage. Narrator James Lailey delivers a virtuoso performance, perfectly capturing Samson’s bone-deep exhaustion, sharp field intellect, and bitter resilience. Standing as a peerless companion to John le Carré’s Karla Trilogy (Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy), *London Match* is a five-star triumph of espionage literature that every spy fiction listener must experience.

Narrator Soundcheck: James Lailey

James Lailey concludes the 'Game, Set and Match' trilogy with an extraordinary vocal performance on London Match. Lailey brings palpable emotional gravitas to Samson’s isolating plight, allowing the character’s dark humor and simmering anger to shine through the bureaucratic fog. His voicing of German bar owners, Soviet interrogators, and condescending Whitehall grandees is pitch-perfect, and his pacing during the climactic Berlin border exchange generates unbearable suspense. A flawless acoustic achievement that will keep listeners riveted.
