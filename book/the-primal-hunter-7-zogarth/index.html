Entering Nevermore: Travis Baldree Launches the Grand Multiversal Proving Grounds in 18 Hours

*The Primal Hunter 7* marks the beginning of the widely acclaimed Nevermore Arc, launching Jake Thayne onto the grand stage of the wider multiverse. Leaving Earth behind, Jake enters Nevermore—a legendary, ancient dungeon facility created by primordial entities to test the greatest D-grade prodigies across countless galaxies. Forming a temporary expedition team alongside multiversal elites and old comrades like the Sword Saint, Jake faces complex floor trials featuring shifting conceptual environments, psychological traps, and ancient god-level challenges.

Travis Baldree commands this cosmic expansion with remarkable theatrical versatility. Voicing a vast array of newly introduced alien species, divine observers, and cosmic dungeon overseers, Baldree keeps each character instantly recognizable and engaging. His delivery of Nevermore's bizarre, shifting floor mechanics—from gravity-defying arenas to conceptual alchemy puzzles—is crystal clear and deeply engrossing. Fans of elite multiversal dungeon crawls like Dungeon Crawler Carl will find this arc completely captivating.

Clocking in at nearly eighteen hours on a single credit, this seventh installment injects immense freshness into the series. Zogarth stretches his world-building muscles to the limit, creating ingenious puzzle floors and thrilling boss skirmishes that test Jake's intellect as much as his bow. It is an exhilarating, world-class listening experience that kicks off the saga's most famous storyline.
