About this Audiobook

Zorian Kazinski, an antisocial magical academy student, is trapped in a repeating month-long time loop as a continental war erupts, forcing him to master the arcane to survive.

Domagoj Kurmaić's 23-Hour Tour-de-Force of Arcane Progression and Groundhog-Day Magical Mastery

Clocking in at nearly twenty-three monumental hours, *Mother of Learning: Arc 1* by Domagoj Kurmaić (writing under the pen name nobody103) is an undisputed pinnacle of progression fantasy and recursive time-loop fiction. The story follows Zorian Kazinski, a cynical, antisocial teenage mage studying at the prestigious Royal Academy of Magical Arts in the city of Cyoria. Expecting a routine semester enduring his overbearing family and arrogant classmates, Zorian's life is violently upended during the city’s summer festival when an invading army of cultists, mercenaries, and giant primordial beasts destroys the academy. Trapped in a catastrophic soul-anchor time loop that resets his life back to the start of the month upon every death, Zorian must set aside his misanthropy and methodically master every branch of arcane science, mind magic, and combat survival to avert global ruin.

In audio format, narrator Jack Voraces brings this massive, intricate world to vibrant life with relentless kinetic momentum. The spoken medium makes Zorian's intellectual growth utterly addictive; watching a paranoid, under-powered student gradually transform into a calculating, hyper-competent combat arcanist through hundreds of failed experiments is pure listening joy. Rather than feeling repetitive, each reset reveals fresh geopolitical intrigue, hidden dungeon labyrinths, and deeper layers of the mysterious invaders' plot. Grouped alongside addictive progression marathons in our library like Dungeon Crawler Carl and the intellectual temporal chess of The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August, this twenty-three-hour opener is impossible to stop listening to.

At nearly twenty-three hours of masterfully constructed fantasy world-building for a single monthly Audible credit, *Mother of Learning: Arc 1* offers unmatched runtime economy and storytelling value. Kurmaić's hard-magic system operates with airtight internal logic, rewarding attentive listeners with clever setups and breathtaking payoffs that never rely on cheap plot armor. Supported by Jack Voraces's indefatigable vocal stamina, this epic time-loop odyssey represents one of the most entertaining and credit-worthy investments in modern independent fantasy.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jack Voraces

Jack Voraces delivers a powerhouse, twenty-three-hour vocal marathon that carries Kurmaić's expansive web-serial transition into professional audio with breathtaking stamina. Voraces captures Zorian's prickly, sardonic inner monologue with spot-on comedic timing, seamlessly evolving his vocal cadence from exasperated adolescent whines to icy, battle-hardened authority as the cycles accumulate. He effortlessly differentiates dozens of distinct voices—including eccentric potion masters, terrifying mind-manipulating giant spiders, and pompous academy instructors—maintaining consistent pitch, clear diction, and infectious energy across every intense arcane duel.
