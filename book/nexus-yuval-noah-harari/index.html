About this Audiobook

Yuval Noah Harari Unpacks the Perils of AI and Global Information Networks

Yuval Noah Harari, the globally renowned historian and philosopher behind Sapiens, delivers a sweeping, urgently needed investigation into how human civilization has organized, manipulated, and endangered itself through information networks. Spanning thousands of years from the canonization of the Hebrew Bible and the catastrophic mass hysteria of early modern European witch hunts to totalitarian Soviet bureaucracy and generative artificial intelligence, Harari demonstrates that communication networks have rarely prioritized objective truth. Instead, information systems have always functioned primarily to generate social order, enforce collective cooperation, and wield institutional power—a delicate equilibrium that is now under existential threat from autonomous, non-human algorithmic agents capable of generating illusions, fabricating synthetic reality, and eroding human agency on a planetary scale.

Engaging with Harari’s monumental synthesis through the medium of spoken audio creates an exceptionally powerful intellectual lecture experience that commands full concentration. Complex philosophical dilemmas regarding bureaucratic self-correcting mechanisms, silicon-based intelligence, algorithmic manipulation, and democratic fragility are presented with conversational warmth, clear analytical precision, and lucidity, making dense academic theories readily accessible during daily commutes and long motorway drives.
