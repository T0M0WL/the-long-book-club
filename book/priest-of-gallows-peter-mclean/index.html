Priest of Gallows

Author: Peter McLean | Narrator: David Morley Hale

Runtime: 11h 05m | Genre: Grimdark Fantasy, Crime, Low Fantasy, Dark Fantasy, Civil War, Gangland

About this Audiobook

Tomas Piety rises to aristocratic rank as a Baron and royal magistrate, but civil war and magical pestilence threaten his entire criminal family.

Baronial Extortion, Magical Plague, and the Dark Ascent of Tomas Piety

Spanning eleven thrilling, smoke-choked hours, *Priest of Gallows* is the phenomenal third entry in Peter McLean’s *War for the Rose Throne*, escalating Tomas Piety’s ruthless criminal trajectory from back-alley extortion to the highest echelons of national power. Having survived the deadly espionage of Dannsburg in Priest of Lies, Tomas is rewarded with an astonishing promotion: he is elevated to the nobility as Lord Baron Piety, tasked by the Crown with restoring order to the war-ravaged province of Ourun.

Yet nobility brings terrifying new burdens. A devastating magical pestilence known as the 'Cough' ravages the slums, and famine threatens widespread civil revolt. As rival noble lords refuse to recognize a former gutter thug as their peer, Tomas must deploy the full, bloody brutality of his Pious Men to enforce royal taxation and crush insubordination. Worse, the horrifying sorcery wielded by his adopted son Billy the Boy is growing increasingly uncontrollable, threatening to consume everyone in Tomas's inner circle. McLean’s sharp, first-person prose provides a merciless deconstruction of power, showing that the only difference between a gang boss and a king is the size of their army.

Narrator David Morley Hale delivers an extraordinary vocal performance. Hale captures Tomas’s weary cynicism and calculating intellect with chilling perfection, contrasting his cold professional violence against his desperate love for his traumatized crew. The pacing is relentless and the underworld betrayals cut deep. For listeners who love Peaky Blinders, grimdark crime fiction, and morally ambiguous antiheroes, *Priest of Gallows* is an absolute triumph.

Narrator Soundcheck: David Morley Hale

David Morley Hale’s performance on Priest of Gallows is cold, gritty, and hypnotic. Hale brings Tomas Piety’s pragmatic street dialect to bear against pompous aristocratic barons with razor-sharp timing, making every verbal interrogation crackle with unspoken violence. His handling of Billy the Boy’s chilling, monotone magical power is deeply unsettling, providing genuine acoustic dread. Produced with pristine studio balance across eleven hours, this audiobook is an outstanding production and a definitive triumph of contemporary grimdark audio storytelling.
