About this Audiobook

Rand al'Thor is the Dragon Reborn, but he does not yet know how to wield the One Power. He must strike at the Dark One's fortress, Sky Valley.

Mat Cauthon’s Roguish Awakening and the Fall of the Stone of Tear in Audio

In an audacious structural gamble that fundamentally redefined the series, *The Dragon Reborn* places the nominal protagonist Rand al'Thor largely in the background, transforming him into a hunted, brooding figure fleeing southward toward his destiny. This brilliant narrative shift allows the supporting ensemble to claim center stage with dazzling effect. Free from the corrupting influence of the Shadar Logoth dagger, Mat Cauthon emerges as a witty, reluctant hero wielding a deadly quarterstaff and uncanny gambler's luck. Accompanied by the fierce Aiel warrior Gaul, Perrin Aybara wrestles with his wolfbrother instincts and the perils of the Dream World, while Egwene, Nynaeve, and Elayne hunt insidious Black Ajah conspirators across the realm.

In audio format, the dynamic interplay between the characters sparkles with newfound comedic energy, roguish charm, and relentless momentum. Michael Kramer’s portrayal of Mat Cauthon is celebrated by fans as one of the most delightful character transformations in modern audiobook history, bringing unforgettable zest to his hilarious quarterstaff duel against Gawyn and Galad in Tar Valon. This contrasts brilliantly against Kate Reading’s fierce, determined voicing of the young Aes Sedai novices navigating deadly conspiracies in the White Tower. Highlighted alongside The Great Hunt and The Shadow Rising in our Epic Fantasy collection, this twenty-six-hour recording turns a multi-pronged race to the fortress of Tear into an addictive auditory feast.

Offering over twenty-six hours of thrilling adventure for a single credit, this release delivers outstanding entertainment value for dedicated fantasy listeners. Jordan proves that his epic world is far larger than a single savior, allowing every member of the Two Rivers party to grow into formidable legends in their own right before unleashing an explosive magical climax inside the Stone of Tear. Spending a monthly token on this fast-moving installment secures an irresistible listening experience that will make long hours fly by in a flash.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer, Kate Reading

Michael Kramer delivers a career-highlight performance in this third installment, officially transforming Mat Cauthon into an immortal fan favorite with his sharp comedic timing, roguish drawl, and exasperated charm. His portrayal of Perrin’s internal torment and quiet steadfastness provides a wonderful counterpoint to Mat’s energetic escapades. Kate Reading matches him step for step, capturing Nynaeve’s fierce temper and Egwene’s burgeoning magical authority with crisp, articulate vocal authority. Mastered with impeccable dynamic range and flawless chapter transitions across twenty-six hours, this production is top-tier fantasy listening.
