About this Audiobook

When a Salvation Army collector is shot point-blank amid Oslo's crowded snowy Christmas square, Harry Hole hunts a ruthless ex-child-soldier assassin from Croatia known as The Little Redeemer before he strikes the real target.

Nearly Fifteen Hours Hunting a Croatian War Ghost Across Snowy Christmas Oslo

Rising from the ashes of The Devil's Star under frosty December skies, Harry Hole responds when a young uniformed Salvation Army soldier is executed point-blank in front of shoppers at Oslo's crowded Egertorget charity concert. Discovering the silent Croatian hitman—known as the Little Redeemer from Vukovar's ruins—accidentally shot the wrong target and remains loose inside Oslo finishing the job pulls Harry deep into charity corruption, broken war orphans, and bitter winter mercy.

*The Redeemer* injects aching moral ambiguity into classic Nordic Noir. Balancing sympathetic killer flashbacks against stubborn Police Procedural pursuit lifts Crime and Detective fiction into genuinely moving Thriller tragedy.

Clocking fourteen hours and fifty-one minutes for one Audible credit, Book 6 shines quietly ahead of global blockbuster The Snowman.
