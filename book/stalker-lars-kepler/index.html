Stalker

Author: Lars Kepler | Narrator: Saul Reichlin

Runtime: 19h 20m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Psychological Thriller, Mystery, Crime, Police Procedural, Detective

About this Audiobook

When Stockholm police receive anonymous video links showing unsuspecting women filmed inside their own homes just hours before brutal murders, Joona Linna reunites with hypnotist Erik Maria Bark to hunt a sadistic voyeur.

Over Nineteen Hours Where Every Window Looks Out Into Unseen Predatory Eyes

When Stockholm's National Crime Unit receives an encrypted link showing a woman inside her own kitchen—filmed through darkness right outside her window—detectives locate her horribly mutilated body only minutes too late. Another chilling voyeuristic video soon hits police inboxes, launching a frantic race across Sweden's shadows. With Joona Linna absent in the immediate aftermath of The Sandman, Detective Margot Silverman turns to traumatized hypnotist Dr. Erik Maria Bark from The Hypnotist—forcing Erik to confront whether a convicted killer he helped lock up years ago was actually innocent.

Stretching beyond nineteen hours, *Stalker* ranks as Lars Kepler's longest, darkest, and most relentlessly unsettling Nordic Noir marathon. Every creak outside an uncurtained window weaponizes domestic privacy into suffocating Psychological Thriller terror, intertwining razor-sharp Police Procedural analysis with twisted Crime psychology, intricate Mystery misdirection, and hard-won Detective intuition.

Clocking in at nineteen hours and twenty minutes for one credit, *Stalker* rewards endurance listeners with shocking late twists that steer directly into Hunter.

Narrator Soundcheck: Saul Reichlin

Narrating nearly nineteen and a half uninterrupted hours of nocturnal dread, Saul Reichlin squeezes relentless tension from every silent suburban frame and frantic police raid. His grave, measured pacing makes Erik Bark's sleep-deprived guilt and Joona's return crackle cleanly through headphones.
