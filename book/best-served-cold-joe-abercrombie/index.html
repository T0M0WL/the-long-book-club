Best Served Cold

Author: Joe Abercrombie | Narrator: Steven Pacey

Runtime: 26h 28m | Genre: Grimdark, Epic Fantasy, Dark Fantasy, Military Fantasy

About this Audiobook

Betrayed and thrown down a mountain by her employer Grand Duke Orso, mercenary captain Monza Murcatto survives to assemble a lethal crew of scoundrels on a blood-soaked vendetta across the city-states of Styria.

The Count of Monte Cristo with Steel and Blood: Steven Pacey Commands 26 Hours of Styrian Vengeance

Widely celebrated as one of the finest revenge fantasies ever written, *Best Served Cold* shifts Joe Abercrombie's gritty world to the sun-drenched, treacherous city-states of Styria. Mercenary commander Monza Murcatto—the feared Snake of Talins—is double-crossed by her employer, Grand Duke Orso, who murders her brother and has her broken body tossed from a fortress cliff. Surviving against all odds through sheer spite, Monza enlists a cast of damaged degenerates: a Northman obsessed with doing the right thing named Shivers, an ex-con poisoner named Castor Morveer, a drunkard mercenary general, and an obsessive mathematician who loves counting things. Together, they embark on a calculated, chaotic purge of Monza’s seven betrayers.

Steven Pacey’s vocal performance in *Best Served Cold* is nothing short of legendary. Widely considered one of the pinnacle achievements in modern fantasy audiobooks, Pacey inhabits every character with theatrical precision. His portrayal of the priggish, neurotic poisoner Morveer is comedic perfection, while his gradual hardening of Caul Shivers—from hopeful adventurer to a hollow-eyed engine of slaughter—is utterly chilling. Monza’s raspy, pain-wracked voice grounds the bloodbath in genuine human anguish. Fans of classic revenge sagas like The Count of Monte Cristo and gritty heist fiction like The Lies of Locke Lamora will find themselves laughing out loud at the black humor one moment and gasping in horror the next.

At twenty-six and a half hours for a single credit, this standalone novel provides astonishing entertainment value. Abercrombie proves that revenge rarely leaves the avenger intact, offering an unsparing deconstruction of vengeance wrapped in blistering pacing and breathless action set-pieces. It is an unmissable, darkly hilarious auditory masterpiece that will dominate your listening for weeks.

Narrator Soundcheck: Steven Pacey
