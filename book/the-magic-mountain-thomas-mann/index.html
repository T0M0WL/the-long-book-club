About this Audiobook

Hans Castorp visits his cousin in a sanatorium in Davos and stays for seven years.

Thomas Mann\u2019s Monumental Thirty-Five-Hour Alpine Epic of Sickness, Time, and Philosophy in Audio

Arriving at the International Sanatorium Berghof in the snow-capped Swiss Alps for a routine three-week visit to his tubercular cousin Joachim, young Hamburg engineering graduate Hans Castorp discovers that the mountain air has plans of its own. Diagnosed with a slight lung infection, Hans is drawn into the sealed, decadent micro-society of wealthy invalids who have abandoned the bustling 'flatland' below. As three weeks stretch into seven hallucinatory years, Hans becomes the prize in a grand ideological duel between the humanist democrat Settembrini and the cynical, authoritarian Jesuit Naphta, while falling under the hypnotic spell of the enigmatic Russian patient Clawdia Chauchat.

On audio, Thomas Mann\u2019s Nobel Prize-winning masterpiece becomes a spellbinding, deeply intellectual headphone journey that plays with the very perception of passing time in ways the printed page cannot match. Narrator David Rintoul gives a commanding, intellectually luminous performance, navigating Mann's intricate debates on science, medicine, politics, and metaphysics with effortless vocal clarity and ironic wit. Featured as a titan in our Classics collection alongside epic philosophical novels like Ulysses and Doctor Zhivago, this thirty-five-hour production turns an alpine sanatorium into a profound auditory allegory for a dying pre-war European civilization, transporting listeners into an intoxicating philosophical retreat high above the ordinary world.

Spanning over thirty-five hours of magnificent European prose for a single credit, this release represents supreme value for dedicated listeners of classical fiction and intellectual history. Mann explores the fragile boundary between sickness and genius, dissecting the spiritual crisis of Western civilization on the eve of the First World War with timeless brilliance.
