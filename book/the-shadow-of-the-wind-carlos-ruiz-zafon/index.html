About this Audiobook

In 1945 Barcelona, young Daniel Sempere is taken by his antiquarian book-dealer father to the secret Cemetery of Forgotten Books, where he selects a rare novel by Julián Carax—unaware that a scarred stranger is burning every remaining copy in the world.

A Gothic Mystery of Forgotten Books, Forbidden Love, and Post-War Barcelona in Hypnotic Audio

Set against the misty, shadow-drenched streets of Barcelona in 1945, Carlos Ruiz Zafón’s international sensation begins when an antiquarian book dealer takes his ten-year-old grieving son, Daniel Sempere, to the secret, labyrinthine Cemetery of Forgotten Books. Allowed to select a single volume to protect for life, Daniel chooses *The Shadow of the Wind* by an elusive, forgotten author named Julián Carax. When Daniel discovers that a mysterious, disfigured phantom smelling of burned paper and sulfur—calling himself Laín Coubert, the devil himself in Carax’s novel—is systematically hunting down and incinerating every remaining copy of Carax’s works, his innocent curiosity plunges him into a lethal web of murder, madness, doomed romance, and Francoist police brutality.

On audio, Zafón’s lush, atmospheric prose becomes a deeply transportive Gothic love letter to literature and historical Barcelona. The clatter of autumn rain on wet cobblestones, the creaking of ancient dust-coated bookshelves, and the menacing footsteps of the sadistic Inspector Fumero echoing down damp Gothic alleyways are rendered with exquisite theatrical tension. Featured prominently in our Magical Realism Collection and our curated Historical Fiction Audiobooks library, this performance turns a sprawling bibliophilic mystery into an addictive, heart-racing auditory thriller that you simply cannot pause.

Offering over seventeen hours of flawless storytelling for a single credit, this recording delivers exceptional long-term entertainment value for bibliophiles. Zafón marries the atmospheric magic of nineteenth-century serials with the relentless momentum of a modern noir thriller, populating the story with unforgettable companions like the razor-witted, tragic vagabond Fermín Romero de Torres. Spending a monthly credit on this beloved modern classic guarantees an enchanting listening voyage that celebrates the immortal power of printed stories to outlive tyranny and heartbreak. By wrapping the seductive wonder of magical realism around a classic noir mystery, Zafón crafts a breathtaking masterpiece that every lover of books and literature will cherish forever.

Narrator Soundcheck: Daniel Weyman

Daniel Weyman delivers a sensational, evocative performance that captures the gothic romance and shadowy danger of Zafón’s Barcelona with peerless dramatic flair. Weyman infuses young Daniel with tender curiosity and growing maturity, while his portrayal of Fermín Romero de Torres—overflowing with flamboyant wit, bombastic humor, and underlying melancholy—is an absolute showstopper. He lends chilling malice to Inspector Fumero and romantic yearning to Carax’s lost tragic figures.
