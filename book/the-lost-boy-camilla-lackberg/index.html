About this Audiobook

When respected town finance chief Mats Sverin is shot dead inside his flat just as his childhood sweetheart Nathalie flees with her young son to isolated Ghost Isle Valö, Patrik Hedström hunts dark secrets on both mainland and island.

Over Fourteen Hours Blending Ghost Isle Fog With Municipal Betrayal

Returning briefly from terrifying personal shocks after The Drowning, Patrik Hedström confronts the cold-blooded execution of golden-boy council treasurer Mats Sverin inside his own flat—discovering the respected official secretly carried deep financial entanglements before his murder. Simultaneously, Mats's first love Nathalie hides out on eerie fogbound lighthouse island Valö with her little son, drawn straight toward supernatural local legends.

*The Lost Boy* layers smoky gothic isolation over crisp Nordic Noir investigation. Shifting cleanly between crooked town-hall Crime and eerie lighthouse hauntings adds irresistible atmosphere to methodical Police Procedural and Detective Mystery sleuthing.

Spanning fourteen hours and thirty-six minutes for one credit, Book 7 builds ghostly seaside tension straight into its Valö sister tale, Buried Angels.
