The eagerly awaited third installment in the record-breaking Empyrean series, Rebecca Yarros’s *Onyx Storm* plunges listeners into twenty-two hours of all-out continental warfare, dark magical corruption, and heartbreaking sacrifices. In the wake of the catastrophic battle of Basgiath, Violet Sorrengail and her bonded dragons must navigate the desperate realities of an open rebellion against both imperial Navarre and the encroaching horde of venin wyvern-riders. With Xaden Riorson fighting against his own inner darkness to preserve his humanity, Violet travels across uncharted territories to forge desperate alliances, uncovering ancient prophecies that threaten to shatter everything she believes in, threatening to destroy the tenuous alliances holding their rebel army together in the face of annihilation.
