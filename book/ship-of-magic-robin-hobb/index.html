About this Audiobook

In Bingtown, the Vestrit family fights to retain their sentient wizardwood liveship Vivacia amidst pirate raiders, sea serpents, and economic ruin.

Wizardwood Consciousness, Pirate Kings, and the Maritime Majesty of Bingtown

Spanning more than thirty-five monumental, richly textured hours, *Ship of Magic* launches Robin Hobb’s acclaimed *Liveship Traders* trilogy, universally regarded as one of the towering masterworks of nautical epic fantasy. Set south of the Six Duchies in the bustling mercantile hub of Bingtown and the untamed Pirate Isles, the story revolves around 'liveships'—sentient sailing vessels carved from rare, enchanted wizardwood that 'quicken' into living, breathing consciousness after three generations of family members have died upon their wooden decks.

When patriarch Ephron Vestrit dies aboard the liveship *Vivacia*, his headstrong, seafaring daughter Althea expects to inherit command. Instead, family politics and crushing merchant debts hand *Vivacia* to her tyrannical brother-in-law Kyle Haven, who banishes Althea and forces his sensitive, monastery-bound son Wintrow to serve as a brutalized ship's boy. Driven by fierce defiance, Althea disguises herself as a common sailor to earn her sea-papers, while far out on the open waves, the charismatic, ruthless pirate captain Kennit schemes to seize a liveship to crown himself sovereign of the pirate islands. Hobb’s unmatched psychological depth, moral complexity, and grand maritime worldbuilding are on full, mesmerizing display throughout every voyage. Her richly drawn cast navigates perilous reefs, treacherous sea serpents, and shifting familial loyalties with visceral realism.

Narrator Anne Flosnik delivers an extraordinary, atmospheric performance across this massive thirty-five-hour epic. Flosnik gives the sentient *Vivacia* an ethereal, haunting acoustic presence, captures Kennit’s calculating narcissism with chilling charisma, and voices young Wintrow’s spiritual agony with profound poignancy. Alongside seafaring epics like The Final Architecture and dark character dramas like The Blade Itself, *Ship of Magic* is an unforgettable credit investment for any fantasy connoisseur seeking majestic prose and oceanic wonder.

Narrator Soundcheck: Anne Flosnik

Anne Flosnik’s narration on Ship of Magic is an atmospheric, deeply theatrical tour de force. Flosnik handles Robin Hobb’s dense psychological characterizations with remarkable patience, giving each family member and pirate a distinct, memorable cadence. Her ethereal, reverberant voice for the living figurehead Vivacia feels genuinely supernatural, while her depiction of violent oceanic storms and sea serpents surges with dramatic power. Pristinely recorded across thirty-five hours, this audiobook is an auditory feast for fantasy lovers.
