Sarah J. Maas returns to the mortal realm and Fae courts in this high-octane ACOTAR installment, chronicling the Night Court's frantic efforts to heal ancient fractures before Koschei the Deathless unleashes total devastation.

Sarah J. Maas's 10-Hour ACOTAR Epic of Shattered Alliances, Ancient Evils, and Cinematic Immersion

Picking up in the turbulent aftermath of the Hybern War and the revelations of recent betrayals, Sarah J. Maas's *A Court of Splintered Harmony* plunges listeners back into the perilous Fae courts of Prythian. Deep in the heart of the continent, the ancient dread of Koschei the Deathless stirs from his millennial confinement, forging sinister alliances aimed squarely at reclaiming the human realms. Faced with creeping darkness, the rulers and guardians of the Night Court find themselves immobilized by internal mistrust and deep personal rifts. To avert catastrophic annihilation, the Valkyries and their inner circle must confront painful truths, heal splintered bonds, and mount a desperate resistance across treacherous magical frontiers.

For audio listeners, this release presents a fascinating choice between two distinct listening experiences. While Elizabeth Evans's standard solo recording delivers the intimate, character-driven storytelling familiar to longtime Maas devotees, GraphicAudio’s ten-hour dramatized adaptation transforms the novel into a full-throttle cinematic spectacle. Produced with a diverse cast of professional voice actors, customized orchestral themes, and rich atmospheric soundscapes—from the howling winds of the Illyrian steppes to the clashing blades of Valkyrie training—it brings Prythian's sprawling battlefields to vivid life. Featured prominently in our Cinematic Audiobooks and Epic Romantasy collections, this production gives the high-stakes romantic tension and explosive combat sequences the immersive acoustic canvas they deserve.

Clocking in at a taut ten hours, this production represents exceptional credit value, amplified significantly by the inclusion of three highly sought-after bonus chapters focusing on Nesta and Cassian, Feyre and Rhysand, and Azriel. Whether you are catching the solo performance on release day or pre-ordering this dramatized production for the ultimate sensory immersion, it marks an essential chapter in the expanding Maas multiverse. It delivers all the emotional devastation, scorching romantic payoffs, and heart-pounding combat that have made Sarah J. Maas a global publishing juggernaut, serving as an irresistible, credit-worthy addition to any fantasy listener's library.
