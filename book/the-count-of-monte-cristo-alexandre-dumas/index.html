The Count of Monte Cristo

Author: Alexandre Dumas | Narrator: Bill Homewood

Runtime: 52h 41m | Genre: Classics, Revenge, Historical Fiction, Picaresque, Sea Story

About this Audiobook

Thrown in prison for a crime he has not committed, Edmond Dantes is confined to the grim fortress of If. There he learns of a great hoard of treasure hidden on the Isle of Monte Cristo.

Alexandre Dumas's 52-Hour Masterclass in Patient, Cold-Blooded Vengeance and Grand Parisian Intrigue

Betrayed by jealous rivals on the eve of his wedding, young sailor Edmond Dantès is wrongfully arrested and cast into the grim, subterranean dungeons of the Château d'If to rot in silence. What follows across fifty-two electrifying hours is universally celebrated as the greatest, most meticulously orchestrated vengeance quest in world literature. Mentored in science, languages, and philosophy by the brilliant fellow prisoner Abbé Faria, Dantès stages an audacious escape from the fortress, unearths an unimaginable treasure trove on the desolate island of Monte Cristo, and re-emerges into the dazzling salons of high Parisian society as a mysterious, omnipotent count. Methodically exploiting the greed, vanity, and buried moral crimes of his former tormentors, he delivers a calculated retribution that feels utterly intoxicating from beginning to end.

Where a physical brick of twelve hundred dense pages can intimidate even seasoned bibliophiles, the spoken word transforms Dumas's serialized masterpiece into a breathless, modern political thriller. Alexandre Dumas originally published the story in daily newspaper installments, and that episodic cliffhanger pacing thrives in audio format. Long transit commutes, marathon workouts, and solitary chores completely disappear as Dantès unfurls his intricate, interlocking traps across the corrupt French aristocracy. As celebrated in our The Bucket List collection and echoed in the epic trials of Les Misérables, the sheer scale of the unabridged audio allows every subplot, disguised identity, and philosophical dilemma regarding divine justice to blossom with unmatched dramatic power.

For sheer return on investment, spending a single credit on this fifty-two-hour tour de force is one of the most rewarding decisions any audiobook lover can make. Rather than burning through three or four fleeting ten-hour titles, you secure over two continuous days of high-stakes drama, aristocratic romance, dungeon escapes, and philosophical depth. It is a triumphant, unputdownable listening experience that redefines what long-form audio storytelling can achieve, providing weeks of captivating entertainment.

Narrator Soundcheck: Bill Homewood

Bill Homewood delivers a tour de force performance that stands as the undisputed benchmark for Dumas audiobooks. His aristocratic, sonorous baritone brings rich theatrical distinction to over forty unique characters, distinguishing Dantès's icy, calculating menace from the pompous arrogance of Danglars and the sleazy desperation of Villefort. Homewood maintains crisp French pronunciation, impeccable dramatic pacing, and electric momentum across all fifty-two hours without ever faltering or succumbing to vocal fatigue.
