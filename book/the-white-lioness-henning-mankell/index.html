The White Lioness

Author: Henning Mankell | Narrator: Sean Barrett (UK) / Dick Hill (US)

Runtime: 15h 34m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Police Procedural, Mystery, Crime, Detective

About this Audiobook

Searching for a vanished estate agent in icy rural Scania uncovers a severed finger and a trail linking modest Swedish farmland straight to rogue white-supremacist assassins plotting political chaos in late-apartheid South Africa.

Over Fifteen Hours Linking Rural Scania to a Deadly Apartheid Conspiracy

Months after returning scarred from Riga in The Dogs of Riga, Inspector Kurt Wallander investigates the routine disappearance of Louise Åkerblom—a respected local estate agent and church volunteer whose burnt car turns up beside a rural well containing her corpse and a mysterious severed finger. Unknown to Ystad police, an extremist Afrikaner cell in 1992 South Africa is training a cold-blooded hitman nearby to derail democratic elections, pulling Kurt straight into the crosshairs of global terror.

Listening to *The White Lioness* showcases Mankell's ambitious stretch across continents while never forgetting Nordic Noir's melancholy roots. Intercutting sweaty Pretoria safehouses with stubborn Police Procedural legwork across soggy Scania turns international Crime into nail-biting Detective suspense filled with tragic Mystery.

Running fifteen hours and thirty-four minutes for one credit, Book 3 hits hard—ending on a haunting line-of-duty trauma that leaves Wallander hollowed out heading into The Man Who Smiled.
