About this Audiobook

When a troubled teenage runaway is found murdered in an Oslo forest clearing—staged inside a ring of candles on a bed of feathers—Holger Munch pulls suspended detective Mia Krüger back into an escalating ritual hunt.

Over Eleven Hours of Ritualistic Oslo Suspicion and Cold-Case Profiling

Following the harrowing events of I'm Travelling Alone, haunted profiler Mia Krüger is officially suspended pending psychological evaluation—struggling through dark isolation and heavy medication. When the emaciated body of a vanished teenage girl is discovered arranged atop a bed of feathers inside an eerie forest pentagram of extinguished candles, veteran homicide boss Holger Munch ignores departmental red tape and recruits Mia off the books. Aided by gifted young hacker Gabriel, the unit uncovers digital clues pointing to a terrifying countdown: the killer is already stalking a second victim.

In audio format, *The Owl Always Hunts at Night* exemplifies everything readers crave in dark Nordic Noir and slow-burn Police Procedural fiction. Bjørk weaves gritty Oslo Crime forensics with suffocating Psychological Thriller dread, earning a standout place inside our Nordic Noir & Scandi Crime showcase.

Clocking in at eleven hours and twenty-three minutes for a single credit, *The Owl Always Hunts at Night* is relentless, deeply atmospheric, and impossible to turn off before midnight.
