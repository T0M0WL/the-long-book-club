Ovid’s 18-Hour Roman Masterpiece of Cosmic Creation, Flood Deluges, and Transformation

Across fifteen sweeping books and two hundred and fifty mythological transformations, Roman poet Publius Ovidius Naso created Western literature’s most astonishing narrative kaleidoscope. Opening at the absolute genesis of the universe—the emergence of ordered matter from primeval chaos—*Metamorphoses* wastes no time confronting cosmic catastrophe. Appalled by the wickedness of King Lycaon and humanity's violent hubris, Jupiter unleashes a colossal tidal cataclysm that drowns the earth beneath an endless sea. Only two pious survivors—Deucalion and his wife Pyrrha—drift atop a fragile raft to the peaks of Mount Parnassus, mourning an emptied world before casting stones behind their backs that magically soften and spring to life as a new human race.

What distinguishes this definitive Penguin Classics unabridged recording is David Raeburn’s acclaimed modern verse translation, brought to life by an extraordinary vocal ensemble led by veteran actor Martin Jarvis OBE. Jarvis treats Ovid not as an intimidating schoolroom relic, but as an irreverent, witty, and deeply psychological storyteller. From the poignant tragedies of Daedalus and Icarus, Orpheus and Eurydice, and Pyramus and Thisbe to the hubris of Arachne and Phaethon driving the sun chariot into flame, Jarvis navigates the rapid tonal shifts between divine comedy and mortal heartbreak with consummate virtuosity. Paired with ancient cosmological anchors like The Books of Enoch and Stephen Fry’s modern Hellenic retellings in Mythos, this production showcases Roman storytelling at its zenith.

Clocking in at nearly eighteen hours for a single Audible credit, *Metamorphoses* delivers extraordinary literary mileage. Every chapter pulses with the fundamental theme that nothing in the universe perishes; everything merely transforms into a new shape. Supported by subtle performances from John Sackville and Maya Saroya, and enriched by a scholarly introduction from Denis Feeney, this is an indispensable, credit-maximizing recording that belongs in every serious audiobook enthusiast’s library.

Narrator Soundcheck: Martin Jarvis, John Sackville, Maya Saroya

Martin Jarvis OBE delivers a bravura performance across nearly eighteen hours, balancing Ovid’s biting Roman irony with sweeping theatrical majesty. Jarvis possesses an unmatched narrative rhythm: he rolls through hexameter verse with effortless naturalism, giving life to petulant Olympians, weeping nymphs, and terrified mortals without ever sounding pedantic or stilted. John Sackville and Maya Saroya provide polished vocal contrast for key narrative interludes, supported by Penguin Audio’s warm, intimate studio production where every poetic cadence rings true.
