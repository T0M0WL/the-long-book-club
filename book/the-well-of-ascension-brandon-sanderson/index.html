About this Audiobook

The impossible has been accomplished. The Lord Ruler – the man who held the world in his iron grip for a thousand years – is dead.

Brandon Sanderson’s Gripping Twenty-Seven-Hour Fantasy Siege and Political Intrigue in Luthadel Audio

In most fantasy epics, defeating the invincible Dark Lord marks the joyous end of the story—but in *The Well of Ascension*, Brandon Sanderson dares to ask the far more complicated question: what happens next? With the immortal Lord Ruler dead, idealistic young scholar Elend Venture ascends the throne of Luthadel, only to find the empire plunging into financial collapse and anarchy. As three colossal rival armies, led by ambitious warlords and terrifying armies of koloss brutes, surround the city walls, Vin takes to the mist-shrouded spires as Elend’s deadliest protector. Plagued by self-doubt over his right to rule and betrayed by treacherous noble factions, Elend relies on Vin's deadly prowess and the ancient Terris keeper Sazed to decode the enigmatic writings inscribed on metal plates, hunting down enemy Allomancers while unraveling a mythical well of divine power.

In audio format, Sanderson’s second Mistborn volume thrives as a claustrophobic, politically charged thriller packed with heart-stopping action and mounting supernatural dread. Narrator Michael Kramer brings immense sensory tension to Vin’s nighttime rooftop patrols, the eerie, pulsing thrum of the Well echoing in her ears, and the terrifying brutality of Allomantic warfare. Linked as the vital bridge between The Final Empire and The Hero of Ages in our Epic Fantasy catalog, this twenty-seven-hour recording turns a desperate urban siege into an electrifying auditory masterclass that will keep you glued to your headphones.

Providing over twenty-seven hours of intricate political maneuvering and explosive magic for a single credit, this unabridged production offers superb long-term listening value. Sanderson deftly subverts classical heroic tropes, showing how good intentions can lead to catastrophic consequences and building an atmosphere of creeping paranoia that leads into one of the most shocking cliffhangers in fantasy literature. Spending a monthly token on this dark, sophisticated middle chapter secures an unforgettable listening voyage that enriches the entire Cosmere mythology and sets the stage for an apocalyptic finale.
