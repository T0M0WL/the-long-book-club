The Lost Metal

Author: Brandon Sanderson | Narrator: Michael Kramer

About this Audiobook

In the explosive Era 2 finale, Wax and Wayne race against The Set and the alien god Autonomy to prevent the atomic destruction of Scadrial.

Nuclear Harmonium, the Ghostbloods, and the Heartbreaking Farewell to Wax and Wayne

Spanning more than nineteen colossal, emotionally shattering hours, *The Lost Metal* is Brandon Sanderson’s epic grand finale to *Mistborn Era 2*, uniting frontier detective grit with full-blown Cosmere crossover spectacle. For years, Waxillium Ladrian has pursued 'The Set', but in this climax, the stakes escalate from local conspiracies to planetary extinction. The alien Shard known as Autonomy has cast her covetous eye on Scadrial, deploying an invading army and arming The Set with an atomic-level bomb made of corrupted Harmonium and Trellium, capable of vaporizing the entire city of Elendel.

To save their world, Wax ascends as an official Mistborn operative of Harmony, while Marasi is recruited by the mysterious inter-world organization known as the Ghostbloods, encountering world-hoppers from Roshar, Nalthis, and Sel. Wayne steps into the spotlight as an extraordinary hero whose eccentric, gold-healing sacrifice becomes one of the most emotional highlights in fantasy literature. Sanderson expertly merges detective chemistry with high-stakes nuclear physics and metaphysical Cosmere warfare, tying off decades of character arcs in an exhilarating, deeply satisfying resolution.

Narrator Michael Kramer delivers what stands as one of the finest vocal exhibitions in his entire career. Kramer voices the massive international cast—including interplanetary Ghostbloods, haughty kandra, and terrified city civilians—with flawless clarity and emotional authority. His handling of Wayne's final heroics is unforgettable, moving listeners to genuine tears while delivering jaw-dropping action. For fantasy listeners seeking maximum credit value and epic worldbuilding, *The Lost Metal* is an indispensable masterpiece.
