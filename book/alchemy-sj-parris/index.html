About this Audiobook

Sent by Queen Elizabeth I in 1588 to Holy Roman Emperor Rudolf II's eccentric court in Prague, Giordano Bruno investigates the murder of a famed court alchemist amid rival spies and the hunt for the philosopher's stone.

Seventeen Hours of Hermetic Secrets and Murder Inside Rudolf II's 1588 Prague

Following Mary Queen of Scots' execution and the shadow of looming Armada war, Queen Elizabeth's government dispatches Giordano Bruno to central Europe in winter 1588—tasked with assessing whether Holy Roman Emperor Rudolf II's eccentric court in Prague will remain neutral. Instead of quiet diplomacy, Bruno steps straight into a snowy hotbed of rival astronomers, necromancers, and imperial spies chasing the legendary philosopher's stone. When a famed court alchemist is discovered murdered inside a locked chamber, Emperor Rudolf himself orders Bruno to catch the killer.

On audio, *Alchemy* brilliantly unites the philosophical roots of Heresy with a grand European Historical Mystery setting. Wandering past Prague's Jewish Quarter, frozen Vltava bridges, and Rudolf's cabinet of curiosities delivers unforgettable Historical Fiction and fair-play Whodunnit puzzlecraft infused with Political Intrigue.

At seventeen hours and seven minutes for one credit, *Alchemy* gives listeners rich Renaissance atmosphere, genuine philosophical wonder, and gripping murder investigation.
