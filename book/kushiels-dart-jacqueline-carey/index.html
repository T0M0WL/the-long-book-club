About this Audiobook

The land of Terre d'Ange is a place of rigorous beauty and grace. It is a country where gods walk the earth. And it is a country where Phèdre nó Delaunay was sold into indentured servitude.

Jacqueline Carey's 31-Hour Lush Masterpiece of Courtesan Espionage, Divine Geas, and Epic Betrayal

Widely heralded as the foundational masterpiece that paved the way for modern epic Romantasy, Jacqueline Carey’s *Kushiel's Dart* is an opulent, thirty-one-hour fantasy saga of court espionage, sacred eroticism, and high political treason. Set in Terre d'Ange—a gilded, Renaissance-inspired realm where the primary commandment is 'Love as thou wilt'—the story follows Phèdre nó Delaunay, a young courtesan marked by the god Kushiel with a scarlet mote in her eye that condemns her to find divine pleasure in physical pain. Trained as both an elite companion and an incisive political spy, Phèdre uncovers an insidious conspiracy that threatens to tear her homeland apart, embarking on a harrowing journey alongside an austere warrior-monk sworn to protect her, forging a complex bond of sacred duty, mutual sacrifice, and profound romantic loyalty.

In audio format, Carey’s ornate, lyrical prose shines with the hypnotic majesty of high historical drama. The unhurried thirty-one-hour runtime allows the intricate diplomatic intrigues, theological philosophies, and complex sexual politics of Terre d'Ange to develop with sophisticated literary depth rather than rushed sensationalism. Featured as a crown jewel in our Epic Romantasy collection and sharing thematic kinship with The Secret History, this recording provides the temporal room necessary to witness Phèdre's transformation from a pawn into a legendary political mastermind who reshapes the geopolitical fate of multiple kingdoms through courage and intellect.

Spending a single credit on thirty-one hours of Carey’s sumptuous storytelling provides an unparalleled return on investment for listeners who appreciate mature, intellectually challenging fantasy romance. It is an intoxicating, unforgettable epic that combines the breathless intrigue of a spy thriller with the devastating emotional power of an epic romance. It is a true modern classic that rewards patient, dedicated listening across several weeks, delivering an unforgettable listening experience that stands among the greatest fantasy achievements ever recorded in the history of the fantasy romance genre.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jessica Whittaker

Jessica Whittaker brings formal elegance, refined diction, and emotional nuance to Jacqueline Carey’s complex, archaic prose style. Whittaker captures Phèdre's intelligent, observant nature with quiet aristocratic grace, contrasting her delicate composure against the stern, stoic vows of her warrior companion Joscelin. Her measured pacing honors the formal cadence of Terre d'Ange's royal court while maintaining dramatic momentum through treacherous kidnappings and perilous escapes, supported by clean, broadcast-standard studio acoustics throughout all thirty-one hours.
