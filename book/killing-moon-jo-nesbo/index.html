About this Audiobook

Drinking himself oblivion-ward in Los Angeles until Mexican loan sharks threaten an elderly friend's life, Harry flies back to Oslo with an eccentric rogue crew to investigate parasite-linked murders tied to tycoon Markus Røed.

Over Sixteen Hours Pulling a Misfit Squad Together Over Debt and Parasites

Drinking himself systematically toward the grave inside Los Angeles dive bars after Knife, Harry Hole only accepts a return flight to Oslo to clear a million-dollar Mexican cartel death debt threatening elderly actress Lucille. His benefactor is ruthless property tycoon Markus Røed, prime suspect after two party-going young women vanish and turn up mutilated via gruesome brain-eating parasites—forcing Harry to assemble an eccentric unofficial crew against Katrine Bratt's official investigation.

*Killing Moon* breathes dark wit and fresh squad friction back into Nordic Noir. Combining twisted biological Crime horror with sly Detective rivalry across Police Procedural lines delivers terrific Thriller bite.

Clocking sixteen hours and nine minutes for one credit, Book 13 reinvents Harry as an outside consultant straight into Kill Shot.
