About this Audiobook

Fresh off their wedding, Reyna and Kianthe uncover a rogue alchemist conspiracy threatening their queendom—even as skyrocketing fame floods quiet Tawney with tourists and forces an agonizing choice about their bookshop.

Over Ten Hours of Bittersweet Closure for Fantasy's Favourite Book-and-Tea Shop

Picking up straight from the cliffhanger ending of Tea You at the Altar, Reyna expects a peaceful cup of tea beside her new wife Kianthe—only to wake up locked inside an abandoned watchtower guarded by mysterious rogue alchemists. Though the realm's strongest mage rescues her swiftly enough, the pair stumble onto radical alchemical traps threatening the Queendom's magic. Meanwhile, an even trickier crisis is unfolding back home in Tawney: word of their cozy heroism has turned *New Leaf Tomes and Tea* into a massive tourist magnet, pushing quiet neighbors to breaking point and forcing the brides to ask if fame has ruined the haven they fought to create.

Listening to *Alchemy and a Cup of Tea* brings Rebecca Thorne's beloved Cozy Fantasy quartet to a remarkably mature, tender conclusion. Balancing magical detective work with poignant Slice of Life reflections on growing older, community care, and Books About Books nostalgia alongside enduring Sapphic Romance, it earns heartfelt applause across our Cozy Fantasy.

At ten hours and twenty-two minutes for a single Audible credit, *Alchemy and a Cup of Tea* wraps up every dragon egg, political promise, and steaming pot of Earl Grey with warmth and grace.

Jessica Threet closes the Tomes & Tea quartet with deep emotional tenderness and lively humor across more than ten hours. Her affectionate chemistry between Reyna and Kianthe feels effortlessly natural, elevating both the rogue-alchemist thrills and the bittersweet bookshop farewells.
