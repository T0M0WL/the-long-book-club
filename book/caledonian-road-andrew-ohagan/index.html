Caledonian Road

Author: Andrew O'Hagan | Narrator: Michael Abubakar

Runtime: 22h 51m | Genre: Contemporary Fiction, London, Crime, Literary Fiction

About this Audiobook

A state-of-the-nation novel centering on Campbell Flynn, a celebrity intellectual whose life unravels over an incendiary year in London. Explores class, race, money, and corruption.

A Sweeping Dickensian Satire of Modern London's High Society and Deep Corruption

Andrew O'Hagan crafts a panoramic, state-of-the-nation social satire that dissects contemporary London with razor-sharp wit, moral urgency, and unsparing psychological insight. At the center of this urban whirlwind stands Campbell Flynn, an acclaimed celebrity art historian, television commentator, and university professor whose glamorous public persona masks a mounting mountain of private debt, vanity, and ethical compromises. When Flynn takes on a brilliantly enigmatic working-class student named Milo Mangasha as his protégé, he unknowingly sets off a catastrophic domino effect that exposes the tangled web connecting Russian oligarchs, corrupt members of parliament, cryptocurrency fraudsters, teenage drill rappers, and exploited sweatshop laborers across the capital's deeply divided boroughs.

On audio, O'Hagan's vast tapestry of contemporary British voices springs to life with vibrant, theatrical energy and startling social realism. The sonic contrast between rarefied Mayfair drawing rooms, chaotic university seminar halls, and gritty council estates becomes immediately visceral, pulling the listener into the frantic cacophony of a great metropolis teetering on the brink of moral collapse. Highlighted in our curated Best Audiobooks of 2025 and our Best Fiction Audiobooks catalog, the performance turns a daunting twenty-three-hour social novel into an addictive, high-society scandal sheet that you simply cannot pause, revealing how interconnected every strata of modern urban life truly is.

Offering nearly twenty-three hours of blistering social commentary, biting humor, and authentic human drama, this ambitious novel provides exceptional credit value for listeners craving intelligent, expansive contemporary literature. O'Hagan combines the grand scope of a classic Victorian serial with the cutting-edge anxieties, economic pressures, and digital rhythms of the smartphone era, keeping suspense taut across dozens of intersecting plotlines. A single credit delivers an entire season's worth of prestige drama, offering brilliant character studies and unforgettable dialogue that reward every single minute of your attention and contemplation, making Campbell Flynn's dramatic fall from grace one of the most compelling audio experiences of the year.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Abubakar

Michael Abubakar delivers an extraordinary tour de force, handling an immense roster of diverse characters with effortless chameleon-like versatility. He pivots flawlessly from the haughty, intellectual cadence of Campbell Flynn to the sharp street slang of young North London drill rappers, aristocratic peeresses, and Eastern European oligarchs without missing a beat. Abubakar’s energetic rhythm drives O’Hagan’s biting satire forward at an exhilarating pace, never sacrificing emotional nuance. The audio production is clean, crisp, and pristinely balanced, making every rapid-fire exchange land with maximum comedic and dramatic impact.
