About this Audiobook

Hunting a calculating Oslo bank robber who executes tellers to exact second-count precision while waking with blackout gaps hours before his former girlfriend's apparent suicide, Harry walks straight into a deadly frame-up.

Nearly Fifteen Hours Racing a Calculating Bank Robber Through Blackout Gaps

Opening with an Oslo bank heist executed to twenty-five-second ruthless choreography—ending with a female teller shot dead in cold blood—The Redbreast veteran Harry Hole partners with razor-minded new investigator Beate Lønn to track a master criminal known only as the Executioner. Worse, Harry wakes one morning from a mysterious twelve-hour alcohol blackout only to discover his bohemian ex-lover Anna shot dead in her apartment across town, dragging ruthless internal-affairs hunter Tom Waaler right onto his heels.

*Nemesis* packs ferocious clockwork twistiness inside classic Nordic Noir. Juggling dual Crime plots with relentless Police Procedural pressure delivers breathless Thriller paranoia and razor-sharp Detective misdirection.

Running fourteen hours and fifty-three minutes for one credit, Book 4 grips fiercely—setting up the final Oslo Trilogy showdown in The Devil's Star.
