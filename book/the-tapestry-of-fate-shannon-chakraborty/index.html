About this Audiobook

In this lush twenty-hour historical fantasy epic, Shannon Chakraborty weaves ancient Middle Eastern and Indian Ocean folklore into a high-stakes tale of colliding empires, perilous magic, and hard-won destiny.

Twenty Lush, Multi-Voice Hours of Myth, Empire, and Historical Fantasy

A Goodreads Choice 2026 Fantasy finalist and one of the year’s most lavishly imagined epics, Shannon Chakraborty’s *The Tapestry of Fate* sweeps listeners across gilded courts, bustling spice ports, and treacherous magical crossroads steeped in Middle Eastern and Indian Ocean Folklore. Blending deft political intrigue with ancient supernatural bargains, Chakraborty crafts an expansive Historical Fantasy world where mortals, mystics, and scheming nobles collide over destinies woven centuries before they were born.

In audio form, the novel’s sensory richness—spices, sea salt, whispered conspiracies, and cracking arcane power—comes vibrantly alive. Featuring as one of the cornerstone 20+ hour anchors of our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 alongside fellow standout The Red Winter and This Kingdom Will Not Kill Me, it delivers the kind of transportive escapism that makes long commutes vanish.

At twenty hours and two minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Tapestry of Fate* is grand, warm-blooded Epic Fantasy crafted with unmistakable care.
