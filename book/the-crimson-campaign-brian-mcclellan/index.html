Winter Retreat and Gunpowder Treason: Christian Rodska Fires on All Cylinders in The Crimson Campaign

In *The Crimson Campaign*, Brian McClellan expands his brilliant flintlock fantasy universe with relentless military stakes and white-knuckle tactical action. Following the disastrous invasion of Kez, Field Marshal Tamas finds himself cut off behind enemy lines with a battered seventh brigade, presumed dead by his nation. Facing overwhelming odds and bitter winter conditions, Tamas must lead his men on a grueling, hundreds-of-miles fighting retreat across hostile territory. Meanwhile, in the capital city of Adopest, his powder mage son Taniel Two-Shot battles corrupt politicians, enemy spies, and a creeping divine threat after the brutal assassination of their newfound republic's leaders.

The late, great Christian Rodska turns in a sensational performance that captures the grit and smoke of Napoleonic-era warfare. Rodska’s gruff, world-weary vocal timbre is the ideal acoustic instrument for Field Marshal Tamas, exuding military discipline, strategic brilliance, and paternal concern. Rodska also shines in depicting the laconic courage of Taniel and the relentless investigative determination of Inspector Adamat, keeping the multi-threaded narrative cohesive and fast-moving. Listeners who love the gunpowder intrigue of Promise of Blood or the grand military maneuvers in Words of Radiance will be enthralled.

At just over twenty hours for a single monthly credit, *The Crimson Campaign* avoids any sophomore slump, delivering non-stop tactical maneuvers, desperate bayonet charges, and explosive sorcery. McClellan balances large-scale battlefield logistics with intimate character grief, making every bullet fired count. It is an electrifying listen that cements the series as a modern military fantasy titan.
