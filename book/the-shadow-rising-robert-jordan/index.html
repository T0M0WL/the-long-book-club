About this Audiobook

The seals of Shayol Ghul are weakening. The Dark One is reaching out. The Shadow is rising to cover the world.

Robert Jordan’s Forty-One-Hour Apex of Aiel Culture and Ancestral History in Audio

Widely acclaimed by readers and critics as the undisputed masterpiece of The Wheel of Time, *The Shadow Rising* shatters conventional high-fantasy tropes to deliver an unparalleled triumph of anthropological world-building and military drama. Leaving the western kingdoms behind, Rand al'Thor leads the Ta'veren across the arid expanse of the Aiel Waste to the sacred glass columns of Rhuidean. Joined by the fiery Maiden Aviendha and wrestling with the treacherous Forsaken Lanfear, Rand must prove his lineage to the clan chiefs as the Car'a'carn. Simultaneously, Perrin Aybara returns to his home province of the Two Rivers, uniting stubborn farmers, archers, and craftsmen into a heroic guerrilla army against brutal Trolloc hordes and religious zealots of the Whitecloaks.

On audio, the monumental emotional scale and breathtaking ancestral revelations of Jordan’s fourth volume achieve an overwhelming, goosebump-inducing resonance. The legendary sequence in Rhuidean—in which Rand walks backward through time to witness the peaceful origins of the Aiel, the Cataclysmic Breaking of the World, and the tragic fall of the Age of Legends—becomes an auditory tour de force under Michael Kramer’s reverent, mythic delivery, elevated further by the tender, heartbreaking romance between Perrin and Faile Bashere. Linked as the crowning summit of The Eye of the World and The Dragon Reborn in our Epic Fantasy catalog, this forty-one-hour recording turns classic fantasy into an astonishing epic of cultural identity and tragic destiny.

Clocking in at nearly forty-one hours of peerless storytelling for a single credit, this release represents one of the most rewarding investments in the entire spoken-word medium. Jordan balances intimate emotional reunions, complex clan diplomacy, and devastating battlefield sieges with absolute narrative mastery, creating a living, breathing tapestry that rewards patient, close listening. Investing a monthly credit in this magnificent doorstopper guarantees weeks of unforgettable headphone immersion, proving beyond doubt why this book remains the shining golden standard of epic high-fantasy literature.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer, Kate Reading

Narrators Michael Kramer and Kate Reading deliver a truly magnificent, tour-de-force performance that captures the rich cultural depth of Jordan’s finest volume. Kramer’s handling of the sacred Rhuidean flashback sequence is an acoustic marvel, infusing centuries of ancestral sorrow with awe-inspiring gravitas, while his voicing of Perrin’s grief during the siege of Emond’s Field will move listeners to tears. Reading is equally phenomenal, establishing the proud, deadly cadence of the Maidens of the Spear with unmistakable vocal distinction. Flawlessly engineered with rich dynamic warmth across forty-one hours, this recording is a monumental listening triumph.
