About this Audiobook

In her deeply anticipated follow-up to Pachinko, Min Jin Lee charts the hopes, sacrifices, and fierce educational ambitions of a Korean immigrant family building a life around after-school academies across modern America.

Nearly Twenty-Three Absorbing Hours of Ambition, Family, and Sacrifice

Few literary releases of 2026 carried the anticipation of Min Jin Lee’s long-awaited follow-up to *Pachinko*, and *American Hagwon* rises to the moment with quiet, devastating mastery. Centered on a Korean immigrant family navigating the cutthroat world of after-school test-prep academies—the *hagwons* where educational perfection is treated as both salvation and currency—Lee widens her lens into a sweeping portrait of class anxiety, generational debt, and the hidden emotional cost of chasing the American Dream.

Lee’s superpower is turning seemingly ordinary choices—a tuition bill, a late-night study session, an unspoken parental expectation—into operatic Family Saga stakes. On audio, her crisp, empathetic Social Commentary unfolds with hypnotic momentum, making it a natural centerpiece of our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 alongside Kathryn Stockett’s The Calamity Club and Barbara Kingsolver’s Demon Copperhead.

Spanning twenty-two hours and fifty-five minutes for a single Audible credit, *American Hagwon* is deeply humane, fiercely intelligent Literary Fiction built for listeners who crave characters they can inhabit for weeks.

Narrator Soundcheck: Yerin Ha

Screen and stage actor Yerin Ha delivers an intimate, luminously nuanced performance across nearly twenty-three hours. Moving effortlessly between Korean phrases, generational accents, and the subtle tension of family dinner tables, Ha captures both the tenderness and relentless pressure pulsing beneath every page—keeping the emotional stakes taut from first chapter to last.
