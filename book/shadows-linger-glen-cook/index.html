About this Audiobook

After fragile victories leave the Empire splintered, Croaker's splinter of the Black Company hides as gravediggers and quack doctors in plague-haunted Juniper—where a living necromantic castle grows while old Taken hunt fugitive Raven.

Over Ten Hours of Plague-Ridden Siege Espionage Inside Juniper's Dark Shadows

Splitting cleanly away from open battlefield marches after The Black Company, Glen Cook drops Croaker and half the surviving mercenary brotherhood straight into the rotting port city of Juniper. Posing awkwardly as neighborhood doctors and coffin-builders while an eerie black castle literally grows stone by stone off corpse deliveries in the dead of night, the soldiers face suffocating urban tension—especially as desperate runaway Raven hides a haunted silent child while the resurrected Taken, Limper, stalks closer for blood.

In audio format, *Shadows Linger* proves Grimdark works just as potently in whispered alleyways as on mud-choked battlefields. Cook marries grubby Low Fantasy slum humor from bickering wizards Goblin and One-Eye with bone-chilling Dark Fantasy horror around the sinister Barter and Marron Shed's creeping moral rot. The result expands the Sword and Sorcery and Military Fantasy blueprint laid down in Book 1 with unforgettable gothic paranoia.

Spanning ten hours and thirty-three minutes for a single Audible credit, *Shadows Linger* tightens every thematic screw—delivering one of fantasy's most haunting slow-burn escalations right into the explosive finale of The White Rose.
