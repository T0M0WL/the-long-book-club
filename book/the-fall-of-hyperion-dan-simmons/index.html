About this Audiobook

In the stunning continuation of the epic adventure begun in Hyperion, Simmons returns us to a far future resplendent with drama and invention.

The Thunderous Climax to Dan Simmons’s Hyperion Duology and the TechnoCore War in Audio

Abandoning the Canterbury Tales anthology structure of its predecessor, *The Fall of Hyperion* plunges directly into the devastating, full-scale galactic war between the Hegemony and the Ousters as the Time Tombs finally begin to open. Viewed through the telepathic dreams of Joseph Severn, a cybernetic cybrid incarnation of poet John Keats, the narrative follows CEO Meina Gladstone in the war room of Web World Government. While Father Hoyt undergoes a horrific resurrection through the twin cruciform parasites and Colonel Kassad confronts the Shrike in a time-distorted duel of blades, the grand planetary Hegemony begins to collapse as the farcaster portals linking hundreds of human worlds are revealed to be a deadly trap orchestrated by the artificial intelligences of the TechnoCore.

On audio, Simmons's breathless, cinematic conclusion achieves an overwhelming emotional resonance and symphonic scale. Narrator Victor Bevine commands the sprawling multi-front conflict with unwavering vocal authority, seamlessly shifting from frantic planetary orbital battles and political betrayals in the Hegemony capital to the surreal, time-bending horrors unfolding in the Valley of the Time Tombs. The climactic sacrifice of the farcaster network, severing thousands of light-years of human civilization in a single instantaneous stroke, detonates in your headphones with unforgettable tragic grandeur. Featured as the crowning resolution to Hyperion in our Science Fiction Audiobooks collection, this twenty-hour recording turns space opera into an unforgettable auditory spectacle.

Clocking in at nearly twenty hours of non-stop narrative velocity for a single credit, this release provides sensational value for listeners eager for resolution. Simmons delivers jaw-dropping cosmic answers to every mystery posed in the first book, resolving the fate of the Shrike, the TechnoCore, and humanity's evolutionary destiny with breathtaking literary craftsmanship. Spending a monthly token on this epic conclusion secures an unputdownable listening experience that will cement the Hyperion Cantos as one of your favorite sci-fi sagas of all time.
