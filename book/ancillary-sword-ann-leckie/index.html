About this Audiobook

Appointed Fleet Captain of the warship Mercy of Kalr, AI Breq travels to Athoek Station to confront plantation corruption and civil war.

Colonial Exploitation, Splintered Consciousness, and Tea Ceremony Tradecraft

Spanning nearly twelve precise, intellectually rigorous hours, *Ancillary Sword* is Ann Leckie’s celebrated second installment in the Imperial Radch trilogy, following the historic Hugo, Nebula, and Arthur C. Clarke Award-winning triumph of Ancillary Justice. Having survived her vendetta against the Lord of the Radch, protagonist Breq—formerly the colossal twenty-troop carrier *Justice of Toren*, now trapped inside a single human ancillary body—finds herself reluctantly drafted by one faction of the multi-bodied ruler Anaander Mianaai as Fleet Captain of the warship *Mercy of Kalr*.

Dispatched to the remote, tea-producing system of Athoek to safeguard the sister of Lieutenant Awn—the human officer whose execution Breq was forced to commit two decades earlier—Breq encounters a society rotted by systemic colonial racism, labor exploitation, and oligarchic corruption. As civil war erupts across the multi-bodied clones of the Lord of the Radch, Breq must use her formidable computing intellect, tactical authority, and unexpected moral compassion to protect vulnerable disenfranchised workers while keeping her volatile, inexperienced human officers from mutiny. Leckie uses space opera to dissect institutional privilege, class cruelty, and justice with surgical precision.

The novel deepens Breq's unique perceptual reality, exploring how an entity designed to coordinate hundreds of human bodies experiences the claustrophobia of singular physical existence. Leckie seamlessly weaves dry comedy into serious sociological critique, particularly through the elaborate manners and tea rituals of Radchaai culture. In audio, the peerless Adjoa Andoh delivers an astonishing performance. Andoh’s crisp, aristocratic diction and uncanny ability to embody an artificial intelligence perceiving hundreds of biometric feeds simultaneously turn Leckie’s intricate prose into pure auditory theater. Alongside intellectual space opera masterpieces like Arkady Martine’s A Memory Called Empire and Iain M. Banks’s The Player of Games, *Ancillary Sword* is a dazzling, essential credit investment.

Narrator Soundcheck: Adjoa Andoh

Adjoa Andoh’s narration on Ancillary Sword is nothing short of iconic. Andoh captures Breq’s unique perspective—a hyper-intelligent artificial consciousness inhabiting fragile human flesh—with measured cadence, dry irony, and subtle emotional sorrow. Her handling of the Radch’s universal female pronouns and complex etiquette rituals is totally natural, while her voicing of panicked junior officers and entitled plantation oligarchs provides vibrant contrast. Pristine audio engineering makes every syllable an intellectual and acoustic pleasure.
