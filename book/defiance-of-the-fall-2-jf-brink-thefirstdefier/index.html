About this Audiobook

Securing his island base, Zac ventures into the wider transformed world, establishing Port Atwood while battling incursions from demonic forces and undead invaders.

Port Atwood Rises: Pavi Proczko Commands 24.5 Hours of Relentless Axe Cleaving and Dao Evolution

In *Defiance of the Fall 2*, JF Brink (TheFirstDefier) expands Zac Atwood’s world beyond his isolated survival island, plunging him into the chaotic geopolitics of an integrated Earth. Having crushed the demon incursion on his territory, Zac establishes Port Atwood as a sanctuary for displaced human survivors. But expanding outward brings him into direct confrontation with rival incursions—including a relentless Undead army led by a lich king and bloodthirsty alien invaders seeking to harvest Earth's fresh planetary core.

Narrator Pavi Proczko proves why he is the definitive voice of gritty, heavy-hitting progression fantasy. Proczko’s deep, resonant vocal timbre gives immense physical gravity to Zac’s crushing axe techniques, conveying every splintered bone, sonic shockwave, and burst of Cosmic Energy with visceral audio texture. His measured pacing keeps the detailed stat screens and Dao comprehension sequences engaging and hypnotic. Fans of brutal base-building and lone-wolf cultivation like The Primal Hunter will find this second volume completely irresistible.

At twenty-four and a half hours for a single monthly credit, this volume delivers an astonishing amount of high-intensity entertainment. Brink seamlessly weaves together territory management, high-tier loot acquisition, and profound Dao philosophy, ensuring that every battle moves Zac closer to cosmic dominance. It is a triumphant, action-packed continuation that cements the saga as a LitRPG giant.
