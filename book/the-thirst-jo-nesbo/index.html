About this Audiobook

Happily married to Rakel and sober at last as a Norwegian Police College lecturer, Harry is dragged unwillingly back into field hunting when women matched on Tinder are slaughtered by a vampirist using iron teeth.

Nearly Eighteen Ferocious Hours Pitting Sober Harry Against a Vampirist Predator

Savoring hard-won domestic stability as a sober lecturer and devoted husband to Rakel after Police, Harry Hole swore never to hunt monsters again—until young women matched through Tinder turn up slaughtered in Oslo with their necks torn open by iron prosthetic teeth. When forensic patterns mirror Valentin Gjertsen, a sadistic psychopath who slipped Harry's net years earlier, Police Chief Bellman leverages unbearable pressure forcing Harry back into a relentless unofficial task force.

*The Thirst* pushes Nordic Noir straight into breathless grand guignol Thriller overdrive. Pitting domestic love against grotesque Crime fetishism turns sharp Police Procedural strategy into agonizing Detective jeopardy.

Spanning seventeen hours and forty-eight minutes for a single credit, Book 11 winds the spring brutally tight right before Knife.
