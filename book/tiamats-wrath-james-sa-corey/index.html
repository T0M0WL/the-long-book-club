About this Audiobook

The underground resistance fights back against Laconia while ancient dark gods awaken in the space between the stars.

Guerrilla War Across the Stars: Jefferson Mays Delivers the Penultimate Expanse Masterpiece

Widely hailed by critics and fans as the finest installment of the entire nine-book saga, *Tiamat’s Wrath* delivers an extraordinary, emotionally crushing masterclass in space opera. With Jim Holden imprisoned as an honored trophy in the high palace of Laconia and the solar system subjugated, Naomi Nagata leads an underground guerrilla network from the hidden crevices of asteroid belts, launching surgical strikes against the Laconian war machine. Meanwhile, high consul Duarte’s reckless experiments with protomolecule immortality awaken the dark, reality-warping predators that killed the Ring builders eons ago.

Jefferson Mays turns in a career-defining performance that will leave listeners in tears. The novel features one of the most stunning, heroic sacrifices in contemporary literature, and Mays delivers the sequence with a quiet, breathtaking dignity that cements his place as an audiobook immortal. The interplay between Naomi's tactical genius, Bobbie's relentless frontline courage, and Amos's eerie loyalty creates non-stop narrative friction. Fans of dark, high-stakes military sci-fi like Dark Age will find this listening experience unforgettable.

At nineteen hours for a single credit, *Tiamat’s Wrath* is an absolute masterpiece of audio storytelling. It balances terrifying cosmic horror with deeply moving human courage, setting up the grand finale with unbearable dramatic tension.
