About this Audiobook

In 2060, three Oxford history students—Polly, Merope, and Mike—drop into Blitz-era London, Warwickshire country estates, and Dunkirk's evacuation beaches, only to find the time-travel drops snapping shut behind them.

Nearly Nineteen Hours Stranded Inside the Chaos and Courage of the 1940 London Blitz

In 2060, Oxford's history department relies on meticulous calculations ensuring modern observers can witness past events—even World War II—without altering history. Three bright students make separate jumps into wartime Britain: Michael Davies lands on the coast to witness heroic civilian boats at Dunkirk, Merope Ward poses as a servant overseeing rowdy London evacuees in a Warwickshire manor, and Polly Churchill clerks in a Knightsbridge shop as Luftwaffe bombers roar overhead. Yet one by one, their scheduled return drops fail to open, trapping every historian amid bombs, blackouts, and rationing.

On audio, *Blackout* showcases Connie Willis's unmistakable mastery of Time Travel and World War II social detail—winning both the Hugo and Nebula Awards alongside its second half, All Clear. Rather than staging tidy war-movie heroism, Willis immerses headphones in crowded Tube station shelters, bureaucratic mix-ups, and the quiet decency of ordinary Londoners keeping calm under Historical Fiction and Drama pressure.

At eighteen hours and forty-four minutes for one credit, *Blackout* builds relentless claustrophobic tension before ending on a cliffhanger that sends listeners racing directly into All Clear.

Narrator Soundcheck: Katherine Kellgren

Audie Award-winning legend Katherine Kellgren delivers a masterclass in regional dialect and emotional warmth across nineteen hours. Switching effortlessly between cheeky East End evacuees, stoic St. Paul's fire-watchers, posh Knightsbridge shoppers, and terrified American-trained historians, Kellgren makes wartime Britain sound startlingly alive.
