About this Audiobook

Darrow reemerges from the darkness to lead the Rising in an all-out, interplanetary war to break the chains of the Society forever.

Break the Chains: Tim Gerard Reynolds Brings the Original Red Rising Trilogy to a Triumphant Climax

*Morning Star* delivers the explosive, emotionally devastating finale that Darrow’s revolution fought two novels to achieve. Broken, tortured, and imprisoned in pitch-black isolation for a year following the Triumph's catastrophic betrayal, Darrow is rescued by the remnants of the Sons of Ares to find the solar system in flames. Forced to rally fractured factions across the asteroid belts, moons, and planetary armies, the Reaper of Mars must lead an all-out assault against the combined might of the Sovereign and the core Gold armadas.

Across nearly twenty-two breathless hours, Tim Gerard Reynolds pulls off a vocal miracle. He conveys the profound physical and psychological trauma Darrow carries while reigniting the mythic fire that makes him a messianic revolutionary icon. Every sacrifice feels painfully earned, and every allied reunion—especially with Sevro, Mustang, and Ragnar—is infused with heart-wrenching intimacy. Fans of sweeping, operatic rebellions like The Way of Kings and Words of Radiance will be enthralled by the scale and emotional payoff.

For the price of a single credit, *Morning Star* provides a masterclass in narrative closure. It avoids easy moral shortcuts, asking difficult questions about the heavy price of liberation while delivering some of the most cinematic fleet battles and hand-to-hand duels in sci-fi history.
