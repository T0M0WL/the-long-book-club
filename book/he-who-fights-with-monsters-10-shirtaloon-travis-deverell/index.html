About this Audiobook

Entering the realm of Diamond-rank diplomacy and cosmic entities, Jason must protect his nascent soul-world while matching wits with primordial forces of reality.

Walking with Cosmic Beings: Heath Miller Commands 25 Hours of Diamond-Tier Politics and Wit

In *He Who Fights with Monsters 10*, Shirtaloon elevates the narrative onto a truly cosmic stage. Having defeated the Messenger vanguard, Jason Asano is forced to navigate the high-stakes political maneuvers of Diamond-rank immortals and primordial Great Astral Beings. As cosmic personifications like Knowledge, Dominion, and the Reaper take an active interest in his burgeoning soul-realm, Jason must defend his autonomy and prevent his friends from being used as pawns in games that span millions of years.

Heath Miller proves master of this celestial expansion, voicing ancient, incomprehensible deities with uncanny vocal weight and chilling detachment. Yet Miller’s true brilliance lies in keeping Jason anchored in his down-to-earth humanity: no matter how terrifying the god before him, Jason still speaks with sarcastic Australian irreverence, demanding dignity and fairness for ordinary mortals. This tension between sublime cosmic dread and irreverent kitchen-table humor makes the twenty-five-hour runtime fly by with effortless charm. Listeners who loved the metaphysical scope of God Emperor of Dune will appreciate the grand scale.

Clocking in at over twenty-five hours on a single credit, this tenth installment is a masterclass in cosmic progression fantasy. Shirtaloon deepens the philosophical stakes of free will, divinity, and friendship, ensuring that the magic never feels hollow or disconnected from human emotion. It is an extraordinary auditory spectacle that continues to push the boundaries of what the LitRPG genre can achieve.
