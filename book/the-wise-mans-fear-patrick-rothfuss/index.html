The Wise Man's Fear

Author: Patrick Rothfuss | Narrator: Rupert Degas

Runtime: 42h 49m | Genre: Fantasy

About this Audiobook

Picking up the tale of Kvothe Kingkiller once again, we follow him into exile, into political intrigue, courtship, adventure, love and magic... and further along the path that has turned Kvothe, the mightiest magician of his age, a legend in his own time, into Kote, the unassuming pub landlord.

Kvothe’s Epic Forty-Two-Hour Odyssey of Aristocratic Courts, Ademre Martial Arts, and Fae Magic

Continuing the intimate, three-day biographical confession begun at the Waystone Inn, *The Wise Man's Fear* whisks listeners far beyond the University’s collegiate walls into the perilous, wider world. Forced into temporary leave from his magical studies following vicious rivalries with noble aristocrats, young Kvothe travels to the opulent court of the Maer in Severen, navigating lethal political court intrigues and thwarted assassination attempts. His adventures soon plunge him into the shadowy Eld forest to hunt bandits alongside hardened mercenaries, before drawing him into the timeless, dreamlike realm of the Fae to court the deadly temptress Felurian, confront the malevolent Cthaeh, and master the philosophical Ketan martial arts of the silent Ademre warriors.

On audio, the sweeping, episodic scope of Rothfuss’s forty-two-hour epic blossoms into a luxurious, transportive headphone journey. Rupert Degas’s vocal mastery turns Kvothe’s wandering odyssey into an acoustic feast—from the whispering rhythms of the Fae realm, the eerie encounter with the prophetic Cthaeh tree, and the poetic cadence of the Ketan martial dance to the crackling thunder of outdoor ambushes in torrential rain. Positioned as the monumental continuation of The Name of the Wind in our Epic Fantasy catalog, this performance transforms Kvothe’s growth into an addictive, heart-filling drama that makes every long commute feel like an ancient mythic quest.

Clocking in at nearly forty-three hours of peerless literary craftsmanship for a single credit, this release represents one of the most staggering returns on investment anywhere in audio. Rothfuss allows his story to breathe, prioritizing exquisite sensory texture, mythological secrets, and the tender joys of friendship over frantic plot mechanics. Investing a single monthly credit in this gentle colossus rewards patient listeners with an unforgettable, life-affirming journey that deepens Kvothe's tragic legend, proving why this captivating narrative holds an immortal place in the hearts of fantasy fans worldwide.

Narrator Soundcheck: Rupert Degas

Rupert Degas turns in another astounding vocal masterclass, seamlessly sustaining and expanding the enormous character roster across forty-two mammoth hours. Degas handles the complex nonverbal gestures and subtle tonal languages of the Ademre warriors with breathtaking precision, while his portrayal of the mercurial Maer Alveron radiates aristocratic power and private vulnerability. His pacing is impeccably measured, capturing the serene magic of the Fae forest and the frantic velocity of sword combat with equal authority. Pristine studio production and immaculate acoustic resonance make this forty-two-hour marathon an absolute pleasure to hear.
