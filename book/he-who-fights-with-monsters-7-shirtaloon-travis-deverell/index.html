About this Audiobook

Returning to the world of Pallimustus transformed into an astral sovereign, Jason arrives in the coastal city of Yaresh to reunite with his original adventuring team.

The Grand Reunion in Yaresh: Heath Miller Powers 18 Hours of Triumphant Magic and Joyful Banter

*He Who Fights with Monsters 7* marks the joyous, triumphant homecoming readers and listeners spent three full books waiting for: Jason Asano is finally back in the magical world of Pallimustus. Materializing in the storm-swept coastal city of Yaresh, Jason is vastly more powerful, wielding divine astral domains and Gold-rank aura control that terrifies local authorities. But the true soul of this volume is the long-awaited reunion with his original team—Humphrey, Clive, Sophie, and Neil—who have grown into formidable Silver-rank champions in their own right.

Heath Miller’s vocal chemistry with these beloved characters reignites with dazzling comedic spark. The reunion scenes are filled with heartfelt banter, tearful hugs, and hilarious updates on Clive's magical research and Neil's ongoing romantic tribulations. Miller effortlessly captures how much both Jason and his friends have matured, infusing their conversations with deep mutual trust and shared history. Fans of party-based fantasy adventures like Gentleman Bastard will revel in the cozy, electric camaraderie of every scene.

At eighteen and a half hours for a single credit, this seventh installment strikes the perfect balance between comfort-food character interaction and expansive cosmic stakes. Shirtaloon introduces new political landscapes, naval adventures, and mysterious transformation zones, breathing fresh life into the world. It is a thoroughly joyful and invigorating audiobook that rewards longtime listeners with supreme satisfaction.
