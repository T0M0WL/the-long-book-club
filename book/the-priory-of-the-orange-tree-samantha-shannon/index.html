About this Audiobook

A world divided. A queendom without an heir. An ancient enemy awakens.

Samantha Shannon's 25-Hour Queernormative High Fantasy Epic of Dragons, Queendoms, and Ancient Magic

Spanning a divided world on the brink of catastrophic war, Samantha Shannon’s *The Priory of the Orange Tree* is a feminist high-fantasy masterpiece that weaves together political intrigue, diverse mythologies, and rich romantic devotion. Across twenty-six majestic hours, the narrative follows four distinct perspectives across opposing continents: Queen Sabran the Ninth of Inys, who must conceive a daughter to protect her realm from an ancient draconic evil; Ead Duryan, an undercover mage from a secretive sisterhood sworn to protect the queen at court; Arteloth Beck, an exiled nobleman; and Tané, a dragon rider from the East who has trained her entire life to bond with a sacred water dragon, binding her destiny to the turbulent skies and ancestral traditions of her homeland.

In audio format, Shannon’s lush, mythic prose flows like an ancient court chronicle, giving the intricate theological histories, dragon lore, and courtly ceremonies the unhurried room they deserve. The central romantic connection between Queen Sabran and her devoted protector Ead develops with tender, slow-burning intimacy that feels profoundly earned. Prominently featured in our Epic Romantasy collection and sharing sweeping scope with foundational classics in The Bucket List, this unabridged production turns an eight-hundred-page epic into an addictive, accessible daily serial that envelops your imagination with the rich majesty of a living, breathing fantasy world.

Clocking in at over twenty-five hours, exchanging a single credit or token for *The Priory of the Orange Tree* yields extraordinary narrative and world-building value. Shannon creates a fully realized, standalone fantasy universe free of patriarchal norms, rich with ancient legends, court intrigue, and breathtaking aerial dragon battles. It is an ambitious, deeply satisfying listening experience that rewards every minute of your attention and leaves an indelible mark on your fantasy reading life, proving that high-stakes epic fantasy can be both profoundly emotional and politically visionary.

Narrator Soundcheck: Liyah Summers

Liyah Summers delivers a regal, versatile narration that anchors Samantha Shannon’s expansive multi-POV epic with exceptional grace and vocal distinction. Summers shifts seamlessly between the restrained, courtly dignity of the West and the spiritual reverence of the Eastern dragon clans, giving each protagonist a unique vocal identity and emotional weight. Her steady, measured pacing handles dense geopolitical exposition and intimate romantic dialogues with equal skill across twenty-six hours, supported by pristine, balanced audio production.
