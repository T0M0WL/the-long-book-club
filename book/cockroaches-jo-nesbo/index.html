About this Audiobook

After Oslo police ship troubled inspector Harry Hole to 1990s Bangkok to quietly handle the Norway ambassador's brothel stabbing, Harry uncovers high-level political embezzlement, sleazy expat circles, and a perilous relapse.

Over Ten Hours Sweating Through Bangkok Neon With Harry Hole

Following his bruised Australian debut in The Bat, Oslo homicide inspector Harry Hole is quietly dispatched to sticky late-1990s Bangkok after the Norwegian ambassador turns up stabbed in the back inside a seedy brothel. Ordered strictly to assist Thai authorities without sparking diplomatic scandal at home, Harry instead uncovers corrupt construction bribes, crooked expat circles, and ruthless street threats while wrestling ferocious temptation to start drinking again.

*Cockroaches* transports Jo Nesbø's signature Nordic Noir melancholy straight into oppressive tropical heat. Pairing gritty diplomatic Crime and relentless Detective sleuthing with high-wire Thriller tension, it serves up lean Police Procedural grit under unfamiliar neon glare.

At ten hours and forty-two minutes for one credit, *Cockroaches* completes Harry's international apprentice travels—setting him loose back in snowy Oslo for The Redbreast.
