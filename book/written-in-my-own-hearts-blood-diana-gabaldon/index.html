About this Audiobook

In 1778, believing Jamie dead at sea, Claire marries Lord John Grey for protection in occupied Philadelphia, only for Jamie to miraculously return as the Battle of Monmouth looms.

The Philadelphia Evacuation, the Battle of Monmouth, and the Ultimate Trial of Faith

*Written in My Own Heart’s Blood* is Diana Gabaldon’s gripping eighth entry in the *Outlander* saga, spanning forty-five hours. Picking up in 1778 Philadelphia as British forces prepare to evacuate the city, the story erupts with drama: Claire Randall Fraser, having received word that Jamie was drowned at sea, agrees to marry Jamie's dearest friend, Lord John Grey, to protect herself from arrest as an American rebel spy. When Jamie returns alive, shock, betrayal, and passion collide.

Simultaneously, the Revolutionary War reaches a fever pitch as Washington’s Continental Army marches toward the brutal Battle of Monmouth, where Jamie serves as a general and Claire acts as a frontline surgeon. Meanwhile, William Ransom uncovers the truth of his parentage, plunging into an existential crisis that upends his aristocratic loyalties. In the twentieth century, Brianna’s son Jem is kidnapped by a rogue time-traveler, forcing Roger to make a desperate leap back through the stones to save his family. Gabaldon weaves these intricate plotlines together with unmatched narrative virtuosity.

Narrator Davina Porter delivers a tour de force across the forty-five-hour runtime. Porter captures the awkwardness, fury, and tender reconciliations of the Claire-Jamie-Lord John triangle with exquisite emotional nuance. Her depictions of General Washington and frontline surgeries are electrifying. For listeners seeking peak Historical Romance and high-stakes military history on a single credit, *Written in My Own Heart’s Blood* is an absolute masterpiece.
