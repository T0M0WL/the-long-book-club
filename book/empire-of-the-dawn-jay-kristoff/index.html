About this Audiobook

The epic conclusion to the internationally bestselling Empire of the Vampire series. Gabriel de León has saved the Grail, but at what cost? Now, he must face the final darkness and determine the price of the dawn.

The Blood-Drenched Finale of Jay Kristoff's Monumental Dark Fantasy Vampire Epic

Jay Kristoff brings his monumental grimdark dark fantasy trilogy to a thunderous, blood-soaked crescendo in this relentless tale of apocalyptic devastation, sacrificial courage, and hard-won redemption. Gabriel de León, the last silversaint of the legendary holy order, stands battered and broken amidst a dying empire smothered under perpetual, suffocating nocturnal gloom. Armed with his fabled silversteel sword and relentlessly haunted by the ghosts of everyone he has ever loved and lost, Gabriel must escort the miraculous holy child Dior through legions of bloodthirsty vampires, horrific mutated abominations, and traitorous human zealots to restore sunlight to a shattered realm. Kristoff spares neither his characters nor his audience, delivering brutal combat sequences alongside profound emotional heartbreak and tragic moral dilemmas.

Experiencing Kristoff's visceral prose through audio elevates the saga's operatic intensity to heights the printed page cannot match. The clashing of silver against cold steel, the guttering hiss of holy fire, and Gabriel's cynical, world-weary confessions ring out with visceral theatrical grandeur and immediate physical threat. Featured prominently in our curated Best Audiobooks of 2025 and standing shoulder-to-shoulder with the greatest titans in our Sci-Fi & Fantasy Audiobooks collection, this dark fantasy epic proves that monumental runtimes allow listeners to fully inhabit the harrowing physical and emotional toll of a prolonged holy war across unforgiving landscapes.

Running nearly thirty-five hours of unyielding dark fantasy brilliance, this colossal listening experience represents the pinnacle of credit efficiency for fantasy aficionados. Kristoff refuses to cut corners, providing a comprehensive, unhurried conclusion that ties together every intricate narrative thread, battle engagement, and lore secret with devastating emotional finality. Exchanging a single monthly credit for this dark masterpiece grants you weeks of pulse-pounding, high-octane storytelling that leaves you completely breathless and thoroughly satisfied by the time the final dying ember fades into silence, setting a high watermark for modern dark fantasy.

Narrator Soundcheck: Damian Lynch, Shakira Shute

Damian Lynch and Shakira Shute provide a powerhouse dual-narration that captures the raw ferocity and gothic tragedy of Kristoff’s dark universe. Lynch’s gravelly, battle-hardened baritone embodies Gabriel de León with extraordinary emotional depth, moving from guttural combat roars to poignant, sorrowful confessions with effortless control. Shute counters brilliantly, bringing fierce resilience and emotional nuance to Dior’s critical perspective. Pacing across thirty-four hours is masterfully sustained, supported by immaculate production values that highlight the story's operatic scale and gritty battlefield clashes.
