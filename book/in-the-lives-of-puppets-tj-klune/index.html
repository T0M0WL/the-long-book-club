TJ Klune's 13-Hour Cozy Sci-Fi Journey of Found Family, Robots, and Hope

Spanning over thirteen heartwarming hours, TJ Klune’s *In the Lives of Puppets* is an enchanting, emotionally resonant standalone fantasy that reimagines Carlo Collodi’s classic *The Adventures of Pinocchio* for our modern technological age. Deep in a serene, hidden forest canopy, Victor Lawson lives as the only human in a world reclaimed by nature, raised alongside an eccentric mechanical family: Gio, a paternal android inventor with a mysterious past; Rambo, a delightfully anxious robotic vacuum cleaner desperate for affection; and Nurse Ratched, a gleefully sociopathic medical droid with a fondness for sharp implements. When Victor repairs an unfamiliar, deactivated android named HAP, their quiet sanctuary is shattered, forcing the ragtag ensemble on an epic, peril-laden journey across a dystopian wasteland to the gleaming City of Electric Dreams to save their family.

In audio format, Klune’s signature blend of tender sentimentality, philosophical warmth, and laugh-out-loud robotic banter finds its ultimate acoustic realization. The spoken delivery gives physical breath to Victor's profound loneliness and longing for connection, while the mechanical idiosyncrasies and comic timing of Rambo and Nurse Ratched provide pure auditory joy that turns every travel chapter into a delight. Sitting proudly alongside beloved cozy listening benchmarks in our library like The House in the Cerulean Sea and Somewhere Beyond the Sea, this production proves that speculative fiction does not require grim cynicism or relentless violence to deliver unforgettable, high-stakes emotional payoffs.

Investing a single credit on thirteen hours of *In the Lives of Puppets* offers an uplifting, cathartic escape from the noisy modern world. Klune delivers a masterclass in chosen family, empathy, forgiveness, and what it truly means to possess a living soul. It is a gentle, comforting audio triumph that will make you laugh aloud through tears, leaving your headphones humming with genuine emotional warmth long after the final chapter closes.

Narrator Soundcheck: Daniel Henning

Longtime TJ Klune collaborator Daniel Henning delivers a virtuoso comedic and heartfelt performance that brings this robotic family to luminous life. Henning imbues Rambo the vacuum with hilarious, breathless nervous energy, while giving Nurse Ratched a delightfully crisp, deadpan sociopathic edge that steals every scene. His tender, grounded voicing of Victor provides the emotional anchor for the entire thirteen-hour journey, backed by crystal-clear studio acoustics and pristine vocal balance.
