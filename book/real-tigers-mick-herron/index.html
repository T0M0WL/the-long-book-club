About this Audiobook

When Catherine Standish is snatched off the street by a rogue syndicate, Jackson Lamb's slow horses must break into Regent’s Park headquarters to steal classified files and save her.

A Lethal Inside-Job Heist and Uncompromising Betrayal Inside Regent's Park

Across nearly eleven gripping, relentless hours, *Real Tigers* ratchets up the emotional stakes and kinetic action of Mick Herron’s Slough House series to explosive heights. When Catherine Standish, the recovering alcoholic office manager and emotional backbone of Slough House, fails to show up for work after being abducted on the London streets, Jackson Lamb instantly recognizes that the rules of bureaucratic engagement have been obliterated. Catherine's captor is Sean Donovan, a former military operative driven by personal vengeance and backed by private security mercenaries who demand an impossible ransom: the slow horses must infiltrate MI5 headquarters at Regent’s Park and steal a heavily guarded, top-secret kompromat dossier.

Herron ingeniously structures *Real Tigers* as an audacious espionage heist, sending River Cartwright into the lion's den of Regent’s Park using brazen bluffing, second-hand tradecraft, and chaotic diversion tactics. But as Lamb orchestrates countermeasures from the greasy confines of Farringdon, a broader conspiracy emerges involving Home Office machinations, private military contractors, and high-level political treachery orchestrated by ambitious mandarins seeking to privatize Britain's intelligence apparatus. The resulting urban warfare across a desolate government storage facility pits Lamb’s desperate misfits against lethal 'Dogs' and seasoned mercenaries in a breathless, bullet-riddled showdown.

Beyond the relentless suspense, *Real Tigers* showcases Herron’s unmatched talent for character development. The affection Lamb harbors for Standish—buried beneath layers of noxious smoke, cheap takeout, and caustic sarcasm—gives the narrative an unexpected emotional resonance. Voiced with razor-sharp authority by Sean Barrett, the audio production highlights every sting of dark comedy and every beat of pulse-pounding tension. It stands alongside high-octane modern spy thrillers like I Am Pilgrim while maintaining Herron’s uniquely cynical British wit.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sean Barrett

Sean Barrett’s narration in *Real Tigers* is a tour de force of suspense and pitch-perfect comedic inflection. His portrayal of Jackson Lamb reaches new heights of gruff, world-weary brilliance, especially during interrogations where Lamb’s deceptive sluggishness masks lethal tactical thinking. Barrett delivers River Cartwright’s escalating panic inside the Regent's Park vault with authentic adrenaline, while giving Catherine Standish an admirable dignity in captivity. The combat sequences are read with crisp, punchy urgency that keeps listeners glued to their earphones.
