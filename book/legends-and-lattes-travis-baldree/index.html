About this Audiobook

Tired of bloodshed and bounty hunting, a battle-hardened orc barbarian hangs up her broadsword to open the very first coffee shop in a fantasy city.

Travis Baldree Narrates His Own Foundational Masterpiece of High Fantasy, Low Stakes, and Coffee

After twenty bloody years spent hacking through battlefields, orc barbarian Viv decides she has swung her greatsword for the final time. Guided by the whispered legend of a mythical luck-stone called the Scalvert's Stone, Viv travels to the bustling, cobblestone city of Thune to chase an impossible dream: opening the first-ever espresso shop in a realm that has never heard of coffee. Stripping away the apocalyptic dread and cynical bloodshed of standard fantasy quests, Travis Baldree’s modern classic focuses on the tender, tactile joys of laying hearth floorboards, installing brass steam valves, hiring a creative succubus partner named Tandri, and baking fragrant thimbleberry cinnamon rolls that win over skeptical gnomes, local carpenters, and ruthless mob enforcers alike.

Hearing the novel narrated by its own author—celebrated throughout the audiobook industry as one of its most versatile voice artists—elevates *Legends & Lattes* into an auditory masterwork of cozy escapism. Baldree knows every emotional beat, every ironic pause, and every sizzling steam-wand release intimately, infusing each scene with genuine affection and gentle humor. Showcased as the defining cornerstone of our Cozy Fantasy Collection alongside culinary companions like Can't Spell Treason Without Tea and The Spellshop, this recording turns coffee-making into an auditory sanctuary that warms your spirit from the inside out.

Clocking in at eight and a half hours of pure comfort listening, this single-credit release represents one of the most repeatedly enjoyed audiobooks in digital audio. Baldree proves that high-stakes violence is never required to create an unputdownable story, substituting battlefield carnage with found-family loyalty, artisanal baking, and tender, slow-burn romantic devotion. Spending a monthly credit on this beloved cozy fantasy pioneer delivers an unforgettable, heart-filling listening experience that will leave you smiling warmly, craving a fresh cinnamon roll and hot latte, and wishing you could spend your afternoons resting in a corner booth at Viv’s cafe.

Narrator Soundcheck: Travis Baldree

Author and master voice talent Travis Baldree delivers a flawless, award-winning performance that represents the gold standard of audiobook narration. Baldree imbues Viv with a deep, weary baritone that softens beautifully in domestic moments, while Tandri’s calm, velvet-toned intelligence and Cal’s gravelly, gruff goblin speech are rendered with incredible individuality and heart.
