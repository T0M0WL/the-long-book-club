About this Audiobook

The exclusive biography of Steve Jobs. Based on more than forty interviews with Jobs conducted over two years.

Walter Isaacson's 25-Hour Unfiltered Portrait of Silicon Valley Genius, Cruelty, and Revolutionary Innovation

Based on more than forty candid, unrestricted interviews with the subject himself, Walter Isaacson’s *Steve Jobs* is the definitive, 25-hour chronicle of the mercurial visionary who revolutionized personal computing, animated movies, music, phones, tablet computing, and digital publishing. Driven by a demonic passion for perfection and an obsessive demand for complete control, Jobs built Apple into the most valuable corporation on earth from his parents' California garage. Yet Isaacson refuses to sanitize his subject: the narrative lays bare Jobs’s notorious 'reality distortion field', his explosive tantrums, personal betrayals, and astonishing cruelty toward colleagues and loved ones, presenting a mesmerizing character study of creative genius intertwined with deeply flawed humanity, providing an unsparing, three-dimensional look at the dark costs of visionary ambition.

Experiencing Isaacson’s biography in audio format brings a sharp, visceral immediacy to Silicon Valley’s greatest boardroom coups and design breakthroughs. Rather than skimming technical product specifications, the audio format allows you to sit directly in the high-stakes design rooms where the Mac, iPod, and iPhone were forged through sheer willpower and relentless argument. Celebrated in our Long Life Stories collection alongside modern business sagas like Titan, this unabridged recording turns product launches and corporate warfare into breathless, addictive drama that enlivens every listening hour, turning tech history into a compelling, high-stakes psychological battlefield between competing design philosophies.

Clocking in at twenty-five and a half hours, spending a single credit on *Steve Jobs* delivers exceptional intellectual and narrative return. It serves as both an indispensable guide to design thinking and innovation, and a sobering cautionary tale about the high human cost of relentless perfectionism. It is a modern non-fiction classic that will permanently change how you interact with the digital devices in your pocket and on your desk, forcing you to reflect on what it truly takes to build iconic products that change global culture.

Narrator Soundcheck: Dylan Baker

Dylan Baker delivers an exceptional, razor-sharp performance that perfectly mirrors the intense energy, biting wit, and mercurial temperament of Steve Jobs. Baker narrates Isaacson’s candid text with crisp clarity and pitch-perfect comedic timing, capturing Jobs’s caustic boardroom dismissals and passionate design lectures without slipping into caricature. His pacing remains consistently engaging across twenty-five hours, supported by clean, modern studio production that ensures effortless listening comprehension and engaging dramatic tension across every single tech breakthrough.
