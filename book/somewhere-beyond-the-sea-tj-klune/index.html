About this Audiobook

Arthur Parnassus and Linus Baker defend their magical island sanctuary against DICOMY's inquisitions in this heartwarming, fifteen-hour sequel.

Found Family, Bureaucratic Reckoning, and Heartfelt Magic on Marsyas Island

Spanning nearly sixteen profoundly moving, joyous hours, *Somewhere Beyond the Sea* is TJ Klune’s triumphant sequel to the beloved cozy fantasy phenomenon The House in the Cerulean Sea. Returning listeners to the idyllic sanctuary of the Marsyas Island Orphanage, the narrative shifts its emotional center from Linus Baker to headmaster Arthur Parnassus himself. Arthur is living the peaceful life he always dreamed of—surrounded by his beloved partner Linus and their six extraordinary, magical charges, including the delightfully dramatic antichrist Lucy, garden-tending wyvern Theodore, shy selkie Sal, and fierce forest sprite Phee. But their sun-drenched coastal peace is abruptly shattered when the Department in Charge of Magical Youths (DICOMY) summons Arthur to the capital for a hostile public inquiry into his own traumatic childhood at the hands of the system.

Faced with a new, ruthless bureaucratic regime determined to categorize magical children as biological threats and strip them of legal protections, Arthur and Linus must step out of seclusion and fight for the civil rights of all magical beings. Klune masterfully balances tender domestic comedy—the daily chaos of burnt toast, children's bedtime negotiations, and quiet romantic reassurance—with a poignant, impassioned defense of human dignity and belonging. The narrative delves deeply into Arthur's hidden scars, examining how cycles of institutional trauma can be dismantled through radical empathy, stubborn courage, and uncompromising community care.

In audio, *Somewhere Beyond the Sea* achieves absolute perfection thanks to Daniel Henning’s virtuoso narration. Henning effortlessly reprises his beloved ensemble of voices, from Lucy’s gravelly, theatrical pronouncements of doom to Arthur’s gentle, stoic vulnerability and Linus’s flustered devotion. For listeners seeking a life-affirming, deeply comforting journey that proves kindness is the greatest magic of all, this sixteen-hour production is a masterpiece of modern Cozy Fantasy that richly justifies your monthly credit.

Narrator Soundcheck: Daniel Henning

Daniel Henning delivers an acoustic masterclass in Somewhere Beyond the Sea, demonstrating why his vocal performance on Marsyas Island is widely considered a landmark in cozy audio fiction. Henning voices over a dozen distinct personalities with immaculate consistency: Lucy’s comical, raspy baritone continues to steal every scene, while Arthur Parnassus’s gentle cadence carries palpable emotional gravity. Henning’s comedic timing and expressive warmth turn nearly sixteen hours into an auditory embrace that listeners will want to revisit again and again.
