About this Audiobook

Set during the snowy aftermath of the Aiel War, young Accepted Moiraine Damodred and the uncrowned king Lan Mandragoran begin their fateful quest.

An Intimate, Atmospheric Prequel of Prophetic Births, Tower Politics, and the Bond That Saved the World

Set nearly two decades before the events of *The Eye of the World*, *New Spring* provides an enchanting and essential prequel to Robert Jordan’s monumental saga. During the freezing winter of the Aiel War outside the walls of Tar Valon, young Aes Sedai Accepted Moiraine Damodred and Siuan Sanche witness the elderly Amyrlin Seat receive a sudden, terrifying Foretelling: the Dragon has been reborn on the snowy slopes of Dragonmount. Sworn to absolute secrecy, Moiraine embarks on a perilous quest across the Borderlands to locate the newborn infant before the shadowy assassins of the Black Ajah can extinguish his life. Along the frozen road, her path crosses with al'Lan Mandragoran, the uncrowned king of shattered Malkier.

Listening to *New Spring* on audio is a wonderful, nostalgic treat that allows listeners to experience the White Tower and the Borderlands through fresh, youthful eyes. Kate Reading delivers a radiant performance as the spirited, younger Moiraine, highlighting her intellectual sharpness and vulnerability before decades of heavy duty hardened her demeanor. Michael Kramer embodies the stoic, tragic fatalism of Lan Mandragoran with quiet dignity, making the forging of their legendary Warder bond deeply moving. Featured prominently in our Epic Fantasy hub, this production is an absolute delight for both newcomers and seasoned series veterans.

Clocking in at twelve hours and thirty-seven minutes, this focused novel offers delightful credit value for anyone who loves Jordan’s rich universe. Jordan focuses on the intimate details of Aes Sedai testing, the brutal mechanics of borderland swordplay, and the slow, earned mutual respect between two iconic heroes. A single credit unlocks over twelve hours of sublime narration from Kramer and Reading, making this prequel a beloved companion piece to the greatest fantasy marathon ever written.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer, Kate Reading

Michael Kramer and Kate Reading bring wonderful warmth, freshness, and intimacy to New Spring.
