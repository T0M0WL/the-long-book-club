About this Audiobook

When an old Cold War spook turns up dead on an Oxfordshire bus, Jackson Lamb and his outcast MI5 rejects untangle a dormant Soviet sleeper agent conspiracy and an oligarch's skyscraper plot.

Dormant Cold War Sleepers and Acid-Tongued Tradecraft in Mick Herron's Masterpiece

Spanning over eleven tense, meticulously plotted hours, *Dead Lions* elevates Mick Herron’s Slough House saga from an audacious debut into one of the most brilliant and enduring British spy thriller series ever penned. Following the chaotic fallout of their first outing, the discarded intelligence officers of Slough House remain exiled to their grim Farringdon purgatory, condemned to administrative drudgery by Regent’s Park mandarins. But the sudden, seemingly mundane death of Dickie Bow—an aging Cold War 'milkman' who once ran low-level surveillance in Berlin and is discovered slumped over on an Oxfordshire bus—ignites Jackson Lamb’s razor-sharp paranoia. Refusing to accept heart failure as the cause of death when Dickie's hidden mobile phone reveals a single unsent text message reading 'cicadas', Lamb dispatches his motley crew into the shadows.

Meanwhile, River Cartwright and Min Harper are loaned out to protect a high-profile Russian oligarch visiting London, a tedious security assignment that rapidly intertwines with Lamb’s investigation into forgotten KGB sleeper networks. Herron masterfully weaves dual plot threads into a breathtaking, collision-course climax involving Canary Wharf, covert assassinations, and long-buried Soviet tradecraft. What makes *Dead Lions* extraordinary is its effortless balance of laugh-out-loud satirical office politics and genuine mortal danger. Jackson Lamb reigns supreme as the most foul-tempered, flatulent, yet terrifyingly competent spymaster in modern literature, weaponizing weaponized filth and tactical genius to shield his damaged subordinates from bureaucratic slaughter.

In audio, the narrative gains an electric, intimate momentum that honors classic mole-hunts like John le Carré’s Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy while establishing its own cynical, contemporary identity. Listening to Sean Barrett bring this intricate labyrinth of deceit to life is a masterclass in modern storytelling. For anyone seeking the absolute pinnacle of intelligent British crime fiction, *Dead Lions* is an essential listen that proves Herron's CWA Gold Dagger victory was thoroughly deserved.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sean Barrett

Sean Barrett’s performance on *Dead Lions* is nothing short of phenomenal. His Jackson Lamb is raspy, phlegmatic, and utterly dripping with contempt, capturing the character’s calculated slovenliness alongside his chilling flashes of lethal Cold War tradecraft. Barrett distinguishes each slow horse with nuanced vocal textures: River’s eager insecurity, Catherine’s quiet steel, and Min Harper’s hapless vulnerability. His pacing during the high-stakes Canary Wharf sequence ratchets up tension smoothly, making all eleven hours fly past with addictive ease.
