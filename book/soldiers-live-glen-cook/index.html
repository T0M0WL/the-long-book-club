About this Audiobook

Returning to the pen as one final war against Kina's apocalyptic cult engulfs the glittering plain, an aging Croaker guides scarred survivors, old Taken ghosts, and a desperate younger generation toward their ultimate reckoning.

Nearly Twenty Sweeping Hours Closing Croaker's Original Nine-Book Mercenary Chronicle

Returning at last to the annalist's inkpot for the monumental close of the classic run begun in The Black Company, an aging, half-crippled Croaker faces one ultimate campaign as the awakened Year of the Skulls looms over Hsien and the Glittering Plain. Aided by an impatient younger generation of Company recruits, battered veterans Goblin, One-Eye, and Sleepy, and fragile alliances forged across decades since She Is the Darkness, Croaker writes through one final apocalyptic reckoning.

*Soldiers Live* stands tall among fantasy finales for refusing tidy sentimentality while delivering heartbreaking Grimdark and Military Fantasy catharsis. Blending multi-front Sword and Sorcery battles with stark Dark Fantasy loss and quiet Low Fantasy humanity proves why soldiers write names down in books.

Spanning nineteen hours and thirty-two minutes for a single Audible credit, *Soldiers Live* earns its title line for line—and bridges straight into Cook's brand-new continuation, Lies Weeping.

Hearing original Book 1 voice Marc Vietor return to Croaker for nearly twenty crowning hours hits with immediate emotional gravity. Older, rougher, and deeply haunted, Vietor's weathered baritone carries battle exhaustion, dark humor, and bittersweet final entries with unforgettable dignity.
