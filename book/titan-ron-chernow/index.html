About this Audiobook

The Life of John D. Rockefeller, Sr. A detailed portrait of the ruthless industrialist and generous philanthropist.

Ron Chernow's 35-Hour Monumental Study of John D. Rockefeller and the Birth of American Monopoly Capitalism

At thirty-five panoramic hours, Ron Chernow’s *Titan* is widely recognized as the greatest business biography ever written. Charting the astonishing life of John D. Rockefeller, Sr.—the world’s first billionaire and architect of Standard Oil—Chernow explores the profound contradictions of a man who was simultaneously the most ruthless monopolist in Gilded Age capitalism and the most generous private philanthropist in global history. Beginning with his impoverished youth under a flamboyant bigamist con-man father, the narrative traces how Rockefeller’s pious Baptist discipline, ice-cold composure, and logistical genius allowed him to secretly consolidate the chaotic American oil industry, subduing railroads, refineries, and rivals into an invincible industrial empire that dictated fuel prices, manipulated transportation logistics, and established the blueprint for modern global corporate power.

In audio format, Chernow’s dense corporate history transforms into an absorbing, high-stakes financial thriller. What might seem like dry nineteenth-century railroad rebates and pipeline construction on the printed page becomes electrifying corporate intrigue when spoken aloud. Featured prominently in our Long Life Stories collection and providing an essential historical companion to The Power Broker, this unabridged recording allows the listener to fully absorb the astonishing scale of Rockefeller’s empire and the momentous legal crusades of Ida Tarbell and the Supreme Court to dismantle it, creating a panoramic portrait of an era where private wealth rivaled the power of national governments.

For sheer entertainment and educational value, exchanging a single credit or token for thirty-five hours of Chernow’s unmatched historical prose is an extraordinary investment. You receive an exhaustive, fascinating anatomy of wealth creation, antitrust law, and modern philanthropy that illuminates the roots of modern global corporate power. It is an unputdownable listening achievement that will keep you captivated through every single chapter, offering unmatched insights into business strategy, personal discipline, and the moral complexities of extreme wealth and American industrial capitalism.

Narrator Soundcheck: Grover Gardner

Grover Gardner delivers a masterclass in non-fiction narration, lending his signature wry warmth, conversational intimacy, and effortless pacing to Chernow’s expansive corporate biography. Gardner’s measured, dry delivery is the perfect acoustic foil for Rockefeller’s unflappable temperament and Standard Oil’s calculating business maneuvers. He handles nineteenth-century economic minutiae and courtroom battles with total narrative command across thirty-five hours, backed by crystal-clear, balanced audio production and immaculate acoustic fidelity that keeps listener fatigue completely at bay.
