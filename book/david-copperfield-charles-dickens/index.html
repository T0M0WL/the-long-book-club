About this Audiobook

David Copperfield is the story of a young man's adventures on his journey from an unhappy and impoverished childhood to the discovery of his vocation as a successful novelist.

Charles Dickens’s Most Beloved Autobiographical Masterpiece of Resilience and eccentric London in Audio

Widely celebrated by Charles Dickens as his own 'favorite child,' *David Copperfield* stands as the quintessential Victorian coming-of-age odyssey, drawing heavily from the author's own impoverished boyhood. Born fatherless in Suffolk, young David endures the cruel tyranny of his stepfather Mr. Murdstone and the squalor of a London bottling factory before escaping across the countryside to the eccentric sanctuary of his great-aunt Betsey Trotwood. As David matures into a writer, he navigates the complex social hierarchies of Victorian England, falling for the fragile Dora Spenlow and uncovering the oily deceit of the treacherous clerk Uriah Heep. Supported by his eccentric friend Mr. Dick and the devoted Agnes Wickfield, David learns that true nobility is forged through perseverance, while witnessing the tragic ruin of Little Em'ly and the pompous financial delusions of the Micawber household.

In its audio incarnation, Dickens’s sprawling, seventy-character canvas becomes an astonishingly vibrant, multi-voiced theatrical performance. Narrator Richard Armitage brings a warm, contemporary vitality to Dickensian prose that strips away any dusty academic stiffness, effortlessly distinguishing the booming, optimistic poverty of Mr. Micawber from Uriah Heep’s clammy, serpent-like sycophancy and the tender loyalty of Peggotty. Highlighted alongside Bleak House and Great Expectations in our Classics library, this thirty-six-hour recording turns an entire Victorian lifespan into an intimate, laugh-out-loud listening marathon. Armitage's heartfelt portrayal of David's tender grief over his mother's death and his quiet maturation into an accomplished author brings exquisite emotional balance to this sprawling Victorian masterpiece.
