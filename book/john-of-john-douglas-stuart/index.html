About this Audiobook

Shortlisted for the 2026 Booker Prize, Douglas Stuart's deeply moving novel follows a young artist returning home to a windswept island in the Outer Hebrides to confront his proud, taciturn crofter father and the secrets of his mother's absence.

Over Fifteen Heart-Wrenching Hours on a Windswept Island in the Outer Hebrides

Shortlisted for the 2026 Booker Prize and a Goodreads Choice Fiction finalist, Douglas Stuart’s *John of John* trades the gritty Glasgow tenements of *Shuggie Bain* for the salt-lashed beauty and claustrophobic silence of the Outer Hebrides. Returning home from art school after years away, young Calum steps back into the cramped croft of his proud, emotionally armored father—forcing two stubborn men to reckon with grief, masculinity, longing, and the haunting absence of the woman who once held their world together.

Stuart’s writing carries a raw, lyrical cadence that hits twice as hard when spoken aloud. Every crash of Atlantic surf, terse kitchen exchange, and quiet flash of rural tenderness lands with devastating precision, making *John of John* an unforgettable pillar of our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 alongside fellow Booker finalist The Disappearers and Paul Murray’s The Bee Sting.

At fifteen hours and thirty minutes for a single Audible credit, *John of John* is intimate, aching Literary Fiction performed at the highest level.

Narrator Soundcheck: Lorne MacFadyen

Scottish actor Lorne MacFadyen delivers a deeply moving fifteen-and-a-half-hour performance steeped in authentic Hebridean rhythm and quiet emotional restraint. He handles the simmering silences between father and son with heartbreaking sensitivity—letting grief, fierce pride, and fragile tenderness resonate without ever forcing a single note.
