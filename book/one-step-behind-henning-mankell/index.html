About this Audiobook

When three youths dressed in eighteenth-century costume are executed on Midsummer Eve and Wallander's colleague Svedberg is shot dead inside his own flat, Ystad's grieving squad chases a killer who stays one step ahead.

Nearly Sixteen Hours Chasing an Unseen Killer Who Strikes Ystad's Own Ranks

When three young friends rehearsing an eighteenth-century picnic inside a remote Scania nature reserve vanish—only to surface months later brutally shot in the head while dressed in period silk—Wallander's squad realizes too late that their own colleague Svedberg had already been secretly probing the disappearances just before he is found murdered in his flat. With exhaustion and untreated diabetes taking a frightening toll after The Fifth Woman, Kurt hunts a cunning phantom always one stride ahead.

*One Step Behind* ranks among the most emotionally devastating installments in Nordic Noir. Turning routine Police Procedural comradeship inside out exposes how little detectives truly know about each other, heightening visceral Crime dread alongside razor-tight Detective and Mystery payoffs.

Spanning fifteen hours and fifty-nine minutes in Blackstone's unabridged UK/US release, Book 7 builds unstoppable urgency straight into the digital paranoia of Firewall.
