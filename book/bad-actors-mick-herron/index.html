About this Audiobook

When a senior government advisor goes missing and a Svengali political strategist manipulates MI5, Jackson Lamb enters the halls of Downing Street power.

Downing Street Machinations, Vanished Advisors, and Lamb's Revenge

Spanning ten and a half dazzling, razor-sharp hours, *Bad Actors* finds Mick Herron firing on every cylinder, dissecting modern British politics with ruthless comedic flair and immaculate plotting. Set against the backdrop of 10 Downing Street during a period of acute national crisis, the plot ignites when Dr. Sophie de Beer, a brilliant think-tank researcher and personal advisor to the Prime Minister, mysteriously disappears from London. Enter Anthony Sparrow: the Prime Minister’s grotesque, megalomaniacal chief political advisor—a Svengali figure obsessed with algorithmic manipulation, psychological nudging, and centralizing absolute power within the cabinet office.

Determined to control the narrative and manufacture an international incident with Russia, Sparrow enlists Claude Whelan and private intelligence contractors to manipulate the investigation. But when Slough House is dragged into the fray, Sparrow quickly realizes he has severely underestimated Jackson Lamb. While Shirley Dander and River Cartwright pursue leads through the grimy underbelly of London, Lamb plays a subterranean chess match against Downing Street's spin doctors, exposing the rot, cowardice, and vanity festering at the very pinnacle of government.

What makes *Bad Actors* such a joyous listen is Herron’s wicked dialogue and Barrett’s unforgettable characterizations. The clashes between Lamb’s filthy, no-nonsense Cold War realism and Sparrow’s vapid modern managerial jargon are comedy of the highest order. Yet beneath the laughs lies a genuinely gripping mystery involving Russian sleeper agents, high-level defection, and deadly statecraft. *Bad Actors* proves once again that Mick Herron and Sean Barrett are the undisputed kings of the contemporary British espionage thriller.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sean Barrett

Sean Barrett’s narration of *Bad Actors* is an extraordinary exhibition of vocal mimicry and satirical bite. His portrayal of Anthony Sparrow drips with smarmy entitlement, while his Jackson Lamb remains the undisputed champion of rumpled, flatulent counter-espionage genius. Barrett nails Shirley Dander’s twitchy, cocaine-craving fury, Catherine Standish’s weary patience, and River Cartwright’s bruised determination. Every scene crackles with energy, making this ten-and-a-half-hour audio production an absolute delight from start to finish.
