Bryce Quinlan and Hunt Athalar are trying to get back to normal—they may have saved Crescent City, but with so much upheaval in their lives lately, they mostly want a chance to relax. Slow down. Figure out what the future holds.

Sarah J. Maas's 27-Hour Crescent City Sequel of Revolutionary Secrets, Angelic Rebellion, and Scorching Romance

Picking up in the turbulent wake of the Spring Summit attacks, Sarah J. Maas’s *House of Sky and Breath* plunges listeners into twenty-seven electrifying hours of political rebellion, covert operations, and scorching romantic passion. Having survived the demonic horrors of the previous year, Bryce Quinlan and Hunt Athalar want nothing more than a quiet, peaceful life together to process their trauma and explore their blossoming romantic bond. But when they are pulled into an underground rebel movement seeking to overthrow the tyrannical Asteri, they must undertake high-stakes covert heists across the continent, unraveling long-buried secrets about the true origin of their world and the terrifying source of the rulers' power, risking everything they hold dear in a desperate bid to free Lunathion from centuries of demonic oppression.

In audio format, Maas’s multi-POV narrative moves with the pulse-pounding tempo of an espionage heist thriller. The spoken performance brings a visceral immediacy to undercover operative missions, treacherous fae courts, and the expanding ensemble cast—including Bryce's fae brother Ruhn Danaan and the enigmatic agent Daybright. Positioned as a core pillar of our Epic Romantasy collection, this unabridged audio edition provides the deliberate pacing necessary for intricate political conspiracies and passionate romantic encounters to unfold with maximum narrative impact, transforming quiet espionage meetings into nail-biting suspense that makes every hour fly by.
