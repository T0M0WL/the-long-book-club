About this Audiobook

The definitive, intimate narrative of the last Russian Tsar and Empress, whose son's tragic haemophilia opened the door to Rasputin and the Bolshevik Revolution.

The Heartbreaking, Definitive 27-Hour Chronicle of the Romanov Dynasty’s Tragic Fall

Spanning twenty-seven masterfully crafted hours, *Nicholas and Alexandra* by Pulitzer Prize-winning historian Robert K. Massie is widely celebrated as one of the finest historical biographies ever written. Massie presents a deeply human, empathetic portrait of the last Tsar of All the Russias, Nicholas II, and his German-born Empress, Alexandra, Queen Victoria’s granddaughter. Central to Massie’s narrative is a profound medical revelation: the devastating haemophilia of their only son and heir, Tsarevich Alexis. The royal couple’s desperate search for a cure led them into the spiritual thrall of Grigori Rasputin, an illiterate Siberian holy man whose influence at court fatally compromised the imperial prestige in the eyes of Russian society.

Massie excels at contrasting the opulent, jewel-encrusted isolation of Tsarskoye Selo against the gathering storms of political revolution, the slaughter of the Eastern Front in World War I, and the catastrophic collapse of the three-hundred-year-old Romanov dynasty. The audio format breathes extraordinary emotional life into Nicholas and Alexandra’s private correspondence, capturing an intimate, devoted marriage caught between historical inevitability and personal tragedy. From the glittering balls of St. Petersburg to the grim basement in Ekaterinburg, Massie tracks each step of the family's journey with cinematic clarity and heart-stopping suspense.

For history lovers looking for rich, novelistic non-fiction that justifies every minute of listening time, *Nicholas and Alexandra* is an essential listen. Orlando Wells delivers a poised, deeply moving British narration that balances aristocratic restraint with profound pathos. Paired with Massie’s acclaimed imperial portraits Peter the Great and Catherine the Great, it stands as an enduring masterpiece of Biography that will leave listeners deeply moved by the fateful destiny of Russia's last imperial family.

Narrator Soundcheck: Orlando Wells

Orlando Wells delivers an exemplary biographical performance marked by aristocratic poise, warmth, and meticulous vocal clarity. Wells conveys the private vulnerability of Nicholas and the frantic motherly anguish of Alexandra without ever tipping into melodrama, while his rendering of court ministers, military generals, and Rasputin adds vivid color to twenty-seven hours of imperial history. His rhythmic pacing makes this monumental scholarly work feel as gripping and emotionally charged as the greatest Russian novels.
