About this Audiobook

While a heavily guarded comatose John Doe clings to life inside an Oslo hospital after Phantom, serving officers across Norway are lured out and savagely murdered on anniversaries of unsolved cold cases they worked.

Over Seventeen Tense Hours Hunting a Cop-Killer Amid Phantom's Aftermath

Picking up straight out of the unbearable suspense closing Phantom, Oslo faces an unprecedented crisis: police officers are being lured to remote scenes of notorious unsolved cases on crime anniversaries and brutally murdered one by one. With crooked brass like Mikael Bellman obsessing over PR spin while a comatose John Doe lies heavily guarded behind anonymous hospital doors, Beate Lønn, Katrine Bratt, and Ståle Aune rally desperately to stop an executioner who knows police procedure from inside.

*Police* brilliantly shakes up Nordic Noir by spotlighting Harry's loyal team under siege. Every razor-tight Police Procedural twist escalates visceral Crime and Detective paranoia into peak Thriller dread.

At seventeen hours and three minutes for one Audible credit, Book 10 delivers masterclass suspense—ushering Harry into fleeting peace before The Thirst.
