The Wisdom of Crowds

Author: Joe Abercrombie | Narrator: Steven Pacey

Runtime: 23h 48m | Genre: Grimdark Fantasy, Epic Fantasy, Dark Fantasy, Industrial Fantasy, Military Fantasy, Court Intrigue

About this Audiobook

The Great Change sweeps across Adua in an apocalyptic French Revolution-style reign of terror as Judge and the Burners execute the aristocracy.

Revolutionary Terror, Mob Rule, and the Scorched-Earth Finale of the Union

Spanning nearly twenty-four ferocious, heartbreaking hours, *The Wisdom of Crowds* brings Joe Abercrombie’s *Age of Madness* trilogy to a devastating, unforgettable crescendo. Picking up in the smoking wreckage of The Trouble with Peace, the capital of Adua is engulfed by the 'Great Change'—a violent populist uprising reminiscent of the French Revolution’s Reign of Terror. The shadowy Burners and rabid Breakers have seized control, establishing kangaroo courts presided over by the deranged Judge, where aristocrats, bankers, and suspected counter-revolutionaries are systematically tried, mocked, and pitched from high towers to the blood-slicked cobblestones below.

In this terrifying new order of mob rule, every character faces their ultimate reckoning. Savine dan Glokta must shed her pride and deploy her sharpest cunning to protect her newborn twins from the slaughterhouse. Leo dan Brock, maimed by battle and consumed by bitter authoritarian zeal, transforms into a ruthless dictator who purges his own allies. Imprisoned in his own palace, King Orso endures humiliation with heartbreaking dignity and razor-sharp gallows humor. Meanwhile, in the frozen North, Rikke executes a daring, cold-blooded gambit that permanently alters the fate of the clans. Abercrombie pulls no punches, dissecting how noble ideals curdle into tyrannical fanaticism and showing that the machinery of power always demands sacrifice.

Narrator Steven Pacey delivers what may well be the crowning performance of his legendary career. His portrayal of the insane Judge is chillingly unhinged, his King Orso radiates quiet tragedy, and his depiction of urban anarchy is breathtakingly visceral. For listeners seeking maximum emotional impact, brilliant thematic complexity, and peerless voice acting on a single credit, *The Wisdom of Crowds* is an absolute triumph of Epic Fantasy storytelling.

Narrator Soundcheck: Steven Pacey

Steven Pacey’s performance on The Wisdom of Crowds is nothing short of legendary. Pacey channels the frantic, blood-soaked hysteria of revolutionary Adua with spine-tingling realism, giving the radical Judge a shrill, manic cadence that will haunt your nightmares. Yet he balances this horror with exquisite tenderness during King Orso’s quietest moments, ensuring the emotional beats land with devastating power. Flawlessly produced and paced over nearly twenty-four hours, this audio production is an immortal fantasy milestone.
