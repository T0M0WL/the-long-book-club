Andrew Rowe Unravels Time Travel and Forbidden Spellcraft in a 14-Hour Arcane Thriller

Now a marked fugitive fleeing the highest authorities on the continent, Corin Cadence is forced to abandon conventional magic in *A Brief History of Chronomancy*. To survive against impossible odds, Corin must unlock the most dangerous, theoretically volatile discipline in the arcane arts: chronomancy, the manipulation of the time stream. As temporal paradoxes, localized time loops, and chronological echoes threaten to unravel his own existence, Corin treats the continuum like the ultimate puzzle, engineering temporal items to outmaneuver pursuers who can foresee his every move.

In audio, this fourteen-and-a-half-hour thriller is a relentless, time-bending joyride. Nick Podehl brings kinetic energy to Corin’s desperate experiments, turning complex temporal logic and causal paradoxes into lucid, exhilarating audio storytelling. Featured in our Hard Magic Systems and Time Travel selections alongside 11/22/63 and Mother of Learning: Arc 1, this volume proves why rule-driven time manipulation is one of fantasy’s greatest thrills.

Clocking in at over fourteen hours for a single credit, this release delivers tremendous value for dedicated series fans. Rowe brings his trademark scientific rigor to temporal mechanics, ensuring that every time loop and causal trick feels completely earned.
