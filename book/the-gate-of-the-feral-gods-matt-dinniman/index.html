About this Audiobook

Entering the bubble-shielded fifth floor, Carl and Princess Donut face awakened deity avatars, boiling subterranean seas, and deadly inter-crawler politics in an epic 18-hour trial.

Matt Dinniman's Colossal Fifth-Floor Deicide Epic and Jeff Hays's Legendary Vocal Tour de Force

Descending onto the fifth floor of the World Dungeon, Carl, Princess Donut, and their expanding survivor guild enter a nightmarish aquatic landscape trapped beneath massive forcefield bubbles. Surrounded by boiling water, poisonous gases, and volcanic vents, the crawlers are forced to compete in a brutal deity-themed survival challenge where ancient, forgotten gods are awakened and deployed as biological weapons by corporate sponsors. As galactic factions pour billions of credits into rigging the game and assassinating high-ranking crawlers, Carl must orchestrate a complex web of alliances, divine deicide pacts, and improvised tactical strikes to keep his found family alive while laying siege to the very rules of the dungeon.

Building upon the explosive sabotage of The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, *The Gate of the Feral Gods* is a triumphant display of narrative scale and vocal virtuosity on audio. Crossing the eighteen-hour threshold, narrator Jeff Hays expands his sonic palette to accommodate towering mythological monsters, conniving alien attorneys, and the increasingly unstable, godlike AI dungeon master. The emotional chemistry between Carl and Donut deepens profoundly here, as the harsh realities of their captivity force both human and cat to mature into formidable battlefield commanders. Alongside landmark sci-fi listening experiences like Children of Time and epic progression tales like Defiance of the Fall, this performance shows how high-stakes world-building thrives in the LitRPG & GameLit space.

For audiobook enthusiasts seeking maximum credit value, gripping tactical complexity, and emotional resonance, this fourth volume is an exceptional investment. Soundbooth Theater’s immaculate engineering delivers thunderous oceanic acoustics, chilling divine whispers, and flawless comedic timing that make the eighteen-hour runtime evaporate. Exchanging a single credit for this sprawling epic rewards you with one of the most thrilling, inventive fantasy-audio productions available anywhere.
