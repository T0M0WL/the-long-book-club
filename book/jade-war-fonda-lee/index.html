About this Audiobook

The second volume of the Green Bone Saga expands the No Peak clan’s turf war onto the global stage as foreign powers, espionage, and black-market jade threaten Janloon’s survival.

Fonda Lee's 20-Hour International Silkpunk Crime Epic of Global Espionage and Clan Survival

Spanning twenty-and-a-half relentless hours, *Jade War* expands Fonda Lee’s Green Bone Saga onto an ambitious, breathtaking global stage. As the brutal turf war between the No Peak and Mountain clans devastates Janloon, the conflict ceases to be merely a local island feud. Foreign superpowers eye Kekon’s bio-magical jade for military applications, smuggling cartels flood the streets with deadly synthetic drugs, and international proxy wars threaten to crush Kekon’s independence. To ensure his family’s survival, newly minted Pillar Kaul Hilo must dispatch sister Shae to negotiate covert alliances with foreign intelligence agencies, while young Anden is exiled to an overseas university where anti-Kekonese prejudice runs rampant.

In audio, Andrew Kishino steps up to match Lee’s expanded scope with an even more versatile and atmospheric vocal performance. Listening to the story unfold aloud highlights Lee’s brilliant fusion of classic wuxia martial arts with 1970s Cold War-style espionage, corporate takeovers, and maritime skirmishes. Kishino effortlessly incorporates foreign accents, covert backroom assassinations, and gut-wrenching family grief, keeping the listener completely enthralled across every chapter. Grouped with grand geopolitical fantasy epics in our library like The Shadow of What Was Lost and the tense statecraft of The Wall of Storms, this middle volume deepens the emotional and narrative stakes exponentially.

Investing a single monthly credit in twenty hours of *Jade War* yields exceptional narrative dividends. Lee avoids middle-book syndrome entirely, ramping up the tension until every decision feels like life or death for the Kaul dynasty. Anchored by Andrew Kishino's peerless narration, this expansive crime epic is a masterclass in modern fantasy storytelling.

Narrator Soundcheck: Andrew Kishino

Narrator Andrew Kishino delivers another masterly, expansive performance in this twenty-hour sequel, seamlessly broadening his vocal range to incorporate international diplomats, foreign mobsters, and espionage agents. Kishino never loses the core emotional connection to the Kaul family, bringing poignant depth to Anden’s struggles abroad and Hilo’s heavy burden as clan Pillar. His pacing during the harrowing duel between clan champions on the international stage is pulse-pounding, delivered with breathless clarity and theatrical precision. With immaculate sound staging and seamless chapter breaks, this is audio excellence.
