Robert Jordan’s Unforgettable Final Solo Masterpiece of Shattering Payoffs, Heroic Rescues, and Golden Alliances

Widely hailed by fans as a triumphant return to blistering narrative velocity, *Knife of Dreams* stands as Robert Jordan’s final completed novel before his untimely passing—and what an unforgettable, emotionally overwhelming triumph it is. Every major narrative thread accelerates at breakneck speed: Perrin Aybara unleashes an audacious combined assault on the Shaido stronghold at Malden to rescue Faile; Mat Cauthon completes his hilarious, heartwarming courtship of Tuon and leads the Band of the Red Hand against overwhelming Seanchan forces; and Rand al'Thor faces horrifying ambush attacks by hordes of Trollocs that cost him dearly.

On audio, *Knife of Dreams* is an absolute tour de force that will leave listeners with tears in their eyes and adrenaline pumping through their veins. Michael Kramer’s delivery of Nynaeve’s stirring plea to the Malkieri merchants in the Borderlands—'Will he ride alone?'—is an immortal highlight of the audiobook medium that delivers goosebumps with every replay. Kate Reading is equally magnificent, capturing Egwene’s defiant courage as a prisoner within the White Tower, turning servitude into a masterclass in psychological warfare. Featured as an essential cornerstone of our Epic Fantasy library, this production is high-fantasy storytelling at its absolute apex.
