The Twenty-Hour Ensemble Finale of Olivie Blake's Cutthroat Alexandrian Trilogy

In *The Atlas Complex*, the surviving medicians of the Alexandrian Society face the final, world-altering consequences of their ruthless pursuit of knowledge. With the Society's true purpose laid bare and the boundaries of time, power, and human sacrifice pushed to their breaking point, Libby, Nico, Reina, Parisa, Callum, and Tristan must make irreversible choices that will reshape their world—or destroy each other in the process.

Experiencing this twenty-and-a-half-hour finale in audio lets the trilogy's theatrical ensemble shine one last time. Rather than getting lost in competing monologues, listeners hear the full emotional weight of three books' worth of betrayals, reluctant intimacies, and intellectual hubris collide in real time. Featured in our Dark Academia alongside The Atlas Six and The Atlas Paradox, this concluding volume delivers a dark, uncompromising finish.

Clocking in at twenty hours and thirty-seven minutes for a single credit, *The Atlas Complex* provides substantial finale value for listeners who invested in the Alexandrian Society's mind games.
