About this Audiobook

Rushing back from Hong Kong after Rakel's teenage son Oleg is locked up for executing a young street dealer amid Oslo's deadly new violin drug wave, a rogue Harry tears through corrupt police brass to save him.

Nearly Fifteen Devastating Hours Fighting Corrupt Oslo Cartels for Oleg's Life

Three years after escaping into Asian anonymity following The Leopard, Harry Hole boards a plane straight back into freezing Oslo under crushing shock: nineteen-year-old Oleg—the boy Harry raised alongside Rakel—has been arrested for gunning down fellow addict Gusto over a deadly new synthetic heroin sweeping the streets. Operating outside official badge power against ruthless kingpin Dubai, crooked politicians, and dying teen confessions, Harry tears Oslo open to prove Oleg's innocence.

*Phantom* strips away cozy procedural distance, turning Nordic Noir into visceral, street-level Crime tragedy. Intercutting the murdered teenager's dying memories with desperate Detective rogue tactics yields ferocious Thriller heartbreak within gritty Police Procedural turf.

Running fourteen hours and fifty-three minutes for a single credit, Book 9 delivers an agonizing cliffhanger resolved only inside Police.
