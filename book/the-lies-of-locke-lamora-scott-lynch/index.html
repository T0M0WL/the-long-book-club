About this Audiobook

In the decadent, canal-laced island city of Camorr, an elite group of light-fingered con artists target the ultra-wealthy aristocracy until a deadly coup in the criminal underworld threatens to drown them in blood.

A Swashbuckling, Blood-Drenched Con Game That Redefined Fantasy Heists

Set in the fantastical, canal-draped merchant republic of Camorr\\u2014a sun-baked city carved from alien Elderglass that evokes a decadent Renaissance Venice\\u2014Scott Lynch\\u2019s modern masterpiece *The Lies of Locke Lamora* is an electrifying, genre-defining hybrid of elaborate heist fiction, low-fantasy grit, and profound brotherly devotion. The narrative follows Locke Lamora, the physically unimposing yet terrifyingly brilliant leader of the Gentlemen Bastards. While the city\\u2019s ruling Capa believes Locke and his tiny crew are small-time pickpockets, they are secretly orchestrating multi-million-crown theatrical confidence games against Camorr\\u2019s wealthiest, most corrupt aristocrats.

What elevates *The Lies of Locke Lamora* into an immortal audio classic is the phenomenal performance of British narrator Michael Page. Lynch\\u2019s prose is an intoxicating feast of vulgar eloquence, elaborate profanity, and culinary poetry, and Page delivers every line with theatrical gusto and wicked charm. The bond between Locke and his hulking, dual-hatchet-wielding best friend Jean Tannen is the emotional heartbeat of the story, making their comic banter sparkle before the plot takes a devastatingly dark, bloody turn. Listeners who loved the intricate underworld schemes and dry antihero cynicism of The Blade Itself or the tactical squad loyalty of The Black Company will be totally consumed by Lynch\\u2019s masterpiece.

At just under 22 hours, *The Lies of Locke Lamora* offers exceptional return for a single Audible credit or free trial token. Lynch seamlessly alternates between Locke\\u2019s impoverished, scrappy childhood training under the enigmatic thief-maker Father Chains, the intricate clockwork mechanics of their noble marks, and their high-stakes present-day swindles, culminating in a ferocious, heartbreak-fueled climax that will leave you breathless. Lynch's brilliance lies in contrasting the dazzling, operatic spectacle of high-society deception against the horrifying, visceral consequences when a con unravels into raw gangland slaughter. It is witty, violent, tear-jerking, and utterly magnificent on audio, delivering an unforgettable listening experience that will make you fall in love with thieves.
