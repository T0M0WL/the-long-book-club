Nearly Thirteen Sparkling Hours of Hoid-Narrated Cosmere Magic and Wit

Written as a brisk, deeply imaginative standalone inside Brandon Sanderson's sprawling Cosmere universe, *The Fires of December* hands the microphone back to fan-favorite world-hopper Hoid—delivering the same playful, fairy-tale warmth and clever mechanical twistiness that made Tress of the Emerald Sea such an instant audio classic. Balancing inventive Magic Systems with heartfelt character stakes and sly Cosmere Easter eggs, it offers a refreshing, self-contained blast of wonder between fifty-hour *Stormlight* doorstoppers.

On audio, Hoid's dry fourth-wall asides and deadpan observations land with pure comedic sparkle. Whether you are a longtime Sanderson completionist or looking for a punchier companion to 2026's massive Epic Fantasy marathons in our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026, it proves Sanderson can pack a full world's worth of charm into a tighter canvas.

At twelve hours and forty-two minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Fires of December* is effortlessly entertaining Fantasy comfort food.
