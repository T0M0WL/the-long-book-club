Andrew Rowe’s 20-Hour Political and Demigod Confrontation in the Arcane Ascension Saga

Returning home to Valia following their chaotic Caelfordian excursion, Corin Cadence finds his homeland on the brink of total civil collapse in *The Silence of Unworthy Gods*. With house politics turning lethal, rival factions threatening his sister and allies, and the mysterious Visages openly intervening in mortal affairs, Corin can no longer afford to remain a passive student researcher. Forced into high-level diplomatic maneuvers and catastrophic arcane trials, Corin must master high-tier mana shaping and deploy experimental enchanted artifacts to challenge beings who consider themselves living gods.

Listening to this nearly twenty-one-hour recording is an electrifying experience that pulls together narrative threads from across Rowe’s entire shared universe. Nick Podehl delivers one of his most powerful performances in the series, capturing the crushing psychological weight Corin bears as he takes charge of his family’s destiny. Beside companion progression titans like Dungeon Crawler Carl and The Will of the Many, this volume proves that intellect and preparation can conquer overwhelming physical might.

Clocking in at twenty and a half hours for a single token, *The Silence of Unworthy Gods* delivers exceptional value for dedicated listeners. Rowe raises the narrative stakes to a fever pitch, delivering jaw-dropping revelations about the spires' true creators. It is an indispensable, thrilling credit spend for every fan of tactical fantasy.
