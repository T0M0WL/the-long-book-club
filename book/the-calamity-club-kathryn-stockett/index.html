About this Audiobook

Set in Depression-era Oxford, Mississippi, Kathryn Stockett's sweeping return follows a group of fiercely resilient women navigating poverty, small-town secrets, and unexpected solidarity across the turbulent 1930s South.

Nearly Twenty-Nine Unforgettable Hours in Depression-Era Mississippi

Over fifteen years after *The Help* became a global phenomenon, Kathryn Stockett returns with her most ambitious, generously scaled novel yet. Set in 1930s Oxford, Mississippi—as the Great Depression strips away genteel facades and forces neighbors to look sharply at who holds power and who gets left in the dust—*The Calamity Club* gathers an unforgettable ensemble of women forced to forge secret alliances, protect buried truths, and survive an era designed to break them.

Experiencing this sweeping Historical Fiction epic on audio is an absolute luxury. Stockett writes with a keen ear for Southern rhythm, razor-sharp humor, and simmering domestic suspense, letting relationships thicken organically across seasons rather than rushing toward neat conclusions. Anchoring our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 alongside fellow literary heavyweights American Hagwon and The Covenant of Water, it is quintessential porch-swing Americana with genuine emotional teeth.

Clocking in at a staggering twenty-eight hours and forty-four minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Calamity Club* delivers nearly a full month of rich, immersive storytelling that rewards every minute.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jenna Lamia, January LaVoy

Pairing two Hall-of-Fame audio titans—Jenna Lamia and January LaVoy—across nearly twenty-nine hours is pure casting alchemy. Both narrators capture the subtle class shadings, warm humor, and quiet steel of 1930s Mississippi without ever slipping into caricature, making every perspective shift feel like tuning into a prestige limited series.
