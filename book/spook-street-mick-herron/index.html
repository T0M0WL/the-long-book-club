About this Audiobook

When a retired Cold War legend loses his memory and shoots an intruder, Jackson Lamb's crew confronts a shopping center bombing and a deadly secret hit squad.

Memory Loss, Dead Man's Switches, and Lethal Cold War Ghosts

In *Spook Street*, Mick Herron delivers what many aficionados consider the emotional pinnacle of the Slough House series, earning both the CWA Ian Fleming Steel Dagger and the CWA Gold Dagger. Spanning ten and a half intense hours, the story centers on the heartbreaking vulnerability of David Cartwright—the legendary former MI5 spymaster known throughout the service as the 'O.B.' (Old Bastard) and the grandfather who raised River Cartwright. Slipping rapidly into dementia, the elder Cartwright lives in paranoid isolation, convinced that old enemies have come to settle ancient scores. When an unidentified intruder breaches his quiet country cottage in the dead of night, the frightened veteran instinctively shoots the man dead.

Simultaneously, a horrifying flash mob suicide bombing at a West London shopping mall kills dozens of teenagers, sending Whitehall into full panic mode. As River rushes to his grandfather’s house, he discovers that the body on the floor triggers a devastating series of deceptions. Jackson Lamb quickly deduces that someone high up in intelligence circles is systematically sanitizing the past, dispatching ruthless contract killers to eliminate anyone tied to an unsanctioned Cold War operation conducted decades earlier. To survive, the slow horses must stay one step ahead of MI5’s lethal internal enforcement wing while unraveling a web of compromised loyalties that threatens to destroy River’s sense of identity.

Herron’s depiction of dementia in an elite intelligence context is deeply moving, contrasting the tragic decline of a once-brilliant mind with the ruthless mechanics of statecraft. The introduction of J.K. Coe, a traumatized psychological profiler who barely speaks, adds another memorable dimension to Slough House’s bizarre ensemble. In audio, Sean Barrett’s masterly delivery brings profound emotional resonance to David Cartwright’s frailty while maintaining the savage bite of Lamb’s razor-sharp banter. It is a stunning, indispensable chapter for any serious espionage fan.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sean Barrett

Sean Barrett’s vocal work in *Spook Street* is among the very finest of his illustrious career. He brings immense warmth and pathos to the fading mind of David Cartwright, capturing the tragic contrast between the Old Bastard's fierce instincts and his heartbreaking confusion. When voicing Jackson Lamb, Barrett delivers caustic insults with effortless, rasping perfection, while never losing the protective loyalty that anchors the character. His subtle pacing during the tense standoff scenes creates unbearable suspense that keeps listeners transfixed.
