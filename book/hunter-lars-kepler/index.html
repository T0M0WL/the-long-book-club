About this Audiobook

Released briefly from high-security prison by panicked officials, Joona Linna joins Saga Bauer to unmask an assassin stalking Sweden's political elite to the chilling verses of an old nursery rhyme.

Nearly Seventeen Hours Racing an Assassin Guided by a Twisted Nursery Rhyme

Published in North America as *The Rabbit Hunter*, *Hunter* opens with Sweden's foreign minister violently assassinated inside a secluded luxury estate—leaving behind a bizarre miniature rabbit figurine and nineteen minutes of terror recorded before death. As other senior politicians receive identical sinister warnings counting down through the verses of a macabre children's song, desperate authorities temporarily release Joona Linna from Kumla high-security prison. Partnered urgently with Saga Bauer, Joona must hunt a lethal phantom driven by a decades-old night of buried brutality.

Listening to *Hunter* injects propulsive political urgency into Kepler's signature Nordic Noir craftsmanship. Blending ferocious action choreography with methodical Police Procedural puzzle-solving, this high-stakes Crime thriller twists classic Detective deduction around devastating Psychological Thriller trauma and an unforgettable Mystery countdown.

Offering sixteen hours and fifty-three minutes of fierce, twist-heavy pacing for a single credit, *Hunter* marks Joona's explosive return to full-strength pursuit straight into Lazarus.
