About this Audiobook

When the Devil arrives in Stalinist Moscow accompanied by a giant, vodka-drinking black cat and a chaotic retinue, bureaucratic hypocrites unravel while a persecuted writer and his fierce lover fight for redemption across Moscow and ancient Jerusalem.

Nearly Seventeen Hours of Wicked Soviet Satire, Talking Cats, and Devilish Philosophy

Written in secret during Stalin's purges and suppressed for decades, Mikhail Bulgakov's *The Master and Margarita* remains one of the wildest, funniest, and most subversive Metaphysical classics ever conceived. The chaos begins on a sweltering Moscow afternoon when Satan—posing as the suave foreign professor Woland—arrives with a retinue that includes a fanged hitman and Behemoth, a giant, vodka-swilling, chess-playing black cat. Within hours, pompous Soviet literary bureaucrats are losing their heads, their apartments, and their sanity.

Braided alongside this anarchic slapstick is a luminous, dead-serious retelling of Pontius Pilate's fateful interrogation of Yeshua in ancient Jerusalem—written by the persecuted 'Master' whom the fearless Margarita will make a literal pact with the Devil to save. On audio, the sharp contrast between wicked Satire and aching philosophical beauty lands with electric energy, making it a natural companion to The Brothers Karamazov and The Castle.

At sixteen hours and fifty-two minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Master and Margarita* is riotous, haunting, and utterly one of a kind.

Narrator Soundcheck: Julian Rhind-Tutt

Julian Rhind-Tutt is pitch-perfect casting for Bulgakov's razor-sharp blend of farce and tragedy. Across nearly seventeen hours, he delivers Woland's aristocratic menace, Behemoth's absurd theatrical bravado, and the bureaucratic panic of 1930s Moscow with sparkling comic timing—then shifts seamlessly into hushed, luminous gravity for the Jerusalem chapters.
