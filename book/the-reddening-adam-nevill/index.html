About this Audiobook

When an archaeological dig on the windswept Devon coast unearths prehistoric human remains smeared in red ochre, journalist Kat and grief-stricken outsider Helene are drawn into the clutches of an ancient, bloodthirsty rural cult.

Over Twelve Suffocating Hours of Prehistoric Blood Rituals on the Devon Coast

Few modern writers capture the bleak, wind-scoured menace of rural Britain like Adam Nevill, and *The Reddening* stands as one of his richest Folk Horror achievements. When a coastal cave collapse in South Devon exposes an untouched Paleolithic burial site smeared with red ochre, lifestyle journalist Kat and grief-bruised newcomer Helene find their lives converging around a secretive local community that never abandoned the oldest, bloodiest traditions of the land.

Nevill excels at turning idyllic seaside scenery—fog-shrouded tors, isolated holiday cottages, and quiet country lanes—into a claustrophobic trap where every smiling neighbour feels faintly wrong. Blending slow-burn Mystery investigation with escalating British Literature unease and primal Horror dread, *The Reddening* pairs seamlessly with Andrew Michael Hurley’s coastal chiller The Loney and Nevill’s own woodland landmark The Ritual.

Spanning twelve hours and nineteen minutes for a single Audible credit, it is a masterclass in creeping paranoia—building methodically from quiet archaeological curiosity to an unforgettable, blood-chilling crescendo.

Narrator Soundcheck: Conner Goff

Conner Goff delivers an understated, deeply atmospheric twelve-hour performance that lets Nevill's creeping dread seep in gradually. His grounded, conversational pacing anchors Kat and Helene's mounting paranoia while giving the eccentric Devon locals a subtly unsettling edge—making quiet coastal walks and midnight knocks at the door feel genuinely nerve-wracking.
