About this Audiobook

Locke Lamora and Jean Tannen attempt the ultimate casino heist in Tal Verrar before finding themselves forced into high-seas piracy.

Swashbuckling Swindlers: Michael Page Delivers 25 Hours of Pure Auditory Joy

In *Red Seas Under Red Skies*, Scott Lynch pulls off one of the most audacious genre mashups in modern fantasy literature. Having fled the blood-soaked ruins of Camorr, Locke Lamora and his steadfast partner Jean Tannen re-emerge in the exotic island city of Tal Verrar, aiming to rob the Sinspire—an impenetrable, multi-story luxury gambling casino protected by lethal magical wards. But just as their convoluted con reaches its delicate zenith, the pair are coerced by the city's ruthless military ruler into taking command of a pirate vessel, despite possessing zero sailing experience and a deadly fear of drowning.

Narrator Michael Page returns with an exuberance and swagger that makes this twenty-five-hour audio production an absolute riot. Page’s chemistry with Locke and Jean's brotherly banter is flawless; his comedic timing during their chaotic attempts to learn nautical terminology under pirate captain Zamira Drakasha is pure audio comedy gold. Yet when tragedy strikes and debts come due, Page shifts into raw, emotional vulnerability with heartbreaking ease. Fans of The Lies of Locke Lamora or the roguish charm of The Blade Itself will be enthralled.

At twenty-five and a half hours for a single credit, this release is the definition of premium audio value. It delivers two complete, interlocking epics for the price of one—a razor-sharp heist caper and a thrilling high-seas naval adventure—capping it with an unforgettable ending that leaves listeners desperate for more.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Page

Michael Page gives a masterclass in vocal personality, infusing Locke Lamora with infectious wit and Jean Tannen with loyal, heavy-set gravitas. His handling of pirate sea battles features booming authority and crackling adrenaline, while his feminine voices—especially Captain Zamira Drakasha—are delivered with dignity and fierce charisma. Flawless audio engineering complements an outstanding performance.
