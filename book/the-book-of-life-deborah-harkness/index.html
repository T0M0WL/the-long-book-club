About this Audiobook

Returning to the present day, Diana and Matthew battle the Congregation, genetic prejudice, and ancestral secrets in the grand All Souls trilogy finale.

Genetic Destiny, Ancient Manuscript Codes, and the Triumph of the Bishop-Clairmont Clan

Spanning twenty-three and a half thrilling, scientifically rigorous hours, *The Book of Life* delivers the grand, emotionally satisfying conclusion to Deborah Harkness’s original *All Souls* trilogy. Following their perilous temporal pilgrimage in Shadow of Night, Diana Bishop and Matthew Clairmont return to the twenty-first century to face devastating tragedy at Sept-Tours, the Clairmont ancestral chateau in France. Armed with missing pages from the enigmatic Ashmole 782 and newly awakened higher weaver magic, Diana is also pregnant with twins—supernatural offspring whose very existence violates the sacred Covenant established by the governing Congregation.

What makes Harkness’s conclusion so uniquely brilliant is her seamless integration of modern molecular biology with medieval esoteric mysticism. Matthew’s Yale genetic laboratory becomes the frontline of a high-stakes scientific investigation into blood rage and creature origins, decoding chromosomes and mitochondrial DNA to dismantle centuries of species segregation. As hostile factions led by the ruthless Gerbert of Aurillac and Peter Knox close in to eradicate the Bishop-Clairmont clan, Diana must unlock the ultimate secret of the Book of Life to protect her children and dismantle the archaic prejudices of the supernatural world forever.

Narrator Jennifer Ikeda brings the trilogy to a triumphant close, infusing Diana’s transformation from reluctant academic to formidable weaver with immense emotional authority. Ikeda handles the brisk intellectual shifts from laboratory genetics to ancient alchemical incantations with effortless fluency, giving every family ally—from the fiery vampire Ysabeau to the loyal demon Hamish—an unmistakable acoustic signature. For listeners seeking a deeply intelligent, romantically sweeping, and meticulously plotted Urban Fantasy conclusion on a single credit, *The Book of Life* is an absolute audio classic.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jennifer Ikeda

Jennifer Ikeda’s narration on The Book of Life is an emotional and technical triumph. Ikeda beautifully balances the tender domestic vulnerability of Diana and Matthew’s growing family with the high-stakes fury of magical and scientific warfare. Her French accents for the Clairmont elders are sophisticated and authentic, while her delivery of technical genetics lectures is crisp, clear, and engaging. Produced with pristine studio balance across twenty-three hours, this audiobook provides an utterly immersive listening finale.
