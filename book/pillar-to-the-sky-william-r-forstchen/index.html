About this Audiobook

With Earth facing terminal energy depletion, visionary scientists and engineers battle political sabotage and extreme physics to construct a 23,000-mile space elevator anchored to an oceanic platform.

William R. Forstchen's Visionary Space Elevator Epic and Grover Gardner's Authoritative 15-Hour Performance

In *Pillar to the Sky*, *New York Times* bestselling author William R. Forstchen—renowned for his gritty technological survival epics—crafts a grand, inspiring hard science fiction procedural that harkens back to the optimistic golden age of Arthur C. Clarke and Robert Heinlein. Set against the backdrop of an impending global energy crisis, the story follows husband-and-wife scientists Gary and Eva Morgan and an eccentric billionaire financier who decide to bypass fossil fuel wars entirely by building humanity's first Space Elevator: a 23,000-mile carbon-nanotube ribbon anchored to an oceanic equatorial platform and stretching out to a counterweighted asteroid in geostationary orbit to harvest clean space solar power.

What transforms this novel into an auditory tour de force is the grounded, authoritative gravitas brought by legendary narrator Grover Gardner. Rather than relying on laser battles or alien invasions, Forstchen builds breathless suspense from real-world engineering obstacles: material stress tolerances, high-altitude atmospheric lightning, orbital collision debris, and vicious political sabotage from oil oligarchs and Washington bureaucrats. Gardner’s warm, steady baritone lends every boardroom argument, construction disaster, and breathless zero-gravity cable climb immense credibility. Paired with companion hard-sci-fi triumphs like Project Hail Mary and visionary mega-engineering sagas like The Pillars of the Earth, this production is a standout favorite in our Science Fiction collection.

Spanning fifteen and a half meticulously paced hours, *Pillar to the Sky* provides sensational single-credit value for lovers of ambitious engineering, scientific optimism, and authentic procedural drama. Gardner’s peerless storytelling rhythm ensures that complex orbital physics never drags, making every engineering milestone feel like a historic human triumph. Exchanging a single credit for this soaring space odyssey guarantees an uplifting, deeply rewarding listening experience that will leave you staring at the night sky in awe.
