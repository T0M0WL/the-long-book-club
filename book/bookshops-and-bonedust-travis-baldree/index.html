About this Audiobook

Wounded in battle, young orc barbarian Viv is stranded in the sleepy seaside town of Murk, where a rundown bookstore, warm cinnamon rolls, and a charming baker change her destiny forever.

Cinnamon Rolls and Secondhand Tomes: Author-Narrator Travis Baldree Delivers 8 Hours of Pure Comfort

Serving as a heartwarming prequel to the bestselling cozy fantasy sensation *Legends & Lattes*, Travis Baldree’s *Bookshops & Bonedust* takes listeners back twenty years to the youthful, reckless mercenary days of Viv the orc barbarian. Wounded in the leg while hunting a powerful necromancer with her mercenary company, Viv is temporarily abandoned in the sleepy, rain-swept seaside town of Murk to recuperate. Frustrated and restless, Viv wanders into a dilapidated, failing bookstore run by a foul-mouthed rattkin named Fern. What begins as a reluctant quest to kill time turns into a transformative summer as Viv helps renovate the shop, falls for a charming satyr baker named Maylee, and discovers that quiet companionship and good stories can heal wounds no sword ever could.

As both the author and one of the most beloved narrators in modern fantasy audio, Travis Baldree delivers an auditory hug of sublime warmth and humor. Baldree’s vocal range is unmatched: he voices young Viv with fiery, impatient vulnerability, the cantankerous bookshop owner Fern with dry comedic perfection, and the endearing gnome artist and townspeople with infectious affection. When an ancient necromantic artifact brings unexpected danger to Murk's quiet cobblestone streets, Baldree seamlessly blends cozy slice-of-life charm with high-stakes fantasy combat. Listeners who fell in love with Legends & Lattes or the whimsical warmth of The House in the Cerulean Sea will find this recording utterly irresistible.

At just over eight hours, *Bookshops & Bonedust* is a rare cozy exception to our long-book catalog, and it justifies every single minute of your listening time. Baldree captures the intoxicating magic of finding a second family, the scent of freshly printed pages, and the bittersweet beauty of first love. It is the ultimate comfort listen for a rainy Sunday afternoon, wrapped in world-class audio performance.
