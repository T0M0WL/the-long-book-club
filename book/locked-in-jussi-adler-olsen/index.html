Locked In

Author: Jussi Adler-Olsen | Narrator: Steven Pacey

Runtime: 16h 14m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Mystery, Crime, Suspense, Police Procedural, Detective

About this Audiobook

Arrested for cocaine smuggling and murder after drugs surface in his attic, Carl Mørck faces lethal prison bounties inside high-security lockdown—forcing Assad, Rose, and Gordon into an unauthorized rogue mission to clear his name.

Over Sixteen Hours Delivering Department Q's High-Stakes Prison Finale and Reckoning

Picking up instantly from the bombshell closing twist of The Shadow Murders, *Locked In* finds Carl Mørck handcuffed, disgraced, and thrown into high-security prison after bags of cocaine tied to his old unsolved nail-gun shooting case surface inside his own home. Marked for assassination by ruthless European traffickers and abandoned by cautious brass, Carl fights to stay alive inside lockdown while Assad, Rose, and Gordon go completely rogue outside to dismantle a corrupt decade-long conspiracy going all the way back to Mercy.

As the roaring tenth capstone to Danish Nordic Noir, *Locked In* swaps dusty basement files for desperate prison-yard Suspense and rebellious Police Procedural teamwork. Every lingering Crime thread and moral sacrifice pays off with explosive Detective justice and deeply earned emotional warmth across its final Mystery.

Spanning sixteen hours and fourteen minutes for a single Audible credit, *Locked In* rewards ten books of listener loyalty with a thunderous, deeply satisfying curtain call.
