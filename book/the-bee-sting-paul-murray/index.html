About this Audiobook

The Barnes family in a small Irish town facing financial ruin and personal crises. Interweaves perspectives of four family members as they deal with secrets, past mistakes, and their dysfunctional present.

A Tragicomic Masterpiece of Irish Family Dysfunction and Financial Collapse in Brilliant Audio

Paul Murray’s Booker-shortlisted tragicomedy plunges listeners directly into the spiralling fortunes of the Barnes family in a small Irish town reeling from catastrophic economic fallout. Once prosperous car dealership owner Dickie Barnes watches his family business crumble into financial ruin, while his glamorous wife Clair secretly sells off her designer wardrobe and vintage jewelry to maintain appearances. Meanwhile, their teenage daughter Cass binges heavily on cheap cider to numb the suffocating anxiety of her impending Leaving Cert exams, and sensitive twelve-year-old PJ quietly stockpiles survival supplies while planning to run away from home. Murray dissects each family member's private terror with staggering wit, heart-wrenching vulnerability, and blistering dark comic irony, weaving a multi-generational tapestry where long-buried secrets, romantic compromises, and ancestral guilt inevitably erupt into the fragile present.

On audio, Murray’s daring structural shifts become an astonishingly intimate and electrifying journey. Each family member commands their own distinct narrative cadence—ranging from breathless, unpunctuated stream-of-consciousness monologues to detached, haunting interior confessions—that come alive with vibrant Midland Irish authenticity. Rather than struggling against the novel’s demanding visual punctuation and layout shifts on the physical page, listeners are pulled effortlessly into the chaotic emotional weather of the household through dynamic spoken performances. Highlighted as a centerpiece of our Best Audiobooks of 2025 and our Best Fiction Audiobooks catalog, this expansive production demonstrates how vocal intimacy transforms ambitious literary fiction into an unforgettable, high-voltage domestic drama.

Clocking in at over twenty-six hours of continuous listening, this sprawling tragicomedy offers unmatched credit value for lovers of complex, character-driven contemporary literature. Murray achieves the exceedingly rare feat of crafting a novel that is simultaneously laugh-out-loud funny, deeply unsettling, and emotionally devastating, sustaining relentless narrative momentum across every single chapter.
