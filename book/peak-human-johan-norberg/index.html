About this Audiobook

Spanning classical Athens, Augustan Rome, Abbasid Baghdad, Song China, Renaissance Italy, the Dutch Republic, and the Anglosphere, Johan Norberg examines why humanity's seven greatest golden ages exploded with creativity—and why they fell.

Over Fifteen Provocative Hours Exploring History's Seven Greatest Golden Ages

Why do certain rare pockets of human history suddenly ignite with astonishing breakthroughs in art, philosophy, trade, and science—only to slam the doors shut and slide back into stagnation? In *Peak Human*, historian Johan Norberg sweeps across two and a half millennia, visiting seven legendary golden ages: classical Athens, the Roman Republic, Abbasid Baghdad, Song Dynasty China, Renaissance Italy, the Dutch Republic, and the modern Anglosphere.

Structured like an intellectual grand tour, *Peak Human* thrives in the audio format. Each civilization gets its own vibrant, story-rich portrait—packed with rival merchants, rebel astronomers, printing presses, and political hubris—making big-picture History and Philosophy feel crisp, lively, and endlessly discussable. Anchoring the non-fiction wing of our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 beside Alexander: God, King, Man and The Boundless Deep, it is ideal commuter brain-food.

Spanning fifteen hours and thirty minutes for a single Audible credit, *Peak Human* delivers sweeping global perspective and crisp narrative momentum from Athens to the present day.
