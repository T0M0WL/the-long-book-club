Neil Gaiman’s Lyrical Fireside Rendition of the Norse Pantheon in Audio

In *Norse Mythology*, master fabulist Neil Gaiman strips away centuries of academic dust and Wagnerian bombast to return the ancient northern myths to their original, primal medium: the oral tradition. Drawing from the thirteenth-century *Prose Edda* of Snorri Sturluson and the anonymous *Poetic Edda*, Gaiman weaves sixteen episodic tales into a continuous novelistic tapestry. Beginning with the primeval void of Ginnungagap and the shaping of the nine realms from the flesh of Ymir the frost giant, the narrative charts the triumphs, squabbles, and tragic flaws of the Aesir—culminating in the cataclysmic, sun-devouring apocalypse of Ragnarök.

In audio format, Gaiman’s performance is nothing short of hypnotic. Narrating his own work with intimate fireside warmth, Gaiman understands instinctively how these stories were meant to be heard around a winter hearth. He delivers the boastful bluster of Thor with booming deadpan humor, imbues Odin with a weary, raven-shadowed wisdom, and captures Loki's insidious, shape-shifting malice with purring charm. Narrator Soundcheck: Neil Gaiman
