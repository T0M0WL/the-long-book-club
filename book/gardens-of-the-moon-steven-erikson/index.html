About this Audiobook

As the ruthless Malazan Empire lays siege to the ancient free city of Pale, a beleaguered squad of elite sappers and scarred sorcerers find themselves caught between warring gods and ascendant tyrants.

A Sprawling, Unforgiving Masterpiece of Sorcerous Warfare and Tragic Heroism

There is epic fantasy, and then there is Steven Erikson\\u2019s *Malazan Book of the Fallen*. First published in 1999, *Gardens of the Moon* is an astonishing, uncompromising military epic that plunges listeners headfirst into the brutal campaigns of the Malazan Empire. Having conquered seven cities across Genabackis, Empress Laseen\\u2019s forces lay siege to the defying metropolis of Pale, aided by the ancient, floating basalt fortress of Moon\\u2019s Spawn. When a cataclysmic sorcerous battle leaves hundreds of mages slaughtered, the battered survivors of the elite Bridgeburners squad realize they are being sacrificed by their own high command.

In the audio format, *Gardens of the Moon* is an awe-inspiring, hypnotic experience guided by the versatile theatrical gravitas of Ralph Lister. Erikson\\u2019s background as an archaeologist and anthropologist imbues every ruined city, elder race, and Warren of magic with millennia of authentic weight. Lister brilliantly distinguishes the battle-weary cynicism of sergeant Whiskeyjack, the paranoid strategic genius of high mage Quick Ben, the lethal precision of assassin Kalam, and the ominous chill of the ancient Tiste Andii lord Anomander Rake. If you were captivated by the tactical squad camaraderie of The Black Company or the ruthless, multi-POV warfare in our Longest Ever Audiobooks, Malazan is the ultimate mountain to climb.

Spanning over 26 hours of wall-to-wall sorcerous warfare, political subterfuge, and godly machinations, *Gardens of the Moon* delivers towering value for a single Audible credit. Erikson steadfastly refuses to patronize listeners with lazy exposition or convenient answers; instead, he crafts a living, breathing cosmos where mortals bleed and gods bleed harder. Erikson's colossal world does not wait for you to catch up; it sweeps you into an ancient current of marching legions, forgotten ascendants, and devastating convergence. It is a demanding, deeply rewarding audio epic that kickstarts a ten-volume legend, offering unmatched immersion for anyone ready to embark on the ultimate fantasy challenge.

Narrator Soundcheck: Ralph Lister

Ralph Lister brings immense theatrical energy, emotional nuance, and distinct character voices to Erikson\u2019s staggering cast of soldiers, assassins, gods, and mages. His portrayal of the cynical, shell-shocked Bridgeburners captures their weary camaraderie and gallows humour, while his voice for Anomander Rake carries ancient, melancholic power. Lister handles the jaw-dropping scale of Malazan\u2019s explosive magical battles with breathless intensity, keeping complex military tactics and arcane terminology clear and engaging across 26 hours.
