About this Audiobook

When the kingdom of Lascanne runs out of sons in a gruelling flintlock war against neighbouring Highfang, gentlewoman Emily Marshwic is conscripted from her country manor into the brutal front-line trenches alongside warlocks and sharpshooters.

Twenty-One Hours of Jane Austen Manners Colliding With Black-Powder Trenches in Tchaikovsky's Standalone

When the prosperous kingdom of Lascanne enters a disastrous war of attrition against neighbouring Highfang, every young gentleman is shipped to the swamps and trenches—and still the casualties mount. Faced with total defeat, the crown takes an unthinkable step: conscripting women from noble estates into the front-line infantry. Gentlewoman Emily Marshwic leaves behind Regency drawing rooms for lice-infested mud, flintlock musket drills, and ruthless battlefield warlocks, discovering that the deadliest lies are told by the aristocrats safely back home.

Listening to *Guns of the Dawn* on audio is an extraordinary emotional triumph. Adrian Tchaikovsky transforms Flintlock Fantasy and Historical Fantasy by pairing crisp black-powder realism with the intimate social wit of classic Regency novels—standing alongside Promise of Blood and Jonathan Strange & Mr Norrell on our Flintlock Fantasy shelf.

At twenty-one hours and eight minutes for a single monthly credit, *Guns of the Dawn* delivers one of Tchaikovsky's finest standalone novels—rich with courage, dry humour, and devastating anti-war bite.

Narrator Soundcheck: Emma Newman

Hugo-shortlisted narrator and author Emma Newman delivers a powerhouse twenty-one-hour performance. Newman traces Emily Marshwic's harrowing evolution from a sheltered polite daughter into a battle-hardened soldier with breathtaking emotional truth, while voicing grizzled sergeants, aristocratic officers, and traumatized trench mates with remarkable vocal range.
