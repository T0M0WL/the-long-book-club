Neal Stephenson\u2019s Mind-Bending Thirty-Two-Hour Philosophical Sci-Fi Epic of Avout Monks in Audio

Set on the distant world of Arbre, where intellectual scholars and mathematicians known as the 'Avout' live cloistered inside fortress-like monastic sanctuaries called 'maths' to protect pure science from the corrupting cycles of decadent secular civilization, Neal Stephenson\u2019s *Anathem* is a breathtaking monument of speculative fiction. Young monk-scholar Fraa Erasmas spends his days ringing monastery bells, studying centuries-old cosmological theories, and debating geometry with his eccentric mentor. However, when an ancient, camouflaged alien starship is suddenly detected in high planetary orbit, the gates of the sanctuary are thrown open for the first time in a thousand years, casting Erasmas and his fellow philosophic monks into an epic planetary and interplanetary crisis.
