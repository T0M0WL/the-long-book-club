About this Audiobook

Sixteen Hours Unearthing Three Mummified Corpses and Sprogø Island Horrors

When workmen renovating an empty Copenhagen flat smash through a false plasterboard wall and uncover three mummified bodies seated neatly around an untouched dinner table—plus one vacant fourth chair—Redemption fans immediately know Department Q has walked into pure macabre territory. Carl Mørck, Assad, and Rose trace the macabre room back to the mid-century disappearance of women banished to Denmark's notorious women's asylum on Sprogø Island, uncovering brutal forced-sterilization abuses and a calculating victim exacting decades-overdue vengeance.

On audio, *Guilt* (US title: *The Purity of Vengeance*) pairs unforgettable Scandinavian gothic imagery with urgent social outrage at the heart of Nordic Noir. Intercutting Nete Rosen's harrowing 1950s survival with modern Police Procedural breakthroughs transforms historical cruelty into piercing Crime and Detective Mystery, keeping genuine Suspense taut across dual timelines.

At sixteen hours for a single Audible credit, *Guilt* cements Department Q's status as an unputdownable moral mirror before the runaway street-chase energy of Buried.
