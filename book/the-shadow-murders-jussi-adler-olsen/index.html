About this Audiobook

Triggered when an apparent suicide echoes a fiery 1988 auto-shop blast, Carl Mørck, Assad, and Rose trace eerie heaps of spilled table salt linking seemingly accidental deaths every two years across three decades.

Nearly Fourteen Hours Stalking a Killer Who Stages Murders Every Two Years

On the morning of her sixtieth birthday—having kept silent grief locked tight since a devastating 1988 auto-repair explosion robbed her of her child—a Danish woman apparently steps forward to end her own life. When Homicide chief Marcus Jacobsen spots an eerie, long-overlooked connection between that blast and scattered heaps of spilled table salt left every two years at supposedly accidental fatalities, Carl Mørck, Assad, Rose, and Gordon launch a pandemic-era hunt in the wake of Victim 2117 to catch an ultra-patient phantom before their next calendar strike.

Listening to *The Shadow Murders* distills Nordic Noir down to lean, chilling clockwork precision. Tightening Police Procedural deduction around thirty years of hidden Crime statistics builds relentless Detective Suspense—while quietly springing a jaw-dropping final trap straight out of Book 1's long-simmering Mystery roots.

At thirteen hours and fifty-nine minutes for one credit, *The Shadow Murders* flies past like a coiled spring—setting up the immediate prison crisis of Locked In.
