About this Audiobook

As the moon-mirror fails and the Scithrowl invasion sweeps the corridor, Nona Grey leads her sisters in a desperate last stand for humanity.

Glacial Extinction, Bloody Sisterhood, and the Final Battle for Abeth

Clocking in at just under fourteen thrilling, emotionally devastating hours, *Holy Sister* delivers the triumphant, blood-soaked finale to Mark Lawrence’s celebrated Book of the Ancestor trilogy, resolving the fates set in motion in Red Sister and Grey Sister. The apocalyptic clock on the dying world of Abeth has run out: the ancient orbital moon that focuses sunlight onto the corridor is drifting out of alignment, causing glaciers miles thick to crush cities beneath creeping walls of ice. Simultaneously, the ruthless barbarian armies of the Scithrowl cross the frozen wastes in an unstoppable, genocidal invasion.

Divided across dual timelines—alternating between Nona’s harrowing expedition across the frozen wilderness with her hated rival Clera and the desperate defense of the Convent of Sweet Mercy years later—the novel showcases Lawrence’s exquisite plotting and emotional depth. Now a fully initiated nun trained in every lethal art from Red combat to Holy battle-magic, Nona must wield the Shipheart: a dangerous alien artifact capable of destroying the moon or igniting the world. But unleashing its power demands supreme sacrifice, forcing Nona to confront the dark truth of her origins and test the bonds of sisterhood against the ultimate crucible of war.

The narrative reaches an astonishing crescendo where every lesson learned within the convent walls converges on the battlefield. Helen Duff’s concluding performance is a masterpiece of dark fantasy narration. She brings breathtaking intensity to every sword clash, incantation, and tearful farewell, anchoring the novel's grand apocalyptic scale in the raw, personal humanity of Nona and her sisters. For listeners seeking a definitive, satisfying, and unsparing conclusion to one of modern fantasy’s greatest trilogies, *Holy Sister* is an absolute triumph that justifies every single credit.

Narrator Soundcheck: Helen Duff

Helen Duff concludes the Book of the Ancestor trilogy with a bravura vocal performance in Holy Sister. Duff’s portrayal of Nona Grey has matured alongside the character, maintaining Nona’s fierce inner wolf while adding layers of tactical wisdom and profound grief. Her pacing during the colossal battlefield climaxes is breathtaking, delivering spell incantations and duel choreography with crystal-clear acoustic velocity. The intimate emotional beats land with devastating precision, making this fourteen-hour finale an acoustic masterpiece.
