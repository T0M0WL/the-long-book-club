About this Audiobook

Separated across Pelphia's glittering aristocratic salons and the brutal slave-tunnels of the Black Trail, Thomas Senlin and his crew close in on a brewing revolution around the mythical Hod King.

Over Twenty Hours of Secret Societies and Underground Revolution in Babel's Crown Jewel

Following the shocking fractures at the close of Arm of the Sphinx, the battered crew of the *State of Art* is scattered on perilous undercover assignments across Pelphia—one of the wealthiest, most corrupt ringdoms in the Tower of Babel. While Edith navigates royal airship pageants and Voleta infiltrates high-society courtships, Thomas Senlin adopts an alias to descend into the suffocating darkness of the Black Trail: the slave-quarries where rebellious laborers whisper of a mythical liberator called the Hod King.

Listening to *The Hod King* on audio captures *The Books of Babel* firing on every cylinder. Bancroft stretches past twenty hours to braid dazzling Steampunk espionage, claustrophobic Mystery, and razor-sharp Political Intrigue—all driven by heartbreaking loyalty between found family. Listeners browsing our Fantasy shelves will marvel at how a modest story about a lost honeymoon swells into full-scale revolutionary drama.

Clocking in at twenty hours and sixteen minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Hod King* stands as the monumental centrepiece of Bancroft's quartet.
