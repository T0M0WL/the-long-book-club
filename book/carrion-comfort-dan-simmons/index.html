About this Audiobook

They are "The Island"—a group of psychics who can control the minds of others to commit terrible acts. Saul Laski, an elderly holocaust survivor, has been hunting them for decades, and the game is finally coming to an end.

Dan Simmons’s Epic Thirty-Nine-Hour Geopolitical Thriller of Mind Control and Psychic Vampires in Audio

Behind the assassinations, political upheavals, and catastrophic conflicts of the twentieth century lurks a secret cabal of human predators who possess 'The Ability'—the telepathic power to hijack human minds and use ordinary people as disposable marionettes in a deadly global chess game. Led by Nazi camp survivor Saul Laski and courageous sheriff Bobby Joe Gentry, a desperate band of hunters traces this trail of psychic carnage from the crematoriums of Chelmno to the decadent celebrity estates of Hollywood and the violent streets of Philadelphia, risking their own sanity to expose and eliminate the aristocratic monsters who feed upon human violence. Joined by courageous psychiatrist Natalie Preston and French resistance veteran Father Joseph, the aging Saul battles the psychic attacks of the ruthless billionaire Quentin Herms and sociopathic producer Tony Harod, pursuing them across violent political rallies and blood-soaked private islands.

On audio, *Carrion Comfort* functions as a colossal, globe-trotting thriller that combines the high-stakes espionage of John le Carré with the supernatural dread of Stephen King. Narrators Mel Foster and Laural Merlington bring chilling psychological depth to the monstrous villains, voicing the cold, predatory detachment of Melanie Fuller and her wealthy co-conspirators with terrifying banality. Narrator Soundcheck: Mel Foster, Laural Merlington

Dual narrators Mel Foster and Laural Merlington deliver a compelling, well-balanced performance that commands Simmons's massive multi-decade narrative with ease. Foster handles the action-packed investigations and Saul Laski’s harrowing Holocaust memories with grounded, empathetic urgency. Merlington counters with a chilling, aristocratic performance as the elderly psychic predator Melanie Fuller, capturing her spiteful vanity and sadistic sociopathy with exquisite subtlety. Cleanly produced with pristine dynamic balance across forty mammoth hours, this audio production maintains sharp narrative tension from start to finish.
