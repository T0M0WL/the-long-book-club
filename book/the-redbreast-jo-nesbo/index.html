About this Audiobook

Demoted to Oslo surveillance after an embarrassing shooting incident, Harry Hole traces a rare smuggled sniper rifle straight back to Norwegian volunteers fighting on WWII's frozen Eastern Front—and a plot targeting Norway's royal family.

Over Fourteen Hours Igniting Nesbø's Epic Oslo Trilogy Through WWII Betrayal

Sidetracked into boring surveillance work in Oslo after embarrassing brass during a royal motorcade after Cockroaches, Harry Hole intercepts customs chatter about a devastatingly rare German-built Märklin sniper rifle smuggled into Norway. Investigating its neo-Nazi trail draws Harry backward to frozen 1940s Leningrad trenches where Norwegian volunteers fought alongside the SS—exposing buried wartime identities and launching the sweeping Oslo Trilogy alongside beloved partner Ellen Gjelten and love-interest Rakel.

Widely revered as the breakout leap of modern Nordic Noir, *The Redbreast* weaves aching WWII history directly into razor-sharp Police Procedural suspense. Every Crime puzzle and Detective clue ratchets toward an explosive Thriller climax.

Spanning fourteen hours and thirty-seven minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Redbreast* breaks your heart—launching Harry directly into Nemesis.
