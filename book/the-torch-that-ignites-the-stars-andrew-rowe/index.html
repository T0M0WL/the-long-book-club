Andrew Rowe Takes Corin Cadence Beyond the Academy on a 17-Hour Foreign Espionage Mission

In *The Torch That Ignites the Stars*, Corin Cadence and his inner circle break free from the classroom walls of Lorian Heights to embark on a hazardous international expedition to the industrial nation of Caelford. Seeking advanced enchanting tutors and foreign master-crafters, Corin quickly discovers that Caelfordian politics are every bit as treacherous as the spires. When an escort mission aboard a luxury train turns into a high-speed magical ambush, the team is forced to rely on unconventional tactics, experimental items, and newly mastered mana types to outsmart elite foreign assassins.

In audio format, this seventeen-hour road-trip adventure brings a refreshing change of scenery and pacing to the *Arcane Ascension* saga. Nick Podehl shines in his portrayals of international dialects and pompous Caelfordian aristocrats, delivering an exhilarating performance during train-top spell duels and clandestine laboratory heists. Paired in our library with fast-paced fantasy capers like Foundryside and Promise of Blood, this recording showcases Rowe’s talent for creative tactical combat.

Running over seventeen hours for a single token, this third volume offers tremendous entertainment value. Rowe treats travel not as dead time, but as an opportunity for character growth, philosophical debate, and ingenious magical experimentation. Exchanging a monthly credit for this installment guarantees high-energy audio fun from start to finish.
