A Rousing Nautical LitRPG Expedition Overflowing with Swashbuckling Shenanigans and Irrepressible Humor

With *Lunatic’s Landing*, author Kyle Kirrin charts an audacious new course for *The Ripple System*, plunging Ned, Frank, and their battle-hardened crew into the uncharted, storm-ravaged archipelago of the Sunken Expanse. Hunting for legendary maritime artifacts and ancient class evolutions, the party must navigate treacherous nautical environments, rogue pirate guilds, and titanic deep-sea horrors. What begins as a daring seafaring expedition quickly spirals into riotous chaos as Frank confronts his ultimate psychological nightmare: infinite oceanic horizons constantly reflecting the mocking face of the night sky’s moon.

Listening to *Lunatic’s Landing* on audio is an absolute treat from the opening broadside to the final harbor clash, fueled by Travis Baldree’s infectious voice acting and rollicking character portrayals. Baldree embraces the swashbuckling nautical setting with relish, delivering grizzled buccaneer brogues, frantic shipboard combat commands, and Frank’s indignantly hysterical maritime rants with impeccable comic timing.
