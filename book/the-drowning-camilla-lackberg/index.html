About this Audiobook

Helping shy local teacher Christian Thydell celebrate his acclaimed debut novel, Erica uncovers menacing anonymous death threats stalking four childhood friends just as one vanishes and turns up encased in coastal ice.

Over Fifteen Hours Probing Menacing Death Threats Around Four Friends

Fresh off surviving historical trauma in The Hidden Child, writer Erica Falck mentors shy Fjällbacka schoolteacher Christian Thydell as his atmospheric debut novel *The Mermaid* hits Swedish bookshelves to rave reviews. Yet Christian barely cracks a smile—secretly confessing to Erica that someone has been stalking both him and his closest boyhood friends with vicious anonymous threats just before one friend disappears into winter sea ice.

Listening to *The Drowning* wraps intimate literary paranoia around classic Nordic Noir. Patrik's methodical Police Procedural legwork chases tangled hometown secrets as every veiled warning explodes into desperate Crime and haunting Detective Mystery.

Running fifteen hours and twenty-eight minutes for one credit, *The Drowning* closes Eamonn Riley's UK narration stint on a fiercely surprising final chapter right before The Lost Boy.
