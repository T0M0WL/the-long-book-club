A Dark and Haunting Romantasy Epic of War, Alchemy, and Lost Memories

SenLinYu’s viral dark fantasy sensation makes its monumental debut in audio format, delivering a harrowing, emotionally devastating epic of wartime survival, dark magic, and fractured identity. In a realm ravaged by years of catastrophic conflict and alchemical horrors, a healer named Helena is captured by enemy forces and imprisoned in the desolate fortress of the ruthless High Commander, Julian. Stripped of her memories by ancient mental wards to protect the secrets of her fallen rebellion, Helena must navigate a brutal world of political intrigue, necromantic alchemy, and lethal secrets, only to discover that her cruel captor holds the key to the forgotten past she desperately fears remembering, forging a desperate battle for emotional and physical survival.
