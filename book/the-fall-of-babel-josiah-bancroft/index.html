About this Audiobook

As civil war erupts across every ringdom and the Tower itself begins to fracture, Thomas Senlin and the fractured crew of the State of Art wage a final battle against the Sphinx for Babel's soul.

Twenty-Five Hours of Airship Battles and Grand Reckoning in Bancroft's Babel Finale

The Tower of Babel is burning from within. With revolution tearing through the ringdoms and Marat's dread airship armada converging on the upper heavens, Thomas Senlin and his separated companions must finally confront the truth about the Sphinx's grand design, the mysterious Bricklayer, and what truly awaits at the pinnacle of human ambition. Old alliances fracture under siege, long-buried grief surfaces, and every promise made way back on the dusty base stairs in Senlin Ascends comes due.

On audio, *The Fall of Babel* rewards every listener who climbed beside Thomas Senlin across nearly seventy-five total series hours. Blending sweeping Epic Fantasy sky-battles with literary Steampunk invention and tender Adventure warmth, Josiah Bancroft closes his quartet with audacity—refusing cheap fairy-tale shortcuts while giving his beloved cast earned grace.

At twenty-four hours and fifty-three minutes for a single credit, *The Fall of Babel* is a towering marathon finale that cements *The Books of Babel* among modern fantasy's most imaginative complete audio cycles.
