About this Audiobook

With liquid seas shimmering across a newly liberated Mars, the surviving pioneers and their descendants must forge a lasting cooperative constitution while navigating crisis on Earth and expansion across the solar system.

Nearly Thirty-Two Triumphant Hours Completing Science Fiction's Grandest Ecological Saga

Crowning the Hugo Award–winning *Mars Trilogy*, *Blue Mars* opens onto a breathtaking world transformed: liquid oceans lap against newly carved northern shorelines, breathable rain falls across terraced highlands, and a newly independent Martian population faces the hardest challenge of all—not winning a revolution, but building a humane, durable society afterward. Across decades of constitutional conventions, interplanetary diplomacy with a flooded Earth, and human voyages outward to the moons of Jupiter and Saturn, Kim Stanley Robinson brings his century-spanning experiment in ecological civilization to an unforgettable close.

In its colossal thirty-two-hour audio edition, *Blue Mars* reads like a great nineteenth-century Russian epic transplanted across the solar system. Where Red Mars pulsed with frontier friction and Green Mars crackled with underground insurgency, this finale balances sweeping solar-system grandeur with quiet, aching meditations on longevity, memory, and what it means to belong to a living landscape. For fans of our Solarpunk and Space Exploration shelves—alongside expansive deep-time journeys like The Terraformers and Children of Time—there is nothing else quite like it.

Offering nearly thirty-two hours of luminous, deeply thoughtful storytelling for one Audible credit, *Blue Mars* delivers an emotionally resonant, hard-won payoff to over eighty hours of unforgettable planetary listening.
