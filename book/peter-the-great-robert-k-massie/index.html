About this Audiobook

Against the monumental canvas of seventeenth- and eighteenth-century Europe and Russia unfolds the magnificent story of Peter the Great.

Robert K. Massie\u2019s Pulitzer-Winning Thirty-Five-Hour Epic of Tsarist Russia\u2019s Transformation in Audio

Winning the Pulitzer Prize for Biography, Robert K. Massie\u2019s *Peter the Great: His Life and World* is a breathtaking, monumental chronicle of the titanic Russian tsar who dragged a medieval, landlocked empire into the modern European world through sheer force of will and relentless energy. Standing six feet seven inches tall, Peter was an elemental force of nature who learned shipbuilding as a common carpenter in Dutch shipyards, waged twenty-one years of brutal warfare against Sweden's boy-king Charles XII, built the imperial city of St. Petersburg upon desolate Baltic swamps, and mercilessly crushed court conspiracies\u2014including ordering the interrogation and execution of his own rebellious son, Tsarevich Alexis.

On audio, Massie\u2019s sweeping historical canvas unfolds with the propulsive grandeur of a Russian epic novel and the meticulous precision of fine historical scholarship. Celebrated British narrator Frederick Davidson brings his signature aristocratic irony, theatrical flair, and formidable vocal presence to the thirty-five-hour production, effortlessly orchestrating grand naval battles, bloody palace coups, and colorful European embassies across a transformed continent. Featured prominently in our Biography and History libraries alongside Massie's companion masterpiece Catherine the Great, this recording transforms Russian history into an exhilarating auditory spectacle that holds you spellbound through every royal intrigue and battlefield clash.

Delivering over thirty-five hours of magnificent historical drama for a single monthly credit, this release represents unmatched listening value for lovers of epic European history. Massie captures both the enlightened modernization and the horrific human cost of Peter\u2019s autocratic ambitions, presenting a nuanced, unforgettable psychological portrait of an empire's founder and the birth of modern Russia. Exchanging a monthly credit for this classic masterpiece secures weeks of transportive historical immersion, proving why Massie remains the undisputed gold standard of Russian biographical literature in audio form, ensuring an unforgettable, deeply atmospheric listening journey through the formation of a sprawling modern Eurasian empire.
