About this Audiobook

Crippled and grieving, Hadrian rallies the human frontier against the Cielcin's final push toward the holy seat of Earth.

From the Ashes: Samuel Roukin Narrates a Blistering Tale of Vengeance and Defense

In *Ashes of Man*, Christopher Ruocchio delivers the furious, cathartic counterpunch to the agonies of *Kingdoms of Death*. Physically broken, missing limbs, and carrying the suffocating grief of devastating personal loss, Hadrian Marlowe crawls back to the Sollan Empire only to discover that the Cielcin armada is marching straight toward humanity’s ancient cradle: Old Earth itself. With the imperial court paralyzed by fear and betrayal, Hadrian must assemble an army of broken veterans, frontier levies, and loyal allies to stand as the shield of mankind.

Samuel Roukin’s vocal performance is nothing short of heroic. He channels Hadrian’s grief into cold, crystalline determination; every speech delivered to his troops feels charged with moral authority and deep tragic weight. The planetary siege sequences and void engagements showcase Ruocchio at the height of his military storytelling powers. Listeners who loved the desperate tactical stands in Rhythm of War or Morning Star will find themselves gripped by the narrative urgency.

At over twenty-two hours for a single credit, *Ashes of Man* provides immense narrative payoff. It delivers thunderous action and breathtaking emotional closures, setting up the cosmic endgame of the series with unforgettable power.
