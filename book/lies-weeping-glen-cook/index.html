About this Audiobook

Picking up after Soldiers Live to launch Glen Cook's A Pitiless Rain continuation, Lies Weeping shifts voices across three surviving storytellers confronting buried sins, perilous new enemies, and an evolving post-war Company.

Over Fourteen Hours Launching Glen Cook's Brand-New Post-Soldiers Live Sequel Arc

Arriving twenty-five years after the apparent closing curtain of Soldiers Live—with its dedicated UK Audible edition open for pre-order ahead of February 11, 2027—*Lies Weeping* opens the brand-new *A Pitiless Rain* cycle inside Glen Cook's enduring mercenary saga. Moving outward from Croaker's desk to alternate between three sharp younger narrators carrying the Company's hard-won banner, fresh political traps, lingering shadows from Port of Shadows, and ominous external enemies converge to test what survives when legends refuse to retire.

True to Grimdark and Military Fantasy tradition forged back in The Black Company, *Lies Weeping* trades bloated nostalgia for tight Low Fantasy cunning, ruthless Dark Fantasy conspiracies, and sharp-edged Sword and Sorcery intrigue.

Spanning fourteen hours and twenty-four minutes for one Audible pre-order credit, *Lies Weeping* promises an electrifying next chapter for longtime listeners tracking every mile of the annals.
