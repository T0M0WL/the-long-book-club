About this Audiobook

Valentine Michael Smith is a human being raised on Mars, newly arrived on Earth.

Robert A. Heinlein\u2019s Legendary Sixteen-Hour Sci-Fi Counterculture Masterpiece in Audio

Born on the red planet to human astronauts and raised from infancy by enigmatic, telepathic Martians following the tragic demise of the first expedition, Valentine Michael Smith returns to Earth as a young adult in Robert A. Heinlein\u2019s revolutionary science fiction landmark, *Stranger in a Strange Land*. Possessing godlike psychic abilities, superhuman empathy, and a complete incomprehension of earthly concepts like jealousy, clothing, religion, and money, 'the Man from Mars' becomes the ultimate outsider. Rescued from government confinement by cynical journalist Ben Caxton and charismatic libertarian lawyer Jubal Harshaw, Mike embarks on an extraordinary cultural and spiritual crusade, founding a revolutionary water-brotherhood church based on Martian communion and the philosophy to 'grok' all that is.

On audio, Heinlein\u2019s Hugo Award-winning counterculture epic unfolds as a provocative, hilarious, and intellectually stimulating spoken-word manifesto that challenged the moral boundaries of 1960s America. Narrator Christopher Hurt commands the sixteen-hour narrative with splendid mid-century elegance, infusing Jubal Harshaw\u2019s bombastic, cynical monologues with razor-sharp comedic timing and portraying Mike\u2019s innocent, serene wisdom with captivating vocal warmth. Featured prominently in our Sci-Fi and Classics collections alongside monumental philosophical classics like Dune, this recording turns theological debates, free love, and political satire into an intoxicating auditory journey.

Heinlein fearlessly interrogates human social hypocrisy, organized religion, sexual possessiveness, and government tyranny, delivering an unforgettable plea for radical empathy and spiritual liberation.
