About this Audiobook

In a fractured post-election Britain, Jackson Lamb's slow horses uncover a high-level treason conspiracy that strikes at the core of Regent's Park.

Political Circus, High Treason, and Jackson Lamb's Defining Hour

Spanning over twelve rich, enthralling hours, *Clown Town* marks the triumphant ninth full-length entry in Mick Herron’s globally celebrated Slough House saga. With Britain navigating political turmoil, institutional decay, and foreign disinformation campaigns, the intelligence community in London has descended into an utter circus—a 'clown town' where ambitious politicians, private security moguls, and media oligarchs jockey for supremacy while ignoring national security. But when a routine surveillance operation conducted by the slow horses accidentally uncovers a catastrophic breach within Regent’s Park itself, Jackson Lamb realizes that high treason has compromised the very highest levels of Whitehall.

As old alliances fracture and fresh assassins stalk the rain-drenched streets of the capital, River Cartwright, Catherine Standish, and Louisa Guy are pushed to their physical and psychological limits. Lamb must deploy every ounce of his formidable tradecraft, relying on razor-sharp instincts honed during the Cold War to outmaneuver bureaucratic enemies who will stop at nothing to cover up their crimes. Herron’s plotting is as intricate as ever, delivering sudden twists, heartbreaking revelations, and the brand of sardonic British dialogue that has made the series a worldwide cultural phenomenon.

Listening to *Clown Town* in audio format is an absolute luxury. Sean Barrett’s iconic voice has become so synonymous with Jackson Lamb that it is impossible to imagine the character without his world-weary rasp and impeccable comic delivery. Clocking in at over twelve hours, this audiobook provides immense credit value for espionage connoisseurs, offering the perfect blend of black humor, pulse-pounding action, and melancholic humanity. It stands as a crown jewel in contemporary crime fiction.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sean Barrett

Sean Barrett’s narration of *Clown Town* is a triumphant masterclass. Over twelve hours, Barrett’s vocal stamina and artistic control are astonishing, effortlessly shifting from Lamb’s rasping, dry-witted put-downs to high-tension shootouts and quiet moments of tragic introspection. He gives each member of Slough House an unmistakable identity, imbuing their banter with emotional depth and comic flair. The sound engineering and pacing are flawless, delivering an immersive listening experience that stands among the very best in contemporary audio fiction.
