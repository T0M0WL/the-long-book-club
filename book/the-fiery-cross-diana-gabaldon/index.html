About this Audiobook

In 1771 North Carolina, Jamie is ordered by the British Crown to raise a militia against the Regulators as the American Revolution looms over Fraser's Ridge.

The Great Gathering, the Battle of Alamance, and the Immense Crucible of Fraser's Ridge

*The Fiery Cross* spans fifty-five and a half hours, offering an unmatched masterclass in deep historical immersion. Set in 1771 colonial North Carolina, the novel begins with the legendary 'Great Gathering' at Mount Helicon—a multi-day Scottish highland assembly where clan oaths are renewed beneath the shadow of rebellion. Jamie Fraser lights the fiery cross, calling upon his mountain tenants to muster in defense of the colony, while knowing that the American Revolution is less than five years away.

Jamie is placed in an agonizing dilemma: appointed colonel of a royal militia, he is tasked with hunting down the 'Regulators'—frontier farmers protesting against corrupt tax collectors and unfair English courts. As tensions culminate in the historic Battle of Alamance, Jamie must fulfill his oath to the British Crown without destroying the people he loves. Meanwhile, on Fraser's Ridge, Claire performs battlefield surgeries and concocts penicillin, while family bonds deepen into the bedrock of the series.

Narrator Davina Porter performs a true acoustic miracle across fifty-five hours. Porter sustains an unhurried, hypnotic cadence that makes every medical consultation, mountain hunt, and campfire conversation utterly mesmerizing. Her portrayal of Jamie's burgeoning authority and Claire's compassionate wisdom anchors this gargantuan narrative with warm fidelity. Alongside mammoth audio epics like The Wheel of Time and The Pillars of the Earth, *The Fiery Cross* is the ultimate investment for lovers of Historical Fiction.
