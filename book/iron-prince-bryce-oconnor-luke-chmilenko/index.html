About this Audiobook

Born with a crippling genetic disease in a futuristic society locked in an interstellar war, Reidon Ward receives a biologically evolving CAD device that boasts an unprecedented, broken Growth stat.

A Colossal Sci-Fi Progression Tournament Epic of Underdog Grit and Neural Mecha Combat

Set in a far-future interstellar society where humanity wages a desperate war against an enigmatic alien threat known as the Archons, eighteen-year-old Rei Hidaka dreams of becoming an elite military brawler despite suffering from a debilitating chronic bone disease. When he is tested for a CAD (Combat Assistance Device)—a sentient bio-mechanical device that bonds with the user's nervous system to manifest modular energy armor and weapons—his starting physical combat parameters are ranked at the lowest possible tier: Rank F0. However, the military AI assigns Rei a growth parameter never before witnessed in human history: Rank S. Authors Bryce O'Connor and Luke Chmilenko construct an electrifying underdog military academy tournament epic that channels the relentless training montages of *My Hero Academia* and *Ender's Game* into a massive, heart-pounding sci-fi tournament.

On audio, the clashing of CAD energy blades, the roaring crowds of the Galens Institute dueling arenas, and Rei's grueling private sparring sessions explode with pulse-pounding theatrical adrenaline. The auditory contrast between Rei’s quiet, agonizing physical therapy sessions and the thunderous sonic booms of high-tier bio-mechanical gladiators creates an emotionally charged listening experience that grips the listener from the opening combat simulator to the thrilling final tournament duel.
