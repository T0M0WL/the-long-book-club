About this Audiobook

In 1773–1776, as the American Revolution erupts and backcountry violence surges, Claire and Jamie Fraser fight to protect their family and survive a prophesied fatal fire.

The Boston Tea Party, Backcountry Vigilantes, and the Looming Shadow of the Revolution

Clocking in at an astounding fifty-seven hours and forty-eight minutes, *A Breath of Snow and Ashes* is the dramatic pinnacle of Diana Gabaldon’s *Outlander* series. Set between 1773 and 1776 on the brink of the American Revolutionary War, the fragile frontier tranquility of Fraser’s Ridge disintegrates. In the cities, the Boston Tea Party ignites rebellion, while in the backcountry, law and order dissolves into brutal vigilantism as neighbor turns against neighbor. Jamie Fraser is forced to abandon his royal commission as Indian Agent, declaring his allegiance to the Continental cause.

Yet perils arise from within. Malva Christie accuses Jamie of fathering her child, sparking tragedies including Malva’s murder and Claire’s trial for witchcraft in New Bern. Meanwhile, Claire and Jamie must reckon with the dreaded 1776 newspaper obituary brought back from the future, which prophesies their fiery deaths in a house fire. Gabaldon orchestrates these storylines with power, blending frontier survival, medicine, and marital fidelity.

Narrator Davina Porter delivers what is widely recognized as the finest vocal achievement of her illustrious career. Porter handles the harrowing emotional gauntlet of this fifty-eight-hour behemoth—from courtroom drama to tender domestic humor—with unmatched dignity and nuance. Her characterizations of Jamie, Claire, Roger, Brianna, and young Ian remain impeccably authentic. For connoisseurs seeking the summit of Historical Fiction on a single credit, *A Breath of Snow and Ashes* is an unmissable masterpiece.
