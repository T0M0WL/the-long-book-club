Buried

Author: Jussi Adler-Olsen | Narrator: Steven Pacey

Runtime: 17h 32m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Mystery, Crime, Suspense, Police Procedural, Detective

About this Audiobook

Running for his life through Copenhagen after stumbling onto a murdered Danish official's passport in the clutches of his ruthless pickpocket clan leader, teenage outsider Marco becomes Department Q's urgent shield against systemic aid-money fraud.

Over Seventeen Hours Racing Criminal Clans and Aid-Money Corruption Around Copenhagen

Changing pace sharply after Guilt, *Buried* (published in North America as *The Marco Effect*) introduces fifteen-year-old Marco—a sharp, multilingual boy trapped under the thumb of his despotic Uncle Zola, who forces homeless youths across Denmark to beg, steal, and surrender their earnings. Discovering the buried corpse and diplomatic passport of a missing civil servant inside Zola's compound, Marco flees across Copenhagen while corrupt foreign-aid officials and cold-blooded gangsters alike race to silence him before Carl Mørck's basement team tracks him down.

In audio form, setting a sympathetic young fugitive on the run reinvigorates Adler-Olsen's Nordic Noir and Police Procedural cadence with furious street-level Suspense. Linking cynical international banking embezzlement directly to exploited urban streets creates gripping Crime urgency, letting Carl's Detective persistence untangle an explosive financial Mystery.

Clocking in at seventeen hours and thirty-two minutes for one credit, *Buried* delivers propulsive urban chase thrills alongside welcome flashes of humor before The Hanging Girl.

Narrator Soundcheck: Steven Pacey

Steven Pacey imbues fifteen-year-old Marco with disarming vulnerability and nimble street smarts across seventeen and a half crisp hours, contrasting his youthful desperation cleanly against Zola's menacing growl, boardroom financiers' slick poise, and Department Q's familiar cellar banter.
