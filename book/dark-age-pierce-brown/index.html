About this Audiobook

The Solar Republic faces total catastrophe as Darrow's forces are besieged on Mercury while bloody coups tear Mars and Luna apart.

The Masterpiece of Modern Grimdark Sci-Fi: A 34-Hour Planetary Meat-Grinder

*Dark Age* is rightly celebrated as the terrifying summit of Pierce Brown's universe—a relentless, thirty-four-hour masterpiece of military grimdark science fiction that pushes every character to the brink of annihilation. Trapped on the radioactive surface of Mercury cut off from all reinforcements, Darrow wages a desperate defense against the vast, converging armadas of the Society Remnant. Meanwhile, on Luna, Sovereign Virginia au Augustus battles ruthless internal oligarchs and religious cults, while on the road to the rim, Ephraim and Lyria find themselves embroiled in savage tribal civil wars.

The audio production is a staggering achievement. Moira Quirk joins the ensemble to voice Mustang, delivering an unforgettable portrait of razor-sharp intellect, statesmanship, and agonizing maternal terror. Tim Gerard Reynolds narrates Darrow’s desperate war journal amidst the radioactive dust storms of Mercury with incandescent intensity. The scale of devastation—EMP storms, chemical warfare, orbital bombardment, and psychological terror—is conveyed with harrowing sonic power. Listeners captivated by the bleak military brilliance of The Poppy War or The Blade Itself will find this listening experience unmatched.

At nearly thirty-four hours for a single credit, *Dark Age* is one of the highest-value investments in the entire Audible catalog. It is harrowing, brutally honest about the costs of war, and breathtaking in its literary ambition, providing a relentless audio marathon that listeners will talk about for years.

Narrator Soundcheck: Tim Gerard Reynolds, John Lee, Moira Quirk, James Langton, Rendah Heywood

The ensemble narration in *Dark Age* represents the gold standard of multi-POV audio production. Moira Quirk's performance as Mustang is an absolute revelation, conveying fierce royal dignity and heartbreaking vulnerability. Tim Gerard Reynolds pushes his voice to volcanic extremes during Mercury's apocalyptic battles, while James Langton's Lysander and John Lee's Ephraim provide exceptional textural balance.
