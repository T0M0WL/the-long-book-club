About this Audiobook

Darrow escalates his rebellion into the highest echelons of Gold society, sparking an interplanetary civil war filled with devastating betrayal.

The Gold Standard of Sci-Fi Sequels: An Electrifying, 19-Hour Masterclass in Space Opera

If *Red Rising* was a razor-sharp blend of Roman gladiatorial games and dystopian revolution, *Golden Son* detonates the premise across the entire solar system. Stepping out from the martial academy of the Institute into the decadent, cutthroat courts of the Sovereign, Darrow of Lykos must navigate high-stakes political intrigue, brutal aristocratic feuds, and full-scale planetary invasions. From the pulse-pounding gala duel where Darrow upends the Gold social order to the apocalyptic chaos of an orbital Iron Rain descending upon Mars, this sequel never lets up on dramatic tension.

On audio, narrator Tim Gerard Reynolds gives what many fans and critics consider one of the single greatest voice performances in contemporary science fiction. Reynolds captures Darrow's inner turmoil—torn between the ruthless persona of a peerless Gold warlord and the bleeding heart of an enslaved Red miner—with haunting poetic eloquence. His vocal timing during action sequences creates an auditory velocity that makes nineteen hours vanish in what feels like an afternoon. Alongside kindred grimdark landmarks like The Blade Itself and space operas like Empire of Silence, this is an essential audio monument.

Clocking in at over nineteen hours for a single Audible credit, *Golden Son* delivers astronomical storytelling return. It culminates in one of the most shocking, heartbreaking climax sequences in modern literature, leaving listeners emotionally winded and immediately reaching for the next volume.

Narrator Soundcheck: Tim Gerard Reynolds

Tim Gerard Reynolds delivers an auditory tour de force that cements his status as an audio legend. His rhythmic Irish brogue lends Darrow's interior monologues Homeric weight, while his character voices—from Sevro's foul-mouthed feral rasp to the Jackal's chillingly polite sociopathy—are instantly iconic. Recording fidelity is immaculate, handling dynamic shifts between quiet grief and deafening space fleet bombardments effortlessly.
