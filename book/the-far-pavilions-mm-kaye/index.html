About this Audiobook

Spanning decades of nineteenth-century British India from Himalayan foothills to royal Rajput palaces, M.M. Kaye's sweeping historical epic follows an orphaned Englishman raised as a Hindu boy, torn between two worlds, forbidden love, and frontier wars.

Nearly Forty-Nine Hours of Himalayan Adventure, Palace Intrigue, and Forbidden Love

For listeners craving the sweeping, Technicolor scale of classic Historical Fiction, M.M. Kaye's *The Far Pavilions* is an unapologetic, richly textured marathon. Born to British parents in nineteenth-century India yet orphaned and raised as a Hindu boy named Ashok in the shadow of the Himalayas, Ashton Pelham-Martyn grows up straddling two irreconcilable cultures—summoned back to England for an officer's commission before returning to the Raj as a cavalryman, spy, and protector caught between royal Rajput courts and the bloody Afghan frontier.

Listening to *The Far Pavilions* in unabridged audio lets Kaye's sensory world-building unfold like an epic cinema serial. From sun-baked fortresses and monsoon caravans to whispered harem conspiracies and doomed cavalry charges, the pacing rewards patient immersion. Fans of sprawling historical canvas like Shantaram or War and Peace will revel in its sheer narrative ambition and romantic sweep.

Clocking in at forty-eight hours and forty-five minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Far Pavilions* delivers weeks of lush, escapist Adventure value.

Narrator Soundcheck: Vikas Adam

His warm pacing and natural command of subcontinental names and cadences keep even the most intricate historical set pieces vivid, engaging, and completely free of marathon fatigue.
