About this Audiobook

Fourteen years after a terrified boy wearing deer antlers stepped onto a frozen Norwegian road, three apparently unrelated victims are staged with a camera pointing at each corpse—pulling Munch and Krüger into a twisted game.

Ten Hours of Randomised Oslo Killings and Haunting Winter Highway Secrets

On a snow-choked Norwegian backroad in the winter of 1996, an elderly driver slams his brakes when his headlights catch what looks like a deer—only to lock eyes with a terrified young boy wearing real antlers strapped firmly to his head. Fourteen years later, a corpse is pulled from an icy mountain lake, followed within weeks by three seemingly random murders bearing cryptic numbered clues and a tripod camera aimed directly at each victim.

Listening to *The Boy in the Headlights* cranks the Detective puzzlecraft of Nordic Noir into razor-sharp Psychological Thriller overdrive. Racing through bitter winter frost alongside Holger Munch and Mia Krüger inside our Nordic Noir & Scandi Crime collection, every false trail forces the unit to confront buried wounds from their own pasts while solving a chilling Mystery with no obvious motive.

At ten hours and thirteen minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Boy in the Headlights* delivers pure nocturnal paranoia—best experienced through dark winter walks and headphones.
