About this Audiobook

Life can turn on a dime—or miss it, but what if you could change it all?

Stephen King’s Thirty-Hour Masterpiece of Time Travel, Mid-Century America, and JFK in Audio

Jake Epping, a disillusioned Maine high school English teacher, discovers an uncanny temporal portal hidden in the pantry of Al's Diner that always deposits him into the autumn of 1958. Tasked by his dying friend with a seemingly righteous mission—preventing the tragic assassination of President John F. Kennedy—Jake steps into a vanished mid-century America. Assuming the identity of George Amberson, he establishes a new life in the idyllic small town of Jodie, Texas, falling deeply in love with librarian Sadie Dunhill while tracking the mercurial, troubled Lee Harvey Oswald across five agonizing years of historical reconnaissance. Confronting the malevolent nature of time itself—an obstinate force that violently pushes back against any attempt to alter recorded events—Jake must make impossible moral sacrifices, balancing his love for Sadie against the ticking countdown toward November 22, 1963 in Dealey Plaza.

On audio, *11/22/63* functions as a hypnotic, nostalgia-soaked time machine where the soundscape of late-1950s America wraps around you like a warm embrace. Craig Wasson’s magnificent performance captures the physical reality of the era—the sizzle of diner burgers, the crackle of vintage radio broadcasts, the sharp bite of root beer, and the creeping dread of the Past pushing back with malevolent force. Positioned as a premier highlight in our Historical Fiction and Horror libraries alongside King classics like It and Under the Dome, this thirty-hour production turns historical speculation into an intimate, heart-wrenching auditory drama that completely captivates the imagination.

Clocking in at nearly thirty-one hours of masterclass narrative velocity for a single credit, this release delivers supreme long-term listening value. King crafts what is arguably the greatest emotional love story of his entire literary career, balancing tight espionage suspense against a profound meditation on fate, unintended consequences, and the butterfly effect. Exchanging a monthly credit for this monumental audiobook guarantees weeks of unforgettable headphone companionship, rewarding listeners with a poignant, tear-jerking climax that lingers in the heart forever.

Narrator Soundcheck: Craig Wasson

Craig Wasson delivers a tour-de-force vocal performance that ranks among the finest interpretations in the entire Stephen King audio canon. Wasson brings a natural, lived-in mid-century warmth to Jake Epping, capturing his transition from cynical modern observer to a man deeply in love with a bygone era. His portrayals of Sadie Dunhill and the twitchy, paranoid Lee Harvey Oswald are remarkably distinct and emotionally authentic. With immaculate pacing and crisp, balanced studio engineering across thirty-one hours, this recording is an absolute audio masterpiece.
