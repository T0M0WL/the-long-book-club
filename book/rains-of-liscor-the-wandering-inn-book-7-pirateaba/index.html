About this Audiobook

Torrential monsoon rains flood the plains of Liscor, unlocking dangerous subterranean crypts and putting Erin's magical door to the ultimate test.

An Atmospheric Flood-Season Odyssey of Underground Horrors, Magical Door Heists, and Resilient Community

In *Rains of Liscor*, the relentless seasonal monsoon transforms the floodplains surrounding Liscor into a vast, muddy lake, cutting off ordinary travel and forcing the diverse residents of the region into close quarters. Inside the Wandering Inn, Erin Solstice experiments with an enchanted magical door that allows instantaneous travel between Liscor, Celum, and distant cities, turning her tavern into a bustling intercontinental transport hub. But as the waters rise, terrifying subterranean horrors awaken from the unexplored depths beneath the city, threatening to submerge both the inn and Liscor in ancient malice.

Listening to *Rains of Liscor* on audio immerses you in the rhythmic patter of falling rain and the cozy, warm sanctuary of a magical tavern. Narrator Andrea Parsneau showcases her breathtaking range, transitioning effortlessly from the slapstick comedy of magical door mishaps to nail-biting dungeon crawl suspense. Her delivery of the eccentric Antinium workers—especially Bird’s relentless obsession with shooting flying birds through the rain—provides delightful humor that balances the story's darker mysteries. Featured prominently in our Cozy Fantasy and Epic Fantasy catalogs, this recording is pure auditory escapism.
