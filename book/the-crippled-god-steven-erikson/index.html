About this Audiobook

In the monumental forty-five-hour conclusion to Malazan Book of the Fallen, broken legions crawl across the Glass Desert for one last compassionate stand beneath the Spire—defying gods and Elder Forkrul Assail alike.

Forty-Five Hours Closing the Colossal Malazan Saga in Compassion and Defiance

Rising from the ashes of Dust of Dreams, the shattered remnants of Adjunct Tavore's Bonehunters stagger across the blistering Glass Desert—no water, no mercy, and no certainty—to stage one defiant battle against the fanatical Forkrul Assail holding the Crippled God chained beneath the Spire. Far across the seas, the Shake guard First Shore at Lightfall, lost heroes return at the brink of doom, and every thread planted back in Gardens of the Moon weaves into one breathtaking tapestry.

Listening to *The Crippled God* crowns nearly four hundred hours of unmatched Epic Fantasy, High Fantasy, and Military Fantasy achievement. Rather than cynically celebrating cruelty, Erikson concludes his Grimdark masterwork on a soaring hymn to unwitnessed courage and ordinary human compassion.

Spanning forty-five hours and twenty minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Crippled God* is a monumental, tear-streaked achievement worthy of our Grimdark and Epic Fantasy shelves.
