The Witch’s Heart

Author: Genevieve Gornichec | Narrator: Jayne Entwistle

Runtime: 11h 40m | Genre: Mythology, Fantasy, Historical Fiction, Folklore, Retelling, Witches

About this Audiobook

The ancient Norse giantess Angrboda flees Odin’s wrath into the Ironwood, where she loves the trickster Loki and fiercely protects her prophesied, world-ending children.

A Haunting, Lyrical Reclamation of Norse Myth’s Mother of Monsters

In traditional Norse mythology and the Icelandic Eddas, the giantess Angrboda is treated as little more than a shadowy, monstrous footnote—the sinister mate of the trickster Loki whose monstrous offspring bring about the catastrophic ruin of Asgard at Ragnarok. In *The Witch’s Heart*, Genevieve Gornichec pulls this maligned figure directly into the glowing center of the cosmos, constructing a deeply poignant, atmospheric domestic epic set against the primeval wilderness of the Nine Realms. Angrboda’s story is not a chronicle of malice, but an unforgettable tale of fierce maternal protection in an uncaring, hostile universe ruled by warlike Aesir gods who crush anything they cannot control.

The emotional brilliance of the novel lies in the tender, quiet domestic life Angrboda painstakingly carves out in the remote, mist-shrouded Ironwood alongside Loki and the fierce huntress giantess Skadi. The depiction of their three children—the colossal wolf Fenrir, the great sea serpent Jormungandr, and Hel, the half-living, half-corpse child who rules the underworld—is handled with extraordinary warmth and empathy. Rather than grotesque beasts destined for slaughter, they are curious, loving children who are cruelly hunted, chained, and banished by the paranoid, power-hungry Aesir gods led by Odin. Angrboda’s daily struggle to shield her brood while bearing the crushing burden of prophetic foresight creates exquisite narrative tension that builds inexorably toward tragedy, challenging the very notion of what makes a monster and what makes a hero.

For listeners seeking a refreshing departure from Mediterranean pantheons, *The Witch’s Heart* provides an enchanting excursion into Scandinavian Folklore and epic Fantasy. Jayne Entwistle’s evocative, quiet performance lends the eleven-hour audiobook a deeply authentic fireside saga quality that captures the icy majesty of the northern woods and the fragile warmth of a hidden hearth. It stands as a profound testament to a mother’s enduring devotion against insurmountable cosmic odds, delivering exceptional emotional depth, rich mythological atmosphere, and lasting credit value for any serious fantasy collection.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jayne Entwistle

Jayne Entwistle’s lyrical, melancholic British delivery is the ideal vocal vehicle for Genevieve Gornichec’s atmospheric Norse fantasy. Entwistle infuses Angrboda with an ancient, bone-weary stoicism that slowly blooms into fierce maternal warmth. Her voicing of Loki is playful, unpredictable, and charismatic, while her portrayal of the Allfather Odin carries a chilling, fanatical menace. The narration feels like an ancient fireside saga recounted in the deep winter wilderness.
