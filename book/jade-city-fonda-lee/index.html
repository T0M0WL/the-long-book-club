Fonda Lee's 19-Hour World Fantasy Award-Winning Crime Epic of Jade Magic, Martial Honor, and Family

Weighing in at over nineteen gripping hours, *Jade City* by Fonda Lee is the World Fantasy Award-winning opener to the celebrated Green Bone Saga—a breathtaking urban fantasy epic famously described as *The Godfather* meets *Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon*. On the tropical island of Kekon, rare bio-magical jade grants superhuman strength, perception, and agility only to trained Green Bone warriors. For generations, the Kaul family’s No Peak clan and their bitter rivals, the Mountain clan, have coexisted in a tense criminal truce. But as modernization, foreign black markets, and addictive jade-doping drugs infiltrate Janloon, an audacious assassination attempt plunges the metropolis into all-out clan warfare.

In audio, narrator Andrew Kishino delivers an electrifying, gritty performance that immerses listeners in the neon-lit alleyways and smoke-filled council rooms of Janloon. The spoken medium is the ultimate vehicle for Lee’s fast-paced, wuxia-inspired combat choreography, where jade-enhanced deflections, flying daggers, and blade strikes are paced with cinematic urgency. Yet what truly elevates the audiobook is Kishino’s rendering of the complex family dynamics between Pillar Kaul Lan, hot-headed Horn Kaul Hilo, and rebellious sister Shae. Standing beside immersive urban crime and fantasy classics in our catalog like The Lies of Locke Lamora and the political machinations of The Grace of Kings, this saga sets the modern gold standard for fantasy worldbuilding.

For a single monthly credit, nineteen hours of *Jade City* is one of the highest-value investments in fantasy audio. Lee crafts a living, breathing metropolis where honor codes, economic monopolies, and family loyalty clash with fatal consequences. Powered by Andrew Kishino’s pitch-perfect vocal swagger, this series debut is an essential, endlessly replayable audio adventure.

Narrator Soundcheck: Andrew Kishino

Andrew Kishino delivers an unforgettable, gritty vocal performance that captures the swagger, street-level brutality, and aristocratic pride of Janloon’s Green Bone clans. Kishino gives Kaul Hilo a dangerous, charismatic rasp that crackles with hair-trigger volatility, while voicing Kaul Shae and Lan with intellectual poise and quiet calculation. His handling of high-speed martial arts fight sequences is rhythmic and razor-sharp, maintaining breathless momentum without slurring tactical details. Crisp studio production and consistent character voices across nineteen hours make this a benchmark of urban fantasy narration.
