About this Audiobook

Refusing both marriage and the convent after fleeing her snowbound village, Vasya disguises herself as a boy and rides her magical stallion toward Grand Prince Dmitrii's Moscow, where mysterious raiders are burning villages and abducting daughters.

Over Thirteen Thrilling Hours Riding Out of the Winter Woods into Medieval Moscow

Picking up directly after the shattering climax of The Bear and the Nightingale, *The Girl in the Tower* widens Katherine Arden’s canvas from a snowbound village to the gilded, treacherous streets of fourteenth-century Moscow. Refusing to surrender her freedom to either a stifling marriage or a convent cell, Vasya cuts her hair, disguises herself as a young boy, and rides her enchanted stallion Solovey out into the wider world—only to cross paths with mysterious, fire-wielding bandits kidnapping young girls across the Russian countryside.

Arden balances breathless adventure and courtly Historical Fantasy intrigue without ever losing the eerie, frost-rimmed Folk Horror core that made Book 1 so distinctive. The clash between Christian orthodoxy, political survival under Grand Prince Dmitrii, and the older, perilous Folklore of firebirds, sorcerers, and winter gods crackles with emotional stakes—especially when Vasya is reunited with her estranged brother Sasha and sister Olga in the heart of the city.

Spanning thirteen hours and two minutes for a single Audible credit, this middle volume deepens every relationship and raises the mythic stakes dramatically ahead of The Winter of the Witch.

Narrator Soundcheck: Kathleen Gati

Returning for thirteen absorbing hours, Kathleen Gati expands her vocal palette effortlessly to match Moscow's crowded courtrooms, bustling markets, and moonlit forests. She captures Vasya's maturing grit and playful bravado as 'Vasilii' while giving Grand Prince Dmitrii, the calculating villain Kasian, and the enigmatic Frost King distinct, unforgettable vocal gravitas.
