About this Audiobook

When 3D printing and open-source automation render hyper-capitalist cities obsolete, bands of 'walkaways' head into the ruined wilderness to build cooperative, post-scarcity communities—until the ultra-rich fight back.

Cory Doctorow's Nineteen-Hour Solarpunk Rebellion of Open-Source Communes and Post-Scarcity

In a near-future world choked by climate change and ruled by ultra-wealthy 'zotta-rich' dynasties, ordinary jobs have become performative drudgery—because 3D printers, drone scavengers, and open-source software can already fabricate food, shelter, and clothing from industrial waste for free. Realizing that the game is rigged, Hubert, Etc., Seth, and rebellious heiress Natalie literally walk away from default society into the polluted wastelands, joining cooperative communities that share every blueprint freely. When the walkaways accidentally crack the code to digital human immortality, the billionaires decide to crush their utopia by force.

On audio, Cory Doctorow's fast-talking, idea-packed manifesto crackles with punk energy and infectious optimism. Rather than wallowing in dystopian misery, the story celebrates human ingenuity—from communal sauna building and disaster relief to hacking mercenary drones. Featured on our Solarpunk hub alongside The Ministry for the Future and A Half-Built Garden, this nineteen-hour recording turns radical economics and engineering into a high-stakes survival adventure.
