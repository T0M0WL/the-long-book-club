John Steinbeck\u2019s Pulitzer-Winning American Epic of the Dust Bowl and the Joad Odyssey in Audio

Driven from their Oklahoma tenant farm by choking Dust Bowl storms, corporate foreclosures, and relentless banking tractors, the Joad family packs their meager belongings onto an overburdened jalopy and joins the desperate westward exodus of hundreds of thousands along Route 66. Paroled convict Tom Joad, alongside his fierce, unshakeable mother Ma Joad and the disillusioned former preacher Jim Casy, journeys across baking southwestern deserts seeking the promised land of California. However, upon arrival, the migrants find a sun-drenched empire of agricultural exploitation, police brutality, and starvation wages, sparking a powerful political and moral awakening that tests their resolve to the absolute limit.
