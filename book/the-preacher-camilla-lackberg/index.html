Over Twelve Hours Beneath Sweltering Swedish Sun and Fanatic Secrets

Trading winter frost for an oppressive Bohuslän summer heatwave after The Ice Princess, Detective Patrik Hedström investigates the battered corpse of a young German tourist inside King's Cleft gorge while heavily pregnant wife Erica Falck sweats out unannounced houseguests at home. Digging beneath the murder scene exposes two forgotten female skeletons buried back in 1979—pointing straight toward the secretive descendants of fire-and-brimstone faith healer Ephraim Hult.

*The Preacher* sharpens Läckberg's trademark Nordic Noir contrast between cosy domestic comedy and pitch-black generational Crime. While small-town Police Procedural friction and sweltering nursery prep add relatable charm, a chilling dual timeline unpacks religious abuse and stubborn Detective Mystery.

At twelve hours and thirty-five minutes for one Audible credit, *The Preacher* races briskly—firmly locking Erica and Patrik's bond in place heading into The Stonecutter.
