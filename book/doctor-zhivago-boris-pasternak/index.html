About this Audiobook

Doctor Zhivago is the story of the life and loves of a poet/physician during the turmoil of the Russian Revolution.

Boris Pasternak\u2019s Nobel Prize-Winning Russian Epic of War, Revolution, and Lara in Audio

Set against the colossal, world-shaking canvas of World War I, the Russian Revolution, and the bloody Russian Civil War, Boris Pasternak\u2019s Nobel Prize-winning masterpiece chronicles the life of physician and poet Yuri Zhivago. Orphaned as a child and possessing a sensitive, artistic soul, Zhivago navigates the disintegration of imperial Russia while torn between his loyal, aristocratic wife Tonya and his passionate, muse-like love for the enigmatic nurse Lara Antipova. Exiled to the snow-covered Ural mountains and conscripted by brutal Bolshevik partisans in the Siberian taiga, Zhivago struggles to preserve his humanity, personal liberty, and poetic voice amid ideological totalitarianism and merciless political purges.

In its audio format, *Doctor Zhivago* unfolds with the sweeping, symphonic majesty of an epic Russian winter brought directly into your mind. Renowned Welsh actor Philip Madoc brings profound gravitas, melodic rhythm, and melancholic antiquity to Pasternak\u2019s poetic prose, making the howling Siberian blizzards, train journeys across war-torn steppes, and tender midnight verses come alive with cinematic richness and emotional depth.
