About this Audiobook

Trapped on the dungeon's deceptive tenth-floor racing gauntlet amid galactic upheaval, Carl and Princess Donut must outmaneuver deadly vehicular tracks and ominous AI glitches before an apocalyptic eleventh-floor spectacle begins.

Matt Dinniman’s Explosive Tenth-Floor Vehicular Gauntlet and Jeff Hays’s Kinetic Narration Masterclass

In the electrifying eighth installment of the record-breaking *Dungeon Crawler Carl* saga, the apocalyptic fallout of the Faction Wars sends shockwaves throughout the galactic syndicates, yet inside the subterranean meat grinder, Carl and Princess Donut find themselves plunged onto the deceptively regimented tenth floor. Here, the chaotic bloodsport transforms into a high-octane demolition derby where surviving crawler teams must pilot heavily fortified combat vehicles across increasingly lethal, obstacle-strewn raceways. But beneath the polished corporate spectacle of vehicle upgrade trees and checkered flags, the dungeon's underlying digital architecture is warping under catastrophic system glitches. Ominous cosmic whispers hint that the artificial intelligence is orchestrating an unprecedented horror for the fast-approaching eleventh floor—a coming-out party dubbed *A Parade of Horribles*—prompting Carl to devise an audacious, clandestine act of sabotage so perilous he dare not utter it aloud.

Following the staggering military collisions of This Inevitable Ruin, *A Parade of Horribles* proves that Matt Dinniman and Soundbooth Theater remain at the absolute vanguard of modern speculative entertainment. Narrator Jeff Hays turns in another breathtaking, vocally astonishing performance that captures the deafening concussive roar of nitrous-injected war rigs, the terrified screeches of goblin mechanics, and the razor-sharp comedic timing of Princess Donut with unbelievable elasticity. The friction between high-speed vehicular carnage, mounting psychological trauma, and Carl’s unyielding rebellion against corporate tyranny elevates this audio production into pure auditory dynamite. Standing alongside landmark progression triumphs like Dungeon Crawler Carl and Life Reset: A LitRPG Novel, this volume solidifies the franchise as the undisputed crown jewel of our LitRPG & GameLit library.

Spanning twenty hours and twenty-seven minutes of relentless, unflagging adrenaline, *A Parade of Horribles* provides unbeatable return on a single Audible credit or subscription token. Soundbooth Theater’s state-of-the-art sound staging, custom engine distortions, and pulse-pounding dynamic mastering ensure that every tire blowout, turret volley, and whispering AI glitch erupts with cinematic three-dimensional punch.
