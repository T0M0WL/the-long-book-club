About this Audiobook

A young New Yorker grieving his mother's death is pulled into a gritty underworld of art and wealth.

Donna Tartt\u2019s Pulitzer-Winning Thirty-Two-Hour Dickensian Epic of Art, Grief, and Betrayal in Audio

Surviving a catastrophic terrorist bombing at the Metropolitan Museum of Art that claims the life of his beloved mother, thirteen-year-old Theo Decker flees the burning rubble carrying a small, priceless seventeenth-century Dutch masterpiece: Carel Fabritius\u2019s *The Goldfinch*. Abandoned by his reckless father and taken in by a wealthy Park Avenue family, Theo clings to the secret painting as his solitary anchor to a vanished world. As Theo spirals into the sun-bleached desert wastes of Las Vegas with the eccentric Ukrainian trickster Boris, he enters a lifelong labyrinth of antique restoration, high-end art forgery, opioid addiction, and lethal international criminality that threatens everything and everyone he holds dear.

On audio, Donna Tartt\u2019s Pulitzer Prize-winning novel blossoms as a luxurious, deeply sensory Dickensian drama that commands your total attention from opening chapter to its philosophical finale. Narrator David Pittu gives an award-winning vocal performance that brings Theo\u2019s traumatic grief and refined antique education into sharp emotional focus, while his hilarious, chaotic, vodka-soaked portrayal of Boris Pavlikovsky steals every scene it touches with irresistible roguish energy and heartfelt loyalty. Highlighted as a crowning achievement in our Literary Fiction and Classics collections alongside Tartt\u2019s The Secret History and David Copperfield, this thirty-two-hour recording turns art theft and existential dread into an intoxicating auditory journey that breathes life into every street corner.

Delivering over thirty-two hours of magnificent, lyrical prose for a single credit, this release provides sensational value for lovers of prestige literature and captivating storytelling. Tartt crafts an unforgettable exploration of how tragedy shapes a human life, illustrating how a solitary yellow bird chained to a perch can become a symbol of immortality, beauty, and redemption in an indifferent universe. Investing a monthly credit in this modern classic guarantees an exquisite listening companion that will linger in your thoughts long after the final chapter concludes, making it an essential addition to any serious audio library and a crowning jewel of modern fiction.

Narrator Soundcheck: David Pittu

Pristine audio mastering makes this recording an absolute classic.
