About this Audiobook

Maggie finds herself transported into the world of her favorite—but famously unfinished—dark fantasy series. Waking up in the gutters of Kair Toren, she discovers she cannot be killed. Armed with her encyclopedic knowledge of the plot, she must navigate a world of rival warlords and magic to save the kingdom from a cataclysmic war.

Ilona Andrews's 17-Hour Portal Fantasy Triumph of Dark Magic, Survival, and Grumpy-Sunshine Romance

The husband-and-wife powerhouse team behind the pseudonym Ilona Andrews delivers a gritty, visceral portal fantasy with *This Kingdom Will Not Kill Me: Maggie the Undying*. Across seventeen propulsive hours, the narrative follows Maggie, a street-smart woman from our modern world who finds herself ripped through reality into a brutal, plague-ridden feudal empire ruled by ruthless mages and terrifying monsters. Blessed—or cursed—with an uncanny ability to resurrect after dying, Maggie must navigate treacherous court intrigue, horrific battlefields, and an enigmatic, morally grey inquisitor who becomes both her greatest threat and her most reluctant protector, creating an addictive enemies-to-lovers slow burn that crackles with palpable tension, emotional heat, and fierce survival instincts.

In audio format, Andrews's trademark kinetic pacing and dry, razor-sharp dialogue leap through your headphones with cinematic immediacy. Rather than getting bogged down in dense magical world-building exposition on a printed page, the audio format allows Maggie's wry survival instincts and witty inner monologue to establish an immediate, intimate bond with the listener. Featured prominently in our curated Epic Romantasy collection alongside modern hits like Fourth Wing, this recording provides the temporal space needed for the complex political conspiracies and simmering emotional heat to mature with deeply earned romantic stakes, pulling you directly into their perilous journey across hostile lands.
