About this Audiobook

Summoned before the Emerald Council, Corin navigates an international summit of elite sorcerers where ancient factions scheme to replace the gods themselves.

Andrew Rowe Assembles Archmages and Ancient Conspiracies in a 19-Hour Audio Summit

In *When Wizards Follow Fools*, Corin Cadence is thrust onto the highest political stage on the continent when he is summoned to attend the legendary Emerald Council. Surrounded by living legends, secretive archmages, and sovereign monarchs, Corin must navigate an unprecedented gathering where the fate of the spires and the mortal races hangs in the balance. But behind closed doors, clandestine factions are plotting to overthrow the divine hierarchy itself, forcing Corin to use every ounce of his analytical genius to decipher who is an ally and who is planning a massacre.

In audio, Nick Podehl turns this nineteen-and-a-half-hour diplomatic thriller into a triumph of vocal ensemble acting. Podehl voices a massive cast of centuries-old archmages, each with distinct cadence, vocabulary, and vocal presence, keeping high-level political debates as gripping as physical combat. Alongside celebrated council chamber epics like A Game of Thrones and Tears of Liscor, this recording showcases fantasy statecraft at its most intricate.

Providing nineteen and a half hours of top-quality narration for a single credit, this fifth volume is a must-listen for long-time followers of the saga. Rowe seamlessly blends universe-spanning lore with personal character stakes, rewarding patient listeners with profound payoffs. Spending a credit here unlocks an unforgettable, high-tension chapter in contemporary audio fantasy.
