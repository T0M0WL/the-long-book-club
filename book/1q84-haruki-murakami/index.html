About this Audiobook

A young woman named Aomame follows a taxi driver's enigmatic suggestion and begins to notice puzzling discrepancies in the world around her.

Haruki Murakami’s Monumental Forty-Seven-Hour Surrealist Epic of Alternate Realities and Lost Love

Haruki Murakami’s most colossal and ambitious novel plunges listeners into a subtly shifted Tokyo in the year 1984—a parallel dimension marked by two moons hanging in the night sky, which assassin Aomame christens 1Q84, with the 'Q' standing for 'question mark.' As Aomame carries out lethal, highly specialized vengeance against abusive men on behalf of an enigmatic dowager, aspiring novelist Tengo ghostwrites an eerie manuscript titled *Air Chrysalis* for a dyslexic teenage prodigy. Unbeknownst to them, their parallel investigations into an elusive religious cult known as Sakigake and the supernatural 'Little People' who weave chrysalises from thin air bring them steadily closer to reuniting the lost childhood romance that shaped their entire lives.

In audio format, Murakami’s deliberate, atmospheric pacing transforms a daunting thousand-page doorstopper into a deeply addictive and meditative listening routine. The tranquil descriptions of cooking simple vegetable stir-fries, sipping scotch while listening to Janáček’s *Sinfonietta*, and staking out Tokyo apartment balconies flow with mesmerizing auditory intimacy. Featured as the towering centerpiece of our Magical Realism Collection alongside Murakami favorites like The Wind-Up Bird Chronicle and The City and Its Uncertain Walls, this multi-voiced recording turns surreal dream logic into a luxurious retreat from modern digital noise.

Clocking in at nearly forty-seven hours, this single-credit release represents an astonishing return on investment, delivering the complete unedited content of three full-length volumes for the cost of a single monthly token. Rather than rushing through plot points, Murakami invites listeners to synchronize with the slow, deliberate pulse of his characters over several months of commuting and daily chores. Investing one credit in this monumental surrealist saga secures nearly two full days of hypnotic, prestige storytelling that rewards patient listening and lingers in your imagination long after the two moons set, standing as one of the most transportive modern masterworks ever recorded.

Narrator Soundcheck: Allison Hiroto, Marc Vietor, Mark Boyett

Allison Hiroto, Marc Vietor, and Mark Boyett form a flawless trio, each taking ownership of a primary viewpoint with distinct psychological resonance. Hiroto captures Aomame’s lethal martial precision and underlying emotional yearning with cool, focused clarity, while Vietor lends Tengo a thoughtful, gentle vulnerability. Boyett’s narration of the eccentric private investigator Ushikawa adds gritty, suspenseful color to the third volume. The audio production is impeccably balanced across all forty-seven hours, maintaining uniform studio warmth, pristine acoustics, and natural pacing that prevents fatigue across a marathon listening journey.
