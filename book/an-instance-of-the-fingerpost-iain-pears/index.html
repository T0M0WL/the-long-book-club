About this Audiobook

Twenty-Nine Hours of Rashomon-Style Murder Mystery in 1663 Restoration Oxford

In 1663, England is still reeling from Civil War and the restoration of King Charles II—a tense crossroads where seventeenth-century alchemy and early modern science collide inside Oxford's colleges. When Dr. Robert Grove, a Fellow of New College, dies of arsenic poisoning, a desperate servant girl named Sarah Blundy is swiftly convicted of witchcraft and murder. Yet four men who witnessed the events—a Venetian doctor, the treacherous son of a Puritan traitor, an Oxford mathematician, and a seasoned antiquary—each step forward to tell the 'true' story across four interlocking narratives.

Listening to *An Instance of the Fingerpost* on audio transforms one of the most ingenious structural puzzles in Historical Mystery and Dark Academia into a mesmerizing four-part courtroom drama. Because every witness lies, omits crucial facts, or misreads the clues around him, assigning a different voice actor to each testimony makes the contradictions—and the breathtaking final revelation—hit harder than on the page. Spanning nearly twenty-nine hours alongside Tombland, it is a masterclass in Full-Cast historical storytelling.
