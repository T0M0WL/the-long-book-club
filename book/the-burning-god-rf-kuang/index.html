About this Audiobook

A Scorched-Earth Masterpiece: The Devastating, Flawless Conclusion to The Poppy War

In *The Burning God*, R.F. Kuang brings her monumental grimdark trilogy to a harrowing, uncompromising finish. Betrayed by those she bled for, Rin returns to the destitute southern provinces of her birth, forging an army of famine-starved peasants and forgotten gods to launch a scorched-earth revolution against both the northern warlords and the technologically terrifying Hesperian invaders. As divine power threatens to incinerate what little remains of her sanity, Rin must confront the horrifying truth that winning a war of liberation may require destroying the very nation she sought to save.

Emily Woo Zeller’s narration in this final volume is nothing short of heroic. She throws herself entirely into Rin’s unhinged, tragic fury, delivering dialogue that feels like breathing fire through tears. The tragic friendship between Rin and her brilliant strategist Kitay forms the beating emotional core of the novel, and Zeller handles their heartbreaking dynamic with exquisite sensitivity. Fans of uncompromising military epics like The Poppy War and The Dragon Republic will find this finale profoundly cathartic.

At nearly twenty-four hours of unrelenting drama, *The Burning God* stands as one of the most powerful credit conversions on Audible. It refuses comforting genre clichés, offering instead an honest, devastating exploration of colonialism, trauma, and political power that cements Kuang and Zeller as an unforgettable storytelling duo.
