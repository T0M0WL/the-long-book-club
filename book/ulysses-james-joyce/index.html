James Joyce\u2019s Lyrical Dublin Masterpiece of Leopold Bloom and Stream of Consciousness in Audio

Set over the course of a single, fateful day in Dublin on June 16, 1904, James Joyce\u2019s revolutionary modernist epic mirrors Homer's *Odyssey* across eighteen innovative chapters, each employing a completely distinct literary style. The narrative follows Leopold Bloom, an ordinary, Jewish advertising canvasser wandering the streets, pubs, and cemeteries of Dublin; his unfaithful wife Molly Bloom, an opera singer lying in bed contemplating her desires; and Stephen Dedalus, an intellectual, guilt-ridden young poet searching for spiritual fatherhood. Together, their paths crisscross the vibrant, chaotic Irish capital in a celebration of the everyday heroic, capturing every nuance of urban human existence with breathtaking detail.

While *Ulysses* is notoriously challenging on the printed page, experiencing it in audio format is a revelation that unlocks Joyce\u2019s true genius and musical humor.
