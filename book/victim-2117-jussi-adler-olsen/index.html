About this Audiobook

When news footage of the 2,117th Mediterranean migrant casualty sparks a shocking personal crisis for Assad, Department Q's enigmatic assistant is forced to reveal his painful intelligence past as an Abu Ghraib tormentor targets Europe.

Over Fifteen and a Half Explosive Hours Unlocking Assad's Secret Intelligence Past

For seven volumes from Mercy through The Scarred Woman, Department Q's tea-brewing, camel-metaphor-loving assistant Assad remained a disarming enigma—until news footage from a Mediterranean beach labels one drowned refugee "Victim 2117." Recognizing the face instantly, Assad shatters before Carl Mørck's eyes, revealing a haunted Syrian intelligence history and a ruthless former Abu Ghraib nemesis plotting mass terror across Europe while an isolated Danish teen plots murder indoors.

In audio format, *Victim 2117* explodes out of traditional Nordic Noir borders into high-stakes international Suspense. Interlacing ticking-clock anti-terrorist Police Procedural action with intimate Middle Eastern war scars deepens every Crime and Detective beat, delivering long-awaited answers to the franchise's greatest ongoing personal Mystery.

Clocking in at fifteen hours and thirty-two minutes for a single Audible credit, *Victim 2117* rewards loyal listeners deeply while priming the fuse for The Shadow Murders.
