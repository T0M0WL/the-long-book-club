About this Audiobook

In the first half of Malazan's two-volume endgame, Adjunct Tavore leads the Bonehunters out into the bleak, unmapped Wastelands while ancient K'Chain Che'Malle nests and forgotten Barghast clans collide ahead of the final reckoning.

Over Forty-Three Hours Marching Across the Wastelands Into Malazan's Two-Part Endgame

Conceived explicitly alongside The Crippled God as the sweeping first half of one monumental finale, *Dust of Dreams* watches Adjunct Tavore lead the exhausted Bonehunters eastward from Lether into uncharted, hostile wastelands—telling her soldiers almost nothing of what awaits at Kolanse. Meanwhile the terrifying Elder race of K'Chain Che'Malle stirs, lost Shake refugees flee across ancient beaches, and warring Barghast clans tear the plains apart in senseless bloodlust.

In audio format, *Dust of Dreams* challenges listeners with grim, philosophical Military Fantasy and unflinching Grimdark—slowly raising the pressure across forty-three hours until a staggering ambush leaves you breathless for Book 10. It builds directly on Gardens of the Moon across our Grimdark and Epic Fantasy shelves.

Clocking in at forty-three hours and thirteen minutes for a single Audible credit, *Dust of Dreams* positions every piece on the board for Malazan's grand reckoning.
