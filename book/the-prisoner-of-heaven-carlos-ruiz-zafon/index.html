About this Audiobook

In Christmas 1957, a shadowy stranger unearths the horrifying secrets of Montjuïc Castle and the true origin of Fermín Romero de Torres.

The Montjuïc Prison Fortress and the Heroic Heart of Fermín Romero de Torres

Spanning eight and a half fast-paced, deeply emotional hours, *The Prisoner of Heaven* is Carlos Ruiz Zafón’s pivotal third volume in *The Cemetery of Forgotten Books* quartet, functioning as the vital linchpin connecting The Shadow of the Wind and The Angel's Game. Set in Barcelona during the bleak Christmas of 1957, Daniel Sempere is happily married and managing the family antiquarian bookshop alongside his beloved father and his irrepressible, street-smart companion Fermín Romero de Torres. But their quiet domestic peace is shattered when a crippled, sinister stranger enters the shop, purchases a rare copy of *The Count of Monte Cristo*, and leaves a chilling inscription for Fermín.

To save Daniel’s family from mortal danger, Fermín is finally forced to reveal his closely guarded past. Through an extended flashback to the brutal aftermath of the Spanish Civil War in 1939, listeners are plunged into the terrifying dungeons of Montjuïc Castle fortress. There, imprisoned beneath the cruel reign of the villainous Mauricio Valls, Fermín met fellow inmate David Martín—the haunted author from *The Angel’s Game*. Zafón masterfully weaves a high-stakes tale of prison escapes, stolen identities, and state-sponsored murder, revealing the tragic, long-hidden connection between David Martín, Fermín, and the murder of Daniel’s mother. The novel balances dark historical reckoning with heartwarming camaraderie and romantic comedy.

Narrator Daniel Weyman shines with dazzling brilliance, giving Fermín his unmistakable, rapid-fire philosophical chatter, Latin aphorisms, and roguish warmth while effortlessly carrying the somber gravity of the Montjuïc flashbacks. At eight and a half hours, it is a lean, emotionally charged Gothic Mystery that sets the stage for the epic grand finale. For Zafón fans, this credit is an absolute necessity.

Narrator Soundcheck: Daniel Weyman

Daniel Weyman’s performance on The Prisoner of Heaven is an absolute delight, showcasing his magnificent range. Weyman embodies the beloved Fermín Romero de Torres with effervescent charm, rapid-fire wit, and theatrical flair, yet shifts with heartbreaking precision into the harrowing despair of the Montjuïc prison scenes. His portrayal of the sinister Mauricio Valls drips with petty bureaucratic cruelty. Produced with flawless clarity across eight and a half hours, this audiobook is pure storytelling pleasure.
