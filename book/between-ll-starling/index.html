About this Audiobook

A kindergarten teacher accepts a job in an eccentric village, only to discover her house borders a chaotic fairy-tale kingdom ruled by an exasperated sorcerer king.

A Thirty-Hour Cozy Fantasy Rom-Com Tour-de-Force of Whimsical Mayhem and Dual-Narrated Perfection

L.L. Starling’s colossal, delightfully eccentric debut blends the cozy autumnal nostalgia of *Practical Magic* with the madcap fairy-tale whimsy of *Labyrinth*. Sasha Royce, a sensible, thirty-something teacher fleeing a chaotic city breakup, accepts a substitute teaching job in the sleepy, picturesque town of Old Middleton, only to discover that her charming new home sits directly on the border of 'Between'—a chaotic, liminal fairy-tale kingdom populated by runaway stone gargoyles, drunken coven witches, and mischievous goblins who steal baked goods. Even more bewildering, through a bizarre magical loophole involving an accidental paperwork signing, Sasha inadvertently finds herself betrothed to Lorn, the exhausted, leather-pants-wearing Sorcerer King who just wants to keep his decaying kingdom from collapsing.

In audio format, Starling’s ingenious split structure—telling Sasha’s comedic misadventures in Part One before rewinding to reveal Lorn’s desperate, frantic perspective in Part Two—becomes an absolute tour-de-force of dual narration. The crackling banter, slapstick magical mishaps, and slow-burn grumpy-meets-sunshine romantic tension flow with irresistible comedic vitality.
