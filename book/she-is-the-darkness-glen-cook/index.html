About this Audiobook

Torn from linear time by occult magic while the Company holds desperate siege lines outside Taglios, standard-bearer Murgen chronicles treacherous schemes, ancient Stranglers, and the awakening shadow of sleep-goddess Kina.

Over Seventeen Hours Surviving Siege Warfare, Stranglers, and Fractured Time Around Taglios

Advancing deep into the Glittering Stone sequence after Dreams of Steel, standard-bearer Murgen writes from inside a claustrophobic siege perimeter in Taglios while haunted by strange magical time-slips throwing his consciousness backward and forward across bloody nights. As rival Shadowmasters clash, ancient Strangler cultists infiltrate every household, and the waking shadow of sleep-goddess Kina stirs beneath the plain, the Company's survival hinges on ruthless traps rather than open charges.

At over seventeen hours, *She Is the Darkness* unleashes Cook's fullest Dark Fantasy and Military Fantasy complexity. Marrying paranoid Grimdark intelligence operations with ancient Sword and Sorcery curses and gritty Low Fantasy trench endurance turns Taglios into an unforgettable pressure cooker.

Delivering seventeen hours and twelve minutes of dense, twist-laden value for one Audible credit, Book 7 builds towering anticipation directly into the series culmination in Soldiers Live. (Note: Book 8 *Water Sleeps* currently lacks a UK audiobook edition.)

Narrator Soundcheck: Jonathan Davis

Sustaining seventeen hours of shifting timelines, Taglian intrigue, and relentless siege pressure, Jonathan Davis brings weary battlefront warmth to Murgen's fragmented entries. His crisp distinction among Soulcatcher's unsettling voices, veteran officers, and eerie temple conspirators keeps every temporal jump easy to follow.
