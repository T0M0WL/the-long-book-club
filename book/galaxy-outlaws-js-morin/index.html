About this Audiobook

J.S. Morin's 85-Hour Space Western Marathon of Wizardry, Starships, and Scoundrel Chemistry

Delivering eighty-five hours of non-stop, rollicking sci-fi adventure, J.S. Morin’s *Galaxy Outlaws: The Complete Black Ocean Mobius Missions* is the ultimate audio binge for fans of *Firefly* and space western escapism. Set in a galaxy where faster-than-light travel is powered not by warp drives but by convicted felons channeling ancient wizardry, the narrative follows Captain Carl Ramsey and his ragtag, morally flexible crew aboard the battered salvage ship *Mobius*. Narrator Soundcheck: Mikael Naramore

Solo narrator Mikael Naramore delivers a career-defining performance, shouldering all eighty-five hours with incredible stamina, humor, and vocal versatility. Naramore gives each member of the *Mobius* crew a distinct, unmistakable vocal personality, from Carl’s weary, cynical rogue drawl to Mort the wizard’s croaky ancient cadence and the alien pilot Kubu’s bubbly enthusiasm. His pacing never flags across dozens of missions, backed by dynamic, clean sound design that makes this massive space western a pure delight.
