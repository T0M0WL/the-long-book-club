Midnight Tides

Author: Steven Erikson | Narrator: Michael Page

Runtime: 31h 03m | Genre: Grimdark, Epic Fantasy, High Fantasy, Magic Systems, Military Fantasy

About this Audiobook

On the distant continent of Lether, where merchant-princes rule through debt slavery, Tiste Edur brothers Trull and Rhulad Sengar forge a catastrophic pact with a poisoned sword while eccentric genius Tehol Beddict plots from the shadows.

Thirty-One Hours of Letherii Debt Empires, Cursed Swords, and Dark Comedy

Stepping far back in timeline to an entirely uncharted continent before the Malazan Empire's clash with Lether, *Midnight Tides* introduces two unforgettable worlds on collision course. Across snowy borderlands, Tiste Edur brothers Trull, Fear, and jealous Rhulad Sengar fall into a cursed bargain with the Crippled God's poisoned sword. Meanwhile inside Letheras's corrupt capital of merchant-princes and debt slaves, disheveled eccentric Tehol Beddict and his manservant Bugg hatch an outrageously funny financial plot to bring the empire crashing down.

In audio format, *Midnight Tides* surprises every listener expecting grim monotony by delivering Malazan's sharpest satirical wit alongside devastating High Fantasy tragedy. Tehol and Bugg's rapid-fire banter rivals fantasy's best double acts while deepening the Magic Systems and Grimdark mythos.

At thirty-one hours and three minutes for a single credit, *Midnight Tides* transforms an audacious standalone detour into a beloved cornerstone of Epic Fantasy.
