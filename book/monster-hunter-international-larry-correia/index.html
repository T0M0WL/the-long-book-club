Larry Correia's 24-Hour High-Octane Urban Fantasy of Mercenary Firepower and Eldritch Horrors

Spanning nearly twenty-four pulse-pounding hours, Larry Correia’s *Monster Hunter International* is an unapologetic, adrenaline-fueled blockbuster that revitalized modern urban fantasy with military realism, relentless pacing, and massive firepower. When ordinary corporate accountant and former pit fighter Owen Zastava Pitt survives being attacked by his werewolf boss by shoving him through a fourteenth-story skyscraper window, his mundane life is permanently obliterated. Recruited by Monster Hunter International—a secretive, elite private mercenary firm that collects substantial federal bounties for neutralizing supernatural threats across the globe—Owen is plunged into a hidden war against vampires, gargoyles, cursed artifacts, and ancient eldritch horrors intent on tearing open the dimensional veil and consuming humanity in darkness.

In audio format, Correia’s kinetic gunplay, tactical weapon mechanics, and pitch-black comedic banter hit with thunderous sonic force. Hearing Owen’s deadpan Midwestern cynicism contrasted with fanatical government agents and eccentric mercenary veterans makes twenty-four hours fly by with breathless, addictive momentum. The audio pacing heightens every narrow escape, tactical shootout, and terrifying monstrosity with cinematic flair that keeps your heart in your throat.
