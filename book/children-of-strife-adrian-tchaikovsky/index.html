About this Audiobook

Expanding his Arthur C. Clarke Award-winning Children of Time universe, Adrian Tchaikovsky plunges an interstellar expedition of humans and uplifted species into a fierce collision with a radically alien evolutionary frontier.

Over Twenty Visionary Hours Expanding the Children of Time Universe

Ten years after *Children of Time* redefined modern evolutionary science fiction, Adrian Tchaikovsky returns to his flagship universe with *Children of Strife*—a towering fourth installment that once again dares to imagine alien intelligence on its own radically non-human terms. Following an interstellar expedition of humans and uplifted species venturing into an uncharted frontier, Tchaikovsky builds a dazzling, high-stakes puzzle of First Contact, conflicting survival imperatives, and unsettling Biopunk evolution.

What makes *Children of Strife* shine so brightly on audio is Tchaikovsky’s unmatched ability to balance rigorous Hard Sci-Fi concepts with sly dark humor and genuine cosmic awe. Sitting proudly in our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 and completing a phenomenal four-book arc alongside Children of Time, Children of Ruin, and Children of Memory, it proves Tchaikovsky remains the sharpest architect in modern Space Opera.

Clocking in at twenty hours and seven minutes for a single Audible credit, *Children of Strife* delivers peak intellectual thrills and phenomenal marathon value.

Narrator Soundcheck: Mel Hudson

Series stalwart Mel Hudson returns for her longest outing yet in the Children of Time universe—and she has never sounded better. Across over twenty crisp, gripping hours, Hudson navigates dense scientific exposition, tense shipboard arguments, and eerily alien perspectives with effortless intelligence and cool British authority, keeping every timeline and species unmistakably distinct.
