About this Audiobook

Thirteen Razor-Sharp Hours Unravelling a Lethal Double Life in Gilded London

From the bestselling investigative journalist behind *Say Nothing* and *Empire of Pain*, Patrick Radden Keefe's *London Falling* reads with the breathless propulsion of a prestige Crime thriller—except every staggering twist actually happened. Beginning with a devastating phone call informing a privileged North London family that their nineteen-year-old son has died in an apparent suicide, Keefe follows the parents' relentless refusal to accept the official verdict—peeling back layer after shocking layer to reveal a secret double life inside the capital's brutal, hidden criminal underworld.

Keefe's gift is marrying forensic investigative journalism with empathetic human portraiture, holding the contradictions of gilded London wealth and street-level violence in one razor-sharp frame. Standing right alongside The Finest Hotel in Kabul and Alexander: God, King, Man as one of 2026's unmissable Non-Fiction audio events, it grips like a vice from chapter one.

Clocking in at twelve hours and fifty-nine minutes for a single Audible credit, *London Falling* is compulsive, haunting, and impossible to turn off.

Narrator Soundcheck: Patrick Radden Keefe

Patrick Radden Keefe has quietly become one of the very best author-narrators in modern non-fiction. Across thirteen taut hours, his calm, measured, New Yorker–crisp delivery lets the astonishing facts speak for themselves—building genuine investigative dread while treating the grieving family at the heart of the mystery with deep restraint and humanity.
