Michael Chabon\u2019s Pulitzer-Winning Twenty-Six-Hour Epic of Comic Books, Magic, and Escapism in Audio

Beginning in 1939 as nineteen-year-old Jewish illusionist and escape artist Josef Kavalier smuggles himself out of Nazi-occupied Prague inside a coffin containing the legendary Golem, Michael Chabon\u2019s Pulitzer Prize-winning masterpiece bursts into the golden age of American comic books. In New York City, Joe unites with his brooklyn-born cousin Sammy Clay, a fierce dreamer with a golden pen and boundless ambition. Together, they create 'The Escapist'\u2014a costumed superhero dedicated to punching Adolf Hitler in the jaw\u2014channeling their grief, artistic ambitions, and secret desires into four-color newsprint as global war closes in around them.

In its audio format, *The Amazing Adventures of Kavalier & Clay* unfolds as an intoxicating, panoramic celebration of twentieth-century American pop culture, love, and Jewish folklore that sweeps listeners off their feet. Narrator David Colacci delivers an extraordinary vocal performance across twenty-six hours, effortlessly capturing Sammy\u2019s fast-talking Brooklyn swagger, Joe\u2019s haunted Central European accent and artistic perfectionism, and the eccentric circle of comic book pioneers who birthed an empire.
