Berlin Game

Author: Len Deighton | Narrator: James Lailey

Runtime: 10h 29m | Genre: British Espionage, Cold War, Historical Fiction, Mystery, Crime, Espionage

About this Audiobook

A cynical MI6 case officer raised in post-war Berlin crosses the Wall to extract a compromised Soviet defector, only to discover a traitor inside his own London circle.

The Gritty Divided-City Masterpiece That Launches the Bernard Samson Sagas

Spanning ten and a half gripping hours, *Berlin Game* is the acclaimed opening volume of Len Deighton’s landmark *Game, Set and Match* trilogy, offering a masterclass in gritty, working-class Cold War tradecraft. Unlike Ian Fleming's extravagant fantasy adventures or John le Carré’s introspective academic corridors, Deighton crafts espionage through the cynical eyes of a man who knows the price of a bad exchange rate, the taste of cheap sausage in East Berlin, and the exact weight of a Walther PPK. Protagonist Bernard Samson is an experienced field operative raised in divided Berlin, whose intimate knowledge of safe houses, border checkpoints, and black-market contacts makes him irreplaceable, yet perpetually despised by the Oxford-educated mandarins running the London desk.

When Brahms Four—an extraordinarily high-ranking intelligence asset embedded within East German military command—sends an urgent signal that he has been betrayed and must be extracted immediately, Samson is reluctantly dispatched back into the frozen shadow of the Berlin Wall. The tension escalates as Samson begins to suspect that the security leak did not originate in the East, but within his own inner circle in London, placing his wealthy, high-ranking wife Fiona and his closest colleagues under suspicion. In audio, Deighton's terse, observant prose and mordant humor make every crossing at Checkpoint Charlie and whispered meeting in smoke-filled bierkellers pulse with authentic, bone-chilling suspense.

For audiobook lovers seeking maximum tension, atmospheric historical immersion, and an unforgettable twist that detonates across future volumes, *Berlin Game* is an essential single-credit investment. James Lailey delivers a superb performance, capturing Samson’s droll, world-weary British sarcasm and the tense, claustrophobic atmosphere of divided Europe with flawless timing. Alongside essential Cold War companions like Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy and The Day of the Jackal, it stands as an enduring pillar of Cold War fiction that will leave you completely hooked on Samson's journey.

Narrator Soundcheck: James Lailey

James Lailey captures the dry, sardonic personality of Bernard Samson with exceptional skill. Lailey balances Samson’s cynical, blue-collar British pragmatism against the pompous affectations of senior Whitehall desk-jockeys, giving their office turf-wars a delicious, barbed realism. His German accents—ranging from hardened Stasi interrogators to nervous East Berlin defectors—are crisp, authentic, and cleanly rendered. Lailey’s steady, unhurried pacing allows the mounting paranoia of a double-agent hunt to tighten like a noose across ten magnificent hours.
