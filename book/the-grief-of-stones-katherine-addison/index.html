About this Audiobook

Assigned an unexpected apprentice, Amalo's Witness for the Dead Thara Celehar investigates a poisoned marquise and uncovers a cruel conspiracy exploiting destitute foundling girls across the city.

Eight and a Half Hours of Mentorship, City Scandal, and Quiet Courage in Amalo

Still carrying heavy exhaustion from his lonely office after The Witness for the Dead, Thara Celehar receives a rare surprise from Amalo's prelate: an unexpected apprentice named Merrem Tomasaran, a grieving widow who discovered her gift for hearing the dead upon losing her husband. Even as Celehar reluctantly teaches her to exorcise ghouls and calm haunted crypts, a poisoned aristocrat's posthumous whispers draw the pair into a heartbreaking conspiracy involving an abusive foundling girls' house and an illicit underworld black market.

Listening to *The Grief of Stones* deepens every quality fans cherish about Cozy Fantasy and compassionate Mystery. Though its central crime carries real emotional gravity, Katherine Addison keeps focus squarely on small human kindnesses, found mentorship, and restorative Slice of Life warmth—earning an effortless place beside The Goblin Emperor in our Cozy Fantasy and Low Fantasy shelves.

At eight hours and twenty-six minutes, *The Grief of Stones* gently nudges its guarded hero out of isolation, setting up an extraordinary final reckoning in The Tomb of Dragons.
