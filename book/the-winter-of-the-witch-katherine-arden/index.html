About this Audiobook

With Moscow ravaged by fire, paranoid mobs hunting witches, and the Golden Horde marching toward war, Vasya must venture into the perilous realm of Midnight to unite mortals and ancient Russian spirits before both worlds perish.

Fourteen Mythic Hours Bringing the Winternight Trilogy to a Blazing Finale

In the sweeping, emotionally devastating conclusion to the *Winternight* trilogy, Moscow lies smoldering in ashes, superstitious mobs blame Vasya for the city’s ruin, and the armies of the Golden Horde mass on the horizon. Following the perilous events of The Girl in the Tower, Vasya must venture beyond the mortal map into the shadowy, perilous dreamscape of Midnight—encountering Baba Yaga, ancient spirits, and the price of her own forbidden power—to forge an impossible alliance between mortals and the old gods of Rus'.

Katherine Arden sticks the landing with rare grace, fusing brutal medieval warfare and witch-hunt Folk Horror with transcendent Folklore wonder. Every thread planted back in The Bear and the Nightingale—from household *domovoi* to Vasya’s aching bond with Morozko—pays off with breathtaking emotional force, elevating the series into an enduring landmark of modern Historical Fantasy.

At fourteen hours flat for a single Audible credit, *The Winter of the Witch* is a stirring, bittersweet triumph that rewards every hour invested in the trilogy.
