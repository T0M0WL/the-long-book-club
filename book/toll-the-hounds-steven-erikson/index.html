Forty-Four Hours Back in Darujhistan Under Gathering Gods and Ancient Bells

Returning at last to the blue-flamed cobbles of Darujhistan where Gardens of the Moon began, retired Bridgeburners run K'rul's Bar while eccentric storyteller Kruppe narrates old feuds and quiet tragedies under the city's eaves. Yet high above Black Coral and along dusty roads, Hood, the Lord of Death, prepares to walk mortal soil—drawing Anomander Rake, Traveller, and the Hounds of Shadow toward the single most momentous convergence in High Fantasy.

On audio, *Toll the Hounds* plays like a majestic forty-four-hour gothic tragedy laced with Magic Systems awe and brooding Grimdark inevitability. Its poetic framing by Kruppe suits spoken performance brilliantly before shattering into thunderous Epic Fantasy action.

At forty-four hours even for a single credit, *Toll the Hounds* rewards patient listeners with the saga's most unforgettable final act.
