About this Audiobook

Diana Bishop travels to Ravenswood in Massachusetts to face her dark ancestral heritage and the forbidden magic of the Ravenscroft witch family.

Dark Witchcraft, Ancient Familiars, and the Unforgiven Secrets of Ravenswood

Spanning seventeen and a half enthralling, atmospheric hours, *The Black Bird Oracle* marks Deborah Harkness’s triumphant return to her flagship series in July 2024, opening a captivating fifth volume in the *All Souls* saga. When the governing Congregation demands that Diana Bishop submit her seven-year-old twins to rigorous testing to determine their supernatural capabilities, Diana refuses to let bureaucratic inquisitors probe their minds. Desperate to understand the full, terrifying extent of the higher magic her children might inherit, Diana accepts an unexpected invitation from her long-estranged maternal relatives: the enigmatic Ravenscroft family, residing at the remote estate of Ravenswood in Massachusetts.

Arriving in the misty, shadow-drenched woods of New England, Diana is thrust into an ancient realm of witchcraft far removed from the academic halls of Oxford. She discovers that her ancestors practiced powerful, forbidden forms of higher magic—including dark divination, animal familiars, and necromantic communion with ancestral spirits—disciplines that were ruthlessly demonized and erased during the Salem Witch Trials. As Diana trains under the sharp, calculating gaze of Great-Aunt Gwyneth, she is forced to confront uncomfortable truths about her mother Rebecca’s hidden past, the true origins of weaver magic, and the heavy price of embracing one’s complete identity.

Narrator Saskia Maarleveld delivers a stellar, emotionally resonant performance, capturing Diana’s fierce maternal protectiveness and the eerie, gothic ambiance of Ravenswood with chilling nuance. Maarleveld voices the eccentric Ravenscroft elders with sharp, weathered dignity, contrasting them against Matthew’s protective vampire vigilance. For listeners seeking rich folkloric Dark Academia, complex witch lore, and captivating family intrigue on a single credit, *The Black Bird Oracle* is a spellbinding return to form.

Narrator Soundcheck: Saskia Maarleveld

Saskia Maarleveld’s performance on The Black Bird Oracle is atmospheric, brooding, and emotionally gripping. Maarleveld leans into the New England gothic setting, utilizing whispered invocations, crisp pacing, and subtle tonal shifts to evoke the rustling corvids and shadowy woods of Ravenswood. Her portrayal of Diana has matured into an authoritative, maternal force, while her sharp characterizations of the elderly Ravenscroft witches crackle with eccentric vitality. Produced with pristine acoustic warmth, this seventeen-hour listen is absolute magic.
