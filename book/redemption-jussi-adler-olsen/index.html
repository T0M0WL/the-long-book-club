About this Audiobook

When a weathered bottle bearing a smudged cry for help written in Danish blood washes ashore in Scotland, Carl Mørck, Assad, and Rose race to trace two kidnapped children from an insular religious sect before a killer executes one.

Over Seventeen Hours Racing a Bloodstained Cry Across Insular Religious Communities

Tossed ashore on a windswept Scottish beach after drifting through cold northern currents for years, a battered bottle hides an almost illegible scrap of paper written in Danish—and human blood. Once forensic labs finally forward the faded letter to Copenhagen's cellar after Disgrace, Carl Mørck, Assad, and Rose realize the boy who threw it into the sea belonged to an ultra-strict evangelical community whose elders kept his abduction completely secret from police, allowing a sadistic serial predator to stalk pairs of siblings untouched.

Widely adored as one of the sharpest entries in Nordic Noir, *Redemption* (known internationally as *A Conspiracy of Faith*) ratchets Suspense to fever pitch. Balancing frantic Police Procedural detective work against the killer's terrifying faith-twisted mind games delivers breathless Crime and Detective payoff, turning decades-old coastal fragments into an urgent Mystery countdown.

Spanning seventeen hours and eighteen minutes for one credit, *Redemption* pairs razor-sharp empathy with relentless pacing—while deepening intriguing clues about Assad's true background ahead of Guilt.

Narrator Soundcheck: Steven Pacey

Across seventeen hours and eighteen minutes, Steven Pacey displays formidable dynamic range—moving effortlessly from basement bickering between Carl, Rose, and Assad to the freezing terror of locked train-car kidnappings and the chilling, soft-voiced conviction of one of Adler-Olsen's creepiest villains.
