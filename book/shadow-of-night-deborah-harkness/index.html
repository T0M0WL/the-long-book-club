Shadow of Night

Author: Deborah Harkness | Narrator: Jennifer Ikeda

Runtime: 24h 50m | Genre: Fantasy, Urban Fantasy, Romance, Dark Academia, Historical Fiction, Time Travel, Witches, Vampires

About this Audiobook

Diana Bishop and vampire Matthew Clairmont time-walk to Elizabethan London in 1590 to seek the bewitched alchemical manuscript Ashmole 782.

Spies, Alchemy, and the School of Night in Shakespeare's England

Spanning nearly twenty-five rich, immersive hours, *Shadow of Night* is Deborah Harkness’s spellbinding historical sequel to A Discovery of Witches. Fleeing the hostile supernatural Congregation in contemporary Oxford, tenured historian and witch Diana Bishop and her vampire husband Matthew Clairmont perform an audacious temporal leap, time-walking back to Elizabethan England in the autumn of 1590. Their dual mission is daunting: locate an intact copy of the bewitched alchemical manuscript Ashmole 782 before it was disassembled, and find a powerful witch mentor capable of teaching Diana how to harness her volatile higher magic.

Arriving in London, Diana discovers that Matthew was not merely a shadowy scholar in the sixteenth century, but a ruthless spymaster serving Queen Elizabeth’s spymaster William Cecil and a prominent member of the enigmatic 'School of Night.' Surrounded by legendary historical figures—including playwright Christopher Marlowe, mathematician Thomas Harriot, and poet Walter Raleigh—Diana must master complex Tudor etiquette, navigate deadly religious espionage, and confront Matthew’s terrifyingly darker, younger self. Harkness, a distinguished professor of Renaissance history, brings the muddy streets of Blackfriars, the alchemical laboratories of Prague, and the courtly intrigue of the Holy Roman Empire to life with breathtaking academic scholarship and sensory richness.

Narrator Jennifer Ikeda delivers a tour-de-force performance, voicing a massive international cast spanning French vampires, Elizabethan aristocrats, Bohemian alchemists, and Scots witches with exquisite linguistic fidelity and atmospheric grace. The romantic chemistry between Diana and Matthew deepens with mature, complex intimacy across every chapter. For listeners who crave scholarly Dark Academia, meticulous Tudor history, and sweeping supernatural romance on a single monthly credit, *Shadow of Night* is an irresistible twenty-five-hour journey.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jennifer Ikeda

Jennifer Ikeda’s narration on Shadow of Night is pure acoustic perfection. Ikeda navigates sixteenth-century English dialects, French court phrases, and Latin invocations with effortless scholarly authority, never allowing the dense historical terminology to stall the narrative momentum. Her vocalization of Matthew’s brooding, archaic intensity contrasts gorgeously with Diana’s modern American wit and determination, infusing their dangerous courtship with palpable romantic tension. Richly textured and pristinely produced, this twenty-five-hour audiobook is a genuine masterclass in historical fantasy narration.
