Diana Gabaldon\u2019s Sweeping Thirty-Two-Hour Highland Epic of Time Travel and Romance in Audio

While enjoying a second honeymoon in the Scottish Highlands following the brutal end of World War II, former British combat nurse Claire Randall walks through a prehistoric standing stone circle at Craigh na Dun and is violently propelled backward through time to the year 1743. Thrust into a savage, war-torn Scotland on the brink of the Jacobite Rising, Claire finds herself suspected of being an English spy and is forced to marry the young, chivalrous Highland warrior Jamie Fraser for her own protection. What begins as a desperate marriage of convenience soon ignites into an all-consuming, epoch-spanning romance that tests Claire's modern medical skills and divided loyalties between two centuries.
