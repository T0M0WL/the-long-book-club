About this Audiobook

Refusing to abandon Darlington to demonic purgatory, Alex Stern and her reluctant Lethe allies raid Yale's forbidden archives to open a doorway straight into Hell.

Alex Stern Storms the Underworld in Leigh Bardugo's Sixteen-Hour Occult Sequel

Following the blood-soaked revelations of *Ninth House*, Alex Stern is determined to pull her vanished mentor Darlington out of Hell—even if Lethe House forbids it and Yale's ancient secret societies would rather let him burn. Recruiting a desperate circle of reluctant allies, including the meticulous Dawes and the dangerous vampire-turned-academic Turner, Alex scours New Haven's hidden vaults for arcane texts and forbidden gateways. Meanwhile, a string of ritualistic faculty murders signals that something ancient and ravenous has already crossed into the mortal world.

On audio, *Hell Bent* widens the gothic campus scope of Ninth House into a pulse-pounding occult heist without sacrificing its biting academic grit. The interplay between desperate archive research, Latin incantations, and sudden bursts of supernatural violence makes for gripping headphone listening. Highlighted in our Dark Academia alongside Katabasis, this sixteen-hour sequel proves that scholarly obsession is even deadlier when the underworld starts keeping score.

Delivering over sixteen hours of dark magic, moral recklessness, and razor-sharp character tension for a single credit, *Hell Bent* is an essential next step for anyone hooked by Alex Stern's bleak, unforgiving world.

Michael David Axtell adds haunting weight to Darlington's transformed presence and the sinister underworld sequences.
