About this Audiobook

After twenty years of relentless study, Sciona becomes the first woman admitted to the High Magistry at the University of Magics and Industry—only to uncover the horrific human cost powering Tiran's utopia.

M.L. Wang's Searing Fifteen-Hour Dark Academia Masterpiece of Hard Magic and Imperial Guilt

For twenty years, Sciona Freynan has devoted every waking hour to mathematical precision and arcane syntax, determined to become the first woman ever admitted to the High Magistry at Tiran's University of Magics and Industry. Yet when she finally claims her white robe, her arrogant male colleagues assign her a Kwen janitor—a survivor from the nomadic tribes beyond the city's protective barrier—instead of a qualified lab assistant. As Sciona and Thomil begin decoding the ancient source equations that siphon energy into Tiran's gleaming streets, they uncover a horrifying truth about what their utopia is actually burning for fuel.

On audio, M.L. Wang—already celebrated on our shelves for The Sword of Kaigen—delivers a standalone triumph that stands shoulder to shoulder with R.F. Kuang's Babel. Because Wang's magic operates like mechanical computer programming hammered onto spellograph keys, hearing the rhythmic click of calculations give way to gut-wrenching ethical horror creates unbearable headphone suspense. Featured in our Dark Academia Collection alongside The Will of the Many, this fifteen-hour listen fuses clockwork hard magic with devastating institutional critique.

Spanning fifteen hours and twenty-one minutes for a single Audible credit, *Blood Over Bright Haven* is one of the fiercest, most emotionally unforgettable standalone fantasy listens of the modern era. It rewards your token with razor-sharp pacing, complex moral reckoning, and a climax that lingers long after the final chapter.

Narrator Soundcheck: Moira Quirk

Fan-favorite narrator Moira Quirk turns in a tour-de-force performance across fifteen hours. Quirk captures Sciona's prickly academic arrogance, obsessive intellectual drive, and eventual shattering grief with remarkable vocal nuance, while voicing Thomil and his young niece Carra with quiet warmth and dignity. Her crisp diction keeps complex magical theory effortless to follow, backed by pristine studio engineering.
