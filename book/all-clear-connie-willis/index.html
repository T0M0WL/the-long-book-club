About this Audiobook

Stranded across wartime Britain as V-weapons fall and every return drop fails to open, Oxford historians Polly, Eileen, and Mike scramble through bombed streets and hospital wards to prevent history from unraveling.

Twenty-Four Hours of Defiant Hope Closing Connie Willis's Epic Blitz Duology

Picking up immediately from the shocking realizations of Blackout, Oxford historians Polly, Eileen, and Michael find themselves marooned deeper inside wartime London as the Blitz intensifies toward V-1 buzz-bombs, rationing fatigue, and heartbreaking personal losses. Unable to reach the 2060 retrieval net—and terrified that their inadvertent meddling with Dunkirk soldiers, evacuated children, and theatrical Shakespeare troupes may already have broken history—the stranded team must hold their nerves while waiting for rescue.

Listening to *All Clear* in audio brings Connie Willis's sweeping World War II and Time Travel diptych to an unforgettable, deeply moving crescendo. Across nearly twenty-four hours of atmospheric Historical Fiction and poignant Drama, Willis honors the fire-watchers saving St. Paul's Cathedral, London's ambulance crews, and everyday acts of kindness that kept a bruised city standing.

Spanning twenty-three hours and forty-one minutes for a single Audible credit, *All Clear* wraps up *Oxford Time Travel* with tears, hard-earned triumph, and one of Science Fiction's finest finales.
