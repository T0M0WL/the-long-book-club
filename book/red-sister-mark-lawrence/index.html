About this Audiobook

Rescued from the gallows at age nine, Nona Grey is brought to the Convent of Sweet Mercy, where martial nuns train young girls in blade combat, deadly poisons, and shadow magic on a dying, frozen world.

A Masterpiece of Deadly Sisterhood, Martial Precision, and Ice-Bound Survival

Opening with one of the most famous first lines in contemporary fantasy literature\\u2014*"It is important, when killing a nun, to ensure that you bring an army of sufficient size"*\\u2014Mark Lawrence\\u2019s *Red Sister* is a breathtaking, razor-sharp masterpiece. Set on Abeth, a dying planet trapped between two advancing polar ice caps where only a narrow green corridor remains habitable beneath the reflected light of a failing orbital mirror, the novel follows Nona Grey. Accused of murder and dragged to the gallows at just nine years old, Nona is rescued by the enigmatic Abbess Glass and brought to the Convent of Sweet Mercy.

In this celebrated audio production, British narrator Helen Duff delivers a tour-de-force reading that captures the novel\\u2019s ferocious kinetic energy and intimate emotional resonance. Sweet Mercy is no quiet sanctuary; its novices are divided into four martial disciplines, studying stealth under the Grey Sisters, hand-to-hand combat under the Red Sisters, mysticism under the Holy Sisters, and esoteric magic under the Mystic Sisters. Duff brilliantly voices Nona\\u2019s feral wariness, her fierce devotion to her found family of novice sisters, and the lethal grace of combat. Fans who loved the dark school training and explosive vengeance of The Poppy War or the gritty assassin craft in The Lies of Locke Lamora will fall head over heels for Lawrence\\u2019s universe.

Clocking in at nearly 20 hours of impeccably choreographed combat, political conspiracy, and deep mythological lore, *Red Sister* represents sublime credit value on Audible. Lawrence avoids coming-of-age cliches to deliver a dark, fiercely loyal story about friendship forged in blood and discipline. Lawrence avoids all coming-of-age sentimentality to forge an ironclad, blood-drenched testament to sisterhood, loyalty, and the lethal beauty of physical discipline.
