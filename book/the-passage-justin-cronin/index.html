About this Audiobook

A government experiment goes wrong, unleashing a viral apocalypse of vampire-like beings. A century later, a colony of survivors must leave their sanctuary to save the world, guided by a girl who has been alive since it all began.

Justin Cronin’s Sweeping Thirty-Six-Hour Epic of Viral Vampires and Human Survival in Audio

Beginning as a breakneck government conspiracy thriller in the snowbound mountains of Colorado, a clandestine military experiment involving an ancient Bolivian virus intended to extend human life spirals into catastrophic failure. Twelve death-row inmates transformed into swift, telepathic, and near-immortal viral predators break containment, collapsing American civilization within weeks. Nearly a century later in a fortified California outpost where survival depends upon battery-powered spotlights, a peaceful colony of survivors discovers an ageless six-year-old girl named Amy Harper Bellafonte, who carries the virus without its monstrous thirst and holds the miraculous key to humanity's salvation. Escaping the catastrophic massacre at the high-security compound with FBI agent Brad Wolgast, Amy spends decades in the remote Oregon wilderness before entering the lives of Peter Jaxon, Alicia Donadio, and the embattled scouts of the California compound.

On audio, *The Passage* succeeds on a grand literary and cinematic scale that elevates the vampire genre into high art. Narrator Scott Brick brings his signature elegiac warmth and emotional sensitivity to Cronin’s lush prose, effortlessly navigating the jarring generational transition from modern FBI agents and death-row cells to the frontier community of First Colony. Highlighted as a cornerstone of our Horror and Post-Apocalyptic collections alongside epics like Swan Song and The Stand, this thirty-seven-hour recording turns an apocalyptic nightmare into a profound auditory study of memory, sacrifice, and found family.
