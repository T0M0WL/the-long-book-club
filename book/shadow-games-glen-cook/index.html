Nearly Ten Hours Marching the Tattered Company South Toward Ancient Khatovar

Rising from the ashes of The White Rose alongside a suddenly mortal Lady, Croaker leads a dangerously small remnant of survivors on an audacious sea-and-overland trek southward toward the Black Company's mythic cradle of Khatovar. Hiring out first through volatile tropical realms before slamming straight into the ruthless Shadowmasters ruling around Taglios, the unit must survive treacherous city-state politics, terrifying flying nightmares, and old Taken enemies who refuse to stay buried.

In audio form, *Shadow Games* revitalizes Cook's Military Fantasy and Grimdark with sweltering jungle atmosphere and dense Dark Fantasy mystery. Pairing lean Sword and Sorcery skirmishes with cynical Low Fantasy pragmatism—plus a wry new standard-bearer in Murgen—transforms a desperate road trip into rich southern empire intrigue.

Qualifying under our Grimdark series exception at nine hours and forty minutes for one credit, *Shadow Games* sets up every betrayal that erupts inside Dreams of Steel.
