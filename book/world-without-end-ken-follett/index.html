World Without End

Author: Ken Follett | Narrator: John Lee

About this Audiobook

Two centuries after the building of Kingsbridge Cathedral, four children witness a lethal royal secret in the shadow of the Black Death and the Hundred Years' War.

The Magnificent 46-Hour Medieval Masterpiece That Rivals Pillars of the Earth

Spanning forty-five and a half breathlessly immersive hours, *World Without End* returns listeners to the fictional priory town of Kingsbridge two centuries after the events of The Pillars of the Earth. The story begins on All Hallows' Eve in 1327, when four children—the visionary builder Merthin, the ambitious monk Ralph, the fiercely intelligent herbalist Caris, and the pious thief Gwenda—flee into the forest and witness two men slain by a fugitive knight. The knight buries a dangerous secret letter and takes refuge in the priory, binding the four youngsters to a lethal oath of silence that echoes across five turbulent decades of English history.

Ken Follett's genius lies in intertwining vast socio-historical tides with intimate human ambition, love, and vengeance. As England is plunged into the Hundred Years' War with France and the apocalyptic devastation of the Black Death sweeps across Europe, Kingsbridge becomes a microcosm of a dying feudal order battling against modern science and architectural daring. Caris fights medieval superstition and monastic corruption to build a hospital, while Merthin dreams of raising the tallest spire in Christendom over the old stone cathedral. The audio format turns this sprawling tapestry into an addictive, soap-operatic epic where every chapter delivers immense narrative payoff, romantic tension, and visceral historical detail.

For audiobook listeners seeking the absolute gold standard of listening value on a single Audible credit, *World Without End* is an essential titan. Narrator John Lee is in peak form, carrying the forty-six-hour marathon with an imperious, aristocratic theatricality and emotional resonance that keeps listeners utterly spellbound. Alongside companion panoramic sagas like War and Peace and The Pillars of the Earth, it stands as a towering achievement of Historical Fiction that will devour weeks of commutes with pure, unadulterated storytelling pleasure.

Narrator Soundcheck: John Lee

John Lee delivers an undisputed masterpiece of vocal stamina, bringing the bustling streets, priory halls, and battlefield skirmishes of medieval Kingsbridge into vivid life. Lee handles dozens of distinct accents with razor-sharp authority, contrasting Caris’s fiery defiance against Ralph’s thuggish cruelty and Prior Godwyn’s oily, sanctimonious greed. His narrative cadence possesses a driving, rhythmic urgency that carries listeners through forty-six hours without a single lull in momentum, setting the benchmark for epic historical narration.
