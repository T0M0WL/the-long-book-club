About this Audiobook

In the religious paranoia of 1543 London, Matthew Shardlake hunts a meticulous killer slaughtering church reformers and lawyers in ritualistic tableaux modeled after the Book of Revelation.

Twenty-One Hours of Apocalyptic Serial Murder Stalking 1543 Tudor London

London in 1543 is simmering with religious persecution: Protestant books are burned in Smithfield, zealots preach end-times doom, and King Henry VIII prepares to marry the quiet reformer Catherine Parr. When Shardlake's own close friend—Lincoln's Inn lawyer Roger Elliard—is found brutally murdered inside a locked privy with an odd religious signet left behind, Shardlake and Jack Barak realize a methodical killer is staging each slaying according to the Seven Vials of Wrath from the Book of Revelation.

On audio, *Revelation* plays like the Tudor grandfather of modern serial-killer Mystery and classic fair-play Whodunnit. Moving seamlessly from dark apothecary backrooms and Bedlam's stark wards to Archbishop Cranmer's fraught palace circles, this fourth installment shines on our Historical Mystery and Historical Fiction hubs for its chilling psychological depth.

Clocking in at twenty-one hours and two minutes for a single Audible credit, *Revelation* delivers twenty-one gripping hours of theological clues, genuine dread, and tender personal stakes for Matthew Shardlake.
