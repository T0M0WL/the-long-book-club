Dalinar Kholin’s Devastating Past and the Fall of Kholinar in Colossal High-Fantasy Audio

In the earth-shattering third volume of The Stormlight Archive, humanity finds itself driven from its strongholds as the ancient city of Urithiru becomes their final refuge against the awakened Voidbringers. Highprince Dalinar Kholin seeks to forge an unprecedented coalition of distrustful global monarchs, but his crusade is threatened by the agonizing return of his long-suppressed memories as the Blackthorn—a ruthless warlord who burned cities and slaughtered innocents in cold blood. Across the realm, Kaladin journeys home to face his estranged family, while Shallan, Adolin, and Elhokar embark on a desperate covert mission into the corrupted, starving capital of Kholinar. Facing the tragic descent of the royal court and uncovering the shocking cosmic origins of humanity and the spren, the fractured Radiants must fight across the surreal, bead-filled cognitive realm of Shadesmar to prevent total annihilation.

On audio, *Oathbringer* delivers an overwhelming, tear-inducing emotional punch that sets a new standard for character-driven epic fantasy. Michael Kramer’s delivery of Dalinar’s desperate confession and climactic defiance against the god of hatred resonates with raw, trembling authority, while Kate Reading explores Shallan’s fractured psyche as her alternate identities, Veil and Radiant, begin taking terrifying control. Positioned alongside Words of Radiance and Rhythm of War in our Epic Fantasy library, this fifty-five-hour epic turns philosophical reflections on redemption and personal accountability into an unforgettable, deeply cathartic headphone journey.

Spanning over fifty-five hours of magnificent world-building for a single credit, this audiobook offers an unbeatable return on your listening investment. Sanderson’s trademark 'Sanderlanche' finale lasts for hours, weaving together multiple battlefronts, magical revelations, and emotional breakthroughs with staggering storytelling craftsmanship. Spending a monthly token on this profound fantasy masterpiece secures weeks of transportive companionship for long commutes and evening sessions, delivering an inspiring meditation on human fallibility that reminds listeners that the most important step a person can take is always the next one.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer, Kate Reading

Michael Kramer and Kate Reading elevate Sanderson’s most emotionally demanding volume with profound dramatic resonance. Kramer’s performance during Dalinar’s traumatic flashbacks is staggering, capturing the bloodthirsty roar of the young Blackthorn and the quiet, broken grief of an aging king seeking redemption with equal conviction. Reading navigates Shallan’s increasingly disoriented mind with exceptional psychological subtlety, giving distinct accents and cadences to Veil and Radiant. The production features immaculate studio mastering, flawless pacing, and pristine sonic separation that keeps all fifty-five hours thoroughly absorbing.
