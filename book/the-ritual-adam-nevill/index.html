About this Audiobook

Reuniting for a hiking trip through the Arctic Circle wilderness of Sweden, four old university friends take a fateful shortcut into an untouched primeval forest where an ancient pagan entity hunts from the canopy.

Over Eleven Harrowing Hours Trapped in Sweden's Primeval Pagan Woods

Adam Nevill’s modern Folk Horror benchmark opens with a deceptively familiar premise: four old university friends reunite for a trekking holiday across the remote Arctic Circle wilderness of northern Sweden. When they cut through an uncharted tract of ancient spruce forest to save time, they stumble upon a gutted elk hanging from the branches, an abandoned house stuffed with pagan effigies, and the sickening realization that something vast and ancient is stalking them through the trees.

What elevates *The Ritual* above ordinary Survival Horror is both its razor-sharp psychological realism—capturing the petty resentments and physical exhaustion of four out-of-shape friends turning on one another—and its bold, divisive second-half shift into brutal Scandinavian cult territory. On audio, every snapped twig, rain-soaked night in a leaking tent, and whispered argument feels painfully close, sitting proudly alongside The Reddening and Slewfoot: A Tale of Bewitchery as essential woodland terror.

At eleven hours and thirty-five minutes for one Audible credit, *The Ritual* is a lean, relentless endurance test that will make you think twice before ever leaving a marked hiking trail.

Narrator Soundcheck: Matthew Lloyd Davies

Matthew Lloyd Davies turns eleven and a half hours of sodden wilderness misery into pure audio adrenaline. He nails the distinct British banter, passive-aggressive friction, and rising panic of Luke and his friends—and when the story pivots into its darker, more grotesque second act, Davies shifts seamlessly into raw, exhausted desperation that keeps the tension pinned to the wall.
