About this Audiobook

Betrayed by his guild and permanently cursed into a lowly level-one goblin monster, former raid leader Oren must build a tribal stronghold from scratch to exact his revenge.

Shemer Kuznits's Foundational Base-Building Masterpiece and Jeff Hays's Iconic Goblin Kingdom Performance

In the fully immersive virtual reality world of *New Era Online*, Oren is a legendary raid leader and top-tier guild officer poised to claim ultimate dominion over the game's contested territories. But on the eve of his greatest triumph, his closest comrades betray him, executing an illicit black-market scroll that permanently resets his avatar, wipes his bank account, and traps his consciousness in the body of a despised, level-one green monster: a lowly goblin. Unable to log out until his massive player bounty is repaid, Oren refuses to accept his fate. Utilizing his elite raid management experience, deep understanding of game mechanics, and sheer vengeful spite, he begins uniting scattered woodland monsters, erecting mud-brick fortifications, and constructing a formidable goblin clan dedicated to crushing the player guilds that cast him aside.

On audio, *Life Reset* is universally revered as the foundational masterpiece that defined the modern base-building LitRPG subgenre. Narrator Jeff Hays delivers a star-making vocal performance that establishes the signature Soundbooth Theater style, expertly blending gritty survival intensity with brilliant tactical problem-solving. Listening to Oren manage timber supplies, defend against feral predators, and train cowardly goblin shamans into disciplined infantry units feels intensely satisfying and tactile. Alongside progression fantasy landmarks like Dungeon Crawler Carl and The Blade Itself, this epic tale stands as an essential pillar of our LitRPG & GameLit collection.

Clocking in at an immense twenty-four hours and forty-one minutes, *Life Reset* delivers unbeatable single-credit value for lovers of 4X strategy, RTS games, and underdog fantasy. Soundbooth Theater’s crisp audio engineering, immersive forest ambient soundscapes, and distinct monster voices make every construction project and defensive siege pulse with high-stakes urgency. Investing a single monthly credit here unlocks an unforgettable, deeply addictive listening experience that kicks off one of the greatest completed sagas in contemporary digital audio, leading directly into Life Reset: EvP.
