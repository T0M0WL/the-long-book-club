About this Audiobook

Facing down an unprecedented malevolent force, Swan, a young girl with the ability to heal the earth, and an unlikely mix of survivors must traverse a nuclear wasteland to save humanity.

Robert McCammon’s Colossal Thirty-Four-Hour Post-Apocalyptic Epic of Magic, Wastelands, and Rebirth in Audio

Following an instantaneous nuclear exchange between superpowers that incinerates global civilization and blots out the sun in eternal volcanic ash, the shattered remnants of humanity must struggle to survive across a frozen, radioactive American wasteland. In the ruins of Kansas, a young girl named Swan possesses a miraculous, dormant magical ability to coax life from dead earth. Protected by a gigantic former wrestler named Josh Hutchins and guided by an eccentric bag lady carrying a mystical glass ring, Swan embarks on an epic overland trek toward salvation, relentlessly pursued by a sadistic child soldier named Roland Croninger and the shape-shifting demonic entity known as the Man with the Scarlet Eye. Along their perilous road through the charred ruins of the American heartland, they are joined by Sister Creep, a traumatized New York street preacher bearing a crown of glass, as rival armies of fascist survivalists clash over the final atomic launch codes.

In its audio format, *Swan Song* unfolds with the sweeping, operatic majesty of an ancient myth retold around a flickering campfire in the dark.
