Sarah A. Parker's 21-Hour Lyrical Fantasy of Fallen Moons, Ancient Dragons, and Fated Enemies

A sweeping, poetic sensation that took the fantasy community by storm, Sarah A. Parker’s *When the Moon Hatched* is a lush, twenty-one-hour romantic fantasy set in an ancient world where dragons are born from shattered, falling celestial moons. The narrative follows Raeve, a lethal, emotionally guarded assassin working for an underground rebellion who possesses no memory of her traumatic past. When a high-stakes mission goes catastrophically wrong and she is captured by the ruthless crown, her path collides with Kaan Vaegor, a battle-hardened dragon king whose grief, hidden secrets, and burning connection to Raeve threaten to tear down the world's elemental balance, setting off a dangerous chain of events that could burn the entire world to ash.

In audio format, Parker’s ornate, lyrical prose sparkles with a dreamy, mythic cadence that feels tailor-made for vocal storytelling. Where silent reading can sometimes feel heavy due to the unique poetic structure and extensive world terminology, the spoken word transforms Raeve’s fractured memories and the elemental dragon lore into a haunting acoustic tapestry. Nestled proudly in our Epic Romantasy collection alongside Kushiel's Dart, this recording provides the meditative, atmospheric room needed for the multi-layered mystery of Raeve’s identity to unfold with genuine emotional power, turning every poetic line and dragon flight into an unforgettable, atmospheric audio feast.

The novel combines visceral dragon skirmishes with deeply felt emotional resonance and intricate magical mysteries that reward close, dedicated listening. It is an enchanting, darkly romantic listening journey that will linger in your imagination long after the final chapter, making it a must-listen for anyone who loves rich fantasy prose heartbreaking romantic devotion, and epic, unforgettable dragon lore.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sarah Mollo-Christensen, Fajer Al-Kaisi

Sarah Mollo-Christensen and Fajer Al-Kaisi provide an evocative dual-narrator performance that brings the poetic language and elemental magic of Sarah A. Parker’s world to life. Mollo-Christensen captures Raeve’s defensive, prickly exterior and underlying trauma with quiet emotional nuance, while Al-Kaisi brings commanding authority, deep sorrow, and smoldering romantic warmth to Kaan Vaegor’s chapters. The contrast between their voices complements the shifting perspectives across twenty-one hours, backed by crystal-clear, balanced audio engineering.
