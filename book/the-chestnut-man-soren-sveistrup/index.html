About this Audiobook

At a gruesome Copenhagen crime scene where a mother is found butchered with a severed hand, detectives discover a handmade chestnut figurine bearing the fingerprint of a cabinet minister's daughter murdered a year earlier.

A Ferocious, Autumnal Danish Nightmare Crafted by the Mastermind Behind The Killing

Written by S\\u00f8ren Sveistrup\\u2014the visionary Danish creator and head writer who sparked the global Nordic Noir television explosion with *The Killing* (Forbrydelsen)\\u2014*The Chestnut Man* is a ferocious, breathtaking psychological procedural. In the windswept, rain-drowned suburbs of Copenhagen, police discover the brutally mutilated corpse of a mother with one hand cleanly amputated. Dangling above the bloody scene is a crude handmade figurine constructed from chestnuts and matchsticks. When forensic examiners pull a latent thumbprint from the chestnut doll belonging to the kidnapped daughter of Denmark\\u2019s Minister for Social Affairs\\u2014a twelve-year-old girl declared dead a year earlier\\u2014two mismatched detectives are thrown into an explosive national conspiracy.

The audio production captures Sveistrup\\u2019s cinematic narrative engine with chilling, visceral intimacy that makes every chapter feel urgent and impossible to pause. Ambitious detective Naia Thulin, desperate to escape homicide for cybercrime, and Mark Hess, a burned-out Europol investigator disgraced and exiled from The Hague, clash across sodden Danish countryside, forensic morgues, and tense parliamentary corridors. The pacing never flags across its sprawling 17-hour runtime, matching the relentless forensic scrutiny of The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo while exploring poignant themes of institutional negligence, foster-care trauma, and political scapegoating.

For listeners seeking an unputdownable autumn binge that justifies every second of its runtime, *The Chestnut Man* is an exemplary return on an Audible credit. Sveistrup orchestrates multiple parallel investigations and gruesome puzzle-box reveals with the seasoned precision of a master dramatist, culminating in a breathless, snow-dusted climax that leaves you completely drained yet desperate for more. Sveistrup infuses every rain-soaked pavement and secluded forest clearing with a palpable sense of predatory menace, creating a sensory experience that feels tailor-made for dark evening commutes. If you want a chilling, atmospheric Scandinavian mystery that will make you double-check your front door locks and view children's autumnal crafts with permanent suspicion, this is your next essential listen.

Narrator Soundcheck: Peter Noble & Charlotte Mel\u00e9n

Narrated with crisp Scandinavian foreboding, the audio performance captures the relentless chill of Sveistrup\u2019s Copenhagen.
