About this Audiobook

In 1777–1778, Jamie and Claire are swept into the Battle of Saratoga while Brianna and Roger face corporate espionage in 1980s Scotland.

The Battle of Saratoga, Naval Espionage, and the Modern Echoes of Lallybroch

Spanning forty-six thrilling hours, *An Echo in the Bone* is Diana Gabaldon’s structured seventh installment in the *Outlander* series, interweaving four major storylines across two centuries with narrative mastery. Set between 1777 and 1778 in the heat of the Revolutionary War, Claire and Jamie Fraser leave Fraser’s Ridge to return to Scotland to retrieve Jamie’s printing press. Instead, they are swept directly into the Northern Campaign, participating in the pivotal Battles of Saratoga, where Jamie faces his secret illegitimate son William Ransom across opposing battle lines.

Meanwhile, William—now an idealistic British redcoat officer—embarks on dangerous courier missions through the Dismal Swamp, unaware of his true parentage. In Philadelphia, Lord John Grey navigates diplomatic espionage, while in the twentieth century, Brianna and Roger have returned to 1980s Scotland with their children to treat their daughter’s heart defect. Settling at a restored Lallybroch, Brianna takes a job as an engineer, only to uncover sinister corporate espionage and time-travel manuscripts left behind by Jamie two centuries earlier.

Narrator Davina Porter shines with dazzling agility across this massive canvas. Porter effortlessly distinguishes William’s British arrogance, Lord John’s polished wit, Roger’s modern Scottish pragmatism, and Jamie’s commanding battlefield baritone. Her depiction of Saratoga is visceral and historically authoritative. For listeners seeking multi-perspective Military History and sweeping time-travel romance, *An Echo in the Bone* delivers sensational value on a single credit.

Narrator Soundcheck: Davina Porter

Davina Porter’s narration on An Echo in the Bone is a clinic in multi-character pacing and dialect control. Porter transitions flawlessly between the gritty gunfire of the Saratoga battlefields, the refined London drawing rooms of Lord John Grey, and the modern 1980s Scottish home life of Brianna and Roger. Her voices for young William Ransom and adult Ian Murray are rich, expressive, and distinct. Captured with pristine audio balance across forty-six hours, this production is an extraordinary listening triumph.
