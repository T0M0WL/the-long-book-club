About this Audiobook

Reaching the ninth floor's catastrophic faction war, Carl and Donut lead an open galactic revolution against the syndicate in a mammoth 28-hour audio tour de force.

Matt Dinniman's Monumental Ninth-Floor Apocalypse and Jeff Hays's Epic 28-Hour Revolutionary Masterpiece

Reaching the long-dreaded ninth floor of the World Dungeon—the legendary Faction Wars—Carl and Princess Donut find themselves at the center of an all-out intergalactic conflagration. Unlike previous levels where crawlers fought localized monsters or survived targeted sponsor hits, floor nine places hundreds of surviving crawlers in command of massive planetary armies, orbital strike craft, and divine avatars battling for control of territorial capitals. With the governing syndicates openly fracturing and galactic citizens protesting in the streets of distant star systems, Carl stops operating as a mere gladiator and steps fully into his role as a revolutionary icon, orchestrating devastating systemic strikes designed to burn the entire corrupt galactic hierarchy to the ground.

Following the high-stakes cosmic warfare of The Eye of the Bedlam Bride, *This Inevitable Ruin* is an absolute triumph of long-form speculative storytelling. Spanning an astonishing twenty-eight hours and forty minutes, narrator Jeff Hays delivers what may be his most ambitious, vocally demanding performance to date. The sheer acoustic scope of commanding massive battle lines, handling emotional character deaths, and delivering Carl’s incendiary broadcast speeches turns your headphones into the command deck of a galactic rebellion. Standing shoulder-to-shoulder with monolithic sci-fi sagas like Red Rising and The Expanse, this volume proves that the LitRPG & GameLit space can achieve profound socio-political weight.

For audiobook listeners seeking the absolute holy grail of credit value, *This Inevitable Ruin* is an indispensable masterpiece. Nearly twenty-nine hours of gripping, heart-stopping narrative power on a single Audible credit represents an unbeatable return on investment. Soundbooth Theater’s peerless production quality, thunderous combat soundscapes, and flawless dramatic pacing make this monumental seventh installment a crowning achievement in contemporary audio publishing, paving the way for A Parade of Horribles.
