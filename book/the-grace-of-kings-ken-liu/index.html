About this Audiobook

Ken Liu's 21-Hour Silkpunk Masterpiece of Empire, Airships, and Moral Philosophy

Clocking in at over twenty-one sweeping hours, *The Grace of Kings* is Ken Liu’s audacious, foundational epic that established the 'silkpunk' subgenre in modern fantasy. Inspired by the historical Chu-Han Contention that birthed China's Han Dynasty, Liu transports listeners to the island archipelago of Dara—a realm unified under a tyrannical emperor through conscripted labor, colossal mechanical engineering, and restless shapeshifting deities. When the empire fractures, two unlikely comrades emerge at the forefront of the rebellion: Kuni Garu, an irreverent, cunning street bandit with a heart of instinctive empathy, and Mata Zyndu, a towering, vengeful aristocratic warrior dedicated to classical martial honor. Their sworn brotherhood shatters the old order, but as the throne sits vacant, their irreconcilable philosophies on governance, justice, and human nature ignite a titanic civil war that permanently alters the fate of Dara.

In spoken audio, Liu’s expansive, mythic prose achieves a hypnotic, bardic cadence reminiscent of ancient oral traditions. The auditory format brings the vibrant engineering of Dara to life with breathtaking clarity, from the rustling silk sails of soaring military airships and submersible whale-bone boats to the thunderous clatter of bamboo cavalry and the whispered schemes of court concubines. Alongside grand political sagas in our library like The Way of Kings and the sweeping historical tapestries of Shōgun, this production demonstrates how thoughtful speculative worldbuilding can seamlessly merge classical Asian storytelling with modern, morally complex political philosophy.

For a single monthly credit or token, twenty-one hours of *The Grace of Kings* delivers extraordinary narrative depth and staggering literary ambition. Liu resists simplistic tropes of good and evil, examining how revolutionary ideals inevitably curdle into autocratic compromise and bureaucratic tragedy. Anchored by sublime voice work and unforgettable dramatic confrontations, this monumental first volume represents an indispensable, value-packed cornerstone for any epic fantasy listener's permanent collection.

Narrator Soundcheck: Kevin Shen

British Audio Award nominee Kevin Shen delivers a tour-de-force narration that perfectly honors the mythic scope of Ken Liu’s silkpunk world. Shen approaches the text with classical oratorical elegance, imbuing the overarching narration with the weight of an imperial chronicler while seamlessly pivoting into distinct, emotive character performances. His contrast between Kuni Garu's relaxed, roguish street wit and Mata Zyndu's booming, aristocratic pride makes their philosophical clash electric. Engineered with crystalline studio fidelity and flawless pacing across nearly twenty-two hours, this recording avoids vocal fatigue and keeps complex dynastic rivalries effortlessly intelligible.
