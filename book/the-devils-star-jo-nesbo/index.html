About this Audiobook

During a blistering empty Oslo July heatwave while awaiting dismissal for drinking, Harry Hole must cooperate with his deadly crooked colleague Tom Waaler to catch a serial killer leaving five-pointed diamond pentagrams beneath victims' eyelids.

Fourteen Blistering Hours Closing the Oslo Trilogy Amid Summer Heat

Drinking steadily toward official police dismissal during a scorching empty July heatwave after Nemesis, Harry Hole gets one unwelcome final assignment when a young woman is found murdered in her flat with a severed finger and a tiny red diamond pentagram tucked under her eyelid. Forced reluctantly into close quarters with corrupt, lethal inspector Tom Waaler as fresh five-day murders multiply, Harry races both a serial killer and his own mortal enemy.

*The Devil's Star* caps Nesbø's interconnected Oslo arc with suffocating Nordic Noir urgency. High-wire Thriller tension crackles inside sharp Police Procedural deduction as twisted Crime clues lead Detective Hole straight into subterranean reckoning.

At fourteen hours and five minutes for a single credit, Book 5 brings explosive emotional closure right before winter returns in The Redeemer.
