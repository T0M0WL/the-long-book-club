About this Audiobook

Five years after the colony falls, the survivors mount a hunt for the original Twelve virals while unearthing horrors in a slave-labor concentration camp.

Post-Apocalyptic Grandeur: Scott Brick Deepens Cronin's Chilling, Sprawling Vampire Epic

In *The Twelve*, Justin Cronin broadens the canvas of his epic post-apocalyptic saga with breathless narrative ambition. Following the cataclysmic events of the first novel, the narrative jumps backward to the initial days of the contagion, introducing a fresh cast of characters desperately trying to survive the viral collapse in Denver, before hurtling forward to rejoin Peter, Alicia, and Amy in the year 97 A.V. Humanity discovers that killing one of the original twelve death-row inmate virals severs the telepathic hold over thousands of subordinate creatures, initiating a deadly crusade across the ruins of America toward a dystopian totalitarian regime known as the Homeland.

Narrator Scott Brick is in absolute peak form throughout this twenty-six-hour marathon. His melancholic, tension-drenched delivery perfectly suits Cronin’s rich literary prose, transforming what could be a pulpy vampire thriller into a sweeping, grief-soaked human tragedy. Brick navigates the disorienting temporal jumps and expansive ensemble cast with consummate ease. If you were mesmerized by the haunting beauty of The Passage or the apocalyptic chill of The Terror, this sequel is an indispensable listen.

With over twenty-six hours of immersive storytelling for a single credit, *The Twelve* is an unbeatable investment for horror and speculative fiction enthusiasts. Cronin combines cinematic action with deep existential reflections, ensuring every commute and marathon listening session flies by.
