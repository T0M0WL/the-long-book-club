About this Audiobook

In a brutal winter blizzard on the Welsh borders, Jackson Lamb's rejects pursue a missing teenager while ghosts from their past return for bloody reckoning.

A Frozen Welsh Border Standoff and the Tragic Price of Espionage

Spanning nearly eleven tense, snow-covered hours, *Joe Country* is widely recognized as the darkest and most emotionally harrowing entry in Mick Herron’s Slough House saga. In the tradecraft jargon of British intelligence, 'Joe Country' refers to the field—the harsh, unpredictable operational territory outside the safe confines of London offices. Still reeling from the tragic events of previous books, the slow horses find themselves pulled into an unofficial, off-the-books rescue mission when River Cartwright is asked to trace a rebellious teenager who has run away to join a sinister eco-commune led by a charismatic former mercenary.

Simultaneously, Jackson Lamb detects the lethal tremors of an old Cold War debt coming due. Frank Harkness, the sociopathic CIA rogue and architect of historical off-the-books assassinations, resurfaces with a private crew of heavily armed hitmen seeking retribution. As an unseasonal blizzard blankets the remote hills of Pembrokeshire, the various factions converge on a derelict farmhouse cut off from the outside world. What follows is a brutal, claustrophobic siege in sub-zero temperatures, where flashlight beams cut through driving snow and every tactical misstep proves fatal. Herron strips away the cozy workplace comedy of Farringdon, plunging his characters into raw, unforgiving survival horror.

The emotional gravity of *Joe Country* is devastating, culminating in a climactic loss that alters the trajectory of Slough House forever. Herron’s ability to transition from dry, sardonic banter to visceral, heartbreaking tragedy is unmatched in modern spy literature. Sean Barrett’s performance is nothing short of sublime, capturing the howling Welsh gale, the crunch of frozen snow, and the grim resolve of Jackson Lamb as he defends his rejects. It is an unforgettable, five-star audio experience.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sean Barrett

Sean Barrett’s narration on *Joe Country* is a masterclass in atmospheric suspense. He evokes the biting chill and isolation of the Welsh snowstorm with chilling vocal precision, lowering his gravelly baritone to mirror the grim tactical stakes. His Jackson Lamb remains shockingly sharp behind the foul exterior, delivering devastating one-liners that cut through the darkness. Barrett navigates the climactic farmhouse shootout with breathtaking intensity, delivering the novel’s tragic emotional revelations with understated, heartbreaking dignity.
