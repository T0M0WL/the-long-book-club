About this Audiobook

Shadow Moon is released from prison only to find himself in the middle of a war between old and new gods. A twenty-hour full-cast, cinematic mythic journey.

Neil Gaiman’s Mythic American Road Trip Epic in an Expansive Tenth Anniversary Full-Cast Edition

Released from prison days early following the sudden death of his beloved wife Laura in a tragic car crash, stoic ex-convict Shadow Moon accepts a job as a bodyguard and driver for an enigmatic, one-eyed con artist who calls himself Mr. Wednesday. As they embark on a strange, cross-country road trip across the roadside attractions, forgotten tourist traps, and quiet heartland towns of the American Midwest, Shadow discovers that Wednesday is actually the Norse Allfather Odin, quietly gathering ancient mythological deities brought to America by centuries of immigrant belief. These fading old gods—including Anubis, Bilquis, Kali, and Czernobog—are preparing to wage a desperate, apocalyptic war against the rising New Gods of technology, media, and global finance.

On audio, this Tenth Anniversary full-cast edition transforms Gaiman’s rich, melancholic road novel into an immersive American radio epic of astonishing emotional depth and atmospheric texture. The auditory contrast between dreary neon motels, quiet funeral parlors, roadside diner chatter, and surreal spiritual dreamscapes is rendered with exquisite acoustic texture that pulls you into the highway grit.
