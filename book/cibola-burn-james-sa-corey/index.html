About this Audiobook

The gold rush for alien worlds begins on Ilus, where Belter refugees, corporate mercenaries, and ancient machines collide.

Frontier Western in the Stars: Jefferson Mays Powers 20 Hours of Alien Catastrophe

In *Cibola Burn*, James S.A. Corey pivots from fleet engagements to craft a gritty, claustrophobic frontier sci-fi Western on the surface of Ilus—the first planet colonized through the Ring gates. When desperate Belter refugee squatters find themselves in an armed standoff with corporate security mercenaries from Royal Charter Energy, the UN dispatches Jim Holden to mediate. But when ancient alien machinery awakens beneath the surface, triggering global tsunamis, toxic rains, and blinding microbial plagues, the two warring factions are trapped together in a desperate struggle for survival.

Narrator Jefferson Mays is in brilliant form, handling the introduction of Adolphus Murtry—a chilling, hyper-competent corporate security chief whose cold sociopathy is delivered with unnerving calm. Mays captures the terrifying degradation of the colonists as blindness spreads through the camp, making the biological horror feel visceral and immediate. If you appreciated the tense survival dynamics of The Terror or the ecological intrigue of Dune, this frontier chapter is deeply compelling.

At over twenty hours on a single credit, *Cibola Burn* offers fantastic value. It grounds the grand expansion of humanity in raw, muddy, human desperation, proving that wherever humans go in the galaxy, they bring their oldest hatreds with them.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jefferson Mays

Jefferson Mays provides another stellar performance. His voice for Murtry is icily terrifying in its reasonable corporate cadence, contrasting sharply with Holden's exasperated moral decency. His portrayal of the Investigator's strange, fragmented consciousness remains a highlight, supported by pristine acoustic engineering throughout.
