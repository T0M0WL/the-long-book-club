Marble Hall Murders

Author: Anthony Horowitz | Narrator: Lesley Manville, Tim McMullan

Runtime: 17h 38m | Genre: Cozy Crime, Mystery, Historical Fiction, Whodunnit

About this Audiobook

Susan Ryeland returns to untangle a notorious cold case murder at Marble Hall through the posthumous pages of an unreleased Alan Conway manuscript. A thrilling 17.5-hour meta-mystery narrated by Lesley Manville and Tim McMullan.

Anthony Horowitz’s 17-Hour Masterwork of Cold Cases, Coded Manuscripts, and Screen Chemistry

Susan Ryeland has sworn off murder mysteries forever. After surviving the fallout of Alan Conway’s death and the harrowing events in Suffolk, Susan is determined to keep her distance from true crime, treacherous publishers, and Conway’s venomous literary estate. But when new evidence surfaces regarding a notorious historical murder that took place decades earlier at the magnificent country seat of Marble Hall, Susan finds herself inexorably drawn back into Conway’s orbit. Hidden among his private papers is an unreleased, deeply secretive Atticus Pünd manuscript—one that holds the key to solving not just the historical cold case, but a web of dangerous secrets that powerful figures will kill to keep buried.

What elevates *Marble Hall Murders* into an extraordinary audio triumph is the magnificent casting coup that reunites its screen stars. Lesley Manville returns as Susan Ryeland alongside Tim McMullan, who voices Atticus Pünd—re-creating the electric on-screen chemistry they share in the acclaimed BBC and Masterpiece PBS television series. Hearing Manville’s sharp, modern cadence interplay with McMullan’s aristocratic, deliberately measured delivery adds a rich layer of meta-narrative delight. Connecting seamlessly with Magpie Murders and Moonflower Murders, the audiobook edition achieves the ideal harmony between Golden Age nostalgia and contemporary psychological thriller.

At over seventeen and a half hours of impeccably plotted detection, *Marble Hall Murders* represents peak credit value for mystery enthusiasts. Horowitz once again delivers two intricately plotted, fully resolved mysteries within a single package, packing each storyline with brilliant anagrams, deceptive suspects, and satisfying reveals. Powered by two titans of British stage and screen at the height of their powers, this doorstopper whodunnit is an absolute must-listen for anyone who treasures classic detective fiction.

Narrator Soundcheck: Lesley Manville, Tim McMullan

Lesley Manville and Tim McMullan form an unbeatable audio partnership, translating their beloved screen dynamic into a sublime auditory showcase. Manville captures Susan Ryeland’s intelligent exasperation, moral courage, and acute editorial scrutiny with consummate ease, maintaining vibrant energy across long investigative passages. Tim McMullan brings his signature drawl and stately, resonant authority to Atticus Pünd, balancing dry humor with profound philosophical gravitas. The production quality from Penguin is pristine, capturing every subtle vocal shift and creating an unforgettable atmosphere across seventeen and a half hours.
