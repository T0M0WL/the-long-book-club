About this Audiobook

A reclusive billionaire buys early-access exclusivity to the world’s biggest full-dive VRMMO, saddling himself with an enormous guild target on his back and a talking, moon-hating divine axe named Frank.

A Hilarious, High-Stakes VRMMO Heist and Progression Adventure Powered by a Snarky Talking Axe

In the ultra-advanced virtual reality MMO *Convergent Worlds*, a mysterious economic system called the Ripple System allows every player's significant actions to trigger global, permanent world-altering events. Wealthy introvert Ned decides to buy up every single early-access pre-order key in existence to gain a solitary three-day head start in the game world, immediately painting an enormous target on his back as millions of furious players prepare to log in and hunt him down. To make matters worse, Ned’s starting companion item is Frank, a sentient, sarcastic, golden talking axe who possesses an immense ego, a deep hatred for moon-gods, and an endless stream of creative insults. Author Kyle Kirrin crafts a brilliantly funny, tactically complex VRMMO adventure that combines high-stakes dungeon heists with laugh-out-loud buddy comedy.

Listening to *Shadeslinger* on audio is an absolute comedic riot from start to finish that revitalizes the virtual-reality subgenre. The dynamic banter between Ned’s straight-man exasperation and Frank’s unhinged, booming ego translates into pure voice-acting gold, transforming standard video-game questing into an infectious, high-energy buddy comedy of epic proportions. Highlighted within our LitRPG & GameLit Audiobooks catalog, this production showcases how top-tier voice delivery can elevate virtual reality game mechanics, guild warfare, and stealth mechanics into an irresistible auditory treat that keeps you smiling and laughing through every single commute and chore.

Spanning nearly eighteen hours of crisp, tightly plotted adventure, this debut novel offers tremendous credit value for listeners looking for smart, engaging, and genuinely funny progression fiction. Kirrin provides intricate game systems with real mechanical depth, avoiding cheap overpowered tropes by forcing Ned and his allies to out-think, out-maneuver, and out-loot rival guilds through clever positioning and unorthodox tactics. A single credit delivers nearly twenty hours of sheer auditory joy, establishing one of the most entertaining, witty, and refreshing series in modern GameLit literature.

Narrator Soundcheck: Travis Baldree

Travis Baldree gives one of the funniest and most dynamic performances of his career, showcasing astonishing comedic range. His portrayal of Frank the Talking Axe is an instant classic—delivering arrogant, booming proclamations and bizarre cosmic grudges with impeccable comic timing that will have you laughing out loud in your car. Baldree perfectly balances Frank’s chaotic bravado against Ned’s grounded, dry-witted pragmatism. With immaculate sound mastering, snappy pacing, and crystal-clear voice delineation, this seventeen-hour production is an absolute masterclass in character-driven comedic narration.
