About this Audiobook

As the tyrannical Letherii Empire fractures under Emperor Rhulad Sengar's madness, the scarred Malazan Bonehunters make a daring amphibious invasion—while Karsa Orlong sails toward an inevitable duel of champions.

Nearly Forty-Four Hours as the Bonehunters Invade Lether in Cold Fury

With Letheras descending into police-state terror under Emperor Rhulad Sengar—madly resurrected time after time by his cursed sword—rebel financiers, Tiste Edur warriors, and enslaved scholars fight back from the shadows. Suddenly, out on the bleak eastern coast, Adjunct Tavore's fugitive Malazan Bonehunters make an audacious, unsupported amphibious landing—unleashing tactical chaos, cussers, and grim marine humor across an empire wholly unprepared for Malazan warfare.

Listening to *Reaper's Gale* brings the twin threads of House of Chains and Midnight Tides into explosive Military Fantasy harmony. Across nearly forty-four hours of Grimdark guerrilla ambushes, heartbreaking sacrifices by beloved heroes, and Karsa Orlong's showdown in the capital, Erikson proves the payoff of his grand Epic Fantasy structure.

Spanning forty-three hours and fifty-six minutes for one credit, *Reaper's Gale* is fierce, deeply moving, and ruthlessly satisfying.
