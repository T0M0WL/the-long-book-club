About this Audiobook

When a lobster fisherman hauls seven-year-old Sara out of Fjällbacka harbour with fresh water inside her lungs, Patrik Hedström investigates a quiet street hiding toxic secrets linked to a doomed 1920s stonecutter's family.

Sixteen Unforgettable Hours Linking Child Murder to 1920s Fjällbacka Sin

Opening in heartbreaking shock not long after The Preacher, a Fjällbacka fisherman pulls seven-year-old Sara—the daughter of Erica's close friend Charlotte—out of icy harbour water, only for autopsy results to reveal fresh bathwater in her lungs. While exhausted new parents Patrik and Erica wrestle sleepless nights beside baby Maja, police sift past jealous neighbors, struggling mothers, and 1920s flashbacks following doomed stonecutter Anders Andersson and cruel heiress Agnes.

*The Stonecutter* elevates coastal Nordic Noir into razor-sharp psychological territory. Every Police Procedural twist lands alongside agonizing family dysfunction, braiding century-old class venom cleanly into modern Crime, Detective, and Mystery suspense.

Spanning sixteen hours and two minutes for one credit, Book 3 marks Läckberg's earliest creative leap—earning huge acclaim before The Gallows Bird.
