The Night Circus

Author: Erin Morgenstern | Narrator: Jim Dale

About this Audiobook

Le Cirque des Rêves arrives without warning and opens only at nightfall. Behind the scenes of its black-and-white striped tents, two young magicians, Celia and Marco, are locked in a fierce competition of imagination where only one can be left standing.

Jim Dale Narrates an Enchanting Black-and-White Victorian Duel of Magical Artistry

Erin Morgenstern’s bewitching debut transports listeners into the gaslit, black-and-white world of *Le Cirque des Rêves*—a mysterious circus that arrives without warning, opens only after sunset, and disappears at dawn without a trace. Beneath its monochrome striped tents lies an astonishing carnival of impossible wonders: gardens sculpted entirely from pure ice, clouds of spun sugar that taste like autumnal nostalgia, pools of fragrant liquid scent, and vertical mazes where illusions blur with reality. Unbeknownst to the performers and the captivated patrons, the circus is actually the enchanting battleground for an intricate, high-stakes duel between two young illusionists, Celia and Marco, bound since childhood by their enigmatic mentors to compete in an ancient contest of magical imagination where only one can survive.

On audio, Morgenstern’s lush, sensory prose finds its ultimate artistic medium. The gentle ticking of the miraculous clock, the smoky aroma of caramel and roasted chestnuts, and the hushed whispers of patrons wandering through midnight pavilions are rendered with intoxicating auditory warmth. Highlighted as a standout favorite in our Magical Realism Collection and our Best Fiction Audiobooks catalog alongside enchanting historical fantasies, this performance turns an intricate battle of romantic imagination into a cozy, luxurious sensory retreat that wraps around the listener like a velvet cloak.

Spanning nearly fourteen hours of sublime, transportive storytelling, this audio edition delivers exceptional credit value for listeners who crave atmosphere, romance, and visual poetry. Morgenstern prioritizes intricate sensory texture, subtle sleight-of-hand, and tender emotional devotion over frantic sword fights, creating an enchanting dreamscape that listeners return to year after year. Narrator Soundcheck: Jim Dale

Legendary narrator Jim Dale—celebrated worldwide for his iconic vocal performances—delivers an absolute masterclass in atmospheric enchantment. His warm, theatrical baritone infuses Morgenstern’s sensory descriptions with vintage Victorian charm, turning every circus tent and clockwork marvel into pure auditory theater. Dale creates distinct, memorable vocal profiles for Celia, Marco, and the eccentric clockmaker Herr Thiessen with effortless nuance and flawless diction. Pristine studio production and immaculate dynamic pacing make all fourteen hours feel like an intimate, spoken-word spell that enchants from the opening curtain to the final bow.
