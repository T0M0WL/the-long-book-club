About this Audiobook

Operating as an undercover spy within the Spring Court, High Lady Feyre plays a deadly game of deceit before all-out continental war erupts against the King of Hybern to save Prythian from ruin.

Sarah J. Maas's 25-Hour War Climax of Spring Court Espionage, Immortal Alliances, and Continent-Shaking Sacrifice

Reaching a fever pitch of continental conflict and diplomatic intrigue, Sarah J. Maas’s *A Court of Wings and Ruin* delivers twenty-five monumental hours of high-stakes espionage, shifting immortal loyalties, and total war. Resuming her role as High Lady of the Night Court while operating deep undercover inside the Spring Court, Feyre plays a lethal game of deception, subtly dismantling Tamlin’s military infrastructure and poisoning his alliance with the tyrannical King of Hybern from within. When her treachery is inevitably unmasked, Feyre must flee across icy frontiers back to the Night Court, where the threat of catastrophic invasion looms over mortal and fae lands alike. To survive the slaughter, Feyre, Rhysand, and the Inner Circle must undertake perilous diplomatic missions to unite the fractured, mutually hostile High Lords of Prythian, strike terrifying pacts with primordial horrors like the Weaver and the Bone Carver, and lead armies into a colossal, continent-shaking clash where victory requires unimaginable personal sacrifice.

In audio format, the sprawling scale of Maas’s wartime epic translates into a pulse-pounding, panoramic listening experience. Where the massive cast of characters and intricate political treaties can feel overwhelming on the page, the spoken performance breathes visceral urgency into every tense High Lord summit, midnight infiltration, and aerial Illyrian dogfight. Featured proudly in our Epic Romantasy collection alongside predecessors A Court of Mist and Fury and A Court of Silver Flames, this recording provides the temporal room necessary to absorb every battlefield strategy, heartbreak, and jaw-dropping magical showdown, ensuring that the emotional payoffs of this trilogy-capping conflict land with maximum cinematic thunder.

Clocking in at over twenty-five hours, spending a credit on *A Court of Wings and Ruin* delivers colossal value for listeners who crave epic military fantasy woven with passionate romantic devotion. Maas orchestrates an explosive climax filled with triumphant battle cries, devastating losses, and poignant reunions that will leave you physically breathless. It stands as a monumental audio achievement that honors every hour invested in Feyre and Rhysand's journey, delivering a deeply satisfying, emotionally overwhelming conclusion to the original ACOTAR trilogy.

Narrator Soundcheck: Elizabeth Evans

Narrated by Elizabeth Evans in the official 10th Anniversary Recording (and originally recorded by Amanda Leigh Cobb), this twenty-five-hour epic showcases Evans’s commanding storytelling authority and battle-hardened vocal range. Evans, renowned as the signature voice of Maas’s Throne of Glass and Crescent City sagas, brings breathtaking emotional consistency to the wider Maasverse, seamlessly balancing Feyre’s sharp tactical mind with the raw grief of continental warfare and the fiery camaraderie of the Inner Circle. For those who enjoy multi-voice radio drama, GraphicAudio also produces a three-volume dramatized adaptation complete with clashing swords, spell sound effects, and full-cast voice acting, but Evans’s solo tour de force provides an unmatched, unified literary performance.
