Gregory David Roberts\u2019s Transportive Forty-Three-Hour Bombay Odyssey of Crime, Love, and Redemption

Following a daring daylight escape from an Australian maximum-security prison, convicted armed robber Lin arrives in the teeming, intoxicating streets of Bombay with a forged New Zealand passport, a fake name, and an empty wallet. Guided by the irrepressible street guide Prabaker, Lin finds a new home in the squalor of a sprawling slum, setting up a free medical clinic to serve his impoverished neighbors. However, his desperate search for survival and moral redemption soon draws him into the inner circle of the Bombay mafia under the philosopher-gangster Abdel Khader Khan, plunging him into the lethal worlds of black-market currency, counterfeit passports, and the bloody battlefields of Soviet-occupied Afghanistan.
