About this Audiobook

Kicked out by Rakel after another brutal drinking relapse and waking up covered in someone else's blood with zero memory, Harry Hole faces his darkest morning ever—and a relentless private hunt for brutal vengeance.

Over Seventeen Shattering Hours Facing Blackouts, Grief, and Personal Vengeance

Finding Harry Hole kicked out by Rakel in brutal alcoholic relapse after The Thirst, Book 12 opens at absolute rock bottom as Harry wakes inside his battered flat wearing bloodstained clothes and missing hours of memory. Moments later, unthinkable horror shatters everything left standing: Rakel has been stabbed dead inside her own kitchen, turning grieving widower Harry simultaneously into chief murder suspect and a ruthless vigilante tearing apart paroled predator Svein Finne's underworld.

*Knife* hits like a sledgehammer among modern Nordic Noir milestones. Replacing detached Police Procedural puzzlecraft with raw bereavement fuels shattering Detective Crime and unforgettable Thriller grief.

Running seventeen hours and twenty-three minutes in its UK edition, *Knife* breaks Harry completely open before his gritty road back in Killing Moon.
