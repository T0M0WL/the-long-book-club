Ship of Destiny

Author: Robin Hobb | Narrator: Anne Flosnik

Runtime: 38h 24m | Genre: Fantasy, Epic Fantasy, Seafaring Fantasy, Pirates, Dragons, High Fantasy, Grand Finale

About this Audiobook

As Bingtown burns in war and Chalcedean fleets invade, liveships clash in a colossal final confrontation that restores dragons to the world.

The Battle of the Others' Island, the Liberation of Vivacia, and the Rebirth of Dragons

Spanning more than thirty-eight monumental, emotionally climactic hours, *Ship of Destiny* brings Robin Hobb’s *Liveship Traders* trilogy to a towering, unforgettable conclusion that fundamentally reshapes the entire Realm of the Elderlings. All the disparate narrative threads converge amidst apocalyptic chaos: Bingtown is engulfed in flames, torn between rebellious Old Traders, uprising indentured slaves, and the brutal invading mercenary fleet of the Chalcedean Empire. Meanwhile, in the Rain Wilds, the magnificent blue dragon Tintaglia is freed from her wizardwood cocoon, taking to the skies as the last true dragon in a world that had forgotten them.

Out on the turbulent ocean, *Paragon* and *Vivacia* hurtle toward an inevitable, heart-stopping naval clash at the mysterious Others' Island. The naval skirmishes are rendered with terrifying tactical precision, capturing the splintering of timber, cannon fire, and the deafening cries of enraged sea serpents. As the true, tragic nature of wizardwood consciousness is fully revealed, every character faces their ultimate trial: Captain Kennit’s dark, manipulative secrets are exposed, forcing Wintrow and Etta to make agonizing moral choices, while Althea and Brashen risk everything to save their family. Malta Vestrit shines as a formidable diplomat, negotiating between dragons, pirates, and ancient Elderling ruins with astonishing maturity. Hobb delivers a breathtaking convergence of visceral naval combat, mythological resurrection, and deep emotional resolution.

Narrator Anne Flosnik delivers the crowning performance of the trilogy across thirty-eight hours. Flosnik handles the majestic fury of Tintaglia’s draconic voice, the chaotic terror of the burning of Bingtown, and the bittersweet reconciliations of the Vestrit family with sublime emotional control. For fantasy listeners seeking the ultimate narrative return on a single credit, *Ship of Destiny* is an epic milestone that belongs in every serious listener's permanent collection.
