About this Audiobook

In the Seven Satrapies, magic is drafted from the visible color spectrum. Gavin Guile is the Prism, the balance-keeper of the world, whose miraculous power comes with a ticking expiration date.

Brent Weeks’s Chromatic Masterpiece of Color-Magic and Political Deception in Audio

In the Seven Satrapies, magic is not an abstract mystery—it is Chromaturgy, the tangible science of converting visible light into luxin, a physical substance with distinct weights, densities, and chemical properties. At the apex of this world stands Gavin Guile, the Prism: high priest, supreme emperor, and the only man alive capable of drafting every color of the spectrum. But the Prism’s miraculous god-like reign is built upon a catastrophic secret: drafting light burns through a sorcerer’s lifespan, and Gavin knows exactly how many years he has left before his mind fractures and he must be put to death. When news arrives that he has an unacknowledged bastard son surviving in a war-torn province, the fragile peace of the empire shatters into open rebellion.

In audio format, Simon Vance’s legendary narration transforms Brent Weeks’s twenty-one-hour fantasy into an astonishingly vivid, kaleidoscopic listening experience. Vance’s rich, cultured baritone brings theatrical dignity to Gavin Guile’s roguish charm and existential terror, while lending vulnerable warmth to the awkward, overweight adolescent Kip as he is thrust into the deadly politics of the Chromeria. Featured prominently in our Hard Magic Systems collection alongside Brandon Sanderson's The Final Empire and Robert Jackson Bennett's Foundryside, this recording stands as one of the most imaginative, logically rigorous magic systems ever brought to life in audio.

Spanning more than twenty-one hours for a single credit, *The Black Prism* delivers exceptional entertainment value for dedicated fantasy listeners. Weeks combines military strategy, hilarious banter, and devastating plot twists with an inventive magic system that turns light itself into artillery, armor, and siege engines. Spending a single token on this series opener unlocks one of the most celebrated and beloved fantasy sagas in modern audio literature.

His vocal differentiation between Gavin's aristocratic swagger, Andross Guile's serpentine malice, and Kip's self-deprecating humor is masterly.
