The Apocalyptic Twenty-Six-Hour Climax to Brandon Sanderson’s Landmark Mistborn Trilogy in Audio

As ash falls endlessly from blood-red skies and the sun blazes as a sickly crimson orb, *The Hero of Ages* brings Brandon Sanderson’s groundbreaking Mistborn trilogy to an earth-shattering, apocalyptic conclusion. Having inadvertently freed the primordial force of destruction known as Ruin, Emperor Elend Venture and Vin must lead the desperate remnants of humanity across a dying Scadrial, seeking the secret underground storage caches left behind by the Lord Ruler. As colossal ash mounds bury once-fertile farmlands and mysterious koloss armies march across the burning horizon, Vin must master the full spectrum of Allomancy and Hemalurgy to oppose the cosmic force of Ruin, while Terris keeper Sazed wanders the starving provinces battling a profound crisis of faith, cataloging hundreds of extinct religions to find any belief system capable of offering genuine solace.

On audio, the sweeping scope and breathtaking philosophical weight of this concluding chapter deliver an unforgettable listening experience. Michael Kramer’s authoritative narration turns Sanderson’s legendary 'Sanderlanche' into a symphonic auditory triumph, where every throwaway clue, metal earring, and enigmatic epigraph from the previous two volumes snaps together with jaw-dropping precision.
