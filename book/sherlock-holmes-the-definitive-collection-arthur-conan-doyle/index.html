Stephen Fry's 71-Hour Definitive Audio Archive of Conan Doyle's Legendary Victorian Detective

Clocking in at over seventy-one hours, *Sherlock Holmes: The Definitive Collection* is the ultimate, unabridged auditory celebration of the greatest consulting detective in world literature. Narrated with infectious passion by Stephen Fry, this magnificent omnibus contains all four full-length novels—including *A Study in Scarlet* and *The Hound of the Baskervilles*—alongside all fifty-six classic short stories featuring Sherlock Holmes and his loyal chronicler, Dr. John Watson. Each section is enhanced by Fry’s deeply personal, insightful introductions, providing fascinating historical context on Sir Arthur Conan Doyle’s life, Victorian London, and the enduring cultural legacy of Baker Street, shedding light on the genius of Arthur Conan Doyle and the timeless appeal of Victorian detective fiction.

In audio format, the cozy, foggy gaslit atmosphere of late-Victorian England envelops the listener with hypnotic charm. The spoken delivery brings out the sparkling intellectual chemistry between Holmes’s razor-sharp deductive reasoning and Watson’s warm, empathetic humanity, turning every mysterious telegram, darting cab ride, and violin meditation into an irresistible theatrical experience. Prominently featured in our Longest Ever collection and beloved alongside British classics like Middlemarch, this recording makes the legendary rooms of 221B Baker Street feel like a second home, filled with roaring hearth fires, Persian slippers, and the thrill of the deductive game.
