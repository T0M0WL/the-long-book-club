About this Audiobook

RinoZ's Colossal Monster-Evolution Triumph and Jeff Hays's 54-Hour Single-Credit Colony Odyssey

When an ordinary human dies and awakens in the deadly subterranean depths of the world of Pangera, he discovers he hasn’t been reborn as an overpowered warrior prince or an archmage—he is a tiny, fragile, newborn monster ant named Anthony. Armed with only his mandibles, a ferocious work ethic, and an obsessive dedication to his insectoid colony, Anthony must navigate lethal dungeon caverns packed with acid-spitting crocodiles, shadow beasts, and rival monster factions. By devouring monster flesh and collecting Biomass, Anthony unlocks astonishing evolutionary paths, transforming his fragile body into an armored, spell-weaving, car-sized dreadnought dedicated to protecting his Queen and building the most formidable ant empire Pangera has ever seen.

On audio, *Chrysalis: Books 1-3* is an absolute triumph of unadulterated entertainment and voice-acting virtuosity. Spanning an astonishing fifty-four hours and forty-one minutes, narrators Jeff Hays and Annie Ellicott deliver a tour de force performance that turns insect survival into pure auditory gold. Anthony’s manic, caffeine-fueled internal monologue, his unyielding love for his ant family, and his sheer joy in unlocking ridiculous biological upgrades keep the pacing remarkably brisk across dozens of hours. Alongside progression fantasy landmarks like The Wandering Inn and Dungeon Crawler Carl, this box set demonstrates the boundless creative joy of the LitRPG & GameLit genre.
