Fyodor Dostoyevsky\u2019s Psychological Masterpiece of Murder, Guilt, and St. Petersburg in Audio

In the sweltering, impoverished tenements and fever-pitch alleys of nineteenth-century St. Petersburg, destitute former law student Rodion Raskolnikov conceives an arrogant philosophical theory: that extraordinary men are above ordinary moral law and possess the right to eliminate worthless human 'lice' for the greater good of humanity. Testing his Napoleonic hypothesis, Raskolnikov murders a vile, predatory old pawnbroker with an axe\u2014and her innocent half-sister when she walks in unexpectedly during the crime. What follows is not an escape thriller, but a harrowing, feverish psychological descent into guilt, paranoia, and moral disintegration as a brilliant detective begins an intellectual cat-and-mouse game to trap his guilty soul.
