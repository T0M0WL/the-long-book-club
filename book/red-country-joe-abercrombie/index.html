About this Audiobook

When her homestead is torched and her young siblings are abducted, Shy South sets out across the lawless Far Country alongside her cowardly stepfather Lamb, who harbors a terrifying, legendary past.

Grimdark Meets the Spaghetti Western: Steven Pacey Unleashes Bloody Frontier Justice in Red Country

Fusing the brutal moral ambiguity of grimdark fantasy with the dusty, sweeping tropes of classic frontier cinema, *Red Country* is Joe Abercrombie’s dark, thrilling homage to *Unforgiven* and *The Searchers*. When Shy South returns to her desolate farm to find her home burning and her young brother and sister kidnapped by child-snatchers, she sets out across the untamed wilderness of the Far Country on a desperate pursuit. Accompanying her is her quiet, cowardly stepfather Lamb—an aging, timid farmer missing nine fingers who flinches from violence. But as they venture deeper into gold-rush boomtowns and bandit territory, Lamb’s buried past violently reawakens, revealing the terrifying return of the Bloody-Nine.

Steven Pacey’s narration reaches operatic heights of tension and dread as Lamb’s facade begins to crack. Pacey portrays Shy South with tough, pragmatic resilience, while voicing the charismatic, self-deluding mercenary Nicomo Cosca with theatrical, drunken hilarity. Most chillingly, when Lamb finally loses control and the berserker emerges, Pacey subtly drops his vocal register into the dread-inducing, guttural whisper that defined Logan Ninefingers in the original trilogy. Listeners who appreciate the harsh, dusty frontiers of Blood Meridian or the moral complexity of Lonesome Dove will be completely captivated.

At nearly twenty hours for a single credit, *Red Country* delivers immense emotional payoff and pulse-pounding excitement. Abercrombie masterfully examines whether violent men can ever truly escape their past, staging thrilling wagon-train shootouts and harrowing underground rescues. It is a triumphant, exhilarating entry that completes the legendary First Law standalone trilogy in glorious, blood-spattered fashion.
