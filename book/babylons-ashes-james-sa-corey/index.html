About this Audiobook

The Free Navy conflict reaches an all-out climax as Earth, Mars, and moderate Belters unite to reclaim the solar system.

The Coalition War: Jefferson Mays Guides an Epic 20-Hour Interplanetary Coalition

In *Babylon’s Ashes*, the devastating fallout of Marco Inaros's asteroid assault triggers a system-wide coalition war of unprecedented scale. With Earth starving and Mars facing economic collapse, the remnants of the inner planets form a fragile, unprecedented alliance with moderate Belters led by Camina Drummer to confront the Free Navy. Reunited aboard the repaired *Rocinante*, Holden, Naomi, Amos, and Alex become the tip of the spear in a desperate campaign to break Inaros's stranglehold on the Ring gates.

Narrator Jefferson Mays faces a monumental challenge with sixteen distinct POV characters, yet he executes it with peerless vocal authority. His handling of Camina Drummer's fierce, gravelly Belter dignity and Michio Pa's conflicted piracy adds immense textural depth to the narrative. The tactical space engagements around Titan and the Medina Station railgun batteries are delivered with pulse-pounding rhythm. Listeners who loved the multi-faction military campaigns of The Dragon Republic or A Clash of Kings will find this audiobook exhilarating.

At twenty hours for a single credit, *Babylon’s Ashes* provides sensational audio value. It delivers a deeply satisfying conclusion to the Free Navy arc, establishing the Transport Union and laying the foundation for the final trilogy's thirty-year leap forward.
