About this Audiobook

After forming a coalition of human resistance against the enemy invasion, Dalinar Kholin and his Knights Radiant have spent a year fighting a protracted, brutal war.

A Colossal Fifty-Seven-Hour High-Fantasy Siege of Urithiru and Rosharan Spren Science in Audio

As a brutal year of total war grinds on across Roshar, *Rhythm of War* shifts the epic battle lines into the claustrophobic corridors of the ancient mountain tower of Urithiru. When the cunning Fused scholar Raboniel launches a surprise coup that suppresses the magical powers of the Knights Radiant, Queen Navani Kholin becomes the tower’s de facto defender, entering a deadly intellectual duel of spren science and light manipulation with her captor. Meanwhile, an exhausted Kaladin Stormblessed, sidelined by severe battle fatigue and combat shock, must fight a solitary, guerilla resistance campaign through the tower’s ventilation shafts. Accompanied only by his loyal honorspren Syl and a handful of wounded companions, Kaladin wages an agonizing war of survival while Navani risks her life in high-stakes metaphysical experiments that will redefine the future of Roshar.

Listening to *Rhythm of War* on audio unlocks the fascinating musical and rhythmic foundations of Sanderson’s world in ways the printed page cannot match. The mathematical harmonies, ancient Singer rhythms, and complex metaphysical theories of stormlight and voidlight feel natural and captivating when voiced with patient clarity by Michael Kramer and Kate Reading.
