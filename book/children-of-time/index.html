About this Audiobook

As the dying remnants of humanity arrive at a terraformed paradise in a desperate generation ship, they discover an uplifted species of jumping spiders has evolved a thriving civilization.

The Visionary Evolutionary Epic That Reinvents the First-Contact Narrative

Winner of the prestigious Arthur C. Clarke Award, *Children of Time* spans sixteen and a half hours of visionary speculative imagination, delivering one of the most astonishing first-contact epics in science fiction history. Adrian Tchaikovsky constructs a dual-timeline narrative spanning thousands of years. On one side is the *Gilgamesh*, an ancient, deteriorating generation ship carrying the last cryo-sleeping remnants of humanity away from an uninhabitable Earth. On the other is Kern’s World, a lush terraformed world where an uplift virus—designed to cultivate a primate civilization—accidentally jump-starts the evolutionary leap of *Portia labiata*, a species of jumping spiders who gradually inherit the planet.

Tchaikovsky’s triumph is his utterly believable, empathetic depiction of non-human intelligence and societal development. Across generations, we witness Portia and her descendants develop language through vibration, master silk-based computation, domesticate parasitic fungi, and wage civil wars against predatory ants. This alien civilization's organic technology contrasts sharply with the claustrophobic decay and tribal infighting aboard the *Gilgamesh*, where desperate human refugees view the planet as their rightful inheritance. The audio format turns this evolutionary saga into a spellbinding spectacle, forcing the listener to question human exceptionalism and contemplate true biological empathy across species as the two civilizations inevitably collide.

For audiobook listeners seeking mind-expanding scale, biological ingenuity, and immense intellectual payoff on a single credit, *Children of Time* is an absolute masterpiece. Mel Hudson delivers a flawless, mesmerizing performance, effortlessly pivoting between the cold mechanical decay of human starship corridors and the vibrant sensory world of the arachnid society. Paired with companion cosmic epics like Shards of Earth and House of Suns, it stands as a triumphant modern classic of Science Fiction that will reshape your perspective forever. It delivers unmatched listening value, philosophical brilliance, and profound cosmic wonder that will stay with you long after the final chapter concludes.

Narrator Soundcheck: Mel Hudson

Mel Hudson delivers a virtuoso performance that bridges human desperation with non-human wonder. Hudson brings crisp, scientific authority to Dr. Avrana Kern’s descent into synthetic madness, while her characterizations of the weary crew aboard the *Gilgamesh* capture the frayed psychology of humanity's dying gasps. Most impressively, Hudson handles the arachnid chapters with breathtaking subtlety, using rhythmic inflections and delicate vocal dynamics to evoke the tactile, chemical, and vibratory communication of Portia’s species, creating an utterly mesmerizing sixteen-hour auditory feast.
