About this Audiobook

Reeling from the carnage of Dumai's Wells, Rand seeks to consolidate his rule over Cairhien and strike down the Forsaken Sammael in the fortified city of Illian.

A Riveting Thirty-Hour Chronicle of Urban Espionage, Crown Politics, and the Ruins of Shadar Logoth

In the direct aftermath of Dumai’s Wells, *A Crown of Swords* follows Rand al'Thor as he wrestles with the terrifying new reality of kneeling Aes Sedai and distrustful nobility. Refusing to allow the Forsaken Sammael to entrench his tyrannical power in the southern kingdom of Illian, Rand launches a daring military campaign while struggling to suppress the erratic voices in his own mind. Meanwhile, in the bustling coastal metropolis of Ebou Dar, Mat Cauthon, Nynaeve, and Elayne navigate treacherous palace politics and dangerous street duels as their search for the weather-altering Bowl of the Winds nears its perilous climax.

On audio, the contrasting story threads of *A Crown of Swords* shine brilliantly through the nuanced vocal performances of Michael Kramer and Kate Reading. Kramer brings swaggering comedic charm to Mat’s colorful escapades in the court of Queen Tylin, while lending somber, lethal gravity to Rand’s apocalyptic confrontation with Sammael amidst the mist-shrouded ruins of Shadar Logoth. Reading excels at portraying the complex sisterhood dynamics of the Aes Sedai and the growing tension within the rebellious camp at Salidar. Featured prominently within our Epic Fantasy library, this production transforms intricate court intrigue and tactical maneuvering into an irresistible auditory pleasure.

Spanning over thirty hours of tightly paced drama, *A Crown of Swords* provides outstanding credit value for fantasy connoisseurs. Jordan excels at world-building the distinct cultural nuances of Altara and Illian, immersing the listener in vibrant festival streets, canal-side taverns, and shadowy royal bedchambers. A single credit grants you thirty hours of captivating voice acting, memorable character evolutions, and thrilling magical combat that keeps you thoroughly invested in Jordan’s vast universe.
