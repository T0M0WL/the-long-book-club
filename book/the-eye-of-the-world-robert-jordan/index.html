About this Audiobook

The Wheel of Time turns. Ages come and go, leaving memories that become legend. Legend fades to myth, and even myth is long forgotten when the Age that gave it birth comes again.

Robert Jordan’s Monumental High-Fantasy Foundation and the Flight from the Two Rivers

In the secluded, pastoral village of Emond's Field, the arrival of spring is shattered by a terrifying nocturnal raid of bestial Trollocs and shadowy Myrddraal. Targeted by the forces of the Dark One, three young village youths—shepherd Rand al'Thor, mischievous trickster Mat Cauthon, and blacksmith apprentice Perrin Aybara—are whisked away by the formidable Aes Sedai Moiraine Damodred and her stoic Warder Lan. Joined along the road by the stubborn wisdom Nynaeve al'Meara and the spirited Egwene al'Vere, the youths must navigate sinister darkfriends and terrifying nightmare visions sent by Ba'alzamon. What begins as a desperate flight into the dark mists of Shadar Logoth blossoms into an epoch-spanning journey across the continent, seeking the mythical Green Man at the edge of the Blight before the Dark One breaks his ancient prison.

Experiencing *The Eye of the World* on audio unlocks the mythic antiquity and sweeping fireside grandeur that Robert Jordan wove into his seminal universe. Narrators Michael Kramer and Kate Reading establish the legendary gold standard of dual fantasy narration, bringing grounded warmth to the Two Rivers companions, vibrant bardic color to the gleeman Thom Merrilin, and chilling, aristocratic authority to the Aes Sedai. Highlighted as a foundational pillar in our Epic Fantasy collection alongside celebrated sagas like The Great Hunt and The Fellowship of the Ring, this thirty-hour production turns classical quest fantasy into an unforgettable auditory pilgrimage that wraps around your imagination.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer, Kate Reading

The celebrated husband-and-wife team of Michael Kramer and Kate Reading deliver a masterclass in dual-narration that defined high-fantasy audio for a generation. Kramer brings earthy sincerity and vulnerability to Rand and his companions, shifting with ease into Thom’s theatrical flourishes and Lan’s gravelly martial discipline. Reading counters with serene, crystalline elegance as Moiraine, infusing every Aes Sedai channeling sequence with formidable presence. Mastered with pristine dynamic range and immaculate acoustic depth across nearly thirty hours, this classic recording remains an absolute triumph of spoken-word fantasy.
