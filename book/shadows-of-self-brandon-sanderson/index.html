About this Audiobook

A shapeshifting kandra assassin terrorizes Elendel's political elite, pushing the city toward working-class revolution and testing Wax's faith in Harmony.

Kandra Assassins, Civil Unrest, and the Heartbreaking Crucible of Harmony's Path

Spanning twelve and a half riveting, deeply emotional hours, *Shadows of Self* darkens the gaslamp adventure of The Alloy of Law into a gripping noir detective thriller with immense Cosmere implications. In the booming metropolis of Elendel, underlying socio-economic tensions reach a boiling point. The working class, laboring in harsh factories beneath the reign of corrupt city governors, threatens open insurrection, stoked by the fiery populist rhetoric of labor agitators.

Matters turn catastrophic when a mysterious assassin infiltrates a private party of corrupt officials, slaughtering them with chilling precision. Waxillium Ladrian is summoned to investigate, only to discover that the killer is Bleeder—a rogue, insane kandra who has removed one of her divine Spikes to escape the mental control of the god Harmony (formerly Sazed). Able to consume corpses and adopt the physical identity of anyone in the city, Bleeder orchestrates a campaign of political chaos designed to overthrow civilization itself. Wax, struggling with his personal devotion to Harmony, finds himself caught between divine mandates and harrowing human tragedy.

Michael Kramer delivers one of the most powerful performances in his storied narration career. Kramer navigates the rapid banter between Wayne and Marasi with effortless charm, but it is his handling of Wax’s devastating emotional climax that elevates this audiobook into a masterpiece of fantasy tragedy. Delivered with immaculate sound design and crisp pacing, this credit is an absolute essential for all Cosmere listeners.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer

Michael Kramer’s performance on Shadows of Self is an emotional juggernaut. While Kramer maintains Wayne’s hilarious, slang-laden banter and Marasi’s analytical intelligence with impeccable ease, his vocalization of Wax’s grief and spiritual torment during the novel’s gut-wrenching final chapters is extraordinary. His pacing during the shadowy street chases and kandra encounters is tense and breathless. Produced with pristine acoustic fidelity across twelve hours, this audiobook represents Cosmere narration at its very best.
