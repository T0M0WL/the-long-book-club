Over Twelve Hypnotic Hours Inside William Faulkner's Crowning Southern Gothic Tragedy

Widely hailed as William Faulkner's supreme Modernist achievement, *Absalom, Absalom!* is a towering American tragedy built like a ghost story told on a sweltering porch. Through four overlapping, obsessed narrators—including young Quentin Compson shivering in his Harvard dorm room—the novel pieces together the rise and ruin of Thomas Sutpen, a ruthless stranger who rides into 1833 Mississippi to carve a grand plantation out of the wilderness, only to watch his dynasty collapse under the weight of race, pride, and buried bloodlines.

Because the entire novel is structured as spoken testimony, rumor, and feverish speculation, listening to *Absalom, Absalom!* in audio unlocks its hypnotic rhythm in a way silent reading rarely matches. Instead of wrestling with marathon paragraphs on the page, you let Faulkner's biblical cadences wash over you like music. Standing proudly alongside Ulysses and East of Eden, it is essential listening for anyone drawn to bold Literary Fiction and brooding Southern Gothic drama.

At twelve hours and thirty-one minutes for a single Audible credit, *Absalom, Absalom!* is fierce, haunted, and unforgettable.
