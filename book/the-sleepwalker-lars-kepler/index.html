About this Audiobook

Arrested drenched in blood beside a butchered body in a quiet campervan, a young witness claims lifelong somnambulism erased the night—compelling Joona Linna and hypnotist Erik Bark to probe sleeping horror.

Over Seventeen Hours Testing Whether a Murderer Can Kill Inside Unbroken Sleep

When police raid an isolated Swedish campsite after neighbors report eerie nighttime noises, they uncover a savagely dismembered corpse—and an exhausted young witness drenched in blood who remembers nothing beyond falling peacefully asleep hours earlier. Convinced they are either staring at an unprecedented case of homicidal somnambulism or an extraordinarily cruel framing, Joona Linna recalls hypnotist Erik Maria Bark from The Hypnotist and The Mirror Man to unlock truth buried beneath waking consciousness.

As the tenth milestone volume in the series, *The Sleepwalker* plunges Nordic Noir straight back into its unsettling medical roots. Blending claustrophobic Psychological Thriller paranoia with stubborn Police Procedural skepticism, Lars Kepler spins shocking Crime misdirection into a gripping Detective Mystery questioning whether the sleeping mind can conceal unspeakable fury.

Clocking in at seventeen hours and seven minutes for one credit, *The Sleepwalker* proves Joona Linna remains just as shocking, fresh, and unputdownable ten books deep.
