Over Thirteen Hours Uncovering an Empty 1974 Easter Table on Valö Island

Returning directly to haunted Ghost Isle Valö after The Lost Boy, grieving couple Ebba and Tobias restore the derelict former boys' boarding school into a quiet bed-and-breakfast—the exact house where Ebba's entire family vanished from an untouched Easter dinner table in 1974, leaving only one-year-old Ebba alive inside her crib. After arson forces investigators under charred kitchen planks revealing decades-old dried blood, Erica's historical digging ties straight into turn-of-the-century angel-maker horrors.

*Buried Angels* pairs one of Scandinavia's eeriest closed-room premises with devastating Nordic Noir Mystery. Contrasting 1900s poverty tragedies with patient Police Procedural forensics fuels chilling Crime and Detective twists.

Clocking thirteen hours and fifteen minutes for a single Audible credit, Book 8 is compact, haunting, and effortlessly gripping before The Ice Child.
