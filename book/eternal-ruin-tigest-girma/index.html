About this Audiobook

In the explosive second volume of the Immortal Dark trilogy, Kidan and Susenyos face ancient dranacht conspiracies, fractured house loyalties, and a desperate race against death inside Uxlay University.

Twenty-One Hours of Multi-Voice Vampire Intrigue in Tigest Girma's Explosive Sequel

Following the devastating betrayals at the close of Immortal Dark, *Eternal Ruin* plunges Kidan Adane and Susenyos Sagad deeper into the ancient conspiracies rotting beneath Uxlay University. With rival houses circling, forbidden dranacht history coming to light, and their volatile bond tested by impossible loyalties, Kidan must confront how much of her humanity she is willing to sacrifice to protect the people she loves.

On audio, this twenty-one-hour second installment expands the sonic scope of the series by introducing a five-voice ensemble cast. Hearing multiple perspectives across Uxlay's warring factions turns the sequel into a sweeping, operatic dark academia thriller. Paired with Immortal Dark and setting the stage for the trilogy's final volume (*Infernal Vow*, releasing 7th January 2027), this recording rewards returning listeners with richer lore and relentless emotional intensity.

Clocking in at twenty-one hours and twenty-one minutes for a single Audible credit, *Eternal Ruin* offers exceptional marathon value for fans of dark vampire romantasy and cutthroat collegiate politics.
