About this Audiobook

Perrin confronts Slayer and forges Mah'alleinir, while Mat Cauthon infiltrates the sinister Tower of Ghenjei to rescue Moiraine Damodred.

A Colossal Thirty-Eight-Hour Heroic Epic of Dream-World Duels, Hammer Strikes, and the Tower of Ghenjei

The penultimate volume of *The Wheel of Time*, *Towers of Midnight* delivers long-awaited resolutions and breathless action set pieces as the disparate champions of the Light rally for the end of the world. Perrin Aybara finally embraces his wolfbrother nature, mastering the shifting realities of the Wolf Dream (Tel'aran'rhiod) in a deadly duel against Slayer and forging the magical power-wrought war hammer Mah'alleinir. Simultaneously, Mat Cauthon, Thom Merrilin, and Noal Charin brave the otherworldly, bronze-walled Tower of Ghenjei, gambling life, sanity, and blood against the eerie Aelfinn and Eelfinn to rescue Moiraine Damodred.

On audio, the dramatic and emotional resonance of *Towers of Midnight* is nothing short of magical. Michael Kramer brings magnificent gravitas to Perrin’s leadership transformation and Mat’s daring infiltration, while Kate Reading provides regal dignity to Egwene’s investigations into the Black Ajah and Elayne’s defense of the Lion Throne. Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer, Kate Reading

Narrators Michael Kramer and Kate Reading demonstrate extraordinary endurance and emotional range across this thirty-eight-hour production. Kramer’s depiction of the rhythmic, mesmerizing forging of Mah'alleinir is an acoustic marvel, while his voicing of Mat Cauthon inside the surreal corridors of the Tower of Ghenjei crackles with panic, grit, and humor.
