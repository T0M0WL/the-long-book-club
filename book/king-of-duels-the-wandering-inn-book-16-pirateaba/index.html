About this Audiobook

A thrilling new chapter begins as acclaimed narrator Erin Bennett takes the helm, bringing tournament rings, dueling champions, and grand royal courts to life.

A Glorious Thirty-Nine-Hour Rebirth of Grand Tournaments, Dueling Honors, and Erin Bennett’s Triumphant Debut

In *King of Duels*, *The Wandering Inn* embarks on an exhilarating new chapter of tournament spectacle, noble pageantry, and international intrigue. Across Izril, the finest swordsmen, spellcasters, and martial champions gather to test their steel in grand dueling arenas, competing for honor, titles, and legendary relics. Inside the inn, Erin navigates complex aristocratic etiquette and high-stakes wagers, proving once again that an innkeeper with an ear for gossip and a heart for justice can outplay the most cunning duelists in the land.

This sixteenth volume marks a historic torch-passing in audiobook publishing: with Andrea Parsneau providing a heartfelt, emotional farewell foreword, acclaimed narrator Erin Bennett takes over the narration of the series. Bennett accomplishes the near-impossible, preserving the beloved cadence, quirks, and spirit of Erin, Ryoka, and the Antinium while bringing her own crisp diction, dramatic vitality, and energetic flair to the recording. Curated proudly within our LitRPG & GameLit Audiobooks collection, this thirty-nine-hour production is a triumphant showcase of voice-acting courage.

Clocking in at thirty-nine hours and thirty minutes, *King of Duels* delivers tremendous credit value and exciting momentum. pirateaba invents ingenious combat mechanics, dazzling weapon arts, and memorable character rivalries that make every arena duel a joy to experience. A single credit grants you nearly forty hours of elite storytelling that ensures the series will continue to thrive for years to come.
