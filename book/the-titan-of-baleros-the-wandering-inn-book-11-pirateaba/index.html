About this Audiobook

Famed Fraerling strategist Niers Astoragon mobilizes his elite academy on Baleros, while Erin hosts grand chess tournaments that capture global attention.

A Masterful Forty-One-Hour Showcase of Continental Grand Strategy, Fraerling Academies, and Global Chess Matches

In *The Titan of Baleros*, the narrative expands to the lush jungle continent of Baleros, home to massive mercenary companies and the tiny, brilliant Fraerling species. Niers Astoragon—the Titan of Baleros and the greatest tactical mind alive—has spent months engaged in anonymous, transcontinental chess games with an enigmatic player in Izril. When he discovers that his intellectual equal is an eccentric human [Innkeeper] named Erin Solstice, the Titan sets off a chain of events that throws the ruthless Forgotten Wing Company and rival mercenary cartels into high alert, threatening to reshape the global balance of power.

Experiencing *The Titan of Baleros* on audio is an intellectual and comedic delight. Andrea Parsneau brilliantly contrasts Niers’s booming, authoritative strategic genius with his diminutive physical stature, creating a magnetic performance that steals every scene. Back in Liscor, Parsneau brings infectious warmth to Erin’s tavern tournaments, where chess matches carry the weight of real military skirmishes. Featured as a premier title in our LitRPG & GameLit Audiobooks collection, this production brings big ideas and miniature warriors to life with dazzling clarity.

Spanning forty hours and fifty-five minutes, this eleventh volume provides extraordinary credit value for fans of tactical fantasy and world-building. pirateaba weaves intricate strategy lessons with hilarious misunderstandings and poignant explorations of cultural differences. A single credit unlocks over forty hours of elite voice acting and high-stakes maneuvering that keeps you thoroughly invested from opening gambit to final checkmate.
