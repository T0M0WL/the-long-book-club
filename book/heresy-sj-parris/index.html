Fifteen Hours of Elizabethan Oxford Espionage and Murder in S.J. Parris's Giordano Bruno Opener

It is 1583, and England sits on a knife-edge of Catholic plots against Queen Elizabeth I. Recruited by spymaster Sir Francis Walsingham, exiled Italian former monk and heretical philosopher Giordano Bruno is sent undercover to Lincoln College, Oxford, where scholars are secretly passing banned Catholic texts ahead of a royal visitation. Yet no sooner does Bruno step onto the rain-slicked cobblestones than an Oxford don is torn apart by dogs—sparking a string of gruesome, staged killings that turn the university into a locked-room hunting ground.

On audio, S.J. Parris (pseudonym of historian Stephanie Merritt) weaves together two of our most popular traditions: Historical Mystery and Dark Academia. Hearing sixteenth-century Oxford's Latin disputations, candlelit common rooms, and spycraft paranoia come alive in your headphones makes *Heresy* an irresistible Whodunnit companion to Dissolution and The Name of the Rose, launching the acclaimed *Giordano Bruno* series.

Clocking in at nearly fifteen hours for a single Audible credit, *Heresy* delivers rich Tudor Historical Fiction, intellectual sparring, and relentless conspiratorial tension.
