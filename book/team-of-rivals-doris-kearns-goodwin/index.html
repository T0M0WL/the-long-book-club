About this Audiobook

The monumental history of Abraham Lincoln and his cabinet. A masterpiece of political biography.

Doris Kearns Goodwin's 41-Hour Masterclass in Lincoln's Political Genius and Moral Leadership

Widely regarded as the supreme political biography of the American Civil War, Doris Kearns Goodwin’s *Team of Rivals* is a panoramic, forty-one-hour study of emotional intelligence and transcendent leadership. When Abraham Lincoln won the contentious Republican presidential nomination in 1860, he stunned the political establishment by assembling a cabinet composed entirely of his bitter rivals—men of superior pedigree, education, and national reputation, including William H. Seward, Salmon P. Chase, and Edward Bates. Facing the catastrophic fracture of the Union, Lincoln navigated jealousy, betrayal, and catastrophic military setbacks with saintly patience, humor, and steely resolve, gradually bending his formidable opponents into a united instrument of emancipation and national salvation, proving that moral magnanimity is the highest form of political strength.

Audio is uniquely suited to Goodwin’s intimate, multiperspective narrative structure. Rather than treating Civil War history as dry military dispatches, the spoken word highlights the vivid letters, diary entries, and witty frontier anecdotes that Lincoln used to disarm his arrogant ministers. Featured as a core pillar of our Long Life Stories collection alongside Grant, this unabridged audio production provides the generous temporal canvas needed to witness Lincoln’s extraordinary magnanimity and the slow, agonizing transformation of nineteenth-century American society in real time, making historic cabinet debates feel urgent, intimate, and profoundly alive in your ears.

At over forty-one hours of peerless narrative history, spending a single credit on *Team of Rivals* delivers an unforgettable return on your investment. The biography offers timeless lessons in conflict resolution, political empathy, and moral courage that remain desperately relevant today. It is a deeply moving, intellectually enriching listening experience that will make you marvel at Lincoln’s singular character and leave you profoundly inspired long after the final chapter concludes, leaving an indelible imprint on your understanding of democratic leadership, emotional resilience, and enduring human decency in times of national crisis.

Narrator Soundcheck: Suzanne Toren

Suzanne Toren commands this massive forty-one-hour history with exceptional narrative stamina, stately pacing, and crystal-clear articulation throughout. Toren navigates Goodwin’s dense archival correspondence, contentious cabinet debates, and dramatic battlefield dispatches with intelligent, dignified authority. Her subtle inflection distinguishes Lincoln’s folksy humor from the haughty aristocratic tones of his ambitious ministers without sliding into broad caricature, backed by pristine, balanced audio engineering that keeps listener fatigue completely at bay across forty-one hours.
