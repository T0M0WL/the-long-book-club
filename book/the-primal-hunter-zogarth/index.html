About this Audiobook

Pulled from his corporate desk job into a lethal tutorial dimension, Jake discovers his antisocial nature and Bloodline instinct make him the ultimate archery-and-alchemy apex predator.

An Instinct-Driven Solo Hunter LitRPG Packed with Deadliest Archery and Dark Alchemy

When a mysterious cosmic System forcibly assimilates Earth and transports corporate office worker Jake Thayne and his colleagues into a brutal tutorial dungeon, Jake quickly discovers that modern civilization was merely holding him back. Blessed with a dormant bloodline that grants him preternatural predatory instincts, superhuman spatial awareness, and a supernatural calm in the heat of mortal combat, Jake grabs a wooden bow and plunges into the primordial wilderness to become the ultimate solitary apex hunter. Author Zogarth crafts a thrilling, deeply immersive LitRPG epic that combines lethal precision archery, lethal poison alchemy, and solitary survival with rich god-tier cosmic lore and enigmatic deities like the ancient serpentine Malefic Viper.

Listening to Jake’s solitary evolution on audio is an intensely compelling, hypnotic, and satisfying experience for fans of solitary progression. Hearing the whistling release of venom-coated arrows, the silent stalking through moonlit alien canopies, and the intimate telepathic conversations between Jake and an ancient reptilian god creates a rich, atmospheric soundscape that hooks progression fantasy fans immediately. Highlighted as an essential pillar of our LitRPG & GameLit Audiobooks collection, this performance demonstrates how auditory pacing can turn detailed alchemical crafting, beast-hunting tactics, and solitary survival routines into an exhilarating, bingeable journey.

Offering over twenty-five hours of continuous listening for a single credit, this release delivers outstanding value for lovers of unrepentant solo-leveling progression fantasy. Zogarth avoids the melodrama of petty party bickering, focusing instead on the intoxicating purity of personal growth, tactical archery, and divine communion in an unforgiving alien wilderness. Exchanging one monthly credit for this debut novel unlocks an addictive, high-octane listening experience that rewards every minute of your attention and sets up a massive multi-volume saga that listeners universally adore and celebrate as one of the definitive benchmark achievements in contemporary progression and LitRPG fiction for years to come.

Narrator Soundcheck: Travis Baldree

Travis Baldree is widely recognized as one of the elite voices in progression fantasy, and his performance here proves exactly why. Baldree imbues Jake with a calm, analytical confidence and predatory stillness that makes his solitary mindset utterly believable. He voices secondary corporate survivors and towering celestial deities with striking vocal contrast, bringing dry wit and majesty to the Malefic Viper. Baldree’s rhythm when reading skill logs and attribute sheets is second to none, keeping the twenty-five-hour runtime energetic, clean, and completely captivating throughout.
