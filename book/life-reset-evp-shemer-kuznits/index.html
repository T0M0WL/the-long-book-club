About this Audiobook

Having secured his forest haven, Oren must expand his goblin settlement into a fortified regional power while battling environmental hazards and encroaching player guilds.

Shemer Kuznits's Deepening Clan Warfare and Jeff Hays's Masterful Orchestration of Monster Politics

Having survived the desperate early days of his transformation and established a fragile stronghold in the deep woods, Oren faces an even deadlier challenge in *Life Reset: EvP*. His growing goblin settlement has caught the attention of neighboring monster tribes, hostile dungeon bosses, and powerful human player guilds seeking easy farming loot. To ensure his clan's long-term survival, Oren must transition from crude camp defense to advanced municipal governance: constructing stone ramparts, researching alchemical siege defenses, managing complex trade routes, and forging uneasy diplomatic pacts with other sentient monster species. But as environmental anomalies begin threatening the very fabric of the virtual world, Oren realizes that the game's artificial intelligence is evolving in dangerous, unpredictable directions.

On audio, *Life Reset: EvP* is a deeply rewarding sequel that expands upon everything that made Life Reset: A LitRPG Novel a genre classic. Narrator Jeff Hays brings Oren's strategic dilemmas to life with magnetic charisma, seamlessly juggling council meetings, tactical army maneuvers, and high-stakes 1-on-1 duels. The tension between Oren’s human memories and his increasingly feral goblin instincts is rendered with striking psychological authenticity. Alongside celebrated fantasy strategy epics like He Who Fights with Monsters and Defiance of the Fall, this entry reinforces the series' status as an unmissable highlight of our LitRPG & GameLit library.

Spanning nearly twenty-two gripping hours, this audio production offers sensational value for a single Audible credit. Soundbooth Theater’s state-of-the-art production captures the thrum of archery volleys, the roar of siege engines, and the lively banter of goblin craftsmen with crystal-clear spatial presence. For listeners who crave deep strategic progression, resourceful problem-solving, and exhilarating underdog warfare, spending your credit on *EvP* delivers non-stop entertainment from start to finish.
