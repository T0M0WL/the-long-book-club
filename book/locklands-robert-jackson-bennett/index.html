About this Audiobook

Eight years into an apocalyptic war for humanity's consciousness, Sancia and Clef wage a desperate final campaign against an entity reshaping the very architecture of the cosmos.

Robert Jackson Bennett Delivers a Mind-Bending 21-Hour Apocalyptic Series Conclusion

In this sweeping twenty-one-hour finale, *Locklands* leaps forward eight years into a shattered world consumed by a war of god-like entities. The immortal hierophants have weaponized scriving on a global scale, linking millions of human minds into hive-minds and systematically consuming reality itself. Operating from a hidden, mobile fortress on the fringes of the wasteland, Sancia Grado and her surviving allies wage a desperate guerrilla campaign to defend the last remnants of human individuality, embarking on an impossible heist into the heart of the hierophants’ domain to reset the architecture of the cosmos.

Narrator Tara Sands brings extraordinary emotional maturity and weight to this twenty-one-hour conclusion. Sands captures the deep toll eight years of unrelenting war have taken on Sancia, grounding high-concept metaphysical concepts—such as collective telepathy and reality alteration—in raw, human heartbreak. Paired in our library with visionary science-fantasy finales like The Burning White and Death's End, this recording is an unforgettable triumph of speculative fiction.

Providing over twenty-one hours of sublime narration for a single token, *Locklands* is an astonishingly brave and satisfying series conclusion. Bennett does not take the easy way out, resolving his trilogy's complex questions about power, capitalism, and human autonomy with staggering intellectual and emotional honesty. Investing a credit in this production rewards listeners with one of the most innovative finales in modern fantasy literature.
