An Epic Chilean Multigenerational Saga of Clairvoyance, Passion, and Political Revolution

Isabel Allende’s dazzling debut novel weaves an expansive, multi-generational epic that charts the turbulent fortunes of the Trueba family across nearly a century of Chilean history. At the center of this domestic whirlwind stands Esteban Trueba, a proud, ferocious, and tyrannical conservative patriarch who builds an agricultural empire at the rural estate of Tres Marías through ruthless authoritarian grit. He marries Clara del Valle, a serene, telekinetic clairvoyant who communicates with wandering spirits, predicts earthquakes, and records the family’s daily triumphs and disasters in her legendary five-hundred-page notebooks. Through their children and grandchildren, the family becomes inextricably bound to the burgeoning socialist revolutions, military coups, and brutal political repressions that reshape their nation forever.

Experiencing Allende’s magnificent blend of political history and supernatural realism on audio provides an extraordinarily intimate, emotionally devastating journey. The dual-perspective narration captures the stark psychological contrast between Esteban’s arrogant, self-justifying conservatism and the ethereal, compassionate spirituality of the Trueba women who quietly defy his tyranny. Narrator Soundcheck: Marisol Ramirez, Thom Rivera

Marisol Ramirez and Thom Rivera deliver a powerhouse dual narration that handles Allende’s expansive multi-generational scope with extraordinary emotional resonance. Rivera captures Esteban Trueba’s tyrannical temper, patriarchal pride, and late-life remorse with gritty conviction, making a deeply flawed man compellingly human. Ramirez counters with luminous warmth, voicing the clairvoyant Clara, rebellious Blanca, and resilient Alba with authentic Latin American inflections and poignant grace. Supported by warm, balanced studio acoustics and measured pacing, this nineteen-hour performance stands as a definitive triumph in translated audio literature.
