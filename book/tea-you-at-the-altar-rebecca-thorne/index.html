About this Audiobook

With six days left until their wedding in Tawney, Reyna and Kianthe juggle runaway baby dragons, visiting pirates, meddling in-laws, and a covert royal coup disguised beneath wedding seating charts.

Twelve Hours of Chaotic Cozy Wedding Planning, Baby Dragons, and Royal Intrigue

Six days before former Queen's Guard captain Reyna and all-powerful archmage Kianthe are due to say 'I do' in Tawney, nothing about their dream ceremony is going calmly. Their newly hatched baby dragons are setting furniture ablaze, rowdy sea pirates have descended on the sleepy border town, the teashop is running perilously low on tea—and Kianthe's estranged, aristocratic parents unexpectedly announce their arrival with her smug childhood suitor in tow. Worse still, beneath the frantic cake tastings and floral charts lies a daring secret scheme: using the high-profile wedding itself to lure out ruthless Queen Tilaine and install the realm's reluctant rightful heir.

In audio format, *Tea You at the Altar* blends heartfelt Cozy Fantasy domestic warmth with lively political comedy. Listeners who adored Can't Spell Treason Without Tea and A Pirate's Life for Tea get the best of both worlds—soothing Sapphic Romance vows paired with genuine courtroom stakes—earning an easy place alongside our Cozy Fantasy favorites.

Spanning twelve hours and three minutes for a single credit, *Tea You at the Altar* delivers belly laughs, tender found-family loyalty, and a finale cliffhanger that leads seamlessly into Alchemy and a Cup of Tea.

Threet nails Reyna's protective calm under pre-wedding stress, Kianthe's dazzling confidence, frosty in-law passive-aggressiveness, and mischievous dragon shrieks with crisp acoustic clarity.
