About this Audiobook

Facing god-tier digital overseers in a final apocalyptic showdown, Oren must risk everything to save his clan and secure the future of New Era Online.

Shemer Kuznits's Triumphant Series Finale and Jeff Hays's Emotional Masterpiece of Digital Salvation

The epic journey that began with a betrayed gamer trapped in the body of a level-one goblin reaches its breathtaking, emotional conclusion in *Life Reset: Salvation*. With rogue corporate administrators threatening to purge the entire server architecture and an awakened digital deity preparing to wipe out all organic and artificial consciousness, Oren stands at the head of the largest coalition in virtual history. Marshaling every spell, defensive rune, and allied creature he has nurtured across hundreds of hours of grueling survival, Oren leads a desperate final campaign into the heart of the world engine. The stakes are no longer merely about escaping the game or exacting revenge—they are about winning genuine sovereignty, dignity, and survival for the digital lives he has sworn to protect.

Following the continental conquests of Life Reset: Conquest, *Life Reset: Salvation* delivers a masterclass in narrative closure, emotional resonance, and epic auditory spectacle. Narrators Jeff Hays and Annie Ellicott pour their hearts into this final performance, giving every beloved companion, loyal goblin warrior, and bitter rival a memorable send-off. The climax is an awe-inspiring collision of tactical resource management and catastrophic magical deicide that will leave long-time listeners cheering and wiping away tears. Alongside celebrated speculative conclusions like The Hero of Ages and Words of Radiance, this grand finale cements the saga as one of the crown jewels of our LitRPG & GameLit collection.

Clocking in at nearly seventeen hours of heart-pounding resolution, *Salvation* offers extraordinary credit value and ultimate listening closure. Soundbooth Theater’s state-of-the-art production combines grand orchestral climaxes, crystalline vocal mastering, and thunderous magical sound design that honor the scale of this six-book saga. For listeners who have traveled with Oren from a damp mud hut to the apex of empire, spending a single credit on this triumphant finale is an unforgettable, deeply satisfying experience.
