Ayn Rand\u2019s Monumental Sixty-Three-Hour Epic of Individualism, Industrial Power, and Capitalism in Audio

Set in a dystopian, economically deteriorating United States where suffocating government regulations strangle private enterprise and the country's most capable industrial leaders mysteriously abandon their factories, *Atlas Shrugged* chronicles the desperate battle of railroad executive Dagny Taggart to keep her transcontinental railway operational against impossible odds. Joined by innovative steel magnate Hank Rearden, Dagny confronts bureaucratic incompetence, political corruption, and impending societal collapse, all while hearing the whispered, enigmatic question that haunts the crumbling nation: 'Who is John Galt?' Her quest to save civilization leads her into a secluded Colorado valley where the greatest productive minds of humanity have gone on strike against a parasitic world.
