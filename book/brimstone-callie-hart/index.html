Callie Hart's 24-Hour Sizzling Fae Sequel of War, Royal Conspiracies, and Unbreakable Bonds

Following directly in the explosive footsteps of *Quicksilver*, Callie Hart’s *Brimstone* plunges listeners into nearly twenty-five hours of escalated fae warfare, court conspiracies, and deepening romantic devotion. Having shattered the wards and revealed the shocking truth of her lineage, Saeris Fane must navigate the treacherous political vipers of the Nightlands court, where ancient rivalries and blood feuds threaten to tear the fae realms apart. Standing shoulder-to-shoulder with Kingfisher, Saeris must master her volatile quicksilver magic before an ancient, shadowy army destroys everything she loves, testing their hard-won romantic bond in the crucible of royal war, proving that true love is forged through shared sacrifice, mutual trust, and unflinching courage in the dark.

In audio format, the expansive twenty-five-hour runtime gives Hart’s expanding magical world and ensemble cast the room they need to breathe and develop. The dual narration captures the emotional vulnerability beneath the characters' battle-hardened exteriors, turning court betrayals and tactical military planning into breathless, character-driven drama. Featured prominently in our Epic Romantasy collection alongside Fourth Wing, this unabridged production provides the deliberate pacing and emotional weight needed to make every battlefield sacrifice and passionate reunion feel completely earned, pulling you deep into the heart of the Nightlands' ancient mysteries and royal betrayals.

At twenty-four hours and fifty minutes of rich, high-stakes romantic fantasy, exchanging a single credit or token for *Brimstone* yields incredible listening value. Hart raises the emotional stakes on every front, combining intense action sequences with tender, slow-burning romantic resolution that fantasy lovers will adore. It is an unputdownable, deeply satisfying audio sequel that will keep your headphones glued to your ears from the first skirmish to the epic finale, cementing Callie Hart's series as an essential, unmissable pillar of contemporary romantic fantasy, leaving you completely spellbound by Saeris and Kingfisher’s unbreakable, fire-tested fire-tested love story and thrilling fantasy world.
