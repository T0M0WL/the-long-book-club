About this Audiobook

Set chronologically in the turbulent years between The Black Company and Shadows Linger, Croaker recounts a strange garrison deployment entangled with Taken scheming, twin mysteries, and dormant sorcery threatening the Empire.

Over Fifteen Hours Unearthing a Strange Lost Chronicle Between Books One and Two

Published eighteen years after Soldiers Live yet slotted directly into the quiet six-year gap between The Black Company and Shadows Linger, *Port of Shadows* opens as Croaker and the brotherhood restlessly pull garrison duty inside the remote Aloe region of the Northern Empire. Beneath tedious guard rotations, bizarre phantom girls, dueling Taken sorcerers, and impossible echoes of the Lady's darkest secrets pull the mercenaries into a deceptive shadow-war unlike any entry they thought they recorded.

In audio form, *Port of Shadows* blends nostalgic camp camaraderie with unsettling Dark Fantasy mystery beneath Cook's trademark Grimdark and Military Fantasy pragmatism. Rich in old-school Sword and Sorcery trickery and Low Fantasy soldier grit, it plays delightfully—though veterans agree it hits hardest once you've lived through the original nine books.

Clocking fifteen hours and fourteen minutes for one credit, *Port of Shadows* makes an intriguing, shadowy companion listen right ahead of Lies Weeping.
