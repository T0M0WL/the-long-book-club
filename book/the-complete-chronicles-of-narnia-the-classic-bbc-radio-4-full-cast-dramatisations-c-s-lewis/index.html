The Definitive Fifteen-Hour BBC Radio Dramatisation of C. S. Lewis’s Complete Mythic Saga

C. S. Lewis’s immortal mythic sequence begins with four wartime evacuee siblings tumbling through the back of an old wardrobe into Narnia, a frozen land trapped in perpetual winter by the cruel White Witch, waiting for the return of the Great Lion, Aslan. Spanning all seven volumes—from the creation of the world in *The Magician's Nephew* to the high seas voyage of *The Dawn Treader* and the apocalyptic conclusion of *The Last Battle*—this legendary chronicle explores faith, bravery, betrayal, and redemption. Lewis blends English fairy tales, Christian allegory, and classical mythology to create one of the most beloved and influential fantasy landscapes in world literature.

This celebrated BBC Radio 4 full-cast dramatisation brings the entire Narnian universe to life with timeless theatrical grandeur and peerless British acting talent. The crunch of fresh snow beneath faun hooves, the clashing of iron broadswords on battlefields, the calls of talking beasts, and the gentle, rumbling voice of Aslan resonate with deep acoustic warmth and nostalgic intimacy.
