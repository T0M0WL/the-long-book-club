About this Audiobook

Thirty years after the Free Navy war, the totalitarian Laconian Empire emerges from the Ring with terrifying alien technology.

The Empire Returns: Jefferson Mays Launches The Expanse's Thrilling Final Trilogy

In *Persepolis Rising*, James S.A. Corey executes one of the boldest narrative leaps in science fiction history, jumping forward thirty years to launch the final trilogy of *The Expanse*. The solar system has enjoyed decades of hard-won peace under the Transport Union, and the crew of the *Rocinante* are now graying, veteran legends contemplating retirement. But from beyond the Ring gate of Laconia, high consul Winston Duarte returns with an invincible armada forged from ancient alien protomolecule technology, demanding the unconditional surrender of all human civilization under his new totalitarian empire.

Jefferson Mays captures the passage of time with breathtaking vocal maturity. Hearing Holden, Naomi, Amos, and Bobbie Draper carry the creaking joints and seasoned wisdom of sixty-year-old veterans adds an extraordinary layer of emotional poignancy to the story. Mays makes the terrifying technological superiority of the Laconian dreadnought *Heart of the Tempest* feel chillingly unstoppable. Fans of high-stakes resistance battles like Red Rising and Iron Gold will find this opening salvo of the final trilogy completely gripping.

At twenty and a half hours for a single credit, *Persepolis Rising* is an indispensable audio investment. It breathes fresh, terrifying energy into the universe, turning aging heroes into underground resistance fighters against impossible odds.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jefferson Mays

Jefferson Mays gracefully ages each character's voice, adding gravel and weary patience to Holden and Bobbie Draper without losing their core essence. His voice for Laconian conqueror Santiago Singh perfectly captures the tragic, bureaucratic banality of authoritarianism. Sound design and mastering are flawless across twenty hours.
