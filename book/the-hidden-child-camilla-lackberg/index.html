The Hidden Child

About this Audiobook

Cleaning out her late mother's keepsake chest, Erica Falck discovers a WWII German Nazi medal wrapped in bloodied cloth—and when the retired history teacher who authenticates it is murdered, dark wartime secrets engulf Fjällbacka.

Over Seventeen Hours Unearthing Nazi Collaborators Inside Erica's Own Attic

Prompted after The Gallows Bird to sift through an old chest left by her late mother Elsy, Erica Falck discovers handwritten wartime journals wrapped beside a gleaming WWII Nazi Iron Cross and an unfamiliar bloodstained baby shirt. When the retired Fjällbacka history teacher she visits for answers is brutally bludgeoned inside his library days later, Patrik investigates rising local neo-Nazi hostility while Erica traces a shocking 1940s resistance circle involving her own family.

*The Hidden Child* ranks among Läckberg's grandest achievements in historical Nordic Noir. Interweaving perilous WWII occupation solidarity with modern Police Procedural tension turns intimate family Mystery into gripping Crime and Detective drama.

Spanning seventeen hours and twenty-nine minutes for a single credit, Book 5 delivers absorbing historical sweep right ahead of The Drowning.
