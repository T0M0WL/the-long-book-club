Twenty-One Hours of Tudor Royal Progress, York Murder, and Traitors' Secrets

In the autumn of 1541, an aging, furious King Henry VIII rides north at the head of three thousand armed courtiers on a grand Royal Progress to York, demanding formal submission from subjects still scarred by the Pilgrimage of Grace. Already stationed in the northern capital to process legal petitions—and secretly hired by Archbishop Cranmer to keep a dangerous royal prisoner alive until London interrogation—Matthew Shardlake and Jack Barak soon stumble upon a murdered glazier and a hidden chest of explosive Tudor papers.

Listening to *Sovereign* in audio immerses you completely in the pomp, mud, and claustrophobic terror of Henry VIII's traveling court. Sansom anchors our Historical Mystery and Historical Fiction shelves with this brilliant third volume, balancing provincial Mystery sleuthing inside York Minster's alleys against high-wire Political Intrigue that threatens the line of succession itself.

Spanning twenty hours and fifty-nine minutes for a single monthly credit, *Sovereign* delivers brooding northern atmosphere, tragic historical resonance, and one of Shardlake's bravest moral stands.
