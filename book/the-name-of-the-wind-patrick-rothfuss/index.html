About this Audiobook

I have stolen princesses back from sleeping barrow kings. I burned down the town of Trebon. I have spent the night with Felurian and left with both my sanity and my life.

Patrick Rothfuss’s Poetic, Lyrical Masterpiece of Music, Sympathy Magic, and Legend in Audio

Set in a quiet, rural tavern called the Waystone Inn, Patrick Rothfuss’s spellbinding debut begins with a humble, red-haired innkeeper named Kote. Behind his unassuming apron, however, hides Kvothe: legendary musician, magician, thief, kingkiller, and the most notorious figure in the four corners of civilization. Persuaded by a wandering chronicler to dictate the true story behind the monstrous legends and tavern ballads that surround him, Kvothe recounts his impoverished childhood among the Edema Ruh traveling performers, the traumatic slaughter of his family by the mysterious demonic Chandrian, his years surviving as a feral street urchin in Tarbean, and his desperate, penniless struggle to study sympathy magic at the storied University.

In audio format, Rothfuss’s poetic, musical prose achieves an extraordinary level of auditory enchantment that transcends traditional genre fantasy. Narrator Rupert Degas does not merely read the novel; he fully embodies Kvothe’s soul, turning every plucked lute chord, every mathematical principle of Sympathy, every terrifying encounter with the draccus, and every hushed breath inside the subterranean Archives into pure spoken-word theater. Highlighted as a standout favorite in our Epic Fantasy collection alongside celebrated epics like The Wise Man's Fear and The Way of Kings, this twenty-eight-hour performance turns a young wizard's survival into a cozy, hypnotic fireside confessional that wraps around your imagination.

Offering over twenty-eight hours of sublime storytelling for a single credit, this recording delivers exceptional long-term entertainment value. Rothfuss prioritizes intimate character depth, romantic yearning, and the sheer joy of storytelling over generic battlefield clashes, creating a rich world that rewards patient, repeated listening. Exchanging a single monthly credit for this modern fantasy classic grants you a transportive, fireside listening voyage that will fundamentally rekindle your love for the spoken word, establishing it as an unshakeable cornerstone of contemporary fantasy literature that every audiobook lover must experience.

Narrator Soundcheck: Rupert Degas

Rupert Degas delivers a legendary, career-defining performance that stands as one of the finest audiobook narrations ever committed to digital audio. Degas captures Kvothe’s sharp arrogance, desperate grief, and musical charm with effortless fluidity, giving life to a colorful roster of eccentric University professors like the mercurial Elodin and noble friends like Simmon and Wilem with distinctive accents. His rhythmic delivery of Rothfuss’s lyrical, poetic prose feels entirely natural and conversational across twenty-eight hours. Cleanly engineered with rich dynamic warmth, this narration is an absolute acoustic triumph.
