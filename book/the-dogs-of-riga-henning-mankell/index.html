The Dogs of Riga

Author: Henning Mankell | Narrator: Sean Barrett

Runtime: 10h 04m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Police Procedural, Mystery, Crime, Detective

About this Audiobook

When two battered corpses wash ashore on the bleak southern Swedish coast inside a life raft and a visiting Latvian major is assassinated upon flying home, Inspector Kurt Wallander crosses the Baltic into post-Soviet Riga.

Over Ten Hours Carrying Kurt Wallander Into Bleak Post-Soviet Latvia

Following the grim winter farmhouse nightmare of Faceless Killers, Ystad detective Kurt Wallander expects a quiet spell of routine provincial policing—until an empty orange life raft washes ashore on Scania's icy coast holding two neatly dressed corpses tortured beyond recognition. Traced quickly to a Baltic drug syndicate inside collapsing early-1990s Latvia, Wallander flies across a frozen sea to Riga after his friendly Latvian counterpart is gunned down at home, plunging headlong into surveillance, paranoia, and lethal corruption.

In audio format, *The Dogs of Riga* proves Nordic Noir shines just as brightly inside bleak Cold War wreckage as on windswept Swedish farmland. Mankell trades flashy spy heroics for damp hotel rooms, shadowed alleyways, and dogged Police Procedural patience, letting every modest Detective clue heighten genuine Crime and political Mystery tension.

At ten hours and four minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Dogs of Riga* marks a pivotal widening of Kurt's world—teaching him lessons in grief and institutional rot that echo onward into The White Lioness.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sean Barrett

Sean Barrett settles comfortably back into Kurt Wallander's heavy winter coat for ten gripping hours, balancing exhausted Swedish skepticism against the furtive whispers of post-Soviet Riga. His gravely understated portrayal of Major Liepa's widow Baiba and shady state officials makes every smoky meeting sound effortlessly authentic.
