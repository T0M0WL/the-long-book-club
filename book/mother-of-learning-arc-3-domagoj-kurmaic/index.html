Domagoj Kurmaić's 27-Hour High-Fantasy Odyssey of Imperial Relics and Impending Collapse

Clocking in at over twenty-seven breathtaking hours, *Mother of Learning: Arc 3* represents the epic, high-stakes penultimate movement of Domagoj Kurmaić’s time-loop masterpiece. With the Sovereign Gate entering its terminal degradation cycle, Zorian and Zach face a terrifying reality: the simulated pocket dimension is running out of mana, and if they cannot collect the five imperial keys left behind by the ancient Ikosian Empire, they will be erased when the world resets for the final time. What follows is a globe-trotting quest confronting legendary primordial beasts, ancient liches, and divine arbiters.

In audio format, narrator Jack Voraces orchestrates an astonishing vocal display, managing an expansive roster of non-human entities, arrogant dragon spellcasters, and fanatical cult leaders with theatrical precision. The spoken word magnifies the immense satisfaction of watching Zorian deploy years of accumulated knowledge in grand-scale combat, balancing intricate golem crafting with devastating mind magic. Paralleling the epic scope of The Name of the Wind and the high-speed tactical problem solving of Dungeon Crawler Carl, this volume turns the ticking clock into an electrifying audio marathon.

Exchanging a single Audible credit or token for twenty-seven hours of *Mother of Learning: Arc 3* provides peerless entertainment value. Kurmaić seamlessly weaves multi-threaded plotlines, answering long-standing mysteries about the loop’s architect while setting up a breathtaking finale. Elevated by Jack Voraces’s passionate vocal acting and dynamic pacing, this penultimate doorstopper is an unmissable triumph of modern fantasy worldbuilding.
