The Hanging Girl

About this Audiobook

Guilt-ridden when a retiring Bornholm detective takes his own life hours after Carl dismisses his call, Department Q travels to the windswept Baltic island to investigate a 1997 hit-and-run tied to a secretive sun-worshipping cult.

Over Seventeen Hours Unraveling a Dead Cop's Bornholm Obsession and Sun-Cult Terror

Shaken awake with brutal guilt after brusquely brushing off a retiring Baltic island policeman—only to learn the veteran officer shot himself hours later during his farewell toast—Carl Mørck travels with Assad and Rose from Copenhagen to windswept Bornholm after Buried. Studying the dead detective's meticulous wall of files, Department Q reopens the 1997 fate of nineteen-year-old Alberte Goldschmid, discovered broken and suspended high in a roadside tree under circumstances pointing straight toward a hypnotic Swedish sun-worshipping cult.

Listening to *The Hanging Girl* delivers atmospheric coastal Nordic Noir drenched in salt wind, eerie charisma, and mounting Suspense. Adler-Olsen contrasts cranky Police Procedural footwork against sinister psychological control inside an isolated sect, peeling away layers of a nearly two-decade-old Mystery where every Crime and Detective clue challenges Rose and Carl's bond.

Spanning seventeen hours and seventeen minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Hanging Girl* pairs chilling island creepiness with poignant character payoff leading right into The Scarred Woman.
