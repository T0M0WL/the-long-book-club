About this Audiobook

In 1545 Portsmouth, as French warships gather across the Solent and Henry VIII prepares his flagship Mary Rose, Shardlake investigates a crooked legal guardianship and a nineteen-year-old Bedlam mystery.

Nearly Twenty-Three Hours of Tudor Naval Warfare, Bedlam Secrets, and Legal Corruption

By the summer of 1545, Henry VIII's reckless war with France has pushed England to financial breaking point, and an immense French invasion armada is massing across the Channel toward Portsmouth. Commissioned by an old servant of Queen Catherine Parr to investigate monstrous financial wrongs—and a suspicious death—surrounding an abused young Hampshire ward, Matthew Shardlake and Jack Barak ride straight into a chaotic naval staging port while simultaneously probing the nineteen-year incarceration of young Ellen Fettiplace inside Bedlam.

Experiencing *Heartstone* in audio lifts this fifth entry into pure Historical Fiction and Historical Mystery brilliance. Sansom builds slowly from intimate domestic legal sleuthing—full of cunning Whodunnit twists—toward the unforgettable maritime set-piece aboard King Henry's doomed flagship, the *Mary Rose*, ringing out across Portsmouth Sound.

At twenty-two hours and forty-two minutes for a single credit, *Heartstone* balances compassionate courtroom justice with sweeping naval tragedy on an epic scale.
