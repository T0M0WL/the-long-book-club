About this Audiobook

After losing his supernatural gift to commune with the dead, Thara Celehar is kidnapped by desperate miners into a haunted mountain vault—where a mass grave of slain dragons demands justice before Emperor Maia's court.

Over Eleven Hours of Haunted Mines and Imperial Justice Closing the Cemeteries of Amalo

When a violent clash with a revenant severs Thara Celehar's supernatural gift to commune with spirits, the quiet prelate faces a devastating crisis of identity: who is he when he can no longer serve as Witness for the Dead? Struggling through municipal cemetery bureaucracy while guiding his apprentice, Celehar is abducted into remote mountain mines by desperate laborers hunted in the dark—only to uncover the restless, wrathful tomb of nearly two hundred massacred dragons demanding justice before The Goblin Emperor himself.

On audio, *The Tomb of Dragons* brings Katherine Addison's *Cemeteries of Amalo* arc full circle, uniting the quiet grief of Mystery and Low Fantasy with high-stakes Court Intrigue and radiant High Fantasy majesty inside our Cozy Fantasy.

Spanning eleven hours and twenty-seven minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Tomb of Dragons* is a deeply earned, emotionally healing finale that proves ordinary kindness can shake empires.
