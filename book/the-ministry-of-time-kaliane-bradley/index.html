The Ministry of Time

Author: Kaliane Bradley | Narrator: Katie Leung, George Weightman

Runtime: 10h 23m | Genre: Science Fiction, Time Travel, Romance, Espionage, Historical Fiction, Thriller

About this Audiobook

A civil servant is assigned as 'bridge' to an Arctic explorer displaced from 1847 into modern London, uncovering a deep conspiracy inside Britain's secret time travel program.

Kaliane Bradley's 10-Hour Masterclass in Bureaucratic Satire, Victorian Displacement, and High-Stakes Espionage

Spanning ten-and-a-half sharply inventive hours, *The Ministry of Time* by Kaliane Bradley is a fresh, razor-sharp collision of speculative time travel, governmental workplace satire, and slow-burn historical romance. Set in near-future London, the British civil service discovers the technology to extract individuals moments before their historical deaths without disrupting the timeline. An unnamed female civil servant is assigned as a "bridge"—a handler, roommate, and cultural translator—to Commander Graham Gore, an upright 19th-century naval officer rescued from Sir John Franklin's doomed 1845 Arctic expedition. What begins as a deadpan domestic comedy of manners as Gore grapples with washing machines, Spotify, and post-imperial Britain gradually spirals into a lethal counter-intelligence conspiracy that threatens the temporal continuum itself.

In audio format, the dry, ironic wit of Bradley's narration crackles with magnetic energy. The spoken performance highlights the hilarious linguistic frictions between Gore's Victorian stoicism and his handler's weary, contemporary bureaucratic cynicism, turning domestic dinner debates and civil service memos into pure auditory gold. As the romantic tension simmers beneath the surface, the audio medium captures the unspoken, harrowing grief of temporal exile—the realization that everyone Gore ever loved turned to dust over a century ago. Echoing the quiet bureaucratic paranoia of our British Espionage masterworks like Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy and the high-stakes historical immersion of 11/22/63, this recording delivers unmatched psychological depth.

Investing a single monthly credit in ten hours of *The Ministry of Time* yields an exceptionally satisfying, fast-paced listening experience. Bradley accomplishes what few modern speculative debuts manage: balancing laugh-out-loud satirical dialogue with genuine ethical dread regarding state power, surveillance, and historical consequence. Driven by charismatic, nuanced voice acting and airtight pacing, this smart, subversive thriller is one of the most original and rewarding standalone listens of recent years.

Narrator Soundcheck: Katie Leung, George Weightman

Katie Leung and George Weightman deliver a sublime, charismatic narration that captures the dry wit and emotional pathos of Bradley's speculative premise. Katie Leung anchors the audiobook with pitch-perfect deadpan timing as the cynical modern handler, subtly revealing the vulnerability beneath her bureaucratic armor as affection deepens. George Weightman gives Commander Graham Gore an authentic, resonant mid-19th-century naval gravitas, impeccably articulating Gore’s stoic grief and dry bewilderment without slipping into caricature. With crisp sound engineering and flawless comedic pacing, this is an exemplary dual-cast production.
