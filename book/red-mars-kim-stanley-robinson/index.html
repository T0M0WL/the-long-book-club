About this Audiobook

In 2026, one hundred elite scientists and engineers land on a frozen, airless Mars to build humanity's first off-world society—igniting a fierce ideological war between terraforming visionaries and ecological preservationists.

Nearly Twenty-Four Monumental Hours Launching Hard Sci-Fi's Greatest Terraforming Epic

Winner of the Nebula Award and widely hailed as the pinnacle of hard planetary science fiction, Kim Stanley Robinson’s *Red Mars* drops listeners straight onto the frost-rimmed canyons of the fourth planet in 2026 alongside the First Hundred—an international cohort of scientists, engineers, and dreamers tasked with turning a dead rock into humanity's second home. Almost immediately, idealism collides with ethics as two factions emerge: the 'Greens,' led by visionary geneticist Sax Russell, who want to thaw Mars's aquifers and seed living ecosystems, and the 'Reds,' championed by fierce geologist Ann Clayborne, who argue the planet's pristine wilderness deserves protection from human conquest.

In unabridged audio, *Red Mars* transforms what can feel on the page like dense scientific exposition into a hypnotic, deeply human chronicle of survival, ambition, and sabotage. As orbital mirrors catch the sun, nuclear moholes warm the permafrost, and Earth's corporate megastates reach across the void to strip-mine the frontier, Richard Ferrone’s grounded delivery turns every town-hall argument, rover expedition across Olympus Mons, and whispered revolutionary conspiracy into taut dramatic theatre. Bridging the classic hard-science ambition of Seveneves with the ecological foresight of Robinson’s later Solarpunk landmark The Ministry for the Future, this opening chapter lays the bedrock for an unforgettable 83-hour trilogy.

Clocking in at twenty-three hours and fifty-one minutes for a single Audible credit, *Red Mars* delivers towering value for listeners who crave plausible science, intricate politics, and genuine planetary scale. By the time the first Martian revolution erupts beneath an amber dust storm, you will be reaching immediately for Green Mars and Blue Mars.

Narrator Soundcheck: Richard Ferrone

Richard Ferrone brings weathered, documentary-style gravitas and effortless narrative stamina to nearly twenty-four demanding hours of Martian geology, orbital physics, and frontier politics. His gravelly, measured baritone differentiates Russian cosmonauts, idealistic American engineers, and stoic scientists without ever slipping into caricature—turning Robinson's lyrical descriptions of Martian dust storms and canyon climbs into immersive, deeply grounded audio.
