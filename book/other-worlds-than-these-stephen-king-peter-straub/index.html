About this Audiobook

Concluding The Talisman trilogy begun over four decades earlier, Stephen King brings Jack Sawyer back into the crumbling borderlands between our world, the Territories, and the looming shadow of Mid-World's Dark Tower.

Nearly Twenty-Three Hours Bringing The Talisman Trilogy to a Dark Tower Climax

Over forty years after Stephen King and the late Peter Straub first sent young Jack Sawyer across the Territories in *The Talisman*—and twenty-five years after *Black House*—King delivers the monumental closing volume, *Other Worlds Than These*. Drawing directly on Straub’s final notes and weaving the Territories tighter than ever into the cosmic mythology of *The Dark Tower*, the novel sends an older, battle-scarred Jack back across crumbling realities where Mid-World’s shadows bleed dangerously into our own.

For Constant Readers and lovers of sprawling Dark Fantasy, the audio format brings out the warm, campfire intimacy of King’s late-career storytelling alongside his sharpest Horror instincts. Standing proudly among our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 and right next to King titans like It and The Stand, it feels like both a thrilling Portal Fantasy climax and a heartfelt tribute to a lifelong creative partnership.

At twenty-two hours and forty-five minutes for a single Audible credit—capped by King himself reading the moving afterword—this is essential 2026 event listening.

Narrator Soundcheck: Patton Oswalt

Casting Patton Oswalt might look surprising on paper, yet across nearly twenty-three hours he proves an inspired, deeply committed steward of King and Straub's dark multiverse. Oswalt brings sharp comic timing to the lighter moments, genuine gruff warmth to Jack Sawyer, and chilling restraint when the supernatural dread closes in—with Stephen King's own afterword delivering a lump-in-the-throat finish.
