About this Audiobook

In the wake of betrayal at the Circus, George Smiley launches a counter-offensive through Southeast Asia, sending an old-school operative into the chaotic jungles of Hong Kong and Cambodia.

The Sweeping 20-Hour Karla Epic That Takes George Smiley to the Far East

Spanning twenty and a half gripping hours, *The Honourable Schoolboy* is the monumental second volume of John le Carré’s legendary Karla Trilogy, bridging the boardroom betrayals of Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy with the climactic duel of *Smiley's People*. In the immediate aftermath of uncovering a high-level Soviet mole at the heart of the British Secret Service ('the Circus'), George Smiley is appointed Chief of a humiliated, cash-strapped agency. Determined to prove the Circus still possesses teeth and strike back at his Soviet nemesis Karla, Smiley uncovers a clandestine Soviet laundering pipeline funnelling hundreds of thousands of dollars into a Hong Kong trust bank.

To unravel the conspiracy, Smiley dispatches Jerry Westerby—the 'honourable schoolboy' of the title, an aristocratic journalist, romantic, and occasional field operative. Le Carré moves the action from the rainy, tea-stained basements of Cambridge Circus out into the humid, chaotic heat of Southeast Asia during the waning days of the Vietnam War. From the colonial watering holes of Hong Kong to the opium routes of Laos and the mortar-shelled perimeter of Phnom Penh, Westerby tracks down a reclusive Russian agent and a billionaire triad financier. The audio format turns this ambitious geopolitical duel into an atmospheric tour-de-force, contrasting Smiley’s intellectual chess game with Westerby’s perilous physical scramble through warzones.

For espionage enthusiasts who crave rich character observation, moral ambiguity, and immersive historical atmosphere from a single credit, *The Honourable Schoolboy* is an exceptional listen. Simon Russell Beale delivers a definitive performance that captures every whisper of civil-service cynicism and jungle desperation. Paired with companion classics like Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy and A Perfect Spy, it stands as an absolute high-water mark of British Espionage audio fiction.

Narrator Soundcheck: Simon Russell Beale

Simon Russell Beale delivers a masterclass in espionage characterization, bringing immense subtlety and nuance to le Carré’s dense narrative. Beale inhabits George Smiley with weary, cerebral precision, while seamlessly transitioning to Jerry Westerby’s bumbling, aristocratic cheer and the world-weary accents of Hong Kong expats and war correspondents. Beale’s deliberate, atmospheric pacing pulls listeners directly into the smoke-filled briefing rooms of Whitehall and the humid danger of Southeast Asian back-alleys, making twenty hours fly by with cinematic suspense.
