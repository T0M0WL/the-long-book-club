About this Audiobook

The story of a boy born in Appalachia to a teenaged single mother in a single-wide trailer.

Barbara Kingsolver\u2019s Pulitzer-Winning Twenty-One-Hour Appalachian Epic of Survival in Audio

Transposing Charles Dickens\u2019s Victorian masterpiece into the rugged, impoverished hollows of contemporary southwestern Virginia, Barbara Kingsolver\u2019s Pulitzer Prize-winning *Demon Copperhead* is a ferocious, heartbreaking, and fiercely comic American epic. Born in a trailer to a teenage, substance-dependent mother, red-headed Damon Fields\u2014known to all as 'Demon Copperhead'\u2014navigates the foster care meat-grinder, brutal agricultural child labor, athletic glory on the high school football gridiron, and the devastating deluge of prescription opioids that ravages his Appalachian community, all while armed with a survivor's razor-sharp wit and an instinctive artistic gift that refuses to be extinguished.

On audio, Kingsolver\u2019s modern masterpiece achieves an electrifying, visceral intimacy that makes the Appalachian hills breathe through your headphones with undeniable authenticity and emotional force. Narrator Charlie Thurston gives an Audie Award-winning tour-de-force performance, delivering Demon\u2019s first-person voice with an unforgettable regional cadence, swaggering humor, and devastating emotional vulnerability. Positioned as a crowning pinnacle in our Literary Fiction and Classics collections alongside its literary forebear David Copperfield and The Goldfinch, this twenty-one-hour recording turns systemic poverty and addiction into an immortal, life-affirming auditory triumph of human survival and spirit.

Delivering over twenty-one hours of masterclass American prose and storytelling for a single credit, this release represents one of the finest returns on investment in contemporary fiction. Kingsolver gives profound voice and dignity to an often-stereotyped culture, celebrating the resilience, dark humor, and communal loyalty of people fighting for survival against impossible odds. Exchanging a monthly credit for this modern landmark secures an unforgettable listening companion that will break your heart and restore your faith in human resilience in equal measure, leaving you in awe of the sheer power of storytelling to illuminate the forgotten corners of our society, expose institutional failure, and celebrate the unbreakable human spirit through thick and thin, making this Pulitzer Prize-winning novel an essential auditory monument of our time.

Narrator Soundcheck: Charlie Thurston

Charlie Thurston delivers a breathtaking, career-defining performance that earned the Audie Award for Audiobook of the Year. Thurston embodies Demon Copperhead with an authentic, pitch-perfect Appalachian accent that captures every shade of the narrator's sarcastic humor, weary trauma, and stubborn survival instinct without ever sounding caricatured. His voicing of secondary characters\u2014from the abusive foster father to the eccentric grandmother\u2014is wonderfully distinct and natural. Pristine studio production and superb audio fidelity ensure that this twenty-one-hour emotional journey resonates with stunning acoustic power.
