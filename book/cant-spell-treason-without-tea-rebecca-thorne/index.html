About this Audiobook

The Queen’s elite bodyguard and the realm’s most powerful sorceress fake their retirements to flee to a quiet border town and open their dream tea and bookshop.

A Delightful Cozy Fantasy Tale of Runaway Royals, Artisanal Tea Blends, and Starting Anew

Rebecca Thorne’s charming cozy fantasy takes two of the most overpowered archetypes in high fantasy—the realm’s deadliest royal bodyguard and its most destructive archmage—and asks a wonderfully subversive question: what if the greatest heroes simply want to retire, bake fresh scones, and open a quiet tea shop together? Reyna and Kianthe have spent years sacrificing their youth, peace of mind, and sanity for a tyrannical, ungrateful queen. Deciding that mutual love and peaceful domesticity outweigh bloody crown duty, the pair secretly desert the capital, loading a sturdy wooden carriage with rare tea leaves, pastry supplies, and stolen spell tomes to escape across the frontier to the sleepy, storm-battered mountain border town of Tawney.

On audio, the cozy dream of opening 'The Tea & Tome' bursts into vibrant, sensory life through every crackling log and steaming cup. Hearing Reyna methodically plan daily bookkeeping and cozy plumbing repairs while Kianthe casually uses world-shattering pyromancy to light the tavern hearth and boil kettle water delivers endless auditory amusement and charm. Showcased as a standout favorite in our Cozy Fantasy Collection alongside coffee-shop comfort hits like Legends & Lattes and The Spellshop, this recording turns domestic renovation, local gossip, and cinnamon tea into an irresistible haven that protects listeners from everyday real-world anxiety.

Offering eleven and a half hours of pure, lighthearted escapism, this unabridged audio release represents supreme single-credit value for fans of low-stakes comfort. Thorne masterfully balances quiet domestic bliss and cozy teashop building against just enough high-fantasy excitement—from wandering mountain gryphons to creeping royal crown assassins—to keep the plot lively without ever shattering its soothing, comforting atmosphere. Spending a monthly token on this warm romance secures a joyful, uplifting audio companion that celebrates queer love, artisanal baking, and the profound courage it takes to build a peaceful, gentle life on your own terms.

Narrator Soundcheck: Kimberley Farr

Kimberley Farr delivers a delightfully witty, energetic narration that perfectly captures the affectionate chemistry and contrasting personalities of Thorne’s dual heroines. Farr voices Reyna with disciplined, grounded composure that reflects her elite soldier training, while infusing Kianthe with theatrical, booming exuberance and playful warmth. Her comedic timing during domestic mishaps—such as magical tea-kettle explosions—is sharp and lively, complemented by tender, heartfelt romantic cadence in quieter evening scenes. Clean acoustic production and crisp diction ensure an engaging, cozy listening experience from opening chapter to closing cup.
