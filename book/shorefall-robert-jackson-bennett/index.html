About this Audiobook

As an ancient, terrifying hierophant is resurrected in Tevanne, Sancia Grado and her crew must pit their underground scriving workshop against an immortal being who rewrites human minds.

Robert Jackson Bennett Escalates Industrial Espionage into God-Tier Warfare in Audio

Set three years after the explosive events of *Foundryside*, *Shorefall* elevates the stakes from rooftop burglary to an existential battle for human consciousness. Sancia, Gregor, and their underground foundry have spent years trying to democratize scriving, offering cheap magical innovations to the impoverished masses outside the merchant enclaves. But when a rival merchant patriarch resurrects Crasedes the Great—the first and most terrifying hierophant who invented scriving thousands of years ago—the city of Tevanne faces total annihilation. Crasedes does not merely alter objects; he rewrites human minds, bending entire populations into a singular, devoted hive mind.

Listening to this nineteen-and-a-half-hour sequel is a breathless, white-knuckle experience. Tara Sands returns with elevated dramatic intensity, bringing terrifying gravitas to Crasedes's calm, inescapable godhood while capturing the desperate camaraderie of Sancia’s ragtag crew. Beside companion fantasy epics like The Broken Eye and Jade War, this production showcases how an inventive hard magic system can create genuine, terrifying horror when pushed to its logical extreme.

Offering nearly twenty hours of non-stop momentum for a single credit, *Shorefall* is an unmissable sequel that deepens every character relationship. Bennett subverts typical fantasy tropes, challenging his heroes to outsmart an immortal god using logic, teamwork, and scientific ingenuity rather than brute force. It is an exceptional audio recording that will leave you desperate for the final volume.

Narrator Soundcheck: Tara Sands

Tara Sands turns in an exceptional, emotionally charged performance across nineteen and a half hours. Sands effortlessly handles the escalated dramatic scope, shifting between intimate romantic warmth between Sancia and Berenice and the chilling, omnipotent detachment of the resurrected hierophant Crasedes. Her action pacing during magical chase sequences through Tevanne’s canals is electrifying, backed by immaculate audio engineering where every scrived command rings out with crystalline clarity.
