About this Audiobook

A saintly, innocent prince returns to St. Petersburg society, only for his boundless compassion to ignite a destructive vortex of jealousy, money, and murder.

Dostoevsky's Fiery 25-Hour Portrait of Holy Innocence in a Corrupted World

Spanning twenty-five riveting hours, *The Idiot* represents Fyodor Dostoevsky’s most deeply personal and philosophically daring creation, completing his holy triumvirate alongside Crime and Punishment and The Brothers Karamazov. The novel follows Prince Lev Nikolayevich Myshkin, an impoverished young nobleman returning to Russia by train after spending years in a Swiss sanatorium being treated for epilepsy. Gentle, completely open, and incapable of deceit, Myshkin enters St. Petersburg high society as a 'positively good and beautiful man'—a modern Russian Christ figure whose pure honesty acts as a mirror that exposes the greed, vanity, and cynical calculations of everyone around him.

Myshkin finds himself drawn into a catastrophic romantic and psychological tempest centered on Nastasya Filippovna, a dazzling, self-destructive woman tormented by her past, and Rogozhin, a brooding merchant consumed by murderous, obsessive passion. Dostoevsky stages unforgettable, claustrophobic salon set-pieces where fortunes are thrown into fire grates and life-or-death confessions erupt in front of gasping dinner parties. The audio format allows Dostoevsky’s feverish psychological dialogues to breathe with electric theatricality, making the novel's climactic vigil one of the most chilling, emotionally devastating sequences in all of world literature.

For audiobook aficionados seeking transcendent philosophical depth and volcanic dramatic power for a single credit, *The Idiot* is an incomparable investment. Constantine Gregory’s legendary Naxos AudioBooks recording brings decades of Russian vocal mastery to every line, giving Myshkin’s angelic gentleness and Rogozhin’s demonic fury unforgettable weight. Standing tall beside The Brothers Karamazov and Tolstoy's Anna Karenina, this twenty-five-hour tour-de-force is an indispensable pillar of Classics listening.

Narrator Soundcheck: Constantine Gregory

Constantine Gregory delivers an astonishing, definitive interpretation of Dostoevsky’s psychological masterpiece. Gregory captures Prince Myshkin’s soft, trembling sincerity with heartbreaking nuance, while imbuing the mercurial Nastasya Filippovna with tragic grandeur and Rogozhin with raspy, volcanic intensity. Gregory’s deep familiarity with Russian cadence and dramatic pauses ensures twenty-five hours flow with mesmerizing emotional tension, turning nineteenth-century salon squabbles into high-stakes moral combat.
