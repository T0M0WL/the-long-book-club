About this Audiobook

Hunted by renegade Asha'man, Rand forms an audacious bond and joins forces with Nynaeve to attempt the unthinkable: cleansing the taint on Saidin forever.

A Watershed Fantasy Triumph Culminating in the Monumental Cleansing of the Male Half of the True Source

In *Winter's Heart*, Robert Jordan delivers one of the most pivotal and consequential turning points in epic fantasy history. On the run from traitorous Asha'man who attempted to assassinate him in the Sun Palace, Rand al'Thor travels covertly to the city of Far Madding—a sanctuary protected by a ter'angreal that prevents all channeling. After executing a daring rooftop hunt with his sword, Rand prepares for the ultimate gamble: traveling to the outskirts of Shadar Logoth alongside Nynaeve to channel the unfathomable power of the Choedan Kal and siphon the Dark One's oily taint off Saidin once and for all.

On audio, the climax of *Winter's Heart* is an absolute masterpiece of tension and auditory spectacle. Narrators Michael Kramer and Kate Reading alternate points of view as the Forsaken descend like hungry wolves upon Shadar Logoth, while Cadsuane and Rand's loyal defenders wage a desperate perimeter war to protect the Cleansing weave. Kramer’s delivery of the momentous psychic conduit between Rand and Nynaeve—and Reading’s heart-stopping account of the duel between Verin and the Forsaken—delivers spine-tingling thrills. Linked prominently in our Epic Fantasy collection, this production elevates a core cosmological shift into an unforgettable auditory milestone.

Spanning twenty-four hours and twelve minutes, this volume provides immense credit value for fantasy listeners. Alongside the world-altering magic of the finale, Jordan gives listeners Mat Cauthon’s hilarious kidnapping of Tuon, the Daughter of the Nine Moons, and Perrin’s relentless, agonizing pursuit of the captured Faile. A single credit unlocks twenty-four hours of sublime voice acting and monumental story payoffs that fundamentally alter the trajectory of the entire series.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer, Kate Reading

Narrators Michael Kramer and Kate Reading bring magnificent stamina and emotional weight to Winter's Heart. Kramer’s handling of the Far Madding rooftop chases and the apocalyptic Cleansing finale is extraordinary, capturing both intimate character vulnerability and colossal magical forces with equal aplomb. Kate Reading excels with Nynaeve’s courageous resolve and Tuon’s imperious, mysterious dignity during Mat’s chaotic caravan journey. Pristine studio production balances deafening explosions of One Power weaves with quiet romantic pledges, creating a deeply satisfying listening experience across all twenty-four hours.
