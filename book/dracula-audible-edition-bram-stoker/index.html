Bram Stoker’s Legendary Victorian Gothic Vampire Masterpiece in an All-Star Full-Cast Edition

Bram Stoker’s 1897 Gothic horror masterpiece unfolds as a chilling mosaic of journal entries, diary reflections, phonograph recordings, and newspaper clippings that chronicle Count Dracula’s sinister invasion of Victorian London. From young solicitor Jonathan Harker’s harrowing captivity within the crumbling battlements of Castle Dracula in the Carpathian Mountains to the mysterious, ghost-ship arrival of the schooner *Demeter* in Whitby harbour, Stoker crafts a deeply unsettling exploration of ancient contagion, Victorian sexuality, and psychological dread. When Lucy Westenra falls victim to a wasting nocturnal disease, Dr. John Seward summons the eccentric Dutch metaphysician Abraham Van Helsing to lead an intrepid band of defenders in a desperate battle to destroy the King Vampire.

In audio format, Stoker’s innovative epistolary structure flourishes with extraordinary dramatic vitality and raw emotional intimacy. Hearing each character’s private diaries and hurried telegrams spoken aloud by distinct veteran actors turns the gothic novel into a terrifying series of intimate, first-hand witness accounts.
