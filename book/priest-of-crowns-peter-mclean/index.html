Priest of Crowns

Author: Peter McLean | Narrator: David Morley Hale

Runtime: 11h 50m | Genre: Grimdark Fantasy, Crime, Low Fantasy, Dark Fantasy, Grand Finale, Gangland

About this Audiobook

In the bloody conclusion of the War for the Rose Throne, Tomas Piety reaches for ultimate power in Dannsburg as the Queen's Men and rival lords clash.

The Final Purge, the Gilded Noose, and the Shattering Destiny of Tomas Piety

Spanning nearly twelve intense, blood-drenched hours, *Priest of Crowns* delivers the devastating, magnificent conclusion to Peter McLean’s *War for the Rose Throne* tetralogy. Tomas Piety’s meteoric ascent from an orphaned slum extortionist to a high-ranking lord has culminated in the royal court of Dannsburg, where the King lies dying and rival factions prepare to seize the Rose Throne. Armed with royal magistracy, a ruthless private army, and the horrifying psychic weapons of Billy the Boy, Tomas prepares to execute the greatest criminal takeover in the nation's history.

Yet every step toward supreme power demands an unbearable toll. As the Queen's Men, treasonous archbishops, and foreign assassins circle, Tomas is forced to turn his cold, calculating tradecraft against his closest friends, purging dissent with ruthless efficiency. McLean pulls no punches, providing a dark, Shakespearean exploration of how ruthless ambition destroys the human heart from within. As street riots flare and the guillotine looms, Tomas faces the ultimate question of whether power gained through bloodshed can ever bring peace. The climactic street battles across Dannsburg are visceral and unsparing, and the final revelation regarding who orchestrates the Rose Throne will leave listeners stunned by its tragic brilliance.

Narrator David Morley Hale delivers a career-defining performance across the nearly twelve-hour runtime. Hale’s vocalization of Tomas’s weary, cold-blooded resignation is chillingly authentic, giving his final confessions a haunting emotional gravity. For listeners who crave grimdark fantasy on par with Joe Abercrombie’s The First Law and criminal drama like Peaky Blinders, *Priest of Crowns* is an absolute masterpiece of audio fiction that delivers staggering credit value.

Narrator Soundcheck: David Morley Hale

David Morley Hale’s performance on Priest of Crowns is a magnificent, chilling achievement in audio narration. Hale delivers Tomas Piety’s grim, fatalistic internal confessions with extraordinary restraint, never raising his voice above a dangerous, conversational murmur. His handling of the fast-paced, bloody melee encounters across Dannsburg is visceral and punchy, while the emotional final scenes hit with devastating dramatic weight. Pristinely engineered throughout, this is a five-star grimdark performance that lingers long in memory after the final chapter.
