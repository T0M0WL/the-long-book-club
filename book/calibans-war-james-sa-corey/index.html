About this Audiobook

A terrifying biological weapon devastates Ganymede, drawing Holden, Avasarala, and Bobbie Draper into an interplanetary crisis.

The Benchmark of Modern Sci-Fi: Jefferson Mays Unleashes Avasarala and Bobbie Draper

In *Caliban’s War*, James S.A. Corey expands *The Expanse* into the stratosphere of modern space opera by introducing two of the most iconic female characters in the genre: foul-mouthed UN deputy undersecretary Chrisjen Avasarala and badass Martian marine Bobbie Draper. When a bioengineered protomolecule hybrid slaughters Bobbie's squad on the agricultural Jovian moon of Ganymede, a frantic father's search for his missing four-year-old daughter pulls Jim Holden and the crew of the *Rocinante* straight into an interplanetary conspiracy designed to weaponize alien biology.

Narrator Jefferson Mays gives a performance of pure genius. His raspy, imperious, and hilariously profanity-laden delivery for Avasarala is widely considered one of the greatest character voices in audiobook history. Mays balances Avasarala’s sharp political calculations with Bobbie’s stoic martial grit and Holden’s stubborn moral crusade, making every chapter crackle with narrative momentum. If you enjoyed the gritty realism of Leviathan Wakes or the expansive scope of Shards of Earth, this sequel is an absolute joy.

At twenty-one hours on a single Audible credit, *Caliban’s War* is a gold-standard investment. It turns complex Newtonian orbital physics and realpolitik into pure, unadulterated auditory entertainment.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jefferson Mays

Jefferson Mays delivers a five-star masterclass in voice acting. His vocal creation of Chrisjen Avasarala—a smoky, elderly Indian diplomat with zero patience for fools—is an audio institution. His pacing during high-G combat burns and zero-gravity gunfights is electrifying, complemented by pristine studio engineering.
