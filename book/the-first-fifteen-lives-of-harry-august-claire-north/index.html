About this Audiobook

Harry August is an kalachakra, living his life over and over with memories intact, until a child visits his deathbed with a warning that the world is ending too soon.

Claire North's 12-Hour Masterpiece of High-Stakes Temporal Espionage and Ouroboros Reincarnation

Spanning over twelve thrilling hours, *The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August* by Claire North is a breathtaking, genre-defining masterpiece of circular time and philosophical espionage. Born in a women’s restroom at Berwick-upon-Tweed railway station in 1919, Harry August is an *ouroboran*—an immortal who is reborn into the exact same life upon death, retaining the complete memories, knowledge, and traumas of every previous existence. Living through World War II, studying medicine, enduring institutional torture, and quietly accumulating generational wealth across multiple lifetimes, Harry’s existence is upended when an eleven-year-old girl visits his bedside in 1989 on his eleventh deathbed with a terrifying transmission from the future: the world is ending too fast, accelerated by a rogue kalachakra who is breaking the club's sacred rules by introducing advanced quantum technology before its time.

On audio, narrator Peter Kenny delivers a masterclass in psychological intimacy, bringing Harry's centuries of weary intellect and quiet determination directly into the listener's headphones. The audio presentation elevates the cat-and-mouse dynamic between Harry and his brilliant, sociopathic rival Vincent Rankis, making their multi-decade chess match spanning universities, research laboratories, and Cold War spy agencies crackle with intellectual tension. Where print can sometimes blur the complex chronological jumps between Harry's first, sixth, and eleventh lives, Kenny’s crystal-clear vocal inflection anchors the listener effortlessly through every historical era. Echoing the psychological tradecraft of our British Espionage classics and the historical depth of Stephen King's 11/22/63, this audio production turns an extraordinary speculative premise into a gripping human drama.

Claire North crafts an airtight, deeply poignant narrative that explores what it truly means to live a meaningful life when death is merely a reset button. Elevated by Peter Kenny’s peerless narration, this standalone speculative triumph delivers gripping tension, exquisite prose, and an emotionally staggering climax that will linger in your mind for years.

Narrator Soundcheck: Peter Kenny

Peter Kenny delivers an unforgettable, career-defining performance that perfectly embodies Harry August's world-weary wisdom, dry British irony, and enduring moral compass. Kenny’s warm, articulate delivery navigates the novel’s non-linear chronological shifts with effortless grace, providing instant auditory cues for Harry’s youthful exuberance, middle-aged exhaustion, and ancient soul. His vocalizations for secondary characters across Europe, America, and Russia are impeccably researched and authentic, while his pacing during high-stakes interrogations and quantum laboratory showdowns sustains heart-stopping suspense throughout twelve flawless hours of audio.
