About this Audiobook

Stranded nearly destitute in 1585 Paris on the brink of civil war, Giordano Bruno turns to an old friend—a fiery priest who is promptly murdered, dragging Bruno deep into Guise plots against the French crown.

Sixteen Hours of Wars-of-Religion Intrigue Inside Volatile 1585 Paris

Forced to flee London in late 1585 without patronage or money, Giordano Bruno washes back up in Paris—a city sliding rapidly toward bloody civil war as King Henri III's fragile court clashes with the fanatical Catholic League under the House of Guise. Seeking shelter from an old acquaintance, fiery parish priest Paul Lefevre, Bruno walks straight into a brutal assassination on Parisian cobblestones—leaving him suspected of murder and ensnared in a lethal conspiracy to topple both French and English thrones.

On audio, *Conspiracy* invigorates the series with gritty continental atmosphere. Swapping foggy London for the teeming, mud-choked streets of Renaissance Paris, Parris blends breathless street-level Mystery with ruthless continental Political Intrigue, making this fifth volume an essential landmark of Elizabethan Historical Mystery and Historical Fiction.

At sixteen hours and fifteen minutes for one monthly credit, *Conspiracy* is razor-sharp, atmospheric, and showcases Bruno at his most resourceful when he has nowhere left to run.

Taking over the series microphone for Book 5, British actor Daniel Philpott proves a seamless, compelling new voice for Bruno across sixteen hours. Philpott adopts a lighter, agile Italian inflection for our wandering philosopher and handles Parisian nobles, furious priests, and street cutthroats with effortless fluency.
