About this Audiobook

Traduttore, traditore: An act of translation is always an act of betrayal.

R.F. Kuang's 21-Hour Linguistic Fantasy of Colonial Exploitation, Oxford Towers, and Magic Silver

Subtitled *An Arcane History of the Oxford Translators' Revolution*, R.F. Kuang’s *Babel* is an intellectually staggering, twenty-one-hour fantasy epic that interrogates the brutal linguistic foundations of British imperial power. Set in an alternate 1830s where Britain’s global supremacy is fueled by enchanted silver bars inscribed with foreign translation match-pairs that harvest the meaning lost between tongues, the story follows Robin Swift, an orphaned Chinese boy groomed from childhood to enter Oxford’s prestigious Royal Institute of Translation. Alongside a close-knit cohort of international prodigies, Robin uncovers the predatory economic machine driving Victorian expansion, forcing him to choose between the safety of academic privilege and the moral imperative of violent revolution, forcing him to confront what must be sacrificed when scholarship serves oppression.

Audio is undeniably the premier format through which to experience Kuang’s multilingual tour de force. Rather than struggling with complex phonetic footnotes and shifting etymological derivations across Mandarin, Latin, Greek, Arabic, and French on the printed page, a gifted narrator speaks them aloud with natural cadence and scholarly authority. Anchored proudly in our curated Dark Academia collection and sharing thematic rebellion with The Secret History, the unabridged recording provides the expansive temporal space needed to turn collegiate camaraderie, Victorian racism, and philosophical debates on political violence into heart-pounding dramatic tension, allowing the collegiate camaraderie to feel authentic and deep.

At nearly twenty-two hours of rich, thought-provoking narrative, spending a single credit on *Babel* delivers unmatched educational and emotional value. Kuang marries the intellectual rigor of an advanced university seminar with the breathless momentum of a revolutionary thriller, creating a story that fundamentally re-evaluates the ethics of scholarship and empire. It is a vital, unforgettable listening experience that will linger in your thoughts long after the final bells of Oxford chime, making it one of the most powerful and provocative speculative fiction experiences of the decade.

Narrator Soundcheck: Chris Lew Kum Hoi

Chris Lew Kum Hoi delivers a virtuosic, Audie-worthy performance that navigates Kuang’s dense multilingual requirements with effortless academic fluency and emotional precision. Lew Kum Hoi moves between Cantonese, Mandarin, French, and Latin dialogue while maintaining crystal-clear English diction and distinct character timbres for the central student quartet. His deliberate pacing prevents intricate etymological footnotes from ever bogging down the plot, ratcheting up dramatic tension toward the tragic, revolutionary climax with immaculate studio sound quality throughout all twenty-one hours.
