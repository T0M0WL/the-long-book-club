About this Audiobook

Set on the desert planet Arrakis, Dune is the story of the boy Paul Atreides, who would become the mysterious man known as Muad'Dib. He would avenge the traitorous plot against his noble family.

Frank Herbert’s Monumental Desert Masterpiece in a Hybrid Full-Cast Auditory Epic

Frank Herbert’s visionary 1965 space epic transports listeners across light-years to Arrakis, a harsh, unforgiving desert world that stands as the sole source of melange—the mystical spice that extends human life, unlocks superhuman precognitive foresight, and makes interstellar space travel possible across the Imperium. When young Paul Atreides moves with his noble house to assume governance of the perilous sand planet, they walk directly into a deadly web of imperial treachery, corporate exploitation, and ancient biological prophecy orchestrated by the monstrous Baron Vladimir Harkonnen. Herbert crafts a profound ecological and political tour de force, exploring environmental determinism, religious fanaticism, and the immense peril of charismatic messianic leaders who inadvertently unleash holy wars across galactic civilization.

Experiencing *Dune* through this ambitious audio production elevates the sweeping scale of Arrakis into an unforgettable auditory spectacle. The eerie whisper of shifting sand dunes, the ominous rhythmic thrumming of Fremen thumpers calling leviathan sandworms, and the layered vocal harmonies of the Bene Gesserit Voice echo with uncanny theatrical resonance. Narrator Soundcheck: Scott Brick, Orlagh Cassidy, Euan Morton, Simon Vance

This unique hybrid production alternates between Simon Vance’s rich, authoritative solo narration and full-cast dramatised scenes supported by subtle acoustic sound design. Scott Brick brings brooding vulnerability to Paul Atreides, while Orlagh Cassidy lends chilling maternal steel and mystical poise to Lady Jessica. Euan Morton delivers a menacingly theatrical turn that elevates secondary character drama. While the shifting balance between multi-cast dramatisation and solo narration is unconventional, the clean, dynamic audio mastering and atmospheric musical score sustain epic desert immersion across all twenty-one hours.
