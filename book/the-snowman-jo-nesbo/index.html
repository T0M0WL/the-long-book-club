The Snowman

Author: Jo Nesbø | Narrator: Sean Barrett

Runtime: 14h 36m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Detective, Crime, Thriller, Police Procedural

About this Audiobook

After Norway's first November snow triggers a chilling pattern of vanished married mothers and sinister snowmen built facing each victim's window, Harry Hole teams with sharp recruit Katrine Bratt to unmask a sadistic monster.

Over Fourteen Breathless Hours Facing Norway's Most Chilling Winter Calling Card

As the first November snowfall settles silently across Norway following The Redeemer, terrified young boy Jonas wakes to find his mother gone—and a grinning snowman built right in their garden wearing her pink scarf while staring straight back into their kitchen window. Paired with brilliant, fiercely guarded Bergen transfer Katrine Bratt, Harry Hole traces missing wives back decades, realizing Norway is dealing with its first true serial killer—and that someone close to Rakel and young Oleg may be targeted next.

*The Snowman* became an international phenomenon for defining pitch-black Nordic Noir Thriller pacing. Sharp Police Procedural red herrings collide with macabre Crime staging, driving Detective paranoia sky-high.

Spanning fourteen hours and thirty-six minutes for one credit, Book 7 breaks Harry wide open—sending him straight toward Hong Kong exile ahead of The Leopard.
