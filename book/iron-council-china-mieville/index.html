About this Audiobook

As New Crobuzon plunges into authoritarian war and worker rebellion, a legendary perpetual train laid across the wilderness by mutinous rail-workers and Remade revolutionaries threatens to return and ignite the city.

Twenty-One Hours of Revolutionary Steampunk Fantasy and Rogue Trains Across Wild Bas-Lag

A quarter-century after the events of Perdido Street Station, New Crobuzon is suffocating under martial law, crooked parliamentarians, and a brutal colonial war against the city-state of Tesh. Beneath the soot-stained bridges, underground radical cells and tortured Remade rebels cling to one desperate rumor: the Iron Council—a mythic perpetual train built by mutinous track-layers who murdered their corporate bosses, forged an egalitarian frontier town on rails, and vanished into the Cacotopic Stain—is finally chugging home to spark total revolution.

Listening to *Iron Council* in audio helps anchor China Miéville's boldest, most jagged structural experiment. Moving between urgent urban Political Intrigue and sweeping frontier flashbacks of railroad strikes and golem-craft, this final Bas-Lag chapter pushes Steampunk and Industrial Fantasy into ferocious anti-imperialist territory—echoing the gritty class struggle found across our Dark Fantasy and Fantasy shelves.

Clocking in at twenty-one hours and four minutes for one monthly credit, *Iron Council* completes the Bas-Lag triptych on an unforgettable, heartbreaking note of defiance and sacrifice.
