Spanning two interconnected eras and continents, Neal Stephenson\u2019s *Cryptonomicon* is a dazzling, encyclopedic techno-epic that weaves together secret histories, military espionage, and digital finance. In the 1940s, mathematical genius Lawrence Pritchard Waterhouse joins a top-secret Allied military intelligence unit alongside legendary codebreaker Alan Turing and morphine-addicted Marine raider Bobby Shaftoe, staging elaborate covert deceptions to prevent the Axis powers from discovering that their Enigma ciphers have been broken. Fifty years later, Lawrence\u2019s grandson Randy Waterhouse, a brilliant computer hacker and telecom entrepreneur in the 1990s dot-com boom, joins forces with cryptographic visionaries to construct an offshore encrypted data haven in Southeast Asia, inadvertently discovering a hidden cache of Yamashita\u2019s wartime gold.
