About this Audiobook

Bound by blood. Tempted by desire. Unleashed by destiny. Bryce Quinlan had the perfect life—working hard all day and partying all night—until a demon murdered her closest friends, leaving her bereft, wounded, and alone.

Sarah J. Maas's 28-Hour Crescent City Blockbuster of Demon Murders, Urban Magic, and Devastating Grief

The triumphant launch of Sarah J. Maas’s contemporary fantasy universe, *House of Earth and Blood* is a monumental, twenty-eight-hour urban fantasy epic that blends gritty noir mystery with high-stakes supernatural romance. Set in Lunathion—a neon-lit metropolis where shifters, angels, fae, and demons coexist under the oppressive thumb of the immortal Asteri—the story follows Bryce Quinlan, a half-human, half-fae party girl whose life is shattered when a demonic assassin brutally slaughters her best friends. Years later, when the killings resume, Bryce is paired with Hunt Athalar, an enslaved fallen angel assassin seeking to buy his freedom, embarking on a dangerous murder investigation that uncovers corruption reaching the highest seats of power, plunging them into a treacherous labyrinth of immortal politics, ancient artifacts, and deadly conspiracies.

In audio format, Maas’s sprawling world-building and complex urban geography unfold with the immersive momentum of a prestige television series. Rather than feeling overwhelmed by hundreds of political factions and arcane crime syndicates on the printed page, the listener is guided through Lunathion’s nightclubs, rooftop sniper perches, and ancient archives with crisp narrative authority. Featured prominently in our Epic Romantasy collection alongside Maas's sequels, this recording provides the temporal space required for Bryce and Hunt’s slow-burn romantic tension and shared trauma to forge an unbreakable, battle-tested bond born from shared loss, quiet understanding, and fierce mutual protection.

At twenty-eight hours of peerless narrative entertainment, spending a single credit on *House of Earth and Blood* yields an exceptional return on investment. Maas masterfully balances explosive urban action, laugh-out-loud humor, and a gut-wrenching emotional climax that will leave you sobbing in your car. It is a modern fantasy classic that will completely overtake your listening routine for weeks, proving why Sarah J. Maas’s contemporary fantasy universe has captured the hearts of millions of passionate listeners worldwide, making it an essential, credit-worthy addition to any serious fantasy lover’s audio library.

Narrator Soundcheck: Elizabeth Evans

Elizabeth Evans delivers a tour de force, Audie-caliber solo performance that stands as one of the finest single-narrator achievements in modern fantasy audio. Evans voices dozens of distinct characters with effortless consistency, from Bryce’s fierce, sarcastic bravado to Hunt Athalar’s brooding, gravelly lethality and Ruhn Danaan’s aristocratic charm. Her emotional range is breathtaking during the novel's devastating final chapters, sustaining explosive tension across twenty-eight hours with flawless studio sound engineering.
