About this Audiobook

In the midst of the Napoleonic Wars in 1806, most people believe magic to have long since disappeared from England - until the reclusive Mr Norrell reveals his powers.

Susanna Clarke's 32-Hour Regency Masterpiece of Academic Sorcery, Fairy Magic, and Forgotten Lore

Set against the misty, coal-smoke backdrop of Napoleonic England, Susanna Clarke’s *Jonathan Strange & Mr Norrell* is a dazzling, thirty-two-hour alternative history that imagines the revival of practical English magic. In 1806, the solitary, obsessive bibliophile Mr. Gilbert Norrell stuns London high society by demonstrating that real magic still exists, demanding that the art be treated with academic respectability and strict regulation. But when he takes on the brilliant, dashing young aristocrat Jonathan Strange as his pupil, ideological disputes and rivalries erupt over the lost, wild sorcery of the medieval Raven King, unleashing a terrifying faerie monarch known as the Gentleman with the Thistledown Hair into the drawing rooms of London, threatening to shatter English society and the very fabric of human sanity.

In audio format, Clarke’s magnificent pastiche of Jane Austen and Charles Dickens sparkles with dry wit, aristocratic irony, and enchanting acoustic atmosphere. The novel is legendary for its hundreds of detailed, pseudo-historical scholarly footnotes, which can break the momentum on a printed page; here, however, the spoken delivery incorporates these historical asides with the effortless comic flair and charm of a master storyteller. Featured prominently in our Dark Academia collection alongside foundational literary sagas like Middlemarch, this production turns Victorian sorcery into an addictive, laugh-out-loud auditory masterpiece, filled with subtle eccentricities and ancient English mysteries.

Spending a single credit on thirty-two and a half hours of Clarke’s peerless historical world-building is one of the most rewarding decisions any fantasy listener can make. Rather than a rushed magical adventure, it is a glorious, unhurried immersion into an entire alternate nineteenth century, filled with war, high-society gossip, and dark ancient legends. It is a triumphant, comforting listening marathon that will keep you captivated across a full month of daily listening, leaving you utterly enchanted by Clarke’s unmatched linguistic brilliance.

Narrator Soundcheck: Neil Gaiman, Richard Armitage, V.E. Schwab

This stellar ensemble production brings Clarke’s Regency world to vivid, theatrical life across thirty-two hours of immersive narration. Richard Armitage anchors the core narrative with aristocratic precision, dry humor, and rich character voicing, distinguishing Strange’s vibrant ambition from Norrell’s pedantic chill. Neil Gaiman steps in to voice Clarke’s famous scholarly footnotes, lending his signature wry, dark-fairytale timbre to the encyclopedic historical lore. The production maintains pristine, balanced acoustics throughout the expansive runtime.
