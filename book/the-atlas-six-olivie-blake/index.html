About this Audiobook

Six extraordinarily gifted magicians are invited to compete for initiation into the secretive Alexandrian Society, where knowledge is limitless and one candidate must be eliminated.

Six Cutthroat Magical Prodigies Compete in a Seventeen-Hour Secret Society Mind Game

Every decade, the clandestine Alexandrian Society selects the six most extraordinarily gifted young medicians in the world for a year of unlimited access to the lost knowledge of the ancient Library of Alexandria. Initiates Libby Rhodes, Nico de Varona, Reina Mori, Parisa Kamali, Callum Nova, and Tristan Caine possess powers spanning physical matter manipulation, telepathy, illusion, and biological control—yet there is a lethal catch. Only five will be chosen for full initiation, while the sixth will be eliminated, forcing six brilliant, deeply flawed narcissists to form fragile alliances inside a secluded London townhouse.

Experiencing *The Atlas Six* on audio transforms Olivie Blake's cerebral, character-driven chess match into a captivating ensemble drama. Because every protagonist harbors hidden motives and philosophical blind spots, hearing each perspective voiced by a dedicated actor turns dense magical theory and psychological sparring into an addictive theatrical duel.
