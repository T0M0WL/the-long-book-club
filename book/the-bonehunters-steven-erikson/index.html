About this Audiobook

Following the aftermath of the Whirlwind, Adjunct Tavore's Fourteenth Army must survive the apocalyptic siege-trap of Y'Ghatan—forging legendary Malazan sappers into the Bonehunters before facing imperial betrayal at home.

Forty-Two Hours of Burning Y'Ghatan Survival and Imperial Betrayal in Seven Cities

Hot on the heels of Leoman of the Flails after the end of the Whirlwind, Adjunct Tavore's Fourteenth Army besieges the fortified holy city of Y'Ghatan—only to walk straight into an infernal oil-firestorm designed to roast a legion alive. As survivors crawl blindly through suffocating subterranean honey-tunnels beneath burning ruins, the reborn Fourteenth Army earns a fierce new name—the Bonehunters—before sailing straight home toward Empress Laseen's treacherous ambush at Malaz City.

On audio, *The Bonehunters* anchors our Military Fantasy and Grimdark shelves with one of the most breathless, claustrophobic siege sequences ever written. Running forty-two monumental hours, Erikson begins weaving every continent, Warren, and soldier from Books 1–5 into a single unstoppable Epic Fantasy convergence.

Clocking in at forty-two hours even for a single credit, *The Bonehunters* offers colossal immersion, ferocious loyalty, and explosive street warfare at sea and land.
