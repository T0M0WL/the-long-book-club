About this Audiobook

Across the sun-scorched continent of Seven Cities, a bloody apocalyptic rebellion erupts under the Whirlwind—forcing Malazan fist Coltaine to lead tens of thousands of refugees across hostile deserts in the brutal Chain of Dogs.

Thirty-Four Hours of Relentless Desert March and the Heartbreak of Coltaine's Chain of Dogs

Leaving Genabackis behind after Gardens of the Moon, Steven Erikson plunges listeners onto the scorching continent of Seven Cities just as the prophetic Whirlwind rebellion ignites into empire-wide slaughter. As Bridgeburner assassin Kalam and rogue mage Quick Ben's surviving comrades scatter across ancient deserts, Malazan historian Duiker rides beside Coltaine—a fiercely taciturn Wickan commander tasked with escorting thirty thousand panicked civilians across eight hundred miles of relentless ambush in the legendary, agonizing Chain of Dogs.

In audio format, *Deadhouse Gates* cements why Malazan defines brutal Military Fantasy and shattering Grimdark. Listening to soldiers sacrifice every drop of water, horse, and bone step-by-step toward the gates of Aren elevates Erikson's anthropological Epic Fantasy into pure tragic myth alongside our Grimdark cornerstones.

Spanning thirty-four hours and four minutes for a single Audible credit, *Deadhouse Gates* breaks listeners' hearts and proves why no fantasy fan ever forgets Coltaine.
