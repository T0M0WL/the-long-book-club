About this Audiobook

With ancient prophecies hanging in the balance, Oren leads his monster coalition on an all-out military conquest to unite the continent against rogue digital overseers.

Shemer Kuznits's Epic Continental Campaign and Jeff Hays's Masterful Military Leadership Performance

In the penultimate chapter of the groundbreaking *Life Reset* saga, the local skirmishes of the past give way to total continental war. An ancient prophecy foretells the awakening of a catastrophic primal force capable of wiping out both monster clans and human survivors alike. To forge a defensive alliance strong enough to withstand the coming cataclysm, Oren can no longer remain content with defending his woodland borders. Marching his grand coalition of goblins, orcs, earth elementals, and allied human defectors across fortified mountain passes, he launches a calculated military conquest to subjugate hostile rival territories and unite the continent under a single unified banner.

Following the digital AI crisis of Life Reset: Human Resource, *Life Reset: Conquest* is a high-octane masterclass in pacing, strategic warfare, and charismatic leadership. Narrators Jeff Hays and Annie Ellicott return to deliver an exhilarating performance that makes every tactical siege and castle breach feel monumental. The novel excels in showcasing Oren’s growth from a bitter, vengeful outcast into a wise, visionary statesman who carries the hopes of an entire digital civilization upon his shoulders. Alongside legendary fantasy war epics like Promise of Blood and progression benchmarks like The Primal Hunter, this book delivers pure adrenaline for fans of our LitRPG & GameLit library.

Spanning thirteen hours and eighteen minutes of relentless momentum, *Conquest* provides exceptional listening value on a single Audible credit. Soundbooth Theater’s immaculate acoustic design brings the clash of enchanted shields, the roar of siege engines, and the crackle of high-tier biomancy spells into razor-sharp focus. Listeners looking for grand tactical maneuvers, thrilling battlefield heroics, and deep emotional payoffs will find this fifth installment an irresistible listen.
