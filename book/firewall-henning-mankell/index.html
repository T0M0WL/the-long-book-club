Firewall

Author: Henning Mankell | Narrator: Dick Hill

About this Audiobook

Linking the savagely beaten corpse of a late-night Ystad taxi driver, an ATM-booth murder, and a dead power-grid programmer in Angola, Wallander races an international cyber-conspiracy aimed straight at the world's banking system.

Over Sixteen Hours Pitting Analog Detective Intuition Against Cyberterrorism

Still carrying scars from One Step Behind, an old-fashioned inspector who barely trusts desktop computers is called to two baffling nights in Ystad: two teenagers confess coldly to slaughtering a taxi driver for spare change, while a brilliant systems programmer collapses outside an ATM holding forty thousand kronor and encrypted banking disks. As Sweden suffers mysterious blackouts and an accomplice dies in Angola, Wallander uncovers an audacious global plot to crash world finance.

Listening to *Firewall* offers a fascinating turn-of-the-millennium collision inside Nordic Noir. Seeing stubby handwritten notepads race nineteen-nineties hacker paranoia injects crackling modern stakes into classic Police Procedural patience, weaving bleak urban Crime and international Mystery cleanly around Detective doggedness.

Clocking sixteen hours and thirteen minutes for one credit, Book 8 sends Wallander into his final decade right before prequel anthology The Pyramid.
