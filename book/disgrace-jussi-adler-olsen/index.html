About this Audiobook

After an anonymous file lands in Department Q, Carl Mørck and Assad reopen the brutal twenty-year-old murder of two siblings—tracing the crime to a powerful clique of former boarding-school elites and a vengeful homeless survivor.

Over Fourteen Hours Targeting Denmark's Untouchable Boarding-School Elite

Following the harrowing subterranean drama of Mercy, grumpy Copenhagen cold-case chief Carl Mørck returns to his basement desk alongside indefatigable assistant Assad when an anonymous parcel revives a brutal twenty-year-old crime. Two teenage twins were bludgeoned to death at a quiet summer cottage in 1987; an unstable local outsider swiftly confessed and went to prison, yet the new dossier points straight toward a clique of ultra-privileged former boarding-school pupils who grew up into respected pillars of Danish finance, politics, and law—and a fierce homeless survivor named Nete hiding everything.

In audio format, *Disgrace* (published in North America as *The Absent One*) sharpens Jussi Adler-Olsen's signature blend of biting social satire and pitch-black Nordic Noir. Alternating seamlessly between Police Procedural excavation and cold-blooded Suspense, Carl and Assad peel back decadent institutional hypocrisy in a gripping Crime and Detective hunt layered with twisted Mystery cover-ups.

Clocking in at fourteen hours and twelve minutes for a single Audible credit, *Disgrace* widens Department Q's basement chemistry effortlessly—adding prickly new assistant Rose and building thrilling momentum straight into Redemption.

Narrator Soundcheck: Steven Pacey

Returning to Martin Aitken's sleek UK translation for over fourteen hours, Steven Pacey captures Carl Mørck's dry, coffee-fueled cynicism alongside Assad's deceptive gentleness and Rose's chaotic arrival in the basement. Pacey shifts smoothly into the chilling arrogance of privileged suspects and Nete's fierce survival grit with pinpoint clarity.
