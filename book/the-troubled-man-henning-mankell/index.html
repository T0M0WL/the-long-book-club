About this Audiobook

Approaching sixty and confronting quiet memory lapses while cherishing his newborn granddaughter, Kurt Wallander dives unofficially into Cold War submarine secrets when her other grandfather—a retired Swedish naval commander—vanishes.

Fifteen Unforgettable Hours Delivering Kurt Wallander's Heartbreaking Cold War Farewell

Closing the curtain nearly two decades after Faceless Killers, an older, increasingly forgetful Kurt Wallander cherishes precious afternoons with newborn granddaughter Klara—until her grandfather-in-law, retired Swedish naval commander Håkan von Enke, walks out into Stockholm forest fog and vanishes without trace. When von Enke's wife turns up murdered too, Kurt digs privately past diplomatic silence into shadowy 1980s Cold War submarine intrusions before time runs out on both the case and his own memory.

*The Troubled Man* stands among the finest final chapters in all of Nordic Noir. Tempering Police Procedural patience and wintry Cold War Mystery with devastating personal vulnerability transforms a gritty Crime and Detective hunt into an unforgettable elegy.

At fifteen hours and eight minutes for a single credit, *The Troubled Man* brings Kurt Wallander's extraordinary journey full circle with quiet grace and aching beauty.
