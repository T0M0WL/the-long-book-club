About this Audiobook

Mysteriously entered into the deadly Triwizard Tournament, fourteen-year-old Harry Potter must survive dragons, lethal underwater trials, and the dark resurrection of Lord Voldemort.

The Monumental Twenty-Hour All-Star Audio Blockbuster of the Triwizard Tournament

Expanding the scope of the wizarding world to a global scale, *Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire* is the colossal twenty-hour epic that irrevocably shatters the innocence of Hogwarts. When the legendary Triwizard Tournament returns after centuries of dormancy, uniting champions from the French academy of Beauxbatons and the rugged Scandinavian fortress of Durmstrang, an unseen saboteur forces fourteen-year-old Harry into the lethal competition. Navigating nesting dragons, treacherous deep-water merfolk colonies, and a shifting hedge-maze populated by lethal magical beasts, Harry must push his magical wits and physical endurance to their absolute limits, completely unaware that the entire tournament has been meticulously engineered to facilitate the return of Lord Voldemort.

On audio, this twenty-hour audio blockbuster is an absolute tour de force that pushes full-cast sound design to spectacular heights. The deafening concussive roar of the Hungarian Horntail, the swirling bagpipes and cheering crowds of the Quidditch World Cup, and the haunting, murky acoustics of the Black Lake erupt with three-dimensional grandeur. But it is the final act in the foggy graveyard of Little Hangleton where this production achieves legendary status: Matthew Macfadyen delivers a chilling, whispering, snake-like performance as the newly resurrected Dark Lord that will make your hair stand on end. Standing shoulder-to-shoulder with monolithic fantasy productions like The Lord of the Rings and A Game of Thrones, this production commands pride of place in our Cinematic Audiobooks library.

Clocking in at over twenty hours of relentless, unflagging momentum, *The Goblet of Fire* delivers staggering value for a single monthly credit. The immense cast of over one hundred voice actors brings foreign delegations, eccentric professors, and terrified students to life with pristine acoustic clarity and cinematic authority, leading directly into the political warfare of Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix. Investing a credit in this epic fourth chapter provides an unforgettable listening odyssey that bridges the innocence of youth with the harrowing realities of war.
