About this Audiobook

Born of Spartan blood and wed to the ruthless Agamemnon, Queen Clytemnestra rules Mycenae with an iron will, orchestrating bloody retribution for her sacrificed daughter.

Reclaiming the Legendary Spartan Queen from the Slanders of Patriarchal Myth

For nearly three millennia of Western literature, Clytemnestra has been branded as antiquity’s ultimate treacherous villainess—the monstrous, adulterous queen who slaughtered King Agamemnon in his bath upon his victorious return from Troy. In *Clytemnestra*, author Costanza Casati delivers a magnificent, visceral reclamation that restores the Spartan princess to her rightful stature as one of the ancient world’s most formidable, tactical, and morally justified avengers. Raised in the brutal, egalitarian courts of Sparta where royal women wrestled, hunted with hounds, and commanded armies, Clytemnestra is bartered away as a political prize into the corrupt, tyrannical slaughterhouse of Mycenae, where women are expected to remain silent decorative pawns.

Casati’s narrative unspools with breathless, cinematic ferocity that grips the listener from its opening chapters. We witness the agonizing trauma of a young mother whose first beloved husband and infant son are butchered before her eyes by Agamemnon, and whose cherished daughter Iphigenia is later deceived and slaughtered upon an altar simply to purchase favorable sailing winds for the Trojan campaign. Rather than collapsing under the weight of grief, Clytemnestra plays the long, deadly game. Over the ten grueling years the Greek kings spend burning Troy, she governs Mycenae with razor-sharp brilliance, forging alliances, securing grain supplies, eliminating political rivals, and sharpening the ceremonial double-bladed axe for her husband’s inevitable homecoming, refusing to be crushed by the men who took everything from her.

Running over thirteen immersive hours, *Clytemnestra* is an essential credit purchase for enthusiasts of dark Historical Fiction and uncompromising Mythology. Karissa Vacker’s resolute, regal narration brings Bronze Age Greece alive with the scent of sea salt, polished bronze armor, and impending retribution. It stands proudly alongside Circe while carving out a darker, more unapologetically defiant space in the pantheon of ancient epics. For any listener searching for a gripping tale of justice, female sovereignty, and ancient vengeance, this audiobook delivers immense emotional satisfaction and lasting credit value.

Narrator Soundcheck: Karissa Vacker

Karissa Vacker delivers a commanding, steely vocal performance that captures Clytemnestra’s ferocious intelligence and suppressed grief. Vacker avoids the trap of melodrama, grounding the Spartan queen in cold, deliberate resolve and simmering fury. Her vocal modulation during scenes of political intrigue in the Mycenaean council is authoritative and sharp, contrasting beautifully with her tender, protective moments with her doomed children. Produced with pristine studio clarity, balanced dynamic range, and rich acoustic warmth, Vacker’s narration elevates this ancient epic into an essential auditory masterpiece.
