About this Audiobook

Set in the universe of the Three-Body Problem, this authorized fan-sequel (paraquel) follows Yun Tianming's journey after the end of the original trilogy. It explores the mysteries of the Entities and the ultimate nature of the universe.

Baoshu’s Authorized Interstellar Paraquel Exploring Yun Tianming and the Cosmic Singer in Audio

Authorized and praised by Cixin Liu himself, *The Redemption of Time* expands the legendary universe of the Remembrance of Earth's Past saga, answering the tantalizing mysteries left behind in the wake of *Death's End*. Centering on Yun Tianming—the cancer-stricken human whose disembodied brain was launched into deep space as an ambassador and captive of the Trisolaran fleet—the novel chronicles his millennia of survival, his subtle fairy-tale warnings to humanity, and his eventual entanglement in an ancient cosmic war. Navigating deep cosmic hierarchies and the tragic backstory of the Trisolarans, Yun discovers the terrifying truth behind the decay of the universe from ten spatial dimensions down to three, embarking on a metaphysical quest alongside enigmatic cosmic beings to restore the primordial universe.

On audio, Baoshu's daring expansion takes on an exhilarating, transportive quality that will delight fans craving closure after the main trilogy. Hearing Yun Tianming's untold struggle inside the alien biosphere and exploring the origins of dimensional warfare brings fresh emotional depth to familiar narrative beats. Listeners who were captivated by the grand philosophical scale of the original trilogy will find immense joy in the detailed exploration of how the Singer cleansed systems and how Yun survived centuries of alien interrogation. Featured as the thrilling epilogue alongside The Three-Body Problem and Death's End in our Science Fiction Audiobooks collection, this twelve-hour performance provides a deeply satisfying cosmic coda that answers long-debated fan questions.

Clocking in at over twelve hours of high-concept space opera for a single credit, this unabridged audio release offers wonderful entertainment value for trilogy completionists. Baoshu honors Liu’s rigorous scientific logic while introducing bold mythological and philosophical concepts that tie the entire timeline together. Spending a monthly token on this fascinating companion novel guarantees an engrossing listening journey that brings one of science fiction’s greatest sagas to a complete, harmonious close.
