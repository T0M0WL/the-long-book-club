Red Rising

Author: Pierce Brown | Narrator: Tim Gerard Reynolds

Runtime: 16h 12m | Genre: Space Opera, Science Fiction, Dystopian, Action, Military Sci-Fi

About this Audiobook

A low-born helium miner on Mars suffers an unspeakable tragedy and undergoes a brutal biological transformation to infiltrate the genetically enhanced ruling class and destroy the Society from within.

The Ferocious Blood-Soaked Epic That Revolutionized Galactic Rebellion

Clocking in at sixteen breathless hours, *Red Rising* detonates the space opera genre with raw kinetic fury, visceral heart, and unyielding mythological swagger. Pierce Brown constructs a stratified interplanetary empire called the Society, where humanity has been genetically engineered into rigid, color-coded castes ranging from the enslaved subterranean Reds to the towering, godlike Golds who rule the solar system with despotic arrogance. Protagonist Darrow begins as a sixteen-year-old Helldiver mining the deep crust of Mars, enduring lethal radiation and soul-crushing labor under the propaganda that his sweat is preparing the surface for future generations. When imperial injustice robs him of his beloved wife Eo, Darrow uncovers the horrifying truth that Mars has been terraformed for centuries, sparking a vengeful revolution.

Recruited by the underground Sons of Ares, Darrow undergoes a brutal, agony-drenched physical metamorphosis known as the Carving. Grafted with denser bone, gilded eyes, and razor-sharp intellect, he is thrust undercover into the Institute—a merciless, Hunger-Games-style wargame where the sons and daughters of the high Gold houses wage tribal warfare across the valleys of Mars to claim command of interplanetary starfleets. Brown’s genius lies in marrying classical Roman iconography and Homeric martial code with relentless futuristic action. In audio, Darrow’s interior battle between savage vengeance and human empathy resonates with operatic intensity, drawing you deep into high-stakes battlefield politics, shifting alliances, and shocking betrayals that keep your heart pounding.

For audiobook listeners looking to maximize a single monthly credit, *Red Rising* is an electrifying adrenaline shot that kicks off a sprawling multi-book space saga. Tim Gerard Reynolds delivers what is widely regarded as one of the single greatest voice performances in modern fantasy and Science Fiction, imbuing Darrow with fierce Irish cadence and incandescent poetic passion. Beside kindred grand-scale rebellions like Empire of Silence and Leviathan Wakes, this sixteen-hour tour de force is an essential, high-calorie addition to any long-listen library, leaving you completely breathless.

Narrator Soundcheck: Tim Gerard Reynolds

Tim Gerard Reynolds delivers an iconic, career-defining performance that turns Brown’s prose into pure auditory electricity. His lyrical Irish brogue gives Darrow’s early Helldiver monologues a raw, melancholic sorrow that grips your chest, before shifting with icy aristocratic poise once Darrow assumes his Gold disguise. Reynolds handles battle sequences with breath-snatching intensity, bellowing war cries with genuine ferocity while preserving delicate emotional vulnerability during intimate laments. The production quality is razor-sharp, making sixteen hours pass with the speed and force of a falling meteor.
