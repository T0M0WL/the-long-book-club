About this Audiobook

Back on war-torn Genabackis, outlawed Bridgeburners join an uneasy alliance with former enemies Caladan Brood and Anomander Rake to stop the cannibalistic empire of the Pannion Domin before it consumes the continent.

Forty-Four Hours of Apocalyptic Siege Warfare and the Bridgeburners' Finer Hour

Returning concurrently to war-ravaged Genabackis right after Gardens of the Moon, outlaw Malazan veterans Whiskeyjack, Quick Ben, and their scarred Bridgeburners forge an unthinkable coalition with old foes Caladan Brood and Anomander Rake. Together they must march against the monstrous Pannion Domin—a fanatical cannibalistic empire advancing behind undead K'Chain Che'Malle horrors and rotting sorcery—while ancient T'lan Imass gather from the dawn of time for one final reckoning.

Listening to *Memories of Ice* delivers the ultimate confluence of High Fantasy, complex Magic Systems, and devastating Military Fantasy. From Lady Envy's wickedly dry banter and Itkovian's staggering burden of compassion to the bloody, unforgettable sieges of Capustan and Coral, Erikson turns forty-four hours into modern Epic Fantasy canon.

At forty-three hours and fifty-four minutes for a single Audible credit, *Memories of Ice* is widely hailed as Malazan's emotional pinnacle and an unmatched marathon investment.

Narrator Soundcheck: Ralph Lister

In his final Malazan tour de force, Ralph Lister powers through nearly forty-four hours with soaring dramatic stamina. From Kruppe's swirling verbose monologues and Tool's ancient stone cadence to the heartbreaking loyalty of the Bridgeburners at Coral, Lister leaves the series on an absolute high.
