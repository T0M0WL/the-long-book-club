About this Audiobook

Sir Una Everlasting died a hero for Dominion, but centuries later, a scholar obsessed with her legend falls through time, tangling them in a recursive historical loop they must break.

Alix E. Harrow's 13-Hour Romantic Speculative Odyssey of Myth, Destiny, and Rewritten Timelines

Spanning nearly fourteen deeply atmospheric hours, *The Everlasting* by Alix E. Harrow is a genre-defying, emotionally devastating exploration of historical myth-making and recursive time. Centered on the nation of Dominion, the story resurrects the legendary Sir Una Everlasting—an orphaned girl turned lady-knight who famously gave her life for queen and country. Centuries later, Owen Mallory, a shell-shocked scholar and failed soldier who has fallen hopelessly in love with her myth, uncovers an ancient grimoire tied to her heraldry. When Owen is violently pulled backward through centuries into the muddy, blood-drenched reality of Una's war, he discovers that the triumphant historical national narrative was a carefully constructed lie—and that he and Una are trapped in a repeating timeline destined to end in catastrophic heartbreak.

In audio format, the emotional intimacy and poetic lyricism of Harrow's prose take flight through alternating dual viewpoints. The spoken medium brilliantly contrasts the harsh, armored brutality of Una's medieval battlefield with Owen's scholarly, world-weary longing, turning every narrow escape and recursive tragedy into an urgent, pulse-pounding drama. Listening to the lovers slowly recognize the inescapable narrative machinery governing their fates carries immense emotional weight. Standing alongside time-loop speculative fiction in our library like The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August and the sweeping historical romance of Outlander, this production transforms a classic time-slip premise into an unforgettable meditation on who gets to author truth.

Harrow completely upends predictable chosen-one and time-travel tropes, grounding the fantastical stakes in the quiet, agonizing sacrifices required to defy destiny. Propelled by world-class dual narration, this rich standalone epic provides an addictive, credit-worthy listening journey that echoes in your thoughts long after the final loop resolves.

Narrator Soundcheck: Moira Quirk, Sid Sagar

Moira Quirk and Sid Sagar deliver a magnificent, perfectly harmonized dual performance that elevates Harrow's poetic speculative prose. Moira Quirk captures Sir Una’s ferocious battlefield grit, physical exhaustion, and buried vulnerability with flinty Scottish determination, refusing to soften the edges of a battle-scarred warrior. Sid Sagar counters with a warm, contemplative, and increasingly desperate delivery as scholar Owen Mallory, making his emotional obsession feel deeply human rather than academic. Recorded with pristine acoustic balance and seamless perspective transitions, this production is an absolute triumph of character-driven fantasy narration.
