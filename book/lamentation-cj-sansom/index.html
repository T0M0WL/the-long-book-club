About this Audiobook

As Henry VIII lies dying in 1546 and court factions turn murderous, Queen Catherine Parr hires Shardlake to recover a stolen, potentially fatal manuscript of religious confessions before Bishop Gardiner strikes.

Twenty-Five Hours of Royal Espionage as Dying Henry VIII's Court Tears Itself Apart

London, 1546: King Henry VIII is dying in agony, swollen and unpredictable at Whitehall Palace, while Catholic conservatives led by Bishop Stephen Gardiner prepare a ruthless purge of Protestant reformers around Queen Catherine Parr. When a radical printer is burned at Smithfield and a private, potentially treasonous manuscript penned by Queen Catherine herself vanishes before publication, Matthew Shardlake is secretly summoned by the Queen—pulled into a deadly race through Fleet Street print shops and court factions alongside Jack Barak and young Nicholas Overton.

Listening to *Lamentation* on audio turns religious brinkmanship into white-knuckle Political Intrigue and Historical Mystery. Bridging the intimate detective tension of Mystery with the vast, tragic sweep of Historical Fiction, this sixth installment sets the stage directly for our monumental finale Tombland.

Spanning twenty-five hours and twenty-one minutes for one Audible credit, *Lamentation* delivers breathless court survival, vivid Tudor street life, and unforgettable stakes right to the final chapter.
