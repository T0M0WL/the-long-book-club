Restless

Author: William Boyd | Narrator: Rosamund Pike

Runtime: 10h 11m | Genre: British Espionage, World War II, Historical Fiction, Mystery, Espionage

About this Audiobook

A young Oxford woman discovers her reclusive mother was a Russian-born British secret agent in WWII, recruited to hunt down a traitor thirty years later.

The Costa Award-Winning Tour de Force of Dual-Identity Wartime Espionage

Spanning nearly eleven hours of rich, evocative suspense, *Restless* is William Boyd’s Costa Novel Award-winning masterpiece, delivering an exquisitely crafted dual-timeline thriller that explores the psychological cost of living a life constructed entirely of lies. The narrative opens during the sweltering British heatwave of 1976, where single mother and Oxford graduate student Ruth Gilmartin visits her aging, paranoid mother Sally in the Cotswolds. Without warning, Sally reveals that her entire identity is a fabrication: she was born Eva Delectorskaya, a young Russian émigré recruited in Paris on the eve of the Second World War by a charismatic British spymaster named Lucas Romer, entering a world of lethal disinformation from which she never truly escaped.

Boyd alternates between Ruth’s bewildered present-day perspective and Eva’s gripping, first-person memoir of wartime tradecraft. Recruited into the shadowy British Security Coordination in New York and Maryland, Eva is trained in cyphers, microdots, surveillance, and seduction, tasked with the perilous assignment of planting planted news stories and falsified maps to draw an isolationist United States into the war against Nazi Germany. When a catastrophic betrayal during an undercover mission in the Netherlands exposes her network, Eva fakes her death, waiting thirty years in quiet rural seclusion until she can recruit her own daughter to hunt down the traitor who destroyed her life. In audio, the psychological intimacy of Eva's confession resonates with hypnotic power.

For discerning listeners seeking an elegant, deeply moving British Espionage novel that combines historical depth with white-knuckle intrigue, *Restless* offers phenomenal credit value. Academy Award nominee Rosamund Pike delivers a spellbinding performance, effortlessly shifting between youthful Russian vulnerability and the icy, calculating survival instincts of an aging spymaster. Alongside kindred character-driven classics like The Human Factor and A Perfect Spy, this eleven-hour gem is an unforgettable exploration of trust, identity, and the lingering scars of covert warfare that demands full attention.

Narrator Soundcheck: Rosamund Pike

Rosamund Pike gives an astonishing, multi-layered performance that captures the complex emotional interiority of Boyd’s heroines. Pike navigates Eva’s Russian background with subtle, aristocratic inflections, conveying both the thrill of youthful tradecraft and the suffocating terror of discovery. Her English cadence for Ruth is grounded, natural, and sharp, while her aristocratic voicing of spymaster Lucas Romer exudes effortless, dangerous charm. Pike’s timing during the novel’s tense espionage operations in Maryland and New York is pitch-perfect, making nearly eleven hours of storytelling feel completely seamless.
