About this Audiobook

The definitive biography of Ulysses S. Grant, confirming his stature as one of history's greatest generals and presidents.

Ron Chernow's 48-Hour Monumental Redemption Saga of the General Who Saved the Union

In this definitive, forty-eight-hour biography, Ron Chernow rescues Ulysses S. Grant from decades of historical distortion, offering a sweeping, deeply humane portrait of one of America's greatest military geniuses and most misunderstood presidents. Beginning with Grant's agonizing early struggles with poverty, business failure, and alcoholism, the narrative follows his sudden, meteoric rise during the American Civil War. Through sheer operational brilliance, relentless tenacity, and unflinching moral clarity, Grant engineered victories at Vicksburg and Appomattox, crushing the Confederacy and saving the Union. His subsequent two-term presidency, long maligned for cabinet corruption, is vindicated as a heroic, embattled crusade to defend Reconstruction and protect freed slaves from Ku Klux Klan terror, championing civil rights with an uncompromising bravery that modern historians now celebrate with profound respect.

In audio format, Chernow’s forty-eight-hour epic becomes an unputdownable drama of resilience, quiet stoicism, and battlefield strategy. Listening by ear captures the visceral shock of Shiloh, the grueling tactical siege of Petersburg, and the heartbreaking final months of Grant's life as he raced against terminal throat cancer to complete his memoirs. Positioned proudly in our Long Life Stories collection alongside Chernow's celebrated portraits of Hamilton and Titan, this unabridged audio recording provides the deep emotional space needed to experience Grant’s humble greatness and quiet personal courage, illuminating the tender family man and steadfast patriot beneath the battle-hardened military commander.

Exchanging a single credit or token for forty-eight hours of Chernow’s masterful historical storytelling yields unbeatable value for biography lovers. You receive a comprehensive masterclass in military leadership, racial justice during Reconstruction, and personal triumph over adversity. It is a powerful, inspiring listening journey that will redefine your view of American history and keep you thoroughly enthralled across several weeks of daily listening, leaving you in awe of a man who saved his nation through humble duty and unbreakable will.

Narrator Soundcheck: Mark Deakins

Mark Deakins delivers a masterful, authoritative narration that anchors Chernow’s expansive forty-eight-hour text with quiet strength and emotional clarity. Deakins captures Grant’s unassuming, plainspoken Midwestern modesty without ever losing the underlying iron resolve that defined his military genius. His pacing across sprawling battlefield campaigns and complex Reconstruction political infighting remains rhythmic, disciplined, and crisp, avoiding vocal fatigue while maintaining pristine audio fidelity and crystal-clear acoustic presence throughout the entire colossal forty-eight-hour runtime.
