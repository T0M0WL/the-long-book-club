About this Audiobook

During India's 1975 State of Emergency, four unlikely strangers—a fierce widow, two impoverished tailors, and a student—share a cramped Bombay flat, forging an unbreakable bond against political upheaval and crushing social hierarchies.

Twenty-Six Unforgettable Hours of Resilience, Friendship, and Heartbreak in 1970s Bombay

Few novels in modern Literary Fiction earn the word 'masterpiece' as fiercely as Rohinton Mistry's *A Fine Balance*. Set in an unnamed coastal city unmistakably modeled on Bombay during the 1975 State of Emergency, the story throws four desperate strangers together inside a cramped apartment: Dina Dalal, a fiercely independent Parsi widow fighting to keep her flat; Maneck Kohlah, a homesick student from the northern mountains; and Ishvar and Omprakash, two uncle-and-nephew tailors fleeing brutal caste violence in their village.

On audio, the slow, tender alchemy that turns four guarded strangers into a makeshift family hits with extraordinary intimacy. Mistry never shrinks from the cruelty of authoritarian politics or poverty, yet he balances every blow with gallows humor, stitching-room camaraderie, and fierce human dignity. Pairing naturally with The Covenant of Water and Shantaram in our India hub, it is a novel that permanently widens your empathy.

At twenty-five hours and forty-nine minutes for a single Audible credit, *A Fine Balance* is emotionally shattering, deeply humane, and impossible to forget.

Narrator Soundcheck: Vikas Adam

Vikas Adam delivers a tour-de-force twenty-six-hour performance that captures both the bustling street-level humor and the quiet devastation of Mistry's prose. His transitions between Dina's brisk pride, Om's youthful impatience, and Ishvar's gentle resilience feel deeply lived-in, keeping listeners completely anchored through every soaring triumph and heartbreaking turn.
