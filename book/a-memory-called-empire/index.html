A Memory Called Empire

Author: Arkady Martine | Narrator: Amy Landon

Runtime: 15h 37m | Genre: Space Opera, Science Fiction, Political, Mystery, Galactic Empire, Literary Sci-Fi

About this Audiobook

A young ambassador from an independent mining station arrives at the dazzling heart of a predatory interstellar empire to investigate her predecessor's mysterious assassination.

The Hugo-Winning Masterpiece of Imperial Seduction and Cultural Conquest

Winner of the Hugo Award for Best Novel, *A Memory Called Empire* offers fifteen hours of spellbinding political intrigue, psychological suspense, and poetic worldbuilding. Arkady Martine, a Byzantine historian and urban planner, crafts the Teixcalaanli Empire—a hyper-civilized interstellar colossus whose weapon of conquest is not merely kinetic armada strikes, but the irresistible glamour of its language, literature, and imperial aesthetic. When Mahit Dzmare, a brilliant young diplomat from the isolated mining habitat of Lsel Station, is dispatched to the imperial capital, she finds herself hopelessly enchanted by the very culture that threatens to swallow her independent home whole, setting up an exquisite psychological crisis of identity and imperial seduction.

Mahit’s diplomatic mission is immediately imperiled by murder, espionage, and neurological sabotage. To preserve institutional knowledge across generations, Lsel ambassadors utilize imago machines—neural implants containing the living memories and personalities of former officials. However, Mahit’s imago chip carries a corrupted, fifteen-year-old snapshot of her assassinated predecessor, leaving her blind to recent court conspiracies and personal betrayals. Listening in audio brings Martine’s linguistic richness alive as Mahit navigates imperial poetry duels, aristocratic salons, and militarist coups alongside imperial cultural liaison Three Seagrass and councillor Nine Hibiscus, realizing that surviving the heart of the empire requires becoming culturally complicit in its schemes.

For listeners seeking intellectually sophisticated Space Opera that trades loud laser skirmishes for razor-sharp bureaucratic maneuvering, diplomatic subterfuge, and linguistic games, this fifteen-hour gem delivers exceptional credit value. Amy Landon’s cool, composed narration captures the dizzying glamour and latent menace of Teixcalaan with astonishing finesse. Complemented by thematic companions like Ancillary Justice and The Player of Games, it stands as one of the most original, beautifully written, and deeply satisfying political science fiction audiobooks of the modern era. It rewards attentive listeners with layers of cultural insight, emotional nuance, and imperial grandeur that linger in the imagination for weeks.

Narrator Soundcheck: Amy Landon

Amy Landon delivers an incisive, mesmerizing performance that brings the poetic and bureaucratic nuances of Teixcalaanli society into sharp focus. Landon navigates Martine’s inventive naming conventions and intricate prose with effortless cadence, granting imperial liaison Three Seagrass a warm, playful intelligence while keeping Mahit Dzmare grounded in sharp, nervous resourcefulness. Her deliberate pacing during tense imperial audiences and whispered palace conspiracies heightens the claustrophobic dread, delivering a polished, emotionally resonant fifteen-hour listening experience of the highest order.
