About this Audiobook

As religious war ravages the Seven Satrapies, Gavin Guile discovers his miraculous prismatic powers are failing him, while an ancient dagger threatens to strip drafting from the world.

Brent Weeks’s David Gemmell Legend Award-Winning Sequel of Shifting Allegiances and Fading Light

Winner of the prestigious David Gemmell Legend Award for Best Fantasy Novel, *The Blinding Knife* accelerates the *Lightbringer* saga into an electrifying war for survival. Gavin Guile's worst nightmare has begun to materialize: his prismatic sight is deteriorating, and with every color he loses, his miraculous authority as the Prism slips through his fingers. Meanwhile, the ruthless Color Prince advances across the satrapies, promising liberation to broken drafters who refuse to submit to the Chromeria’s ritual executions. At the center of the growing storm lies an ancient, mythical dagger with the power to strip drafting from a soul—or bestow it.

In audio, Simon Vance elevates Weeks’s escalating political and martial stakes into a masterclass in vocal pacing across twenty-four hours. Vance captures Kip’s rapid maturation from an insecure, bullied outcast into a disciplined member of the elite Blackguard, while imbuing Gavin’s desperate attempts to conceal his waning powers with heartbreaking vulnerability. Alongside celebrated hard magic epics like Words of Radiance and Promise of Blood, this second volume strips away plot armor to deliver genuine, pulse-pounding consequence.

Offering more than twenty-four hours of high-caliber fantasy storytelling for a single credit, *The Blinding Knife* avoids every sophomore slump trap. Weeks deepens the philosophical costs of drafting while delivering naval skirmishes, cannonades, and political duels that make the hours vanish. Exchanging a single monthly token for this award-winning production rewards listeners with one of contemporary fantasy’s finest, most unpredictable installments.

Narrator Soundcheck: Simon Vance

Simon Vance turns in an extraordinary performance across twenty-four hours, navigating complex multi-POV transitions with effortless grace. Vance is particularly brilliant during Kip’s brutal Blackguard induction trials, bringing gravelly grit to Ironfist and steely poise to Karris. His vocal modulation during Gavin’s moments of panic as his chromatic spectrum fails him is profoundly affecting. Flawless production values ensure that every whisper and cannon burst lands with cinematic precision.
