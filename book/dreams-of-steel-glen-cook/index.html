About this Audiobook

With Croaker left for dead and the Company shattered across Dejagore and Taglios after Soulcatcher's brutal offensive, the Lady seizes command of the annals herself—rebuilding a deadly resistance from betrayal and mud.

Nearly Ten Hours Handing the Company's Annals Directly to the Lady Herself

Shattered after catastrophic ambushes left Croaker presumed dead at the close of Shadow Games, the broken remnants of the Black Company cling to survival inside besieged Dejagore while Soulcatcher orchestrates ruthless conquest across the South. Stepping out from behind Croaker's journal, the Lady takes up the pen as annalist herself—rebuilding scarred veterans alongside fierce newcomer Willow Swan while plotting cold, calculated resistance against her own psychotic sister.

Experiencing *Dreams of Steel* on audio flips the series' Grimdark and Military Fantasy lens thrillingly upside down. Watching a former immortal tyrant lead scrappy Low Fantasy insurgent campaigns amid weird Dark Fantasy assassinations and jagged Sword and Sorcery espionage adds remarkable psychological depth.

Included under our series exception at nine hours and thirty-seven minutes, *Dreams of Steel* packs razor-lean momentum across every battlefield setback before handing the chronicle onward toward She Is the Darkness. (Note: *Bleak Seasons*, Book 6, has no UK audiobook edition.)

Narrator Soundcheck: Rachel Butera

Stepping seamlessly into the Lady's steel-lined perspective for nearly ten focused hours, Rachel Butera projects icy imperial poise tinged with fierce tactical desperation. Switching from Croaker's male camp cadence to Butera's razor-sharp clarity highlights how dramatically the Company's internal power dynamic shifts.
