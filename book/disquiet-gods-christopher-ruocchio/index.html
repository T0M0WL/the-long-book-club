About this Audiobook

The Metaphysical Summit: Samuel Roukin Elevates Ruocchio's Penultimate Masterpiece

In *Disquiet Gods*, Christopher Ruocchio elevates the Sun Eater saga into the sublime heights of cosmic mythology. Nearly two centuries after the cataclysms of *Ashes of Man*, an aged, reclusive Hadrian Marlowe lives in quiet exile on a tranquil frontier world, having laid down his sword. But the universe refuses to let the Halfmortal rest: summoned by the new Emperor, Hadrian must embark on a final, perilous pilgrimage into the galactic core to confront the Watchers and the dark gods of the deep abyss before humanity's suns are snuffed out forever.

Samuel Roukin’s narration across these thirty-two hours is an absolute revelation. He captures the immense weight of centuries that Hadrian carries—the weariness of an old soldier who has outlived generations of friends, lovers, and empires—while imbuing his search for metaphysical truth with profound spiritual beauty. The planetary explorations of cyclopean alien ruins evoke the cosmic wonder of classic sci-fi landmarks like Children of Time and Dune.

At nearly thirty-two hours for a single Audible credit, *Disquiet Gods* is an awe-inspiring return on investment. It provides breathtaking revelations regarding the nature of the universe, preparing listeners for the grand apocalyptic finale with unmatched literary grace.
