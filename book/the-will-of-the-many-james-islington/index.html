About this Audiobook

In the ruthless Catenan Republic, a fugitive prince infiltrates an elite academy where citizens cede their personal Will up a pyramidal hierarchy of devastating power.

James Islington's Roman-Inspired Masterpiece of Pyramidal Magic and Academy Intrigue in Audio

In the ruthless Catenan Republic, power is not merely metaphorical—it is physical, quantifiable, and ruthlessly extracted. Millions of lower-caste citizens cede their strength, health, and mental vitality upward into an intricate, pyramidal social hierarchy, granting the aristocratic elite superhuman speed, strength, and arcane mastery. Into this vicious meat-grinder steps Vis Telimus, an orphaned royal fugitive from a slaughtered kingdom who has survived in the fighting pits by hiding his true bloodline. Coerced into an espionage mission by an influential senator, Vis infiltrates the prestigious Catenan Academy, competing against ruthless patrician scions in gladiatorial combat and academic gauntlets to uncover the lethal mystery behind a fallen civilization.

In audio format, Islington’s twenty-eight-hour epic achieves a breakneck velocity that makes classic print doorstoppers feel pedestrian. Narrator Euan Morton—renowned for his nuanced British cadence and aristocratic poise—turns Vis's high-wire undercover existence into a masterclass in spoken suspense. Morton captures the icy calculation of Roman-inspired political machinations, the thunderous adrenaline of aquatic labyrinth trials, and the quiet heartbreak of a young man fighting to preserve his soul under an authoritarian regime. Highlighted as the crowning centerpiece of our Hard Magic Systems collection alongside The Way of Kings and Babel, this performance transforms an intricate magic system into pulse-pounding acoustic drama.

Clocking in at over twenty-eight hours of sublime storytelling for a single credit, *The Will of the Many* offers an unbeatable return on investment for lovers of deep, intellectual fantasy. Islington’s magic system operates with the ruthless precision of clockwork, rewarding attentive listeners who enjoy piecing together ancient historical conspiracies and tactical combat puzzles. Exchanging a monthly credit for this modern masterpiece unlocks one of the most addicting, intellectually stimulating marathon audio experiences of the decade, cementing Islington as one of epic fantasy’s most formidable new voices.

Narrator Soundcheck: Euan Morton

Euan Morton delivers a stunning, tour-de-force performance across twenty-eight hours, balancing Vis's cynical internal monologue with razor-sharp public deference. Morton gives every academy rival, Catenan magistrate, and rebellious ally a distinct vocal identity, deftly distinguishing between patrician arrogance and gutter desperation. His pacing during the labyrinthine trial sequences is relentless, yet he allows quiet moments of grief to linger with profound emotional resonance. Mastered with crystal clarity and pristine dynamic range, this is modern audio engineering at its finest.
