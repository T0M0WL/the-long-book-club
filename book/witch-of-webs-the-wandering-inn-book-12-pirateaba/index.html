About this Audiobook

The sinister Stitch Witch Belavierr spins ancient curses across Riverfarm, forcing Erin to embrace strange witchcraft and string magic to defend her realm.

A Chilling Forty-Six-Hour Dark Fantasy Epic of Ancient Witchcraft, Thread Magic, and Terrifying Immortals

In *Witch of Webs*, pirateaba weaves a spellbinding, spine-chilling tale that introduces one of the most terrifying antagonists in contemporary fantasy: Belavierr, the immortal Stitch Witch of String. As Belavierr glides through the river settlements of Izril, sewing flesh, emotions, and souls into grotesque puppet creations, the blind Emperor Laken Godart and Erin Solstice must confront the dark realities of ancient folklore. To resist the spider-like machinations of the coven, Erin begins studying the esoteric craft of witchcraft, discovering that emotion, sorrow, and memory are the most potent magical ingredients in existence.

On audio, *Witch of Webs* is an atmospheric and haunting triumph that showcases Andrea Parsneau’s versatility in dark fantasy. Parsneau’s depiction of Belavierr—soft-spoken, polite, utterly devoid of empathy, yet chillingly intimate—will send shivers down your spine every time she appears. Yet true to the series’ charm, this eerie horror is counterbalanced by heartwarming scenes of riverboat odysseys, goblin friendships, and cozy culinary innovations. Curated prominently in our Fantasy catalog, this production is an unforgettable listening experience.

Clocking in at an immense forty-five hours and fifty-three minutes, this release provides magnificent credit value. pirateaba masterfully crafts a deep philosophical exploration of morality, immortality, and the price of convenience, proving why *The Wandering Inn* remains endlessly inventive. Spending a monthly token on this forty-six-hour tour de force awards you with some of the most chilling and beautiful audiobook narration ever produced.
