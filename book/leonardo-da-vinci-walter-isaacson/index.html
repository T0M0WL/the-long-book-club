Based on thousands of pages from Leonardo da Vinci\u2019s astonishing personal notebooks and recent art historical discoveries, master biographer Walter Isaacson weaves an intimate, luminous narrative of history\u2019s most creatively versatile mind. Isaacson explores how Leonardo\u2019s boundless, insatiable curiosity\u2014ranging from the anatomy of a woodpecker's tongue and the mechanics of flight to the optics of light, water vortices, and the mathematics of fossils\u2014fueled his legendary artistic triumphs, including *The Last Supper* and the *Mona Lisa*. Leonardo emerges not as an unapproachable divine prodigy, but as a deeply human, illegitimate, vegetarian, left-handed, easily distracted misfit whose genius was forged through relentless observation, passionate curiosity, and playful experimentation across disciplines.
