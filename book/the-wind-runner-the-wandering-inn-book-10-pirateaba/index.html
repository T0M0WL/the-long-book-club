About this Audiobook

Courier Ryoka Griffin embarks on a perilous sprint into the treacherous High Passes to deliver an urgent cure, confronting immortal fae and territorial wyverns.

A Breathless Thirty-Six-Hour Sprint of High-Pass Courier Runs, Ancient Fae Magic, and Draconic Wonders

In *The Wind Runner*, the spotlight shifts to the fierce, fiercely independent City Runner Ryoka Griffin as she undertakes the most dangerous and vital courier mission of her career. Tasked with delivering an urgent potion across the deadly, blizzard-swept High Passes, Ryoka must run barefoot through territories infested with savage wyverns, stone gargoyles, and treacherous terrain. Along her journey, Ryoka encounters the mysterious and haughty Court of Winter, ancient dragons, and the enigmatic wind spirits that hold the secrets of primal magic, pushing her athletic endurance and mental fortitude to their absolute breaking points.

On audio, *The Wind Runner* is a pulse-pounding, kinetic spectacle brought to life by Andrea Parsneau’s electrifying vocal stamina. Parsneau captures Ryoka’s inner fire, fierce stubbornness, and escalating exhaustion with visceral immediacy, making listeners feel the icy bite of mountain winds and the searing ache of running on shattered stone.
