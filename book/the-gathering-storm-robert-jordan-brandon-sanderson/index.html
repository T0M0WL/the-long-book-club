Sanderson’s Electrifying Entry into the Saga Tracking Rand’s Abyss and Egwene’s Stand for Tar Valon

Following Robert Jordan’s passing, acclaimed fantasy author Brandon Sanderson stepped in alongside Jordan's editor and widow Harriet McDougal to complete the master’s unfinished vision. The result, *The Gathering Storm*, is an astounding, emotionally shattering masterpiece of narrative momentum. The novel tracks two parallel, thematic journeys: Rand al'Thor’s agonizing descent into emotional numbness and cruel detachment as 'Darth Rand,' and Egwene al'Vere’s heroic, indomitable resistance inside the White Tower, where she unifies a fractured Sisterhood and leads a desperate aerial defense against Seanchan To'raken raiders.

Listening to *The Gathering Storm* on audio is an overwhelming, breathtaking experience. Michael Kramer gives the performance of a lifetime as Rand’s psychological torment escalates toward an apocalyptic crisis atop Dragonmount, where the fate of the entire Pattern hangs in the balance. Kate Reading delivers equal emotional brilliance, voicing Egwene’s fierce stand with the Vora’s sa'angreal with hair-raising intensity. Prominently featured in our Epic Fantasy collection, this production showcases how Sanderson’s crisp pacing and Jordan’s foundational depth combine to create an auditory masterpiece.
