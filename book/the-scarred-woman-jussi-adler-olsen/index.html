About this Audiobook

Under political threat of department shutdown as random killings target young women across Copenhagen, Carl Mørck and Assad are blindsided when loyal colleague Rose unravels beneath haunting childhood trauma tied directly to the case.

Sixteen Turbulent Hours Fighting Department Shutdown While Rose Faces Hidden Trauma

Opening in bleak crisis right after The Hanging Girl, Department Q faces imminent budget axing unless Carl Mørck clears cold cases fast—just as a red-headed woman is bludgeoned in Frederiksberg Gardens in an eerie echo of a decade-old unsolved homicide, and a ruthless car-borne stalker hunts vulnerable young women across Denmark's social-support system. Worse still, loyal basement pillar Rose suffers a sudden psychotic spiral rooted in terrifying repressed childhood memories tied dangerously close to the investigation.

*The Scarred Woman* shakes up classic Nordic Noir brilliantly by putting the investigative heart of the basement team in mortal peril. Watching Carl, Assad, and newcomer Gordon scramble through high-stress Police Procedural pressure while trying to rescue Rose adds devastating emotional Suspense to dark Crime and Detective plotting steeped in psychological Mystery.

Running sixteen hours and three minutes for one credit, *The Scarred Woman* turns Department Q from wry workplace comedy into fiercely loyal found family right before Victim 2117.
