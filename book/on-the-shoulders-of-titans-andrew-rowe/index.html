Andrew Rowe Expands Spire Mysteries and Tactical Enchanting Across 24 Thrilling Hours

Armed with evidence that his brother Tristan is still alive within the upper reaches of the spires, Corin Cadence returns to Lorian Heights academy for his second year of rigorous arcane training in *On the Shoulders of Titans*. But higher education brings deadly complications: rival noble houses scheme for political supremacy, terrifying god-beasts awaken in the wilderness, and Corin’s forbidden research into artificial attunements draws the suspicious scrutiny of the continental Visages. To protect his friends, Corin must push his enchanting abilities to the absolute bleeding edge, crafting specialized gear to survive encounters with ancient, god-level entities.

Listening to this nearly twenty-five-hour sequel is pure listening joy for fans of intricate world-building and tactical problem-solving. Nick Podehl returns with supreme vocal confidence, effortlessly navigating the delicate balance between Corin's dry, self-deprecating humor and the pulse-pounding terror of sudden spire ambushes. Beside companion progression audiobooks like Mother of Learning: Arc 2, this production proves that detailed magical crafting can be just as thrilling as a traditional sword duel.

Providing almost twenty-five hours of top-tier narration for a single credit, *On the Shoulders of Titans* surpasses the debut in every metric. Rowe deepens the emotional bonds between Corin’s core group while expanding the lore of the continent of Kaldwyn. It is an exceptional credit spend that will keep your headphones glued to your ears for days of immersive fantasy entertainment.
