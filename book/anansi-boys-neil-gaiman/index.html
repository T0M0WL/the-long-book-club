About this Audiobook

When mild-mannered London accountant Fat Charlie Nancy discovers his late father was the West African spider god Anansi, his life is upended by the sudden arrival of an outrageously charismatic, divine twin brother.

Neil Gaiman's Mythological London Romp and Lenny Henry's Legendary Audie-Winning Performance

Serving as a joyous, standalone companion to American Gods, Neil Gaiman’s *Anansi Boys* centers on Charles 'Fat Charlie' Nancy: an exasperated, intensely respectable London accountant who has spent his entire adult life cringing at the flamboyant antics of his estranged father. When Mr. Nancy abruptly drops dead of a heart attack while singing karaoke in a Florida bar, Charlie learns two mind-boggling truths: first, his father was actually Anansi, the ancient West African trickster spider god; and second, Charlie has an estranged, god-tier twin brother named Spider who inherited all their father’s charm, swagger, and chaotic divine magic. When Spider shows up in London and begins casually stealing Charlie’s fiancée, career, and sanity, a hilarious supernatural feud erupts across London and the mythical World Before.

On audio, *Anansi Boys* is universally recognized as one of the finest audiobook performances ever recorded. British comedy icon Sir Lenny Henry delivers a breathtaking tour de force that earned the prestigious Audie Award for Audiobook of the Year. Henry’s vocal range is staggering: effortlessly shifting from Fat Charlie’s flustered South London cadence to Spider’s silk-smooth West Indian charm, terrifying Caribbean bird spirits, and cackling elderly West Indian church gossips. Alongside fantasy benchmarks like Good Omens and The House in the Cerulean Sea, this production stands as a radiant gem in our Magical Realism collection.

Clocking in at ten delightful hours, *Anansi Boys* delivers exceptional credit value through sheer, unadulterated comedic genius. Henry’s musicality, flawless comic timing, and joyous Caribbean patois turn Gaiman’s witty prose into pure audio sunshine. Spending a single monthly credit here guarantees hours of genuine laugh-out-loud entertainment, making this Audie-winning performance an indispensable comfort listen for your library.
