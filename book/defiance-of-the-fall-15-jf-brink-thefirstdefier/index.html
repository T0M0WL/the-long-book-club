About this Audiobook

Entering the celestial proving grounds of the ancient Ultom empire, Zac navigates lethal planar mazes and outwits primordial geniuses in pursuit of universe-altering legacies.

The Ultom Courts: Pavi Proczko Navigates 21.5 Hours of Ancient Dynasties and Multiversal Scheming

*Defiance of the Fall 15* opens an exciting, high-stakes new saga: the Ultom Courts. An ancient, long-vanished cosmic dynasty that once ruled vast swathes of the multiverse, the remnants of the Ultom empire emerge from the void, opening their legendary trial courts to the greatest D-grade cultivators across multiple sectors. Newly ascended into D-grade power and wielding terrifying Void Emperor authority, Zac Atwood enters the shifting planar labyrinths, competing against ancient scions, divine champions, and mechanized construct armies for the ultimate Imperial Seals.

Pavi Proczko commands this fresh cosmic expansion with trademark gravitas and sharp character distinction. Proczko captures the vast, enigmatic grandeur of the Ultom architecture with evocative pacing, contrasting the cold, calculating schemes of rival sect leaders with Zac’s relentless, instinctive axe combat. His handling of spatial puzzles and conceptual gravity traps is lucid and engaging, keeping tension high throughout every trial floor. Fans of high-tier multiversal competitions like The Primal Hunter 12 will be hooked.

Clocking in at over twenty-one hours for a single monthly credit, this fifteenth installment delivers outstanding entertainment value. Brink continues to demonstrate limitless creative ambition, constructing intricate dungeon mechanics and multi-faction mind games that test Zac’s intellect as much as his blade. It is an exhilarating, highly polished listen that fans will thoroughly enjoy.
