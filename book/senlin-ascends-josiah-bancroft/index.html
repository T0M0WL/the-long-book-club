About this Audiobook

Mild-mannered headmaster Thomas Senlin arrives at the colossal, steam-powered Tower of Babel for his honeymoon—only to lose his bride Marya in the crowded market at its base and begin a perilous climb through its bizarre ringdoms.

Fourteen Hours of Inventive Gaslamp Steampunk Inside a Colossal Clockwork Tower of Babel

To rural schoolmaster Thomas Senlin, the Tower of Babel is the pinnacle of human civilization—a mile-high stone cylinder housing thousands of baths, theatres, airship docks, and mechanical wonders. Yet within minutes of arriving at its crowded base for his honeymoon, Senlin is separated from his new wife, Marya. To find her, this polite, bookish teacher must ascend through the Tower's treacherous 'ringdoms'—each a bizarre, self-contained society governed by decadent aristocrats, coerced labor, and theatrical deception.

On audio, Josiah Bancroft's *Senlin Ascends* feels like a marriage of Jules Verne, *Spirited Away*, and classic nineteenth-century adventure. Hearing Senlin slowly shed his naive trust in guidebooks to become a cunning, battle-tested survivor makes for deeply rewarding headphone listening. Highlighted as the Genre Pillar on our Steampunk hub alongside Perdido Street Station, this fourteen-and-a-half-hour opener launches the acclaimed *Books of Babel* quartet.

At fourteen hours and thirty-seven minutes for one monthly credit, *Senlin Ascends* delivers literary wit, clockwork mystery, and one of modern Fantasy's most endearing protagonists.

Narrator Soundcheck: John Banks

Veteran British stage and audio actor John Banks delivers a wonderfully nuanced performance across fourteen and a half hours. Banks captures Thomas Senlin's initial fussy, academic politeness and his gradual hardening into a resourceful rogue with warmth and precision, while giving the Tower's eccentric actors, pirates, and noblewomen distinct vocal flair.
