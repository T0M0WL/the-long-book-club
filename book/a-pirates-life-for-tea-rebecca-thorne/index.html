About this Audiobook

Reyna and Kianthe trade their cozy tearoom for the high seas, boarding a pirate vessel to rescue a kidnapped friend in this charming cozy fantasy.

High Seas Swashbuckling, Cozy Tea Rituals, and Sapphic Romance

Spanning thirteen delightful, sunshine-filled hours, *A Pirate's Life for Tea* is Rebecca Thorne’s enchanting follow-up to the hit cozy fantasy Can't Spell Treason Without Tea. Having successfully established their beloved book-and-tea haven in the quiet border town of Tawney, former Queen’s Guard captain Reyna and powerful rogue mage Kianthe have settled into a blissfully domestic rhythm of baking fresh pastries, brewing rare infusions, and shelving literary treasures. But their peaceful routine is abruptly upended when a mysterious distress letter arrives revealing that Kianthe’s eccentric arcane mentor has been captured by ruthless sea scoundrels.

Forced to temporarily shutter their shop, Reyna and Kianthe embark on a coastal expedition that quickly lands them aboard a surprisingly gentlemanly pirate vessel. What follows is a delicious blend of lighthearted swashbuckling adventure and comfort-read coziness, featuring quirky seafaring crews, magical kraken encounters, secret identities, and heated debates over the proper brewing temperature of peppermint tea. Thorne deepens the central sapphic romance with humor, tender emotional communication, and zero unnecessary relationship angst, proving that high-stakes peril can coexist beautifully with warm blankets, shipboard domesticity, and steaming hot mugs.

The narrative explores how mutual trust allows two fiercely independent women to face external dangers together without losing their hard-won peace. Their witty, affectionate chemistry turns potentially terrifying encounters with sea marauders into moments of heartwarming triumph. In audio, narrator Jessica Threet is an absolute delight. Threet captures Reyna’s grounded, protective soldier instincts and Kianthe’s flamboyant, tea-obsessed arcane genius with infectious enthusiasm and impeccable comedic timing. For listeners who fell in love with Travis Baldree’s Legends & Lattes or TJ Klune’s The House in the Cerulean Sea, this thirteen-hour audio treat delivers heartwarming escapism, lively dialogue, and irresistible charm from start to finish.

Narrator Soundcheck: Jessica Threet

Jessica Threet’s performance on A Pirate's Life for Tea is pure auditory comfort. Threet excels at the playful banter between Reyna and Kianthe, giving Reyna a firm, steady baritone-edge and Kianthe a sparky, theatrical lilt that sparkles with comedic energy. Her supporting character voices—especially the blustering yet polite pirate captain—add rich flavor to the maritime setting without ever distracting from the central romance. Crisp recording fidelity and lively pacing make this thirteen-hour cozy fantasy fly past with effortless charm.
