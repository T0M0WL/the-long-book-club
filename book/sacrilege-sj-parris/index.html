About this Audiobook

After plague returns to London and a Protestant prince is assassinated, Giordano Bruno follows his fugitive former love to Canterbury—investigating counterfeit relics and murder in the shadow of England's holiest shrine.

Sixteen and a Half Hours of Tudor Plague, Fake Relics, and Canterbury Murder

London in the spring of 1584 is paralyzed by fear: plague stalks the streets, rumors spread following the assassination of Protestant champion William of Orange, and Queen Elizabeth's spies sweep up suspects across the city. When Giordano Bruno realizes the shadowy figure trailing him through the fog is Sophia—the woman he once loved, now wanted for murder—he risks everything to follow her to Canterbury on a double mission: clear Sophia's name and trace a brutal assassin smuggling counterfeit saintly relics around England's holiest cathedral town.

On audio, *Sacrilege* pairs S.J. Parris's sharpest emotional stakes yet with haunting Historical Mystery atmosphere. Moving through plague-ridden Kentish inns, crooked relic markets, and ancient monastic ruins, this third volume stands proudly on our Historical Fiction and Mystery shelves alongside the sharpest Whodunnit storytelling of C.J. Sansom.

Spanning sixteen hours and thirty-one minutes for one monthly credit, *Sacrilege* weaves heartbreak, religious hypocrisy, and edge-of-the-seat peril into a standout Tudor thriller.
