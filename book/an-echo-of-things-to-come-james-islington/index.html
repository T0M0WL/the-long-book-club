About this Audiobook

As the northern Boundary weakens and war looms, Davian and his allies race to master their powers while Caeden journeys into his forgotten past to uncover terrible truths.

James Islington's 27-Hour High Fantasy Tour de Force of Fractured Timelines and Moral Reckoning

Extending across more than twenty-seven pulse-pounding hours, *An Echo of Things to Come* avoids the common pitfalls of fantasy middle volumes, accelerating the stakes of *The Licanius Trilogy* with breathless narrative velocity. The mystical Boundary that protects Andarra from the monstrous forces of the north is steadily decaying, while shapeshifting infiltrators sow paranoia throughout the capital of Ilin Illan. Separated across vast distances, Davian must journey into hostile territory to learn the true nature of his Augur powers, while Wirr ascends the political hierarchy to defend the Gifted from extremist zealots. Meanwhile, Caeden confronts the horrific echoes of his ancient past, discovering that the enemy threatening to destroy their world may be bound to his own forgotten oaths.

In spoken audio, Islington’s bold chronological architecture delivers breathtaking revelations that reward close headphone attention. The spoken word turns temporal paradoxes, hidden identities, and dual-perspective confrontations into electrifying auditory cinema. Listening to the tragic revelations of the Venerate and the desperate political chess match in the capital makes twenty-seven hours pass with remarkable speed. Grouped naturally with monumental fantasy sagas like Words of Radiance and the dark moral depth of The Shadow Rising, this second installment elevates Islington into the absolute upper echelon of modern worldbuilders.

Exchanging a single credit or token for twenty-seven hours of *An Echo of Things to Come* offers astounding entertainment value. Islington deepens the philosophical dilemma at the heart of the series: whether destiny is immutable, and whether terrible means can ever justify the preservation of mankind. With stellar voice work that keeps every historical epoch and political faction crystal clear, this magnificent middle volume is a triumphant, unmissable experience for any serious fantasy listener.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer

Michael Kramer delivers another masterclass performance, capturing the escalating emotional and psychological stakes of Islington’s expanding narrative. Kramer navigates the dense, shifting identities of the immortal Venerate with astonishing finesse, ensuring that complex flashbacks and philosophical dialogues never muddy the storyline. His delivery of high-stakes battle scenes crackles with dramatic intensity, while his portrayal of Wirr’s statesmanlike resolve and Caeden’s existential agony brings listeners to the edge of their seats. Impeccable studio clarity and vocal stamina make this twenty-seven-hour marathon an absolute auditory triumph.
