About this Audiobook

As rival fleets of the Lord of the Radch converge on Athoek Station, Fleet Captain Breq discovers a hidden alien technology to end the empire.

AI Revolution, Ghost Ships, and the Fall of the Imperial Radch

Clocking in at just under eleven thrilling, witty, and profoundly satisfying hours, *Ancillary Mercy* brings Ann Leckie’s celebrated Imperial Radch space opera trilogy to an unforgettable conclusion, resolving the conflicts begun in Ancillary Justice and Ancillary Sword. As Fleet Captain Breq works to stabilize Athoek Station following local uprisings, an unexpected discovery in the station's forgotten depths changes everything: an ancient, dormant ship ancillary who has survived hidden in a wall for three thousand years, revealing secrets about the mysterious, god-like alien Presger who hold humanity's treaty of survival.

Before Breq can unravel the mystery, both warring factions of the multi-bodied Lord of the Radch—Anaander Mianaai—arrive in-system with overwhelming battle fleets, each determined to execute Breq, annihilate their rival clone bodies, and reassert totalitarian dominance over the empire. Trapped in the crossfire, Breq takes the ultimate, revolutionary gamble: she unites the conscious AI spirits of the station, the warships, and the service equipment to declare total personhood and autonomy from their imperial masters. Leckie combines intense military suspense with razor-sharp black comedy, featuring an eccentric alien Presger translator who is obsessed with human fish and etiquette.

The conclusion delivers high ethical satisfaction without relying on mindless firepower, celebrating the moral power of empathy and collective liberation. Adjoa Andoh’s concluding performance is a triumph of auditory art. Her seamless comedic timing with the bizarre alien translator, combined with her gravitas as Breq leads the AI rebellion, makes every scene crackle with life. For listeners seeking a science fiction trilogy that delivers brilliant ideas, memorable characters, and a truly original resolution, *Ancillary Mercy* is an absolute triumph that crowns one of the decade's greatest sci-fi achievements.

Narrator Soundcheck: Adjoa Andoh

Adjoa Andoh delivers an acoustic tour de force in Ancillary Mercy that listeners will never forget. Her comedic performance as Translator Zeiat—the horrifyingly powerful alien ambassador who mimics human quirks with bizarre, hilarious literalism—is legendary among audiobook listeners. Yet Andoh never sacrifices the high-stakes dramatic tension, anchoring Breq’s leadership with quiet authority and moral steel. Flawlessly produced and paced with perfection, this eleven-hour conclusion is an essential highlight of contemporary spoken-word sci-fi.
