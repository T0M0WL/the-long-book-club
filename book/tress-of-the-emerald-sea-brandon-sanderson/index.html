Tress of the Emerald Sea

Author: Brandon Sanderson | Narrator: Michael Kramer

Runtime: 12h 31m | Genre: Cozy Fantasy, Adventure, Fairy Tale, Humour, Sea Story

About this Audiobook

A determined girl sets sail across a sea of deadly spores to rescue her true love from an evil sorceress, accompanied by pirates, a talking rat, and whimsical magic.

A Whimsical Fairy-Tale Adventure of Spore Oceans, Talking Rats, and Practical Courage in Audio

Inspired by *The Princess Bride*, Brandon Sanderson’s delightful Cosmere Secret Project adventure whisks listeners away to the Rock of Oos, an emerald island surrounded by a deadly ocean not of water, but of shimmering, spore-filled green dust that sprouts lethal vines upon contact with moisture. Tress is a humble window washer whose simple, quiet life revolves around collecting decorative ceramic cups and sharing sweet conversations with Charlie, the Duke’s thoughtful son. When Charlie is carried away by his tyrannical father to marry a foreign princess and goes missing in the perilous Sorceress’s midnight sea, Tress packs three loaves of bread, her favorite cups, and sneaks aboard a pirate ship on an audacious, globe-trotting rescue mission.

Narrated in the witty, irreverent first-person voice of the legendary Cosmere storyteller Hoid, *Tress of the Emerald Sea* is tailor-made for the spoken audio format. Hoid’s meta-commentary, philosophical tangents on tea and bureaucracy, and wry observations make the listener feel as though they are seated beside a tavern fire hearing a master bard spin an ancient yarn. Positioned as an adventurous highlight in our Cozy Fantasy Collection alongside grander Cosmere epics like The Way of Kings and Words of Radiance, this recording delivers a breezy, joyful fantasy escape that will enchant both hardcore Cosmere scholars and casual listeners alike.

Spanning twelve and a half hours of whimsical humor, ingenious spore mechanics, and heartwarming romance, this production provides exceptional credit value. Tress stands out as one of Sanderson’s most refreshing protagonists—solving lethal naval crises and outsmarting terrifying sorceresses through practical common sense, empathy, and scientific observation rather than superhuman martial prowess. Investing a single monthly credit in this vibrant, standalone nautical fairy tale grants you a deeply comforting, laugh-out-loud auditory journey that leaves you thoroughly uplifted and marveling at the boundless storytelling imagination of Brandon Sanderson’s Cosmere.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Kramer

Legendary voice artist Michael Kramer trades his usual grave, epic-fantasy cadence for a delightfully witty, theatrical performance that captures Hoid’s narrative voice with sparkling charm. Kramer balances Hoid’s flamboyant, tongue-in-cheek humor against Tress’s pragmatic, gentle determination with effortless finesse, bringing a colorful crew of cannon-firing pirates, talking rats, and grumpy ship's doctors to vivid life. The pacing is lively and buoyant across all twelve and a half hours, supported by pristine, balanced audio mastering that captures every subtle joke and dramatic gasp with crystal clarity.
