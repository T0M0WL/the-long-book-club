A Riveting Thirty-Four-Hour Military Saga of Fabled Drake Generals, Tactical Banter, and Hearthside Camaraderie

In *The General of Izril*, the military and political realities of pirateaba’s world take center stage as the legendary Drake general Zel Shiverwing—the hero who halted the Antinium Wars—steps through the doors of the Wandering Inn. Seeking a moment of respite from corrupt politicians and ruthless commanders, Zel finds himself disarmed by Erin Solstice’s complete disregard for royal protocol and her stubborn insistence on treating every sentient creature with dignity. As Zel forms an unlikely friendship with the Antinium worker Bird and dines alongside goblin visitors, war clouds gather on the horizon as the ruthless Goblin Lord prepares his assault.

Parsneau handles rapid-fire tactical debates and intimate hearthside confessions with equal brilliance, ensuring every comedic quip and somber tactical assessment resonates with authentic weight.
