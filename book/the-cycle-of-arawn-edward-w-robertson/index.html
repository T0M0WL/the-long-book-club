Edward W. Robertson's 65-Hour Fantasy Trilogy of Snarky Banter, Dark Sorcery, and Epic Warfare

Delivering an astonishing sixty-six hours of immersive fantasy adventure, Edward W. Robertson’s *The Cycle of Arawn: The Complete Trilogy* brings together all three novels of the beloved epic—*The White Tree*, *The Great Rift*, and *The Black Whistle*—into a single, high-value audio omnibus. The narrative follows Dante Galand, an impoverished youth who teaches himself the forbidden, deadly art of nethermancy using an ancient grimoire, and Blays Dyer, an arrogant, quick-witted swordsman with an unmatched talent for survival. As their unlikely partnership evolves from petty thievery into continental leadership, they are thrust into a brutal war against tyrannical gods and ancient sorcerers, fighting for the survival of their civilization across vast deserts, besieged citadels, and shadowy underworlds.

In audio format, the dynamic buddy-comedy chemistry and sharp-tongued banter between Dante and Blays becomes the beating heart of the listening experience. Rather than treating high fantasy with dry solemnity, Robertson infuses the narrative with witty, irreverent humor, intricate magical duels, and inventive military strategy that feels fresh and propulsive. Featured proudly in our Longest Ever collection alongside massive fantasy worlds like Galaxy Outlaws: The Complete Black Ocean Mobius Missions, this recording provides the temporal space needed for their complex friendship and magical powers to evolve over years of continental conflict, balancing epic magical combat with laugh-out-loud dialogue that never takes itself too seriously.

Spending a single credit on sixty-six hours of Robertson’s complete trilogy yields unbeatable value for fantasy lovers seeking adventure without filler. It offers a massive, satisfying overarching narrative arc, fantastic magical combat, and a central bromance that rivals the best duos in modern speculative fiction. It is an addictive, laugh-out-loud listening marathon that will keep you thoroughly engaged through weeks of dedicated listening, delivering fantastic return on investment for anyone who loves clever fantasy adventure and sharp character banter from the opening heist to the final legendary confrontation.

Narrator Soundcheck: Tim Gerard Reynolds

Narrator hall-of-famer Tim Gerard Reynolds delivers an exceptional performance that elevates Robertson’s fantasy trilogy to audio perfection. Reynolds’s gravelly, rhythmic Irish cadence is the ideal instrument for Dante’s dry, introspective sarcasm and Blays’s swaggering comedic timing, creating unmatched banter chemistry that drives the entire sixty-six-hour narrative. His pacing during complex nethermancy duels and massive siege battles is breathless and compelling, supported by pristine, balanced audio engineering throughout, ensuring dynamic contrast and crisp intelligibility across all sixty-six hours.
