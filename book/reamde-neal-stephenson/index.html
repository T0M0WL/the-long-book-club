Neal Stephenson\u2019s Adrenaline-Fueled Thirty-Eight-Hour Global Techno-Thriller of Hackers and Hostages in Audio

Centering on Richard 'Dodge' Forthrast, a former marijuana smuggler who turned his fortune into T&A, a multi-billion-dollar massively multiplayer online role-playing game called T'Rain, Neal Stephenson\u2019s *Reamde* is a blistering, globe-trotting techno-thriller. When a rogue hacker in China releases a vicious ransomware virus dubbed 'Reamde' that encrypts players' financial data, a group of ruthless Russian cyber-mobsters trace the digital trail to Dodge's adopted niece Zula in Seattle. Kidnapped and forced to locate the original programmers, Zula is dragged on a terrifying international odyssey across Xiamen, Manila, and the rugged wilderness of the Idaho-Canada border as Islamic jihadists, MI6 operatives, and Chinese hackers collide in high-stakes combat.
