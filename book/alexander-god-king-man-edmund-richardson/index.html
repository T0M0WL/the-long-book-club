About this Audiobook

Winner of the 2026 Baillie Gifford Prize, historian Edmund Richardson strips away centuries of marble myth to deliver an electric, cinematic biography of Alexander the Great's meteoric conquests from Macedon to the edge of India.

Over Fifteen Dazzling Hours Inside the Baillie Gifford Prize-Winning Alexander Epic

Winner of the 2026 Baillie Gifford Prize and hailed by *The Guardian* as one of the year’s defining History releases, Edmund Richardson’s *Alexander: God, King, Man* rescues Alexander the Great from dry military textbooks and restores him as a brilliant, reckless, terrifyingly magnetic human being. Tracing his meteoric march from turbulent Macedon through Persia, Egypt, and the mountain passes of Central Asia to the rivers of India, Richardson captures both the intoxicating glamour of conquest and the paranoia that devoured Alexander’s court from within.

What sets this Biography apart on audio is Richardson’s electric, novelist’s eye for forgotten eyewitnesses, camp gossip, and vivid classical detail—turning ancient geopolitics into a propulsive adventure story. Featured among our standout non-fiction picks in Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 alongside Johan Norberg’s Peak Human and Thucydides’s The History of the Peloponnesian War, it sets a new modern standard for Ancient History listening.

At fifteen hours and four minutes for a single Audible credit, *Alexander: God, King, Man* moves with the pace of an epic thriller while delivering prize-winning scholarship.

Narrator Soundcheck: John Telfer

British actor John Telfer delivers a crisp, commanding fifteen-hour performance packed with theatrical polish and clear classical pronunciation. Telfer shifts smoothly from wry historical irony to battlefield urgency, giving ancient court intrigues, desert marches, and drunken Macedonian banquets the vivid immediacy of prestige historical drama.
