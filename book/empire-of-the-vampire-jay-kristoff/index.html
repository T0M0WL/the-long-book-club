About this Audiobook

From the New York Times bestselling author of the Nevernight Chronicle, Jay Kristoff, comes the first book of an astonishing new dark fantasy series. It has been twenty-seven long years since the last sunrise. For nearly three decades, vampires have waged war against humanity; building their eternal empire even as they tear down our own.

Jay Kristoff\u2019s Blood-Soaked Twenty-Seven-Hour Dark Fantasy Epic of the Last Silversaint in Audio

In a desolate, ruined world where the sun has not risen in nearly three decades and a perpetual, suffocating gloom known as the 'Daysdeath' has allowed bloodthirsty vampire dynasties to enslave humanity, Jay Kristoff\u2019s *Empire of the Vampire* is a ferocious, operatic dark fantasy epic. The novel is told through the prison cell confession of Gabriel de Le\u00f3n, the last surviving member of the Silver Order\u2014holy warrior-monks who took sacred vows, injected purified vampire blood into their veins, and wielded silversteel swords to hunt the night's abominations. Imprisoned and awaiting execution by the vampire empress, Gabriel recounts his tragic youth, the fall of his holy order, and his desperate quest to locate the Holy Grail and restore light to a dying world.

Experiencing Kristoff\u2019s gritty, poetic prose in audio format elevates the narrative to the heights of high-octane gothic tragedy.
