About this Audiobook

The Last Battle arrives. The forces of the Light clash with the Dark One in an apocalyptic, forty-two-hour finale featuring the legendary nine-hour continuous battle chapter.

The Monumental Forty-Two-Hour Climax to Fantasy’s Greatest Marathon, Culminating in the Legendary Nine-Hour Last Battle

Over twenty years and four million words in the making, *A Memory of Light* brings Robert Jordan’s monumental saga to an earth-shattering, unforgettable conclusion. Tarmon Gai'don has finally arrived. Across four massive fronts in the Field of Merrilor, the Gap, and Shayol Ghul, millions of human warriors, Borderland lancers, Aiel spears, and Asha'man stand against inexhaustible hordes of Trollocs, dreadlords, and the remaining Forsaken. As Mat Cauthon takes command of the entire allied war effort and Perrin battles Slayer across the dimensions, Rand al'Thor steps into the Pit of Doom to confront the Dark One in a metaphysical duel that tests the very nature of human existence.

On audio, *A Memory of Light* stands as one of the greatest accomplishments in the history of spoken-word storytelling. Most famously, Chapter 37—titled simply 'The Last Battle'—runs for an astonishing nine continuous hours, delivering an unprecedented, non-stop auditory experience that will keep you glued to your headphones through sleepless nights. Michael Kramer and Kate Reading bring every ounce of their legendary artistry to bear, voicing hundreds of characters, honoring heartbreaking heroic sacrifices, and delivering an emotional catharsis that leaves listeners weeping tears of profound awe. Highlighted as the crowning peak of our Epic Fantasy collection, this production is the ultimate testament to the magic of long-form audio.

Spanning forty-one hours and forty-seven minutes, this final volume delivers unbeatable credit value. Jordan and Sanderson pull off an impossible narrative landing, providing deep emotional closure for every beloved companion who left the Two Rivers so many hundreds of hours ago. Spending a monthly credit on this monumental finale awards you with one of the most sublime and rewarding listening achievements in the history of fiction.
