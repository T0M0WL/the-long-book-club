About this Audiobook

Five years after vanishing on her walk home from school, a young woman is found hanged in a central Stockholm park—prompting Joona Linna and Erik Maria Bark to unlock a shattered witness's repressed memories.

Nearly Sixteen Hours Probing Fractured Memories and Shattered Identities in Stockholm

Five years after sixteen-year-old Jenny Lind disappears on her walk home from school, her horribly brutalized body is discovered hanging conspicuously inside a bright Stockholm children's playground. When security footage reveals a shaken, severely traumatized witness whose mind fragmented under years of captivity and abuse, Joona Linna knows conventional interrogation will destroy her fragile psyche. Turning once more to veteran hypnotherapist Erik Maria Bark from The Hypnotist and Stalker, Joona descends into shattered altered memories before a calculating killer can silence his remaining survivor.

Listening to *The Mirror Man* recaptures the unsettling clinical intimacy of Kepler's very first hit while raising the emotional stakes higher than ever. Weaving raw Nordic Noir urgency together with deeply empathetic Psychological Thriller trauma work, intricate Police Procedural forensics, grim Crime revelations, and harrowing Detective Mystery sleuthing, Book 8 grips effortlessly from its opening scene.

Spanning fifteen hours and forty-three minutes for a single credit, *The Mirror Man* delivers piercing psychological suspense right before The Spider.
