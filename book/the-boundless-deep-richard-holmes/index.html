Fifteen Luminous Hours Rediscovering Young Tennyson, Grief, and Victorian Science

Named a Book of the Year across *The Times*, *Financial Times*, and *The Observer*, master biographer Richard Holmes’s *The Boundless Deep* performs a breathtaking act of literary resurrection. Scraping away the solemn, bearded Poet Laureate stereotype, Holmes zeroes in on the turbulent youth of Alfred, Lord Tennyson—an obsessive, darkly funny, electrically curious young man shattered by the sudden death of his closest friend Arthur Hallam just as revolutionary Victorian geology, astronomy, and evolutionary theory began shaking the foundations of religious faith.

On audio, Holmes’s signature blend of investigative detective work, lush Victorian atmosphere, and deep emotional sympathy makes fifteen hours glide past effortlessly.
