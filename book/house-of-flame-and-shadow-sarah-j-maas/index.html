The stunning third book in the sexy, action-packed Crescent City series, following the global bestsellers House of Earth and Blood and House of Sky and Breath. Bryce Quinlan never expected to see a world other than Midgard, but now that she has, all she wants is to get back.

Sarah J. Maas's 29-Hour Multiverse Climax of Ancient Worlds, Cosmic Tyranny, and Epic Rebellions

The explosive, twenty-nine-hour culmination of Sarah J. Maas’s Crescent City trilogy, *House of Flame and Shadow* delivers a breathtaking collision of worlds that unites the beloved heroines and magical lore of the Maas multiverse. Stranded in a strange, ancient realm of Prythian surrounded by unfamiliar fae warriors, Bryce Quinlan must use every ounce of her cunning, courage, and starlight magic to find a way back home. Meanwhile, in Lunathion, Hunt Athalar is imprisoned and tortured in the subterranean dungeons of the Asteri, testing his unbreakable devotion to Bryce as their rebel allies mobilize for a desperate, final stand against cosmic tyrants who have harvested the magic and souls of entire worlds for thousands of years.
