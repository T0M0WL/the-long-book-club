About this Audiobook

Darrow journeys across deep space to reconnect with his brothers in arms and reclaim his soul before the final war for Mars begins.

A Triumphant Return to Brotherhood: Tim Gerard Reynolds Shines in a 30-Hour Emotional Epic

In *Light Bringer*, Pierce Brown achieves what few sprawling sagas manage: he pulls his universe back from the brink of nihilism to deliver a profound, emotionally restorative epic about friendship, redemption, and the true cost of leadership. After the catastrophic events of *Dark Age*, Darrow and his surviving companions flee across deep space toward the Kuiper Belt and the Jovian moons, seeking to rally the Rim Golds and uncover an ancient biological superweapon before Lysander au Lune can unleash it upon the Republic.

Returning exclusively to Tim Gerard Reynolds as the sole narrator is a masterstroke that restores the intimate, electric connection between listener and protagonist. Reynolds delivers a vocal performance of breathtaking nuance, bringing Cassius and Darrow's evolving bond—from mortal enemies to brothers-in-arms—to life with tears, laughter, and hard-earned mutual respect. The climactic razor duels on Europa and the moon-fall battle sequences stand among the greatest martial scenes ever written. Fans who cherish emotional character arcs like Oathbringer will find this production deeply satisfying.

Clocking in at over thirty hours for a single token, *Light Bringer* delivers sensational audiobook value. It balances pulse-pounding space adventure with heartbreaking thematic depth, setting the stage for the upcoming final volume, *Red God*, with unforgettable majesty.

Narrator Soundcheck: Tim Gerard Reynolds

Tim Gerard Reynolds delivers what may well be his career-best performance in *Light Bringer*. Voicing every single character across thirty hours, Reynolds brings immense warmth to Cassius au Bellona's dashing, wounded honor, feral hilarity to Sevro, and icy menace to Lysander. The pacing is pitch-perfect, effortlessly pivoting between intimate philosophical fireside chats and thunderous close-quarters razor combat.
