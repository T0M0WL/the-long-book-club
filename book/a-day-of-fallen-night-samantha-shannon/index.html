About this Audiobook

Five centuries before The Priory of the Orange Tree, the catastrophic Dreadmount eruption unleashes apocalyptic dragons across a divided world.

Volcanic Cataclysm, Sisterhood, and Dragonfire in Shannon's Prequel Epic

Spanning a colossal thirty-eight hours and forty-eight minutes, *A Day of Fallen Night* is Samantha Shannon’s magnificent, expansive prequel to her groundbreaking standalone epic The Priory of the Orange Tree. Set five hundred years before the events of *Priory*, the story chronicles the devastating eruption of the Dreadmount and the catastrophic awakening of the Nameless One's fiery draconic hordes, known to history as the Great Sorrow. Across the divided realms of the East, West, South, and North, four exceptional women find their lives irrevocably altered as geopolitical rivalries collide with existential disaster.

In the South, Tunuva Melim is an elite sister of the Priory, having spent five decades training to slay wyrms while mourning the children she was forced to give up. In the West, Glorian of the House of Berethnet must navigate the suffocating pressures of carrying the royal bloodline while religious zealotry threatens her throne. In the East, Dumai has spent her youth in high mountain temples honoring the water dragons, only to be thrust into imperial succession politics. Meanwhile, in the frozen North, Wulfnor's widow and warrior princess must defend their snowy shores against ancient horrors. Shannon weaves these intricate points of view with majestic architectural precision, exploring motherhood, duty, faith, and queer love amidst the ash and thunder of apocalyptic war.

The audiobook experience is an acoustic wonder, featuring a stellar full-cast performance led by Ellie Kendrick, Sheila Atim, and Saara El-Arifi. Each narrator brings authentic cultural resonance and dramatic weight to their respective kingdom, creating an immersive, cinematic tapestry that makes almost thirty-nine hours feel profoundly rewarding. For epic fantasy devotees who crave panoramic worldbuilding, rich emotional depth, and ferocious aerial dragon combat, *A Day of Fallen Night* stands as one of the greatest long-form listening investments in contemporary literature.
