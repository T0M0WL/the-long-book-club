A Magnificent Forty-Six-Hour Conflagration of Draconic Destiny, Aristocratic Intrigue, and Bennett’s Vocal Mastery

In *Lady of Fire*, the seventeenth volume of *The Wandering Inn*, the smoldering political rivalries of the northern noble houses ignite into an open conflagration. Centered around ancient draconic lineages, fiery magical awakenings, and high-stakes court diplomacy, the story explores the heavy responsibilities of legacy and the destructive allure of unchecked power. As wildfires and wyvern flights sweep across the north, Erin and her allies must use every ounce of magical ingenuity and tavern solidarity to protect innocents caught between warring dragons and ambitious lords.

Narrator Erin Bennett hits her full, confident stride in *Lady of Fire*, delivering a stunning performance across forty-six hours and twenty-one minutes. Bennett’s command of aristocratic snobbery, furious battle cries, and intimate moments of character vulnerability is exceptional. Her handling of dragon lore and blazing elemental magic resonates with cinematic grandeur, while her depiction of Erin Solstice remains wonderfully grounded, funny, and deeply compassionate.
