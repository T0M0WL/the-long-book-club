About this Audiobook

Shaken by the brutal nail-gun execution of a famous photographer inside his Fjällbacka gallery and a shocking island attack on an acclaimed novelist's family, Patrik and Erica trace deadly ties back to 1980s Stockholm.

Nearly Fourteen Hours Pulling Fjällbacka Into 1980s Literary Ghosts

Returning eagerly to Tanumshede six years after The Girl in the Woods, *The Cuckoo* rocks Erica and Patrik with twin shocking crimes inside forty-eight hours: beloved Fjällbacka photographer Rolf Stenklo is found nailed dead inside his gallery hours before his big exhibition, right before savage bloodshed erupts at Nobel-tipped author Henning Bauer's glittering private island wedding celebration. While Patrik juggles baffled detectives and a meddling new chief, Erica uncovers ugly 1980s Stockholm literary betrayals.

*The Cuckoo* delivers crisp, vintage Nordic Noir comfort fused with cutting Police Procedural puzzlecraft. Balancing sharp social satire with brutal Crime, Erica and Patrik's enduring partnership crackles across every Detective turn and twisted Mystery secret.

Clocking thirteen hours and forty-six minutes for one credit, Book 11 completes all currently translated English chapters in timeless coastal style.
