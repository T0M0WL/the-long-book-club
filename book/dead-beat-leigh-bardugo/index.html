Leigh Bardugo Escalates Yale's Occult War in the Fifteen-Hour Third Alex Stern Novel

Picking up from the shattering aftermath of *Hell Bent*, *Dead Beat* plunges Alex Stern deeper into the rot beneath New Haven's ivory towers. As the fragile balance between Yale's wealthiest secret societies collapses under the weight of infernal debts, Alex must confront enemies both mortal and demonic while reckoning with the cost of every bargain she has struck. Ancient archives, bloodline curses, and treacherous faculty alliances converge in a relentless supernatural thriller.

In audio format, the third chapter of the Alex Stern saga thrives on atmospheric dread and hard-won character chemistry. Hearing Alex's guarded survivalism clash against the aristocratic entitlement of Yale's magical elite turns every confrontation into high-wire auditory drama. Featured in our Dark Academia alongside Ninth House and Hell Bent, this fifteen-and-a-half-hour installment raises both the emotional and supernatural stakes.

Spanning fifteen hours and thirty-three minutes for a single Audible credit, *Dead Beat* rewards returning listeners with taut mystery plotting, dark humor, and visceral occult world-building.
