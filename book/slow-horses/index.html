About this Audiobook

In a drab Farringdon dumping ground for disgraced MI5 operatives, cynical spymaster Jackson Lamb leads his failed agents into a high-stakes hostage conspiracy.

The Disgraced Rejects of Slough House Who Reinvented the British Spy Thriller

Spanning nearly ten sharp, acid-tongued hours, *Slow Horses* is the explosive debut of Mick Herron’s acclaimed Slough House series, injecting raw cynical vitality, dark workplace satire, and working-class grit into the traditional British espionage thriller. Slough House is MI5’s dumping ground for 'slow horses'—intelligence officers who have committed career-ending blunders, from leaving classified dossiers on commuter trains to botching undercover surveillance operations. Exiled to a dilapidated brick building in Aldersgate far away from Regent’s Park headquarters, these disgraced spooks are expected to spend their days pushing meaningless paper until despair drives them to resign.

Presiding over this purgatory is Jackson Lamb: a flatulent, scotch-soaked, chain-smoking Cold War relic whose grotesque habits and savage personal insults barely conceal one of the deadliest counter-espionage minds in Britain. When a right-wing extremist cell kidnaps a young British-Pakistani student and threatens his live-streamed execution, the slow horses uncover an unsanctioned false-flag black-op orchestrated by senior Regent’s Park mandarins that has spiraled out of control. Suddenly targeted by their own service's lethal 'Dogs' to cover up the scandal, Lamb’s rejects—including disgraced hothead River Cartwright and recovering alcoholic Catherine Standish—must deploy their rusty tradecraft to survive the night. In audio, Herron’s biting office farce, rain-drenched London dread, and sudden bursts of lethal violence deliver an exhilarating, addictive momentum that keeps listeners completely spellbound.

For audiobook listeners seeking maximum character-driven entertainment, mordant British humor, and high-stakes tradecraft on a single monthly credit, *Slow Horses* is an absolute triumph of contemporary storytelling. Narrator Sean Barrett delivers a virtuoso performance, perfectly voicing Jackson Lamb’s raspy, tobacco-stained drawl and wicked comic timing with unforgettable flair. It serves as the darkly hilarious companion to classic mole-hunts like Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy and modern manhunts like I Am Pilgrim, kicking off an eight-volume Spy Thriller listening addiction that justifies every single credit.

Narrator Soundcheck: Sean Barrett

Sean Barrett gives a masterclass in audiobook performance, defining Jackson Lamb's voice so definitively that you can practically smell the stale whisky, cheap cigarettes, and grease on his breath. Barrett handles the rapid transitions between bleak office comedy and deadly physical peril with flawless timing, giving each eccentric spook—from River Cartwright's earnest desperation to Catherine Standish's quiet dignity—an unmistakable personality. His gritty, gravelly baritone grounds Herron’s London in authentic, rain-soaked realism, making nearly ten hours fly by at breakneck speed.
