About this Audiobook

Celebrated as a hero, Hadrian faces deadly imperial court politics on Forum while cosmic revelations push humanity toward total war.

The Crown Jewel of the Saga: Samuel Roukin Powers 36 Hours of Supreme Space Opera

*Demon in White* is widely acclaimed by readers and critics as the undisputed masterpiece of the Sun Eater series—a monumental, thirty-six-hour epic that harmonizes thrilling space combat with Byzantine court politics and grand theological mystery. Returning to the capital planet Forum as a celebrated war hero, Hadrian Marlowe is knighted by the Emperor himself. But imperial favor breeds vicious envy: high nobles, religious inquisitors of the Chantry, and rival generals conspire to see him humiliated or assassinated, even as the Cielcin launch a coordinated offensive that threatens humanity's core systems.

Samuel Roukin’s narration across this colossal runtime is nothing short of majestic. His voice maneuvers through the labyrinthine intrigues of the Sollan court with aristocratic swagger, conveying the subtle barbs, backroom betrayals, and grand public ceremonies with effortless precision. When the narrative explodes into the orbital Battle of Berenike, Roukin’s cadence surges with thunderous intensity. Fans who revere the dynastic grandeur of Dune or the epic philosophical scale of Words of Radiance will recognize this as a modern classic.

At thirty-six hours on a single monthly credit, *Demon in White* represents the absolute summit of audiobook value. It balances dazzling gladiatorial duels and cosmic revelations with rich human emotion, ensuring that not a single minute feels wasted.
