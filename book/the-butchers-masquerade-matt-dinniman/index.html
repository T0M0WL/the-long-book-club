About this Audiobook

On the dreaded sixth floor, wealthy galactic tourists descend as Hunters to slaughter surviving crawlers for sport, prompting Carl and Donut to mount a revolutionary counter-offensive.

Matt Dinniman's Crowning Masterpiece of Emotional Devastation and Jeff Hays's Career-Defining Vocal Tour de Force

On the dreaded sixth floor of the World Dungeon, the masquerade drops and the crawl turns overtly monstrous. Known as the Hunting Grounds, floor six allows wealthy, aristocratic galactic citizens to buy custom god-tier avatars and descend into the arena as 'Hunters' to legally slaughter surviving crawlers for leisure sport and broadcast points. Unbeknownst to the tourists and the corporate executives orchestrating the slaughter, Carl and Princess Donut have spent five floors preparing for this moment. Refusing to play the role of prey, Carl rallies the scattered crawlers into a coordinated, ruthless insurgency, turning the hunters into the hunted and delivering an unforgettable message to the galaxy that humanity will not go quietly into the dark.

Following the treacherous underwater gauntlet of The Gate of the Feral Gods, *The Butcher's Masquerade* is widely celebrated on audio by listeners and critics as the undisputed masterpiece of the entire saga. Spanning twenty-three and a half breathtaking hours, narrator Jeff Hays achieves a level of emotional depth and dramatic ferocity that transcends genre boundaries. The transition from hilarious situational comedy to gut-wrenching battlefield tragedy is handled with breathtaking nuance, culminating in an epic third-act battle that leaves listeners in stunned silence. Alongside literary monuments like The Way of Kings and grimdark classics like The Lies of Locke Lamora, this production cements its place in the pantheon of modern Epic Fantasy and speculative fiction.

For audiobook listeners seeking the absolute pinnacle of single-credit value, *The Butcher's Masquerade* is a triumphant, mandatory listen. Soundbooth Theater’s cinematic audio design, haunting musical motifs, and earth-shattering combat engineering make every hour feel monumental. Trading a single credit for this twenty-three-hour emotional roller-coaster provides unmatched listening satisfaction, delivering a cathartic, tear-soaked triumph that will linger in your memory long after the credits roll.
