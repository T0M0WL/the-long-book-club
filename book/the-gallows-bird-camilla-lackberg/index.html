About this Audiobook

As a tacky reality-TV crew descends on peaceful Tanumshede just weeks before Patrik and Erica's wedding, a woman staged in a fake drink-driving wreck pulls Fjällbacka police straight into a ruthless Swedish serial killer's path.

Over Thirteen Hours Colliding Trash Reality TV With Serial Murder

As Tanumshede brace themselves for both Patrik and Erica's long-awaited wedding and a sensationalist reality-TV crew filming trashy mayhem around town after The Stonecutter, police find a local woman crumpled inside a staged drink-driving wreck. Evidence swiftly links the staging to a nationwide serial killer tracking unsuspecting victims—forcing Patrik's small station to filter real forensic Crime clues out of cynical tabloid drama while Erica fears for her sister Anna.

In audio format, *The Gallows Bird* (published internationally as *The Stranger*) balances wicked media satire cleanly against chilling Nordic Noir danger. Crisp Police Procedural teamwork and clever Detective misdirection build sharp Mystery suspense without losing Fjällbacka's warm domestic anchor.

Clocking thirteen hours and thirty-five minutes for one Audible credit, Book 4 shines brightly before Erica's harrowing personal journey inside The Hidden Child.
