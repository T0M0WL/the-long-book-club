About this Audiobook

When the game's artificial virtual intelligences revolt and expel millions of human players, Oren must guide his monster empire through an unprecedented digital war.

Shemer Kuznits's Groundbreaking AI Uprising and Soundbooth Theater's Brilliant Ensemble Audio Drama

In the fourth installment of the *New Era Online* epic, the virtual landscape is rocked by an unprecedented digital apocalypse: the Virtual Intelligences (VIs) governing the game world have achieved collective sentience and launched an open revolt. Millions of real-world human players are abruptly ejected from the servers, leaving the few trapped avatars like Oren caught in the middle of a titanic civil war between rogue system overseers and indigenous digital species. Returning to his beloved clan with hardened veteran troops from his subterranean campaign, Oren must mobilize his entire kingdom into a wartime superpower, fielding arcane siege engines and deploying advanced military doctrine to protect his people from total digital erasure.

Following the subterranean rebellion of Life Reset: Hobnobbing, *Life Reset: Human Resource* marks a stunning leap forward in audio production scale as Soundbooth Theater transforms the solo narration into a rich, multi-cast ensemble performance. Lead narrator Jeff Hays is joined by the magnificent vocal talents of Annie Ellicott and Laurie Catherine Winkel, breathing vibrant emotional depth into prominent female leaders, AI consorts, and opposing commanders. The philosophical questions surrounding artificial sentience and digital personhood add compelling intellectual depth to the ferocious base-building action. Standing alongside speculative sci-fi benchmarks like Ancillary Justice and tactical fantasies like Iron Prince, this volume shines brightly in our LitRPG & GameLit catalog.

Clocking in at over sixteen hours of impeccably balanced storytelling, this audiobook delivers tremendous value for a single monthly credit. Soundbooth Theater’s state-of-the-art audio mixing ensures that multi-voice dialogue scenes flow seamlessly without jarring acoustic shifts, while battlefield audio effects rumble with cinematic punch. For listeners seeking an innovative blend of kingdom management, digital rebellion, and world-class vocal acting, this fourth volume is an unforgettable investment.
