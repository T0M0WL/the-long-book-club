About this Audiobook

Sent on a suicidal mission to the frontlines, Hadrian and his Red Company suffer catastrophic captivity under the Cielcin warlords.

Into the Abyss: Samuel Roukin Delivers a Raw, Heartbreaking Tour de Force

In *Kingdoms of Death*, Christopher Ruocchio plunges his protagonist into the deepest, most harrowing crucible of the entire saga. Dispatched by a paranoid emperor on a suicidal mission to relieve the besieged Lothian Commonwealth, Hadrian Marlowe and his beloved Red Company are ambushed and captured by the Cielcin grand armada. What follows is not a tale of heroic victories, but an agonizing, claustrophobic journey through the dark bowels of alien vessels where starvation, psychological torture, and irreversible losses strip Hadrian of everything he holds dear.

Samuel Roukin’s vocal work in this fourth volume is astonishing in its emotional vulnerability. Rather than relying on romantic heroism, Roukin captures Hadrian's physical degradation, delirium, and unyielding spirit with agonizing authenticity. The depiction of the Cielcin—famine-starved, fanatical, and worshipping cosmic darkness—is genuinely terrifying in audio format. Fans who appreciated the grim, unflinching survival journeys in The Road or The Blade Itself will find this installment unforgettable.

At nearly twenty-three hours, *Kingdoms of Death* is a punishing yet essential chapter that redefines Hadrian Marlowe. It provides profound credit value by stripping away the comfortable myths of space opera, proving that true heroism is forged in enduring unbearable suffering.
