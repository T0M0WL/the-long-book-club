Arm of the Sphinx

Author: Josiah Bancroft | Narrator: John Banks

Runtime: 14h 07m | Genre: Steampunk, Fantasy, Adventure, Mystery

About this Audiobook

Now captain of an outcast airship crew, Thomas Senlin raids the skies and infiltrates the Silk Garden ringdom in desperate pursuit of his lost wife Marya and the mysterious Sphinx's true agenda.

Fourteen Hours of Airship Heists and Gaslamp Mystery Inside Babel's Upper Ringdoms

When last we left polite schoolteacher Thomas Senlin at the end of Senlin Ascends, survival had already forced him to lie, brawl, and steal his way out of the Tower of Babel's brutal lower parlors. As Book 2 opens, Senlin commands the stolen airship *State of Art* alongside a misfits' crew of loyal outcasts—raiding Babel's bloated merchant skies while desperately hunting clues to his wife Marya's disappearance. The trail leads skyward into the lush, deceptive Silk Garden ringdom and straight into a dangerous bargain with the elusive, enigmatic Sphinx.

In audio format, *Arm of the Sphinx* shifts brilliantly from a lonely fish-out-of-water Adventure into a sharp, multi-perspective Steampunk caper and Mystery. Josiah Bancroft lets each crew member—from prickly Voleta to scarred warrior Edith and sharp-tongued Iren—step into the spotlight, enriching the Tower's brass-and-steam mythology while keeping every twist tight.

Spanning fourteen hours and seven minutes for a single credit, *Arm of the Sphinx* proves the rare middle chapter that expands both emotional heart and Fantasy spectacle without losing an ounce of momentum.
