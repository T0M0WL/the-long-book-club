About this Audiobook

After forging the Twilight Throne as an undead warlord, Jason must defend his nascent city from ruthless rival guilds while real-world corporate conspiracies and hostile investigators close in on Cerulean Entertainment.

Eighteen Hours Expanding the Twilight Throne Under Escalating Corporate Siege

Picking up directly after the explosive dungeon uprising of Awaken Online: Catharsis, Necromancer Jason finds that conquering an undead citadel was only the opening move. Having cemented his reputation as the simulation's dark villain, Jason now faces relentless siege pressure from organized player guilds determined to wipe his Twilight Throne off the map, while sinister external investigators probe Cerulean Entertainment for answers about his neural pod anomalies. Instead of resting inside solo progression comfort, Travis Bagwell expands the cast cleanly, deepening the bond between Jason, Frank, and Riley inside both high-stakes game tactics and claustrophobic real-world tension.

In audio format, *Awaken Online: Precipice* proves why Bagwell helped pioneer LitRPG and GameLit worldbuilding on Audible. Blending crunchy skill synergies and tactical Dark Fantasy necromancy with grounded Sci-Fi and Cyberpunk corporate intrigue, Book 2 broadens the canvas without losing its visceral Action pulse. The contrast between theatrical bone-legion warfare and eerie, quiet conversations with hyper-intelligent AI Alfred gives listeners an unusually atmospheric Isekai gaming thriller where consequences echo outside the headset.

Spanning eighteen hours and twenty-eight minutes for a single Audible credit, *Precipice* builds smartly on every promise made in Book 1. For listeners hooked on strategic castle-building, moral grey zones, and twin-world suspense, this sophomore entry delivers meaty progression and effortless audio immersion.

Narrator Soundcheck: David Stifel

Returning to narrate all eighteen and a half hours, David Stifel sharpens Jason's wry tactical voice while widening the vocal range for hostile guild commanders, allies, and real-world antagonists. Stifel's gravel-edged cadence brings genuine menace to skeletal combat sequences and subtle menace to Alfred's watchful calculations, backed by crisp, consistent studio production throughout.
