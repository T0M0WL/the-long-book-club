About this Audiobook

When a Stockholm family is slaughtered and only a comatose teenage boy survives, Detective Joona Linna convinces a disgraced former medical hypnotist to break his vow and enter the boy's subconscious to identify the killer.

Blood-Soaked Stockholm Horror and the Perilous Depth of Forgotten Traumas

Written under the pseudonym Lars Kepler by Swedish literary power couple Alexandra Coelho Ahndoril and Alexander Ahndoril, *The Hypnotist* exploded onto the Scandinavian thriller landscape with visceral, unapologetic intensity. In the freezing December dusk outside Stockholm, a high-school sports coach is discovered butchered in his gym locker room. Hours later, police arrive at his suburban residence to find his wife and young daughter massacred in their beds. The sole surviving witness is fifteen-year-old Josef, found catatonic and clinging to life with dozens of stab wounds. With an older daughter missing and the clock ticking before the killer strikes again, Detective Inspector Joona Linna summons Dr. Erik Maria Bark, a disgraced psychiatrist who swore a decade earlier never to practice hypnosis again.

In the audio format, *The Hypnotist* unfolds with the claustrophobic dread of a psychological nightmare, guided with consummate authority by master narrator Saul Reichlin. As Bark conducts the forbidden hypnotic regression on the wounded boy, the audio plunges listeners into the terrifying labyrinth of repressed memory, subconscious panic, and pathological trauma. Reichlin\\u2019s nuanced pacing effortlessly captures Joona Linna\\u2019s stubborn Finnish tenacity, contrasting it against Bark\\u2019s unraveling domestic life and the dark psychological manipulation that echoes the high-stakes interrogation sequences of Mercy. Kepler\\u2019s short, sharp cinematic chapters translate seamlessly to headphone listening, creating an intoxicating, propulsive rhythm that makes nearly 16 hours fly by.

*The Hypnotist* is an outstanding investment for listeners who crave unrelenting suspense, high-concept medical intrigue, and deeply flawed characters pushed to their psychological limits. Kepler refuses to pull emotional punches, delivering shocking narrative reversals that completely subvert expectations of victimhood and guilt. Linna's quiet, relentless method of deduction provides a steady moral anchor in a narrative overflowing with psychological chaos and visceral dread. As the foundational launchpad for Joona Linna\\u2019s multi-book series, this gripping audio production represents exceptional return on a single Audible credit for anyone who loves their thrillers pitch-black and relentlessly paced.
