About this Audiobook

As the dying planet Abeth cools and political rivals close in, Nona Grey advances into the deadly martial trials of the Convent of Sweet Mercy.

Poison Craft, Sisterhood, and Lethal Martial Trials on a Dying Planet

Spanning sixteen razor-sharp, action-packed hours, *Grey Sister* is Mark Lawrence’s breathless, critically acclaimed second volume in the Book of the Ancestor trilogy, following the explosive events of Red Sister. Set on the dying world of Abeth—where a dying red sun and encroaching glaciers leave only a narrow, fifty-mile-wide strip of habitable land orbiting beneath an ancient orbital moon-mirror—the novel plunges Nona Grey into the most dangerous phase of her training at the Convent of Sweet Mercy.

Having survived her novice year, Nona is elevated to Mystic Class, where the lethal arts of the Grey Sisters (stealth, poisons, and targeted assassination) replace classroom theory with brutal martial trials. But the convent's ancient sanctuary offers little protection against the outside world. Emperor's sister Sherzal and the corrupt High Priest Tacsis have set their sights on Nona’s unique bloodline, which combines the hulking strength of the Hunska, the blinding speed of the Marjal, and the shadow-magic of the Quantal. When a vicious conspiracy breaches the convent walls, Nona must rely on her fierce bond with her fellow novices—including the aristocratic Arabella and the calculating Apple—to survive political purge and deadly blood-feuds.

Lawrence masterfully expands the grimdark worldbuilding while keeping the emotional heart firmly anchored in the fierce, unbreakable loyalty of female friendship. The combat choreography is jaw-dropping, translated into audio with ferocious energy by Helen Duff. Duff brings an intoxicating blend of youthful defiance, lethal grit, and emotional vulnerability to Nona, making every blade clash and rooftop flight feel immediate and visceral. Her depiction of the deep bond between the novice sisters gives the brutal violence a powerful moral center. For fans of Joe Abercrombie’s The Blade Itself and Robin Hobb’s Assassin's Apprentice, *Grey Sister* is an essential, five-star dark fantasy listen.

Narrator Soundcheck: Helen Duff

Helen Duff’s narration on Grey Sister is ferocious, poetic, and utterly captivating. Duff captures Nona Grey’s volatile balance of feral instinct and desperate longing for belonging with extraordinary vocal agility. Her combat delivery is fast, punchy, and visceral, matching Lawrence’s kinetic fight scenes stride for stride, while her handling of Abbess Glass’s measured, political cunning is delivered with chilling authority. Recorded with immaculate dynamic clarity, this sixteen-hour production is a tour de force of spoken-word fantasy.
