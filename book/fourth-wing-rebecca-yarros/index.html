About this Audiobook

Twenty-year-old Violet Sorrengail was supposed to enter the Scribe Quadrant, living a quiet life among books and history. Now, the commanding general—also known as her tough-as-talons mother—has ordered Violet to join the hundreds of candidates striving to become the elite of Navarre: dragon riders.

The explosive international phenomenon that redefined modern Romantasy, Rebecca Yarros’s *Fourth Wing* delivers twenty-one breathless hours of deadly military academy trials, fire-breathing dragons, and scorching romantic tension. Twenty-year-old Violet Sorrengail, physically fragile and destined for the quiet life of a bookish scribe, is suddenly forced by her commanding general mother into the brutal Riders Quadrant at Basgiath War College.
