About this Audiobook

In a Tokyo suburb, Toru Okada's search for his wife's missing cat leads him into a hidden realm beneath the city's placid surface, encountering psychic sisters, a dry well, and haunted veterans of the Manchurian campaign.

A Hypnotic Subterranean Odyssey of Missing Cats, Dream Wells, and War Trauma

Haruki Murakami’s defining masterpiece begins with the most mundane domestic dilemma: unemployed suburban househusband Toru Okada is boiling spaghetti in his quiet Tokyo kitchen when he receives a bizarre, sexually suggestive phone call, right before noticing that his beloved family cat, Noboru Wataya, has mysteriously vanished. What starts as an eccentric neighborhood search for a missing pet soon fractures into a surreal, existential labyrinth when Toru’s wife Kumiko abruptly disappears without explanation. To unravel the mystery of her psychological abandonment, Toru descends to the bottom of a dry, pitch-black neighborhood well, navigating a liminal realm of clairvoyant psychic sisters, charismatic evil politicians, and the buried wartime atrocities of the Japanese occupation of Manchuria.

On audio, Murakami’s trademark blend of dry suburban deadpan and horrifying metaphysical dread becomes an intensely visceral, hypnotic headphone journey. Hearing Toru sit in absolute sensory deprivation at the bottom of the dried-up well, listening to the mechanical clicking of the invisible wind-up bird that supposedly keeps the entire universe in motion, induces a profound state of contemplative focus. Narrator Soundcheck: Rupert Degas

Rupert Degas delivers an absolute tour-de-force narration, handling Murakami’s immense cast of eccentrics with breathtaking chameleon-like vocal versatility. He perfectly embodies Toru Okada’s quiet, bewildered detachment while pivoting effortlessly to the bubbly teenage nihilism of May Kasahara, the sinister arrogance of Noboru Wataya, and the harrowing, rasping trauma of Lieutenant Mamiya’s wartime confessions. Degas’s deliberate pacing allows Murakami’s poetic, surreal prose to breathe naturally, supported by clean, warm studio mastering that captures every eerie well echo and whispered secret with crystalline clarity.
