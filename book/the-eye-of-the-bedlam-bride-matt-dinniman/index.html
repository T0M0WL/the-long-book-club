Matt Dinniman's Sprawling Celestial War and Jeff Hays's Ensemble Audio Epic of Revolutionary Madness

Descending into the madness of floor eight—the dreaded celestial level governed by the Bedlam Bride—Carl, Princess Donut, and their battle-scarred comrades find the World Dungeon fundamentally broken. The AI running the game has descended into unchecked eccentricity, corporate executives are panicking behind closed boardroom doors, and a complex system of faction cards forces crawlers to strategically allocate territory while dodging god-tier celestial entities. Armed with forbidden knowledge, devastating alchemical weaponry, and a growing intergalactic revolutionary following, Carl continues his unyielding quest to dismantle the entire televised death show from within, proving that no empire is too grand to be shattered by a man in boxer shorts.
