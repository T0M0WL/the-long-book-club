About this Audiobook

Deep in the stacks of Oxford's Bodleian Library, young scholar Diana Bishop unwittingly calls up a bewitched alchemical manuscript in the course of her research.

Deborah Harkness's 24-Hour Oxford Odyssey of Alchemical History, Forbidden Romance, and Secret Manuscripts

Written by a real-life professor of history and the history of science, Deborah Harkness’s *A Discovery of Witches* is a lush, twenty-four-hour supernatural romance infused with authentic academic scholarship. Deep in the vaulted stacks of Oxford's Bodleian Library, reluctant witch and tenured Yale historian Diana Bishop unwittingly checks out Ashmole 782, a long-lost enchanted manuscript that has been missing for centuries. Her accidental discovery sends shockwaves across the hidden supernatural world, drawing witches, daemons, and vampires to Oxford in a desperate race for power. Foremost among them is Matthew Clairmont, a thousand-year-old geneticist and vampire, sparking a forbidden alliance that threatens the fragile treaty governing magical creatures, setting off a high-stakes supernatural pursuit that spans ancient libraries and historic European châteaux.

In audio format, Harkness’s scholarly expertise transforms what could be standard fantasy tropes into an intoxicating, deeply textured sensory journey. The auditory experience lingers on the tactile details that make Dark Academia so irresistible: the smell of ancient vellum, the rustle of seventeenth-century alchemical engravings, and the hushed, rainy atmosphere of historic Oxford quadrangles. Featured prominently in our curated Dark Academia collection and bridging into our Epic Romantasy selections, this unabridged recording provides the unhurried space required for centuries of supernatural history and simmering romantic tension to unfold with maximum emotional resonance, drawing you completely into its rich world of genealogical puzzles and hidden covens.
