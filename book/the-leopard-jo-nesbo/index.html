Over Nineteen Ferocious Hours Dragging Harry Hole Back From Hong Kong

Shattered after the trauma of The Snowman, Harry Hole hides inside Hong Kong opium dens drowning memories of Norway—until earnest young investigator Kaja Solness lures him back toward his dying father and a baffling Oslo slaughter. Two women have died drowned inside their own blood via a horrifying spiked brass sphere known as Leopold's Apple, tying survivors of a snowed-in mountain hostel together across ruthless inter-police rivalries and Congolese shadows.

Clocking over nineteen sprawling hours, *The Leopard* cranks Nesbø's Nordic Noir Thriller scale to blockbuster dimensions. Blending vicious Crime traps with sharp Police Procedural turf wars between Harry's loyalists and sleazy police chief Mikael Bellman creates epic Detective warfare.

At nineteen hours and twenty-six minutes for one credit, Book 8 serves huge marathon value before intimate heartbreak erupts in Phantom.
