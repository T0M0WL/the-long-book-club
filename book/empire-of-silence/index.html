About this Audiobook

Exiled nobleman Hadrian Marlowe recounts his journey from gladiator on a backwater world to commander of the Sollan Empire and destroyer of an entire alien sun.

The Sumptuous Feudal Space Opera That Merges Dune with Classic Literature

Spanning twenty-six magnificent hours, *Empire of Silence* is the triumphant opening volume of Christopher Ruocchio’s Sun Eater series, delivering a luxurious, philosophically rich space opera on an astronomical scale. Set tens of thousands of years in the future, humanity is ruled by the Sollan Empire—a decadent, neo-Roman feudal aristocracy that forbids advanced artificial intelligence, worships ancient terrestrial memory, and relies on genetic manipulation to extend noble lifespans across centuries. Ruocchio frames the epic as the retrospective memoir of Hadrian Marlowe: scholar, gladiator, courtier, and savior who ultimately extinguished an entire star to end an existential war against the predatory alien Cielcin, earning the eternal hatred of billions across known space.

Hadrian’s narrative begins not with battlefield glory, but with familial treachery. Fleeing his sadistic father’s plan to force him into the dogmatic Chantry priesthood, Hadrian is betrayed and cast adrift in cryo-sleep, waking destitute upon a rain-swept frontier planet where he survives as a beggar and gladiator in subterranean arena rings. Listening in audio magnifies the literary grandeur of Ruocchio's prose. As Hadrian claws his way from poverty to the high imperial court, exploring ancient alien ruins and confronting human xenophobia, the story combines the sweeping planetary majesty of *Dune* with the melancholic introspective depth of classic autobiographical fantasy, creating an immersive, multi-layered world that feels endlessly vast, physically tangible, and rich with intellectual curiosity.

For discerning listeners seeking maximum credit value, this twenty-six-hour epic is a feast of worldbuilding, linguistic elegance, and slow-burn dramatic payoff. Narrator Samuel Roukin delivers a masterclass of aristocratic nuance, effortlessly capturing Hadrian's weariness, intellectual passion, and sorrowful wisdom across hundreds of scenes. Alongside fellow galactic epics like Pandora's Star and The Player of Games, *Empire of Silence* is an indispensable pillar of modern Space Opera, offering deep intellectual immersion that rewards every single hour of your investment with profound narrative resonance and emotional truth.

Narrator Soundcheck: Samuel Roukin

Samuel Roukin gives an extraordinary, aristocratic performance that perfectly embodies the world-weary retrospective voice of Hadrian Marlowe. His English diction is impeccably crisp, lending an antique, classical weight to the novel’s philosophical musings and imperial ceremonies. Roukin distinguishes alien Cielcin speech, rough street-urchin dialects, and condescending patrician lords with absolute precision. His deliberate pacing allows the vast historical scope of the Sollan Empire to breathe, elevating twenty-six hours of high-concept science fiction into an unforgettable, atmospheric audio experience.
