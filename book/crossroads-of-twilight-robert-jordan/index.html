About this Audiobook

As the cataclysmic magical aftershocks of the Cleansing ripple across the continent, Egwene lays siege to Tar Valon and Perrin pursues the Shaido.

A Sprawling Geopolitical Tapestry of Continental Aftershocks, Royal Sieges, and Moral Compromise

In *Crossroads of Twilight*, the tenth volume of *The Wheel of Time*, Robert Jordan steps back to examine the profound global ramifications of the Cleansing of Saidin. Across every border and realm, channelers stop in their tracks as an unprecedented tidal wave of One Power washes over the land, heralding an era where male channelers need no longer fear the madness. As Rand recovers in hiding, Perrin Aybara faces agonizing moral compromises in his desperate quest to rescue his wife Faile from the brutal Shaido camps, while Egwene al'Vere tightens the blockade around Tar Valon, culminating in a daring nocturnal harbor infiltration.

Experiencing *Crossroads of Twilight* through audio is by far the most enjoyable way to appreciate Jordan’s vast geopolitical maneuvering. Narrators Michael Kramer and Kate Reading infuse every diplomatic council and icy camp meeting with dramatic urgency, ensuring that character motivations remain clear and gripping. Kramer’s depiction of Perrin’s internal torment and Mat Cauthon’s blooming courtship of Tuon provides rich entertainment, while Reading’s masterful handling of Egwene’s courage during the harbor sabotage is electrifying.
