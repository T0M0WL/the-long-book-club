Nearly Fourteen Hours of Hopeful Planetary Engineering and Sentient Moose in Newitz's Solarpunk Epic

Fifty-nine thousand years in the future, on the corporate-owned planet Sask-E, Environmental Rescue Team ranger Destry spends her days soaring over thriving prairies alongside her genetically uplifted moose partner, Whistle, protecting fledgling ecosystems across centuries. When Destry stumbles upon an unauthorized underground city full of diverse human and non-human workers inside an active volcano, she sparks a multi-generational struggle over who truly owns a living world: real-estate conglomerates building designer luxury worlds, or the beings actually inhabiting the soil.

Listening to *The Terraformers* on audio captures Solarpunk and Hard Sci-Fi at their most imaginative. Annalee Newitz blends rigorous climate science, talking transit systems, and deep solidarity between humans, animals, and AIs—making it an uplifting sister read to A Half-Built Garden and Walkaway on our Solarpunk shelf.

Spanning thirteen hours and thirty-six minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Terraformers* packs centuries of invention, queer warmth, and evolutionary hope into one sleek standalone listen.

Narrator Soundcheck: Emily Lawrence

Emily Lawrence delivers a warm, expressive, and wonderfully agile narration across nearly fourteen hours. Lawrence keeps the story's massive multi-century time jumps grounded through intimate character emotion—giving Destry, her loyal moose companion Whistle, and even sentient flying trains endearing, distinct personalities with clean studio clarity.
