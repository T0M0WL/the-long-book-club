About this Audiobook

Concluding The Civil War Trilogy, Jeff Shaara chronicles the brutal final two years from the aftermath of Gettysburg through the Wilderness, Petersburg, and Appomattox as Ulysses S. Grant takes command of the Union armies against Robert E. Lee.

Over Twenty-Six Relentless Hours from Gettysburg's Aftermath to Appomattox

Picking up in the rain-soaked aftermath of The Killer Angels, Jeff Shaara’s monumental finale *The Last Full Measure* follows the final, grueling two years of the American Civil War through the interlocking perspectives of Ulysses S. Grant, Robert E. Lee, and Joshua Lawrence Chamberlain. Summoned east by Abraham Lincoln to break the Virginia stalemate, Grant abandons cautious maneuver warfare for relentless pressure—plunging both armies into the smoky nightmare of the Wilderness, Spotsylvania, Cold Harbor, and the agonizing siege of Petersburg.

Clocking in as the longest and most emotionally devastating volume of the trilogy, *The Last Full Measure* shines brightest in its portrait of Grant—quiet, cigar-chewing, fiercely determined, and haunted by the terrible arithmetic of ending the War for good—making it a superb dramatic companion to Ron Chernow’s biography Grant and Doris Kearns Goodwin’s Team of Rivals. By the time Lee and Grant finally sit across from one another in Wilmer McLean’s parlor at Appomattox, every single hour of the trilogy pays off with lump-in-the-throat weight.

At twenty-six hours and forty-two minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Last Full Measure* is a towering, deeply rewarding capstone to Historical Fiction’s premier Civil War saga.

Narrator Soundcheck: Mark Bramhall

Returning for nearly twenty-seven gripping hours, Mark Bramhall brings continuity, stamina, and quiet emotional authority to the trilogy's final stretch. His understated, bulldog-steady voice for Ulysses S. Grant contrasts brilliantly against an aging, visibly exhausted Robert E. Lee—turning the long march from the Wilderness to the quiet dignity of Appomattox into deeply moving marathon listening.
