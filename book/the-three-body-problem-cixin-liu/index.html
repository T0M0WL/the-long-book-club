About this Audiobook

Set against the backdrop of China's Cultural Revolution, a secret military project sends signals into space to establish contact with aliens. An alien civilization on the brink of destruction captures the signal and plans to invade Earth.

Cixin Liu’s Mind-Bending First-Contact Masterpiece of Physics and Cosmic Dread in Audio

Beginning amid the bloody chaos of China's Cultural Revolution, astrophysicist Ye Wenjie makes a fateful, desperate decision inside the remote military Red Coast Base: broadcasting a high-powered radio signal into deep space that invites an advanced alien civilization to cleanse a corrupt humanity. Decades later in modern Beijing, nanomaterials researcher Wang Miao investigates a bizarre epidemic of suicides among elite theoretical physicists. Operating alongside physicist Ding Yi and assisted by the street-smart detective Shi Qiang, Wang discovers that human traitors have organized the Earth-Trisolaris Movement under oil billionaire Mike Evans. Drawn into the surreal, physics-defying virtual reality world of the Three-Body game, Wang uncovers an imminent interstellar invasion from the dying world of Trisolaris.

In its audio incarnation, Cixin Liu’s Hugo Award-winning masterpiece becomes a terrifying, all-consuming headphone journey that turns quantum mechanics and astrophysics into pure existential suspense. The unnerving countdown burned into Wang Miao's retina, the terrifying cinematic ambush on the Judgment Day using microscopic nanofilament wires in the Panama Canal, the cosmic flickering of the microwave background radiation, and the chilling sophon dimensions unfold in your headphones with breathtaking intellectual power.
