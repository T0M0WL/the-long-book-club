About this Audiobook

Working quietly as an insurance investigator after leaving Oslo homicide behind, Harry Hole is pulled back into dark film-set murders tied to an uncompleted project called Final Cut—when his stepson, Officer Oleg Fauke, begs for help.

Over Fifteen Cinematic Hours Passing Oslo's Homicide Torch Toward Oleg

Stepping quietly out of policing into routine insurance fraud claims after surviving Killing Moon, an aging Harry Hole enjoys unaccustomed calm—until stepson Oleg Fauke, now proudly carrying an Oslo homicide badge himself, begs Harry for help on a macabre string of staged killings recreating scenes from an abandoned curse-ridden movie called *Final Cut*. Drawn back into treacherous creative rivalries where every victim plays a filmed role, mentor and stepson navigate duplicity while the camera rolls.

*Kill Shot* flips fourteen books of generational history brilliantly inside Nordic Noir. Seeing Oleg lead formal Police Procedural inquiries beside Harry's sharp outsider Detective gaze injects fresh Crime warmth and razor-edge Thriller deception.

Running fifteen hours and twenty-six minutes for a single Audible credit, *Kill Shot* proves Harry Hole still commands Oslo shadows effortlessly fourteen volumes deep.
