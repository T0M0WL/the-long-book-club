About this Audiobook

The Assassin in White has killed the Alethi king. Now he awaits the weeping of the world.

Brandon Sanderson’s Monumental High-Fantasy Sequel of Radiants, Duels, and Shattered Plains in Audio

Picking up immediately in the aftermath of the colossal battles on the Shattered Plains, *Words of Radiance* elevates Brandon Sanderson’s epic to breathtaking new heights of martial spectacle and emotional grandeur. Kaladin Stormblessed, now captain of Dalinar Kholin’s personal bodyguard, must navigate lethal royal court politics and treacherous highprinces while struggling to master his burgeoning Windrunner powers alongside his erratic honorspren Syl. Meanwhile, Shallan Davar arrives at the warcamps hiding painful family secrets, infiltrating the secretive Ghostbloods while learning the elusive, deceptive art of Lightweaving. Facing the terrifying resurgence of the Assassin in White Szeth and unmasking royal conspiracies orchestrated by King Taravangian, Kaladin must choose between his thirst for vengeance and his sworn radiant oaths, all while an ancient Parshendi ritual threatens to summon the apocalyptic Everstorm across Roshar.

Experiencing *Words of Radiance* on audio transforms Sanderson’s cinematic action sequences and intricate magic systems into an electrifying headphone masterclass. The vocal chemistry of Michael Kramer and Kate Reading brings every clash of Shardplate, every buzzing vibration of Pattern, and the legendary, pulse-pounding four-on-one duel in the arena into breathtaking auditory focus.
