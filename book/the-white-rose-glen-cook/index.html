About this Audiobook

Defecting from the Lady to join the rebel White Rose resistance across the cursed Plain of Fear, Croaker and the Black Company must survive floating whale-beasts, undead immortals, and the reawakening of the Dominator himself.

Nearly Twelve Hours Turning Deserters Loose Across the Strange Plain of Fear

Fleeing the burning wreckage of Juniper after Shadows Linger, Croaker's rebel splinter of the Black Company abandons their imperial paymasters altogether to march alongside the teenage prophesied White Rose. Camping across the bizarre, shifting wasteland of the Plain of Fear—patrolled by airborne windwhales, razor-beaked manta horrors, and ancient silent sentinels—the scarred mercenaries wage desperate guerrilla warfare while both the Lady and an awakening Dominator tear the North apart around Barrowland.

Listening to *The White Rose* crowns the original Northern trilogy with unforgettable Grimdark and Military Fantasy weirdness. Rather than neat heroic battles, Cook contrasts stark Sword and Sorcery logistics against surreal Dark Fantasy horror and aching Low Fantasy sacrifice, turning Croaker's conflicted obsession with the Lady into genuine tragic gravity.

Clocking in at eleven hours and forty minutes for one credit, *The White Rose* delivers a blisteringly earned trilogy climax while sending the tattered few straight southward toward Shadow Games.
