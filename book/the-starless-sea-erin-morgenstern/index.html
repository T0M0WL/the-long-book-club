About this Audiobook

When graduate student Zachary Ezra Rawlins discovers an unmarked library book containing a memory from his own childhood, his search for answers leads to a subterranean harbor of endless stories, secret guardians, and shifting mythic realms.

Over Eighteen Enchanted Full-Cast Hours Inside an Endless Subterranean Library of Stories

From the bestselling author of The Night Circus, Erin Morgenstern's *The Starless Sea* is a lush, unabashed love letter to storytelling itself. When graduate student Zachary Ezra Rawlins pulls an uncatalogued book of fables from his university library shelves, he is stunned to find a vivid scene from his own childhood printed right alongside tales of pirates, owls, and lost keys. Following the clues—a bee, a key, and a sword—he descends beneath the earth into an ancient, crumbling harbor of books floating on a honey-sweet Metaphysical sea.

Because Morgenstern weaves Zachary's modern quest together with interlocking fables, mythic love stories, and whispered folklore, the audiobook feels like being read to by candlelight inside a secret vault. Listeners who adore atmospheric Books About Books and dreamlike Magical Realism like The City and Its Uncertain Walls will find themselves completely seduced by its mood and mystery.

At eighteen hours and thirty-seven minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Starless Sea* is pure, velvety escapism best savored slowly after dark.

Narrator Soundcheck: Full Cast

This six-narrator full-cast production—featuring Dominic Hoffman, Dion Graham, Bahni Turpin, Allan Corduner, Fiona Hardingham, and Jorjeana Marie—elevates Morgenstern's nested fairy tales into hypnotic audio theater. Each voice distinguishes the outer mystery from the mythic interpolated fables, giving nearly nineteen hours an effortless, spellbinding flow.
