Drums of Autumn

Author: Diana Gabaldon | Narrator: Davina Porter

Runtime: 44h 47m | Genre: Historical Fiction, Romance, Time Travel, Colonial America, Wilderness Survival, Frontier, Family Saga

About this Audiobook

Claire and Jamie carve out a new life on the North Carolina frontier at Fraser's Ridge while their daughter Brianna journeys through the stones to warn them of tragedy.

Frontier Survival, the Fraser's Ridge Hearth, and the Perilous Leap Across Centuries

Spanning nearly forty-five richly detailed hours, *Drums of Autumn* transports Claire and Jamie Fraser to the untamed wilderness of colonial America in 1767. Shipwrecked on the coast of Georgia, the couple travels north into the rugged Blue Ridge Mountains of North Carolina. Jamie accepts a royal land grant to establish Fraser’s Ridge—a mountain sanctuary where Highland emigrants can clear timber, sow crops, and worship in peace.

Yet the danger is relentless: predatory river pirates led by the sadistic Stephen Bonnet, fierce encounters with Native tribes, and the encroaching corruption of colonial crown authorities threaten their fragile new home. Running parallel to their wilderness struggles is a desperate storyline in the twentieth century: Brianna Randall discovers a historical clipping reporting that her parents will die in a house fire on Fraser's Ridge. Propelled by fierce love, Brianna steps through the stones at Craigh na Dun, followed by Roger Wakefield, setting off a harrowing trans-century pursuit fraught with mistaken identity and peril.

Narrator Davina Porter is an absolute marvel throughout this sprawling family epic. Porter navigates the vast cast of colonial American settlers, Cherokee warriors, and Scottish clansmen with effortless distinction, giving young Brianna an earnest modern American determination and Roger an endearing Scottish warmth. Her depiction of frontier survival is rich in tactile realism. For listeners seeking sweeping Historical Fiction and passionate family saga storytelling on a single credit, *Drums of Autumn* is an unmissable investment.

Narrator Soundcheck: Davina Porter

Davina Porter delivers a masterclass in atmospheric narration across Drums of Autumn. Porter handles the dramatic expansion of Gabaldon’s cast with astonishing ease, capturing the menacing villainy of Stephen Bonnet, the guttural cadence of Cherokee leaders, and Brianna’s modern American accent with pinpoint accuracy. Her pacing during the tense, wilderness-bound manhunt sequences keeps listener adrenaline surging across nearly forty-five hours. Produced with immaculate studio clarity and warm acoustic resonance, this audiobook is an extraordinary auditory feat.
