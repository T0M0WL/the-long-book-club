About this Audiobook

Opening with the ferocious solo odyssey of giant Teblor warrior Karsa Orlong before turning back to Seven Cities, Adjunct Tavore leads the raw Fourteenth Army into the heart of Sha'ik's Whirlwind rebellion.

Thirty-Five Hours of Teblor Warlord Origins and the Fourteenth Army's Desert March

Book Four opens boldly by handing the entire first movement to a single towering warrior: Karsa Orlong, a ferocious giant descending from snowbound Teblor mountains on a brutal quest of slaughter and disillusionment across Malazan-occupied lands. The story then snaps back to Seven Cities, where grim, stoic Adjunct Tavore Paran leads the unproven Fourteenth Army—a ragtag mix of raw recruits and battle-weary veterans like Fiddler—straight into the heart of Raraku to confront the Whirlwind Goddess and her own doomed sister.

On audio, *House of Chains* marks a thrilling shift in pace and scale across our Grimdark and Military Fantasy shelves. Watching Karsa's fierce refrain—'Witness!'—collide with Tavore's silent command sets up the second half of the entire ten-book Epic Fantasy saga.

Spanning thirty-five hours and four minutes for one monthly credit, *House of Chains* delivers colossal character transformation and fierce tactical tension.

Narrator Soundcheck: Michael Page

Stepping in seamlessly for Ralph Lister from Book 4 onward, veteran narrator Michael Page commands thirty-five rugged hours. Page brings granite menace to giant warrior Karsa Orlong, stoic authority to Adjunct Tavore, and dry soldier wit to Fiddler and Strings across crisp studio mastering.
