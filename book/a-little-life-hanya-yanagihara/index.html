About this Audiobook

A profound and powerful story of brotherly love and the limits of human endurance.

Hanya Yanagihara\u2019s Shattering Thirty-Three-Hour Epic of Trauma, Friendship, and Devotion in Audio

Following four ambitious college roommates who move to New York City to build their lives and careers\u2014aspiring architect Malcolm, charismatic actor Willem, bohemian painter JB, and brilliant, enigmatic litigator Jude St. Francis\u2014*A Little Life* begins as a glamorous post-collegiate coming-of-age story full of artistic ambition and urban camaraderie. However, as the decades pass and the four men achieve immense professional success, the narrative deepens into a devastating, intimate portrait of Jude\u2019s horrifying childhood trauma and debilitating physical injuries. Supported by Willem\u2019s boundless unconditional love and his adoptive father Harold, Jude struggles against the suffocating ghosts of his past in a desperate, lifelong battle to believe he is truly worthy of being loved.

In its audio incarnation, Hanya Yanagihara\u2019s celebrated, Booker-shortlisted masterpiece becomes an emotionally overwhelming listening experience that will move you to tears and keep you completely transfixed. Narrator Oliver Wyman turns Jude\u2019s silent torment and vulnerable friendships into an intensely real, heartbreaking auditory journey, navigating the harrowing flashbacks and tender domestic joys with exquisite vocal sensitivity, boundless empathy, and emotional restraint. Positioned as a modern titan in our Literary Fiction library alongside contemporary emotional epics like The Goldfinch and Demon Copperhead, this thirty-three-hour recording turns human suffering and radical devotion into an unforgettable auditory pilgrimage that touches the very core of human nature.

Clocking in at nearly thirty-three hours of peerless emotional velocity for a single credit, this release offers an extraordinary return on investment for listeners prepared for an intense emotional marathon. Yanagihara challenges conventional redemption narratives, delivering a profound, uncompromising study of the limits of therapy, the enduring scars of childhood abuse, and the miraculous, enduring beauty of chosen family. Spending a monthly token on this modern landmark guarantees an unforgettable listening experience that will leave an indelible mark on your soul, proving the transformative power of compassionate storytelling and vocal art at its highest peak for all dedicated audiobook lovers.

Narrator Soundcheck: Oliver Wyman

Oliver Wyman delivers an astonishing, emotionally devastating vocal performance that captures the delicate heartbreak of Yanagihara\u2019s prose with supreme empathy. Wyman voices Jude with a fragile, tremulous dignity that communicates decades of hidden agony without ever tipping into melodrama, while infusing Willem and Harold with boundless paternal and romantic warmth. His pacing across thirty-three emotionally grueling hours is impeccably disciplined, giving the quiet moments of domestic joy room to breathe. Supported by pristine acoustic clarity and balanced dynamic range, Wyman\u2019s narration is a monumental achievement in contemporary audio.
