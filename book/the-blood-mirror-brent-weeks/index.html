About this Audiobook

Stripped of his magic and imprisoned in his own chromatic cells, Gavin Guile must confront the terrible truth of his past as the White King’s armies prepare to crush the Chromeria.

Brent Weeks’s Intimate 20-Hour Psychological Duel of Family Secrets and Chromatic Prison Cells

Stripped of his miraculous powers and imprisoned within the very subterranean chromatic prison cells he designed for his brother, Gavin Guile faces his darkest existential reckoning in *The Blood Mirror*. Cut off from the sun and tormented by the mysterious entity behind the mirror, Gavin must sift through decades of suppressed memories and catastrophic lies to uncover the true origin of the Prism’s war. Outside the prison walls, Kip Guile leads a desperate guerrilla defense against the White King’s colossal armada, fighting to unite squabbling satrapies before the light of civilization is extinguished forever.

In audio, this penultimate volume functions as a tight, psychological chamber drama that contrasts brilliantly with the sprawling geopolitical clashes of the previous books. Simon Vance brings theatrical gravitas to Gavin’s solitary unraveling, transforming an enclosed dungeon setting into a gripping battleground of memory and madness. Paired in our catalog with intense psychological fantasy duels like The Way of Kings and Oathbringer, Vance's spoken performance keeps listeners on the edge of their seats.

Clocking in at twenty and a half hours for a single token, *The Blood Mirror* delivers unmatched emotional depth. Weeks sets the stage for the final apocalypse by peeling back the layers of his characters' deepest traumas and moral failures. Spending a credit on this penultimate recording yields an unforgettable, intimate listening experience that sets up one of the grandest finales in modern fantasy literature.

Narrator Soundcheck: Simon Vance

Simon Vance delivers a tour-de-force vocal performance in this twenty-hour psychological thriller. Vance's portrayal of Gavin’s feverish, hallucination-haunted isolation in the dark cell is raw, authentic, and emotionally shattering. In stark contrast, he gives Kip’s battlefield leadership a booming, newfound confidence that reflects years of hard-won growth. Mastered with warm acoustics and flawless pacing, Vance ensures that even the most claustrophobic chapters pulse with energy.
