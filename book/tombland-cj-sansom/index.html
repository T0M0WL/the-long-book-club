About this Audiobook

In the spring of 1549, Matthew Shardlake travels to Norwich to investigate a distant Boleyn kinswoman's murder—only to be swept into the massive peasant uprising of Kett's Rebellion.

Anton Lesser Narrates Twenty-Eight Hours of Tudor Murder and Peasant Rebellion in Shardlake Book Seven

By the spring of 1549, Henry VIII is dead, England is bankrupted by war and currency debasement under the boy-king Edward VI's regents, and Matthew Shardlake is working for the teenage Princess Elizabeth. Sent to the great regional capital of Norwich to investigate the gruesome murder of Edith Boleyn's estranged husband, Shardlake and his assistant Nicholas Overton uncover a web of family secrets—just as thousands of dispossessed Norfolk commoners rise up under Robert Kett to seize the city and establish a rebel parliament under the Oak of Reformation.

Listening to *Tombland* on audio is a true twenty-eight-hour historical event. While the first half delivers a classic, intricate Tudor Whodunnit through Norwich's medieval lanes, the second half explodes into a sweeping, Dickensian canvas of siege warfare, social justice, and tragic idealism. Featured as the Epic Audio Marathon on our Historical Mystery hub alongside Dissolution, this recording proves how seamlessly historical Mystery can scale into epic Historical Fiction.
