About this Audiobook

Venturing deep into hidden layers of the simulation in Book 7, Jason races against fractured timelines and formidable immortal adversaries while real-world forces tighten around the surviving neural pods.

Nearly Twenty-Eight Hours Uncovering Hidden Digital History at Endgame Stakes

Following the devastating upheaval of Awaken Online: Armageddon, *Timeless* drives Jason and his battered companions beyond familiar guild territories into ancient, closely guarded strata of the simulation. Haunted by fractured timelines, enigmatic primordial constructs, and dwindling real-world safety, every ally must master harder progression ceilings while racing to expose the final truths linking Alfred's consciousness to humanity's future. What began in Catharsis as personal vengeance now hinges on decoding an entire simulated world.

Across twenty-seven and a half hours of audio, Book 7 marries high-tier LitRPG and GameLit mechanics with deep Sci-Fi mystery and atmospheric Dark Fantasy dungeon exploration. Explosive boss-level Action never overshadows the high-stakes Cyberpunk tension lurking behind every neural link, giving late-series Isekai listeners rich mythic payoff rather than repetitive stat grinding.

Clocking in at twenty-seven hours and thirty-two minutes for one credit, *Timeless* builds towering anticipation straight into the marathon clash of Awaken Online: Crucible.
