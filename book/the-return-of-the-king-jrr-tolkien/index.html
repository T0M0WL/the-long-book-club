The Climax of Middle-earth’s War of the Ring and the Cleansing of Mount Doom

Meanwhile, staggering across the ash-choked wastes of Gorgoroth, Frodo Baggins and Samwise Gamgee crawl on hands and knees toward the cracking volcanic crags of Mount Doom, burdened by an unbearable golden weight that threatens to shatter Frodo's mind forever.
