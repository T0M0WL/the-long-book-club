About this Audiobook

In the grey city of Amalo, weary Witness for the Dead Thara Celehar uses his rare gift of speaking to the newly deceased to untangle an arrogant opera singer's drowning, contested wills, and quiet city secrets.

Eight Hours of Quiet Fantasy Sleuthing and Moral Grace in Katherine Addison's Amalo

Readers who remember minor prelate Thara Celehar from Emperor Maia's court in The Goblin Emperor meet him again living in dreary obscurity in the bustling provincial city of Amalo. Serving as a Witness for the Dead—a solitary cleric of Ulis gifted to touch newly deceased bodies and hear their final truths—Celehar spends his rainy days officiating lonely funerals, authenticating contested wills, and calming restless ghouls. When the corpse of flamboyant opera star Min Shelsin is fished from an icy canal, Celehar is pulled into a sprawling web of theater patrons, blackmail, and unloved suspects.

In audio format, *The Witness for the Dead* swaps imperial Court Intrigue for meditative, atmospheric Mystery and gentle Slice of Life. Rather than dazzling swordplay, Addison crafts an empathetic character study where patience, tea breaks, and quiet decency solve what violence never could—making it a beloved staple of our Cozy Fantasy and Low Fantasy shelves.

Clocking in at eight hours and twelve minutes, this deeply humane detective story rewards listeners looking for restorative melancholy, subtle High Fantasy world-building, and a protagonist worth rooting for.

Narrator Soundcheck: Liam Gerrard

Narrator Liam Gerrard sets an unforgettable tone across eight rich hours. Embracing a gentle, weary stillness that suits Celehar's quiet honesty to perfection, Gerrard gives glittering opera divas, grieving widows, and suspicious dockworkers nuanced individuality with immaculate elven pronunciation.
