Neil Gaiman’s Iconic Dark Fantasy Graphic Masterpiece Brought to Life in Staggering Audio

When Morpheus, the Lord of Dreams and one of the seven immortal Endless, is captured in an amateur occult ritual in 1916 and imprisoned in a glass cage for over seven decades, the mortal world suffers a catastrophic waking nightmare known as the sleeping sickness. Escaping his captors in the modern era, Dream finds his majestic kingdom in ruins, his magical vestments—the pouch of sand, the helm, and the ruby—stolen, and rogue nightmares wreaking havoc among unsuspecting mortals. Neil Gaiman constructs a dark, mythic masterpiece that weaves historical figures, urban fantasy, and cosmic horror, as Dream journeys through Victorian asylum corridors, the depths of Hell, and rainy London streets to reclaim his sovereign power.

Experiencing *The Sandman* on audio is an extraordinary, mind-bending journey that proves visual graphic novels can find an even more potent home through sound design. The flapping of raven wings, the sulfurous hiss of Lucifer’s infernal court, the clatter of bone gates, and James McAvoy’s quiet, wounded baritone resonate with haunting emotional weight that visual comic panels can never fully convey.
