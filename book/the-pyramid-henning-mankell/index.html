The Pyramid

Author: Henning Mankell | Narrator: Sean Barrett (UK) / Dick Hill (US)

Runtime: 15h 20m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Police Procedural, Mystery, Crime, Detective

About this Audiobook

Spanning five chronological prequel tales from 1969 through 1990—ending days before Faceless Killers—The Pyramid traces young patrolman Kurt Wallander through his near-fatal first stabbing, collapsing marriage, and early Ystad cases.

Over Fifteen Hours Tracing Twenty-One Formative Years Before Faceless Killers

Originally conceived right after Firewall to reveal how naive twenty-one-year-old Malmö beat constable Kurt Wallander grew into the weary inspector we meet in Faceless Killers, *The Pyramid* spans five rich novellas stretching from 1969 through autumn 1990. Inside these origin stories, young Kurt survives a near-fatal stabbing, watches mentor Hemberg model patient deduction, juggles an unraveling marriage to Mona, and confronts a bizarre plane crash linked to the Giza pyramids.

In audio format, *The Pyramid* glows with poignant nostalgia that every devoted Nordic Noir listener treasures. Experiencing young Wallander make stubborn Police Procedural blunders while learning quiet compassion turns every modest Crime, Detective, and Mystery outing into essential emotional context.

Running fifteen hours and twenty minutes for one Audible credit, *The Pyramid* enriches every previous volume—and deepens the heartbreaking farewell awaiting in The Troubled Man. (Note: daughter-led companion *Before the Frost* has no UK audiobook edition.)
