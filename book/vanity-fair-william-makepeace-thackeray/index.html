About this Audiobook

The novel follows the lives of Becky Sharp and Amelia Sedley amid their friends and families during and after the Napoleonic Wars.

Thackeray’s Sparkling Thirty-One-Hour Satire of Becky Sharp and High-Society Hypocrisy in Audio

Subtitled *A Novel Without a Hero*, William Makepeace Thackeray’s sprawling, satirical epic takes aim at the pretensions, snobbery, and mercenary social-climbing of Regency-era British high society. Armed with razor-sharp wit, unyielding ambition, and total moral flexibility, penniless orphan Becky Sharp schemes her way out of Miss Pinkerton’s Academy for Young Ladies and into the opulent drawing rooms of London aristocracy. In stark contrast stands her sweet, naive friend Amelia Sedley, whose blind devotion to a self-centered military officer blinds her to the cynical realities of the world as the shadow of the Napoleonic Wars and the Battle of Waterloo looms over their generation. From the glittering, decadent ballrooms of Brussels where panicked aristocrats dance while cannon fire thunders in the distance at Waterloo, to the squalid debtors' prisons of London, Becky uses her wit, beauty, and cynical understanding of human vanity to manipulate foolish noblemen.

In its audio incarnation, Thackeray’s biting social comedy and playful puppet-master narration sparkle with infectious theatrical energy. Narrator Helen Taylor delivers a tour-de-force performance, capturing Becky Sharp’s charismatic, conniving brilliance with effortless charm while voicing the pompous aristocrats, cowardly dandy Jos Sedley, and hypocritical clergymen with delicious satirical bite. Highlighted alongside David Copperfield and Bleak House in our Classics collection, this thirty-one-hour production turns early-nineteenth-century social satire into an endlessly entertaining auditory feast. Taylor captures the tragic nobility of Captain William Dobbin, whose patient, unrequited devotion to the naive Amelia provides a poignant moral counterweight to Becky's scandalous, high-stakes social climbing across Europe.
