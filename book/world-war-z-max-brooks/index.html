About this Audiobook

An oral history of the zombie apocalypse told by the survivors. This 14-hour star-studded production captures the global collapse and eventual human victory.

The Legendary Star-Studded Oral History That Set the Benchmark for Full-Cast Audiobooks

Max Brooks's landmark apocalyptic novel bypasses cheap action clichés to present the global zombie pandemic as an authentic, meticulously researched United Nations oral history. Traveling across continents a decade after the great plague, an unnamed UN investigator interviews soldiers, doctors, government officials, smugglers, and ordinary civilians who survived the Great Panic and humanity’s brutal, decade-long counter-offensive. From the initial outbreaks in rural China and the terrifying collapse of urban infrastructure to the tactical horrors of the Battle of Yonkers and the frozen tundra of Siberia, Brooks dissects geopolitical bureaucracy, human panic, and military adaptation with chilling documentary realism.

In audio format, *World War Z* is widely recognized as one of the greatest audiobook productions ever recorded in publishing history. Hearing an all-star ensemble of Hollywood veterans, acclaimed character actors, and international voices recount their traumatic wartime memories feels like listening to authentic, unearthed documentary tapes rather than fiction.
