About this Audiobook

When four-year-old Nea vanishes from an isolated farm outside Fjällbacka—the exact spot where a little girl was murdered thirty years earlier—Patrik hunts a child abductor while Erica probes witch hunts spanning three centuries.

Over Twenty-One Epic Hours Connecting Missing Children and Seventeenth-Century Witch Hunts

Towering at nearly twenty-two hours after The Ice Child, *The Girl in the Woods* unleashes Läckberg's grandest multi-generational saga yet. When four-year-old Nea vanishes from an isolated riding farm—the exact spot where another little girl, Stella, was found murdered thirty years earlier—panic sweeps Tanumshede just as one of the two girls convicted and later acquitted for Stella's death returns home as a celebrity actress, igniting vigilante fury mirroring 1670s Swedish witch trials.

*The Girl in the Woods* expands coastal Nordic Noir into sweeping social allegory. Layering desperate modern Police Procedural searches over small-town hysteria exposes how scapegoating drives Crime, blurring guilt across three centuries of harrowing Detective Mystery.

Offering twenty-one hours and forty-one minutes for one Audible credit, Book 10 stands as the series' longest marathon right ahead of The Cuckoo.
