The Man Who Smiled

Author: Henning Mankell | Narrator: Sean Barrett

Runtime: 11h 09m | Genre: Nordic Noir, Police Procedural, Mystery, Crime, Detective

About this Audiobook

On sick leave and drinking himself toward resignation after a fatal line-of-duty shooting, Kurt Wallander reluctantly returns to Ystad headquarters when an old solicitor friend is executed after questioning his father's car crash.

Over Eleven Hours Watching a Broken Detective Claw Back From Grief

Opening in bleak personal fallout after The White Lioness, a depressed, heavy-drinking Kurt Wallander sits months into sick leave convinced his career is finished—until old solicitor friend Sten Torstensson asks him privately to inspect his elderly father's suspicious car crash in fog. Days later, Torstensson himself is shot dead inside his office, jolting Kurt back into Ystad headquarters straight onto a collision course with smiling humanitarian magnate Alfred Harderberg.

*The Man Who Smiled* proves why Nordic Noir endures beyond cheap gore: Kurt's genuine psychological fragility gives every reluctant Police Procedural step deep moral gravity. Watching meticulous Detective instinct confront polished corporate Crime hypocrisy delivers chilly, unforgettable Mystery payoff.

Clocking eleven hours and nine minutes for one Audible credit, Book 4 is tight, incisive, and deeply humane—clearing the path directly toward Wallander's defining masterpiece, Sidetracked.
