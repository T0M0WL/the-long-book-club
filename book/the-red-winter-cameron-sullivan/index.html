About this Audiobook

In snowbound 1785 France on the eve of revolution, a bitter immortal monster-slayer and an enigmatic demon are drawn into the frozen highlands of Gévaudan to hunt a legendary beast—and confront the rot inside a decadent kingdom.

Nearly Seventeen Blood-Soaked Hours Hunting the Beast of Gévaudan in 1785 France

One of Audible’s breakout dark fantasy debuts of 2026, Cameron Sullivan’s *The Red Winter* drops listeners straight into the frostbitten, paranoid highlands of pre-revolutionary France. As the legendary Beast of Gévaudan tears through snowbound villages and decadent aristocrats play dangerous parlor games in the shadow of coming revolution, an immortal, battle-worn monster-slayer and an enigmatic demon form an uneasy partnership to track the terror to its ancient root.

Sullivan balances grim Folk Horror atmosphere and razor-sharp historical detail with wicked wit and kinetic action, creating a Dark Fantasy debut that feels simultaneously gothic, cinematic, and fiercely original. Earning its spotlight in our Best Long Audiobooks of 2026 alongside The Tapestry of Fate and winter folklore classics like The Bear and the Nightingale, it is catnip for fans of moody Historical Fantasy.

At sixteen hours and forty-five minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Red Winter* is lush, bloody, and thoroughly addictive from its opening snowfall.

Narrator Soundcheck: Imogen Church, Rory Barnett

Dual narrators Imogen Church and Rory Barnett turn nearly seventeen hours of frozen French gothic dread into pure audio gold. Church brings velvety poise, biting irony, and chilling intensity, while Barnett nails the gruff, weathered grit of an immortal hunter—giving their alternating chapters sparkling chemistry and relentless late-night momentum.
