A Colossal Forty-Six-Hour Full-Cast Sci-Fi Radio Epic with Over Sixty-Five Voice Actors

In the year 3000, planet Earth has been conquered and stripped of its mineral wealth for a thousand years by the Psychlos—a ruthless, predatory alien empire of nine-foot-tall gas-breathing corporate conquerors. Humanity has been reduced to an endangered species of primitive tribal hunter-gatherers struggling for survival in the Rocky Mountains. When a cunning, ambitious human named Jonnie Goodboy Tyler is captured by the Psychlo security chief Terl to be used as a slave laborer in a secret gold-mining operation, Jonnie secretly learns the aliens' language and technology, organizing a brilliant planetary rebellion to overthrow the invaders and save humanity from total extinction.
