A Gripping Military Campaign of Mountain Clashes, Royal Betrayals, and Uncontrolled Channeling Fury

In *The Path of Daggers*, the eighth volume of *The Wheel of Time*, the grim reality of ruling multiple contentious nations threatens to overwhelm Rand al'Thor. When the foreign, disciplined legions of the Seanchan empire sweep across the western seaboard, Rand leads a combined army of Cairhienin, Illianers, and Asha'man into the foggy defiles of Altara to halt their advance. But as the atmospheric weather twists uncontrollably following the activation of the Bowl of the Winds, Rand discovers that the male half of the Source has become wildly erratic, leading to a catastrophic misuse of the legendary crystal blade Callandor. Meanwhile, Egwene al'Vere executes a brilliant political masterstroke to declare war and march her rebel Aes Sedai on Tar Valon.

Listening to *The Path of Daggers* on audio emphasizes the claustrophobic dread and chaotic fog of war that Robert Jordan crafted with military precision. Michael Kramer delivers a haunting, gritty vocal performance as Rand grapples with physical illness, paranoia, and mutinous Asha'man. Kate Reading shines as Egwene maneuvers around treacherous Sitters and arrogant nobles, transforming courtroom dialogue into a breathless tactical duel.
