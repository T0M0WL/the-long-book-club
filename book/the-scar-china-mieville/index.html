Twenty-Seven Hours of Nautical Steampunk Aboard a Floating City of Lashed-Together Ships

Fleeing the political fallout of New Crobuzon aboard a battered merchant vessel, cold-eyed linguist Bellis Coldwine hopes to outrun her past on the far side of the Swollen Ocean. Instead, pirates seize her ship and press every passenger into Armada: a vast, anarchic floating metropolis built from thousands of salvaged hulls, barges, and war-galleons lashed together across miles of open water. Ruled by rival factions and a mysterious scarred pair known only as the Lovers, Armada is towing its captive population toward a legendary oceanic rift—a literal tear in reality where possibility magic runs wild.

On audio, China Miéville's follow-up to Perdido Street Station is arguably his finest hour—fusing maritime swashbuckling with Steampunk, Seafaring Fantasy, and eerie Cosmic Horror. From vampiric mosquito-folk and Remade sea-surgeons to colossal aquatic leviathans dragged beneath the keel, the sheer sensory richness of Armada comes roaring to life through headphones, standing tall alongside our Steampunk pillars.

At twenty-six hours and fifty-nine minutes for a single Audible credit, *The Scar* delivers unmatched dark maritime imagination—a self-contained masterpiece that expands the Bas-Lag world in breathtaking directions.

Narrator Soundcheck: Gildart Jackson

Stepping in for Book 2, seasoned British actor Gildart Jackson tackles nearly twenty-seven hours of oceanic chaos with theatrical command. Jackson nails Bellis Coldwine's clipped, intellectual detachment while giving swaggering pirates, chittering Anophelii, and the sinister Lovers menacing vocal distinctness against crisp studio mastering.
