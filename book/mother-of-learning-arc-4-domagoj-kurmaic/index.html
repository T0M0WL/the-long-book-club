About this Audiobook

Escaping the pocket dimension back into the real world with only one chance to save Cyoria, Zorian and Zach face Silverlake, Red Robe, and an apocalyptic invasion.

Domagoj Kurmaić's 22-Hour Grand Finale of Real-World Consequences and Apocalyptic Payoffs

Culminating in over twenty-two breathless hours, *Mother of Learning: Arc 4* delivers one of the most satisfying, meticulously plotted series conclusions in modern fantasy history. After hundreds of restarts and nearly a century of subjective training inside the time loop, Zorian and Zach finally break through the Sovereign Gate and emerge back into the real world. But escape is only half the battle: in the real timeline, death is permanent, there are no more resets, and their enemies—including the treacherous Red Robe and a colossal primordial behemoth—are already mobilizing to annihilate the city of Cyoria.

In audio format, Jack Voraces turns this grand finale into a white-knuckle listening experience of staggering emotional resonance. The sudden absence of the reset safety net injects every tactical decision, sacrifice, and confrontation with palpable dread. Hearing Zorian coordinate an entire continent's worth of allies—from reclusive spider swarms to royal military commanders—showcases the magnificent culmination of his growth from a resentful teenage apprentice into a master arcanist. Standing alongside legendary fantasy finales like The Rise of Endymion and Jade Legacy, this concluding volume sticks the landing with flawless precision.

For a single monthly credit, twenty-two hours of *Mother of Learning: Arc 4* is a triumphant payoff that validates every minute of the ninety-seven-hour series journey. Kurmaić avoids lazy deus ex machina resolutions, delivering intelligent, emotionally earned closures for every major character and subplot. Anchored by Jack Voraces’s career-defining performance, this grand finale confirms *Mother of Learning* as an enduring masterpiece of speculative time-loop fiction.
