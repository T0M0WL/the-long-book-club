A Half-Built Garden

Author: Ruthanna Emrys | Narrator: Emily Ellet

Runtime: 15h 50m | Genre: Solarpunk, Science Fiction, First Contact, Cozy Fantasy

About this Audiobook

In 2083, humanity is finally healing the Chesapeake Bay through decentralized watershed networks—when alien emissaries land with plans to evacuate humans from Earth to save the planet.

Sixteen Hours of Hopeful Solarpunk First Contact and Ecological Diplomacy

On a mild March night in 2083, Judy Wallach-Stevens is checking sensor alerts for the Chesapeake Bay Dandelion Network when an alien spaceship lands in the middle of her watershed. Decades after the climate wars, humanity has sidelined nation-states and corporations in favor of self-governing ecological watershed networks that are slowly nursing Earth's rivers and soil back to health. Yet the newly arrived Ringers—eight-legged alien diplomats terrified by the scars of human industrialization—have come to evacuate humanity into space before we ruin the planet again, forcing Judy and her extended two-mother family to prove that humans can live in partnership with their world.

In audio format, Ruthanna Emrys's *A Half-Built Garden* is the ultimate marriage of Cozy Fantasy warmth and rigorous Hard Sci-Fi world-building. Hearing the day-to-day rhythm of consensus voting, nocturnal child-rearing, and intertidal marsh restoration contrasted with alien linguistics makes for a deeply soothing yet intellectually sharp listen. Highlighted on our Solarpunk hub alongside The Ministry for the Future, this nearly sixteen-hour standalone shows Solarpunk at its most human.

Spanning fifteen hours and fifty minutes for one Audible credit, *A Half-Built Garden* is a refreshing antidote to bleak alien-invasion tropes—rewarding listeners with humor, family warmth, and hard-won ecological hope.

Narrator Soundcheck: Emily Ellet

Narrator Emily Ellet delivers a warm, grounded, and remarkably agile performance across nearly sixteen hours. Ellet captures Judy's sleep-deprived maternal wit and fierce protective streak with natural ease, while giving the alien Ringers, corporate holdouts, and global network delegates distinct, expressive vocal personalities. Clean studio production makes every chapter a pleasure.
