The story of a German princess who became the Empress of all the Russias.

Robert K. Massie\u2019s Masterful Twenty-Three-Hour Portrait of Russia\u2019s Enlightenment Empress in Audio

Arriving in imperial Russia as a penniless, fourteen-year-old German princess named Sophia of Anhalt-Zerbst, the young girl who would become Catherine the Great survived a loveless marriage to the neurotic, hostile Grand Duke Peter, outmaneuvered lethal palace rivals, and seized the imperial throne in a daring bloodless military coup. Robert K. Massie\u2019s *Catherine the Great: Portrait of a Woman* tells the spellbinding story of an extraordinary political survivor who transformed Russia into a formidable global superpower while corresponding with Voltaire, amassing Europe\u2019s finest art collection, reforming education, and governing alongside charismatic lovers like Grigory Potemkin during decades of dramatic expansion.
