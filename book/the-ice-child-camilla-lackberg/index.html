About this Audiobook

Stumbling half-frozen out of winter forest onto snowy Fjällbacka tarmac four months after disappearing, an escaped teenage girl reveals unspeakable torture—linking Patrik's hunt for a monster straight to Erica's cold-case prison research.

Over Twelve Hours Tracking Captive Teenagers Into Freezing Winter Terror

Diving far darker after Buried Angels, *The Ice Child* opens on a snow-choked January road outside Fjällbacka where a frostbitten, blinded teenager named Victoria runs out of dark woods straight before an oncoming car—four months after disappearing without trace. Discovering a horrifying trail of vanished young girls, Patrik Hedström hunts a sadistic collector while Erica independently investigates a tragic domestic murder prison file hiding uncanny links back to the abductor.

*The Ice Child* cranks classic coastal Nordic Noir tension to breathless heights. Balancing chilling serial-abductor Crime alongside tireless Police Procedural detective work turns snowy Bohuslän into an urgent Detective and Mystery trap.

At twelve hours and twenty-three minutes for one credit, Book 9 delivers sharp psychological bite right before Läckberg's sweeping centerpiece, The Girl in the Woods.
