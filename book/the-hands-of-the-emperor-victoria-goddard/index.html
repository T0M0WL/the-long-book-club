About this Audiobook

The dedicated personal secretary to an immortal living god invites his lonely master on a quiet holiday to his tropical island home, forever reshaping an empire.

A Monumental Thirty-One-Hour Cozy Epic of Deep Empathy, Bureaucratic Competence, and Lifelong Friendship

Victoria Goddard’s sprawling, singular masterpiece accomplishes something truly astonishing in modern fantasy literature: it builds a monumental, thirty-one-hour high-stakes epic entirely around administrative competence, restorative rest, and profound interpersonal connection. Kip, the Lord Chancellor of the World and right hand to the divine, god-like Emperor of Astandalas, has spent nearly half a century managing the planetary logistics of an empire recovering from magical catastrophe. Exhausted by decades of selfless service and burdened by the suffocating, crushing etiquette of a palace court that views their monarch as an untouchable living divinity, Kip quietly arranges for the solitary, deeply lonely emperor to take a simple, unprecedented secret vacation to Kip's remote Pacific-style island homeland, setting off a quiet emotional ripple effect that will remake civilization.

On audio, the deliberate, immersive cadence of Goddard’s prose becomes a hypnotic, deeply therapeutic headphone retreat. While many fantasy epics rely on breathless sword fights and world-ending military skirmishes, Kip’s story champions the quiet bravery of drafting humanitarian budgets, reforming exploitative taxation codes, and extending genuine hospitality to a lonely sovereign. Showcased as the most expansive behemoth in our Cozy Fantasy Collection and standing proudly alongside philosophical masterpieces in our Longest Ever Audiobooks library, this performance turns bureaucratic benevolence into an addictive, heart-filling drama that will hold you spellbound across dozens of hours.

Offering over thirty-one hours of exquisitely written fiction for a single credit, this recording delivers extraordinary long-term entertainment value. Goddard avoids cheap melodrama, choosing instead to explore the profound beauty of adult friendship, the healing power of returning to one’s ancestral roots, and the radical grace of institutional empathy. Investing a single monthly credit in this gentle colossus rewards patient listeners with an unforgettable, life-affirming journey that redefines what epic fantasy can achieve, proving that genuine emotional warmth and monumental scale can walk hand in hand.

Narrator Soundcheck: Niel Hellegers

Niel Hellegers delivers a masterclass in measured, resonant storytelling, perfectly embodying Kip’s soft-spoken wisdom, diplomatic grace, and underlying emotional fatigue. Hellegers commands Goddard’s enormous, ceremonial court vocabulary with effortless fluidity, distinguishing between haughty imperial aristocrats and earthy island elders with subtle shifts in cadence and warmth. His pacing across thirty-one hours is impeccably sustained, maintaining an intimate, fireside atmosphere that never flags. With crystal-clear audio mastering and rich vocal presence, this performance elevates Goddard’s gentle epic to the highest echelon of audio literature.
