About this Audiobook

An unloved, half-goblin fourth son unexpectedly inherits the imperial throne after an airship crash, choosing empathy and kindness to navigate treacherous court intrigue.

The Masterpiece of Courtly Intrigue Where Empathy, Patience, and Decency Triumph in Beautiful Audio

Maia, the half-goblin, half-elven youngest son of the Emperor, has lived his entire youth in miserable, isolated exile, routinely beaten and emotionally abused by his bitter guardian in a decaying rural estate. His desolate life is abruptly upended when a catastrophic, mysterious airship disaster kills the Emperor and all three elder heirs, thrusting an unprepared, terrified eighteen-year-old Maia onto the labyrinthine throne of the Ethuveraz as the new emperor. Surrounded by haughty elven courtiers who despise his dark goblin skin, treacherous imperial advisers who quietly plot his assassination, and an impenetrable web of archaic court etiquette, Maia must navigate deadly political minefields using only his innate honesty, profound patience, and refusal to surrender his tender humanity to cruelty.

On audio, Katherine Addison’s intricate court drama becomes an extraordinarily intimate, emotionally gripping experience. The novel's notoriously complex elven names, formal titling conventions, and archaic plural pronouns—which can sometimes stall readers on the printed page—flow with effortless natural music when spoken aloud. Highlighted prominently as an essential masterpiece in our Cozy Fantasy Collection alongside celebrated courtly and bureaucratic epics like The Hands of the Emperor and grand sagas in our Best Fiction Audiobooks catalog, this performance transforms courtly survival into an uplifting, unforgettable study of institutional kindness and quiet moral strength.

Spanning nearly twelve hours of peerless courtly world-building and psychological intimacy, this unabridged release offers magnificent credit value for lovers of character-driven fantasy. Addison sidesteps standard magical battles in favor of nuanced political maneuvering and quiet personal connections, showing how a single leader’s empathy and basic human fairness can dismantle decades of systemic malice. Exchanging a single monthly credit for this beloved modern classic guarantees a deeply touching, suspenseful listening journey that lingers in the heart long after the final audience concludes, standing as one of the most rewarding and re-listenable audiobooks in modern fantasy.

Narrator Soundcheck: Kyle McCarley

Kyle McCarley gives a sublime, emotionally nuanced performance that handles Addison’s intricate elven linguism and formal Court etiquette with breathtaking finesse. McCarley captures Maia’s gentle vulnerability, panic-stricken isolation, and blossoming inner resolve with heartbreaking authenticity, making listeners fiercely root for his survival. His vocal differentiation across dozens of scheming ministers, venomous noblemen, and loyal bodyguards like Cala is impeccably clear and distinct. With pristine acoustic balance, flawless diction, and steady dramatic pacing, this eleven-hour narration is an acoustic work of art that elevates the novel’s quiet triumph.
