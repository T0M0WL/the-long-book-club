About this Audiobook

In the thirty-hour grand finale of the Hyperion Cantos, Aenea and Raul confront the Core's digital extermination and the Void Which Binds.

Cosmic Transcendence, The Void Which Binds, and the Hyperion Climax

Clocking in at just under thirty monumental hours, *The Rise of Endymion* brings Dan Simmons’ four-volume Hyperion Cantos to a heartbreaking, intellectually awe-inspiring conclusion. Winner of the Locus Award for Best Science Fiction Novel, this massive finale resolves every lingering mystery left by Hyperion, The Fall of Hyperion, and Endymion—from the true origins and purpose of the bladed Shrike to the terrifying hidden agenda of the TechnoCore and the nature of the cosmic medium known as the 'Void Which Binds.'

Now grown into a charismatic young woman, Aenea travels across the galaxy as a radical teacher, offering humanity a shared communion that allows them to hear the music of the spheres and travel without TechnoCore farsasters. In response, the corrupt Vatican Pax, led by the fanatical Pope Julius XIV and manipulated by Core parasites, constructs an apocalyptic armada of death-ships armed with the 'Archangel' drive—a hyper-dimensional propulsion system that instantly kills its crew on every jump and relies on the cruciform to resurrect them in agony. As Raul Endymion and Aenea prepare for a tragic, inevitable martyrdom, the narrative reaches heights of emotional poignancy, philosophical grandeur, and romantic sorrow rarely matched in science fiction.

Victor Bevine’s narration across thirty hours is nothing short of sublime. Bevine handles the sprawling cosmological concepts, intimate farewells, and explosive space battles with total artistic control, delivering the novel’s devastating emotional resolution with unforgettable grace. For listeners who have journeyed through the earlier ninety hours of the Cantos, *The Rise of Endymion* is an essential, life-changing listening experience that validates the entire epic as one of the greatest achievements in modern literature.

Narrator Soundcheck: Victor Bevine

Victor Bevine’s vocal performance on The Rise of Endymion is a masterclass in long-form dramatic stamina. Narrating nearly thirty hours of complex philosophical debate, theological intrigue, and emotional devastation, Bevine never falters. His depiction of Raul’s grief and enduring devotion is deeply touching, while his chilling voicing of the Core's biomechanical assassin Nemesis radiates ruthless lethality. The sound staging is pristine, giving the novel’s climactic revelations an acoustic majesty that leaves listeners completely awestruck.
