About this Audiobook

Hadrian Marlowe journeys into deep uncharted space to make contact with the alien Cielcin, encountering cybernetic monstrosities and cosmic terrors.

Heart of Darkness in the Stars: Samuel Roukin Mesmerizes Across 31 Cosmic Hours

In *Howling Dark*, Christopher Ruocchio pulls off a staggering escalation in both scope and terror, transforming the Sun Eater saga into a profound meditation on the boundaries of humanity. Refusing to accept that the predatory, xenocidal alien Cielcin cannot be reasoned with, Hadrian Marlowe spends decades in cryo-sleep traversing the uncharted galactic rim. His desperate search for peaceful contact leads him instead to the Extrasolarians—a terrifying offshoot of humanity that has merged with cybernetic AI monstrosities—and ancient, reality-warping entities slumbering in the abyss.

Narrator Samuel Roukin delivers a vocal performance of sublime elegance and philosophical gravitas. Roukin’s voice possesses a dark, aristocratic Shakespearean cadence that elevates Ruocchio’s lush literary prose into pure auditory poetry. The encounters with Extrasolarian cyborgs and the nightmarish first dialogues with the Cielcin chiefs are rendered with skin-crawling tension. Listeners who were enraptured by the grand planetary majesty of Empire of Silence or the existential terror of Hyperion will find this audiobook hypnotic.

At over thirty-one hours for a single monthly credit, *Howling Dark* is an extraordinary value proposition. It bridges grand space fleet operations with cosmic horror and deep philosophical inquiry, cementing Ruocchio as one of the finest worldbuilders of our time.
