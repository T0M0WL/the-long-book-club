About this Audiobook

Haunted by genocide and consumed by vengeance, Rin aligns with the Dragon Warlord to overthrow the Empress in a war of catastrophic naval battles.

Brutal Military Fantasy: Emily Woo Zeller Delivers a Masterclass in Grief, Fire, and War

*The Dragon Republic* avoids the conventional pitfalls of middle volumes to deliver a military fantasy of ferocious psychological depth and devastating tactical scope. In the harrowing wake of the Third Poppy War, shaman warrior Rin is shattered by guilt, addicted to opium to suppress the vengeful Phoenix god burning in her veins, and consumed by a single obsessive goal: assassinating the Empress Su Daji. Joining forces with the charismatic Dragon Warlord Yin Vaisra, Rin leads the Cike into a brutal nationwide civil war that pits indigenous shamans and peasant levies against modernized European weaponry and catastrophic river blockades.

Narrator Emily Woo Zeller gives a tour-de-force performance that is impossible to look away from. Her portrayal of Rin is raw, volatile, and profoundly human; she captures the protagonist's descent into paranoia, grief, and ruthless military calculation with visceral honesty. The naval skirmishes and amphibious sieges along the Murui River crackle with thunderous audio momentum. Listeners who were floored by the dark historical intensity of The Poppy War or the intellectual fury of Babel will find this audiobook utterly devastating.

Clocking in at nearly twenty-four hours for a single credit, this production represents an exceptional investment for listeners who appreciate complex antiheroes and uncompromising historical parallels. Kuang pulls no punches regarding the catastrophic cost of warfare, and Zeller ensures every tragedy lands with unforgettable force.
