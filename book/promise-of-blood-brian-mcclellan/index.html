About this Audiobook

Field Marshal Tamas stages a bloody military coup against his corrupt monarch, only to trigger a desperate battle against foreign empires, ancient gods, and treachery within his own ranks.

A Masterpiece of Gunpowder Sorcery, High-Stakes Detective Work, and Revolutionary Bloodshed

Brian McClellan\\u2019s 2013 debut, *Promise of Blood*, pioneered the explosive "flintlock fantasy" movement by pairing 18th-century French Revolutionary aesthetics with visceral grimdark action. The novel opens not with the slow buildup to rebellion, but with its brutal culmination: Field Marshal Tamas leads an armed coup against the decadent King of Adro, executing the royal court by firing squad and guillotine to prevent the nation from being sold into foreign slavery. Yet seizing power proves far simpler than holding it, as neighboring superpower Kez prepares for total invasion and royalist loyalists wage urban guerrilla warfare.

The audio edition of *Promise of Blood* is propelled to legendary status by the gravelly, battle-hardened narration of veteran British actor Christian Rodska. McClellan\\u2019s magic system is breathtakingly inventive: "powder mages" who ingest and burn black powder to alter bullet trajectories, enhance physical reflexes, and trigger remote detonations. Rodska gives distinct, unforgettable life to Tamas\\u2019s immense military burden and private grief, his marksman son Taniel Two-Shot\\u2019s lethal sharpshooter calculations and addiction to powder, and retired police inspector Adamat\\u2019s gritty procedural investigations. If you love the tactical military discipline of The Black Company or the ruthless street-level gang warfare in Priest of Bones, this 19-hour listen is pure adrenaline.

At 19 hours and 28 minutes, *Promise of Blood* delivers colossal return on investment for an Audible credit. McClellan balances grand geopolitical maneuvering with intimate, blood-spattered duels where every bullet counts and every sorcerous expenditure drains the mind. It marries the breathless pacing of a military thriller with the intricate puzzle-solving of a locked-room murder mystery and the epic scale of divine warfare. McClellan brilliantly explores the volatile aftermath of revolution, showing how easily the noble overthrow of a tyrant can descend into martial terror and foreign invasion. An absolute triumph of modern fantasy that will ignite your imagination with the smell of black powder, bayonet steel, and revolutionary blood.
