About this Audiobook

Saeris Fane inadvertently reopens a gateway between realms, transporting her to a land of ice and snow. There, she binds herself to Kingfisher, a Fae warrior with his own secrets, in this addictive enemies-to-lovers phenomenon.

Callie Hart's 23-Hour Viral Romantasy of Desert Quicksand, Fae Princes, and Scorching Enemies-to-Lovers Fire

The runaway viral Romantasy phenomenon of the year, Callie Hart’s *Quicksilver* is a pulse-pounding, twenty-three-hour fantasy adventure of desert survival, fae politics, and undeniable romantic obsession. Saeris Fane, a fierce and pragmatic twenty-four-year-old scavenger scraping by in the impoverished mortal city of Rekk, discovers that she harbors forbidden magical blood that makes her a valuable prize. When a bizarre mishap pulls her into the deadly, freezing fae realm of the Nightlands, she is captured by Kingfisher, a brooding, morally grey fae warrior prince with deadly secrets of his own. Forced into a reluctant alliance across hostile sands, their mutual hostility rapidly ignites into a scorching, undeniable enemies-to-lovers slow burn, testing their loyalties as ancient shadows gather across the scorching mortal dunes and icy fae courts.

In audio format, Hart’s snappy, contemporary banter and high-stakes survival challenges come alive with irresistible dramatic energy. The spoken performance highlights the hilarious friction between Saeris’s sharp-tongued defiance and Kingfisher’s arrogant, overprotective fae swagger, making hours of listening fly by in an instant. Positioned as a standout favorite in our curated Epic Romantasy collection alongside its sequel Brimstone, this recording provides the expansive narrative room needed for the ancient political conflicts and simmering physical chemistry to mature with maximum emotional payoff, allowing the dangerous wilderness treks and high-heat romantic moments to develop with irresistible charm.

Spending a single credit on twenty-three and a half hours of *Quicksilver* offers unmatched escapist entertainment for fans of fast-paced romantic fantasy. The book masterfully blends dangerous magical trials, laugh-out-loud dialogue, and high-heat romantic tension that will keep you eagerly binge-listening through every free moment. It is an addictive, utterly charming audio adventure that proves why Callie Hart has become an overnight sensation in the genre, delivering hours of breathless excitement, laugh-out-loud banter, and pure romantic escapism, cementing Callie Hart as an essential new voice in epic fantasy romance.

Narrator Soundcheck: Stella Bloom, Anthony Palmini

Stella Bloom and Anthony Palmini deliver a tour de force dual narration that crackles with explosive romantic chemistry and comedic timing. Bloom voices Saeris with defiant grit, street-smart swagger, and endearing vulnerability, making her survival struggles deeply compelling. Anthony Palmini provides a rich, devastatingly attractive gravelly baritone for Kingfisher, delivering the fae warrior’s arrogant taunts and protective declarations with effortless charisma. The audio production is clean, crisp, and dynamically balanced throughout all twenty-three hours.
