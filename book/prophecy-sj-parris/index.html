Fourteen and a Half Hours of Occult Court Conspiracy in Elizabethan London

Following his explosive university investigation in Heresy, exiled philosopher-monk Giordano Bruno has settled into the French ambassador's London household in the autumn of 1583—watching court astrologer John Dee and Catholic sympathizers whisper about a looming planetary alignment. When a terrified young serving maid is murdered on Elizabeth I's palace grounds with bizarre alchemical sigils carved into her skin, spymaster Sir Francis Walsingham sends Bruno undercover to infiltrate a circle of aristocratic plotters backing Mary, Queen of Scots.

In audio format, *Prophecy* widens S.J. Parris's canvas from Oxford's quiet quadrangles to Thameside mansions, candlelit seances, and Tudor London's perilous alleyways. Blending gripping Historical Mystery with treacherous Political Intrigue and classic Whodunnit sleuthing, the novel illuminates how seriously Renaissance kings and spymasters feared magic, star charts, and banned grimoires.

Clocking in at fourteen hours and thirty-nine minutes for a single Audible credit, *Prophecy* is fast-moving Historical Fiction thick with superstition, cipher letters, and genuine danger.
