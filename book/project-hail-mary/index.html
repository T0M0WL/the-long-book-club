About this Audiobook

A lone surviving astronaut awakens with amnesia on an interstellar mission to save Earth from a star-eating organism, only to forge an extraordinary alliance with an unexpected alien engineer.

Andy Weir's Audie-Winning Masterpiece of Interstellar Problem Solving and Unforgettable Interspecies Chemistry

Ryland Grace awakens aboard the Hail Mary with complete amnesia, surrounded by two dead crewmates and millions of miles from Earth. Slowly piecing together his identity as a disgraced molecular biologist turned junior-high science teacher, Grace realizes he was drafted into an apocalyptic suicide mission: a microscopic space organism dubbed Astrophage is breeding across the sun's surface, cooling our star and dooming terrestrial life to global famine within decades. Traveling twelve light-years to the Tau Ceti system—the only nearby star mysteriously immune to the infection—Grace must conduct high-stakes experiments to isolate a cure, turning scientific inquiry, duct tape, and physics equations into breathless survival suspense.

What elevates *Project Hail Mary* from a clever hard sci-fi thriller into one of the most celebrated audiobooks of the twenty-first century is its revolutionary auditory presentation. When Grace encounters the *Blip-A*, an alien vessel piloted by an arachnid-like engineer from 40 Eridani named Rocky, Andy Weir confronts the ultimate first-contact puzzle. Rather than relying on telepathy or universal translators, Rocky communicates through tonal chords and harmonic frequencies. Audible’s brilliant sound design synthesizes two-tone musical chords layered beneath Ray Porter’s vocal translations, creating an auditory language that feels utterly magical. The escalating camaraderie, mutual loyalty, and emotional warmth between Grace and Rocky rivals the beloved character chemistry found in Dungeon Crawler Carl and the deep-space wonder of Children of Time, proving that genuine scientific optimism and interspecies love make for unforgettable listening.
