About this Audiobook

Just as Kurt Wallander returns from mourning his elderly father in Rome, a string of seemingly unconnected murders—an elderly birdwatcher impaled in bamboo stakes and an orchard owner strangled—forces Ystad police to hunt a patient vigilante.

Nearly Fifteen Hours Hunting a Methodical Killer Targeting Everyday Victims

Returning from his father's final trip to Rome after Sidetracked, Kurt Wallander steps into Ystad autumn amid troubling omens: four African nuns executed in a brutal monastery massacre abroad, followed swiftly back in Scania by respectable elderly men dying inside bizarrely patient, elaborate traps—an ornithologist impaled on sharpened bamboo stakes, an orchardist strangled cleanly at dusk.

In audio format, *The Fifth Woman* digs relentlessly into questions of vigilante justice at the bruised core of Nordic Noir. Balancing grueling Police Procedural dead-ends with chilling killer viewpoints makes this patient Crime and Detective hunt agonizingly tense—while Kurt's private grief over his father anchors every Mystery beat in poignant truth.

At fourteen hours and forty-eight minutes for a single Audible credit, Book 6 showcases Mankell at fierce creative height ahead of One Step Behind.
