About this Audiobook

Suspended from active duty, Joona Linna observes a savage hammer killing at a secluded home for troubled teenage girls while a con-artist psychic claims genuine visions of bloodied sand and an escaped suspect.

Nearly Fourteen Hours Inside a Haunted Swedish Youth Home Covered in Secrets

Temporarily suspended pending internal affairs inquiry after The Nightmare, Joona Linna is dispatched solely as an observer when an eighteen-year-old girl is found bludgeoned to death at Birgittagården—a remote rural residential home for troubled teenage girls. Evidence points squarely toward another vanished resident spotted fleeing through rain-soaked woods with a stolen hammer, until an eccentric con-artist spiritualist named Flora Hansen contacts police claiming genuine psychic visions of dark red sand under the victim's fingernails.

Listening to *The Fire Witness* turns up the oppressive gothic chill of Kepler's Nordic Noir formula. Setting the hunt inside an isolated institutional sanctuary lets bleak Police Procedural legwork collide with unsettling Psychological Thriller paranoia and haunting Mystery clues. As Joona peels back systemic cruelty and unspoken loyalty among vulnerable youths, every brutal Crime and Detective revelation lands with real emotional ache.

At thirteen hours and fifty-six minutes for one credit, *The Fire Witness* offers tight, unrelenting nocturnal listening while quietly planting the terrifying personal stakes that explode in The Sandman.
