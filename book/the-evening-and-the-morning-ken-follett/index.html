About this Audiobook

The monumental Dark Ages prequel to The Pillars of the Earth, chronicling the birth of Kingsbridge amidst Viking raids and feudal tyranny.

The Dawn of Kingsbridge: John Lee Narrates Follett's Riveting Dark Ages Prequel

In *The Evening and the Morning*, Ken Follett takes listeners back to 997 AD, the end of the Dark Ages in England, to reveal how the humble hamlet of Dreng's Ferry was forged into the bustling medieval priory town of Kingsbridge. England is besieged from without by savage Viking longships and terrorized from within by corrupt noblemen and unscrupulous church leaders who exploit the absence of the rule of law. Follett weaves the fates of three resilient protagonists together: a gifted young boatbuilder whose home is razed by raiders; a spirited Norman noblewoman navigating a loveless marriage of political convenience; and an idealistic monk fighting to preserve scholarship against ecclesiastical greed.

Narrator John Lee is in complete command of this twenty-four-hour historical epic. Lee’s distinguished, resonant British baritone breathes authentic historical gravity into every muddy road, timbered bridge construction, and brutal judicial ordeal. Follett's trademark narrative engine—pitting honest craftsmanship and intellectual enlightenment against feudal cruelty—grips the listener from the opening raid. Fans of The Pillars of the Earth and World Without End will savor discovering the origins of the sacred ground they love.

At over twenty-four hours for a single credit, *The Evening and the Morning* is an unbeatable investment for historical fiction enthusiasts. It is an immersive, beautifully paced journey that turns historical struggle into pure listening pleasure.
