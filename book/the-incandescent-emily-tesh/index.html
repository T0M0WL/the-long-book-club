Emily Tesh Flips the Magic School Trope in a Twelve-Hour Faculty-Side Dark Academia Thriller

Most dark academia novels follow wide-eyed freshmen discovering that their elite school wants to kill them. In *The Incandescent*, Hugo Award winner Emily Tesh flips the desk around: you are following Doctor Saffy Walden, the exhausted, fiercely competent Director of Magic at Chetwood Academy—a centuries-old English boarding school where six hundred hormonally charged teenagers are taught to summon demons without getting eaten. Between managing endowment politics, grammar-school rivalries, and a prickly linguistic specialist, Saffy realizes that a catastrophic security breach is already inside the campus walls.

Listening to *The Incandescent* on audio is an absolute treat for anyone who loves the institutional mechanics of Babel or Katabasis paired with razor-sharp British wit. Instead of teenage angst, you get the gripping competence of an expert administrator conducting high-risk demonic containment while trying to drink a single hot cup of tea. Featured in our Dark Academia Collection, this twelve-and-a-half-hour standalone brings fresh, grown-up perspective to magical academia.

Spanning twelve hours and twenty-six minutes for a single credit, *The Incandescent* packs rigorous demonology rules, smart class critique, and heart-pounding magical defense into a tightly paced, deeply satisfying listen.

Narrator Soundcheck: Zara Ramm

Veteran British narrator Zara Ramm—celebrated for the *Chronicles of St Mary's*—is pitch-perfect as Dr. Saffy Walden. Ramm delivers brisk, exasperated authority during faculty meetings and teenage emergencies, then pivots seamlessly into chilling intensity when demonic incursions strike Chetwood's wards. Her comedic timing and crisp regional accents make all twelve and a half hours fly by.
