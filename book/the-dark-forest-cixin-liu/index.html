About this Audiobook

The Trisolaran fleet is on its way. In response, Earth forms the Wallfacer Project, granting four individuals near-infinite resources to design secret strategies hidden from the sophons that monitor all human communication.

The Terrifying Cosmic Realpolitik of the Fermi Paradox and the Wallfacers in Audio

With the hostile Trisolaran fleet four centuries away from Earth and sophons actively intercepting every human communication and scientific breakthrough, humanity faces an impossible strategic dilemma: how do you prepare for war when the enemy can hear every thought you utter aloud? In response, the United Nations establishes the Wallfacer Project, granting four individuals absolute global authority and unlimited resources to construct covert defense plans locked entirely within the impenetrable vaults of their own minds. Confronting the existential despair of defeatism and the collapse of space fleet morale, rival Wallfacers pursue catastrophic gambits while their designated Wallbreakers dismantle each deception with chilling intellectual cruelty, leaving cynical sociologist Luo Ji as humanity's final, desperate defense against impending annihilation.

On audio, *The Dark Forest* delivers some of the most heart-stopping, psychologically gripping sequences anywhere in modern science fiction. Hearing Luo Ji’s solitary philosophical contemplations, the haunting winter of the post-crisis era, the terrifying, mirror-smooth arrival of the deadly Trisolaran droplet probe, and the instantaneous, devastating annihilation of the combined human space fleet in Jupiter orbital space creates an auditory spectacle that will leave your jaw on the floor. Linked as the undisputed high-water mark of our Science Fiction Audiobooks alongside The Three-Body Problem and Death's End, this twenty-two-hour recording turns interstellar realpolitik into a chilling headphone masterpiece.
