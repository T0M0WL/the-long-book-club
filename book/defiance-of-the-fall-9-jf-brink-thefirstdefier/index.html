About this Audiobook

Entering the treacherous Twilight Ocean, Zac navigates a sunken cosmic realm of forgotten gods and abyssal leviathans to unlock the secrets of Creation and Oblivion.

The Twilight Ocean: Pavi Proczko Commands 22.5 Hours of Abyssal Horrors and Primordial Daos

*Defiance of the Fall 9* shifts the saga into an atmospheric, cosmic aquatic realm: the mysterious Twilight Ocean. Hidden away in an unstable dimensional pocket, this sunken graveyard of dead star systems and forgotten gods is filled with primordial miasma, abyssal leviathans, and ancient ruins holding fragments of pure Creation and Oblivion. Seeking resources to stabilize his expanding soul space and advance his bloodlines, Zac Atwood must navigate treacherous oceanic currents while hunted by rival Hegemonic forces.

Pavi Proczko brings an eerie, atmospheric acoustic depth to this oceanic expedition. Proczko’s measured, gravelly cadence captures the crushing hydrostatic pressure, bioluminescent wonders, and ancient cosmic dread of the Twilight Ocean. His narration of underwater melee duels—where Zac uses gravity and void fields to cleave massive abyssal serpents in half—is rendered with visceral clarity. Fans who loved the deep cosmic exploration of Heretics of Dune will find this volume rich and evocative.

At twenty-two and a half hours for a single credit, this ninth volume is a refreshing, imaginative change of pace that expands the lore of the multiverse. Brink balances mysterious archaeology and cosmic horror with thrilling progression milestones, ensuring every chapter remains deeply engaging. It is a taut, expertly crafted listening experience that progression enthusiasts will adore.
