About this Audiobook

Germany's deadliest field operative uncovers the Allied D-Day deception and races to escape Britain, only to be shipwrecked on a storm-lashed Scottish island.

The Ruthless Wartime Cat-and-Mouse Duel That Redefined WWII Thrillers

Spanning eleven white-knuckle hours, *Eye of the Needle* is Ken Follett’s breakout Edgar Award-winning espionage masterpiece, an electrifying World War II procedural that pits a ruthless Nazi master spy against an unsuspecting Scottish island household. Set in the tense spring of 1944, Allied forces are preparing for Operation Overlord, employing an elaborate deception campaign featuring plywood dummy tanks and fake radio traffic in East Anglia to convince the German High Command that the invasion will land at the Pas-de-Calais rather than Normandy. Only one German agent embedded in Britain sees through the illusion: Henry Faber, code-named 'Die Nadel' (The Needle), an icy, calculating assassin who kills cleanly with a stiletto.

Armed with photographs that could betray the entire D-Day invasion and alter the outcome of World War II, Faber flees north toward a rendezvous with a waiting German U-boat. What begins as a nationwide manhunt directed by MI5 counter-espionage officers turns intensely personal when a furious North Sea storm shipwrecks Faber on Storm Island, an isolated, wave-lashed rocky outcrop off the Scottish coast. There, he finds himself trapped alongside a disabled former pilot and his lonely, resilient wife Lucy, triggering a claustrophobic, high-stakes game of cat-and-mouse where one woman's courage determines the future of Western democracy.

For audiobook enthusiasts seeking historical authenticity, white-knuckle suspense, and exceptional credit value on a single token, *Eye of the Needle* is an absolute essential. Acclaimed British actor Samuel West delivers a masterclass in atmospheric storytelling, balancing the clinical menace of Faber with the desperate grit of wartime Britain. Beside companion intelligence epics like The Day of the Jackal and Restless, this eleven-hour tour de force is an enduring pillar of Historical Fiction and wartime espionage that rewards every second of listening with profound satisfaction, delivering an edge-of-your-seat climax that showcases classic British thriller craft at its absolute zenith.

Narrator Soundcheck: Samuel West

Samuel West delivers a masterclass in psychological tension and atmospheric wartime drama. West brings a smooth, cultured menace to Heinrich Faber, masking the assassin's lethal sociopathy behind a mild, polite English facade that makes his sudden acts of brutality intensely chilling. His vocal handling of Lucy Rose is filled with poignant vulnerability and fierce courage, while his regional British and German accents remain impeccable throughout. West sustains relentless momentum across eleven hours, turning the climactic island duel into unforgettable listening.
